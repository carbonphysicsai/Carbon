# 1. Executive summary

Carbon is building the **discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models**.

The company addresses a growing gap in engineering-AI adoption: organizations can increasingly build fast surrogates, learned operators, reduced-order models, and hybrid physical models, but they still struggle to answer four expensive questions:

1. Which construction approach should we use?
2. Does the resulting model actually survive the physics and important regimes?
3. Can this exact artifact support this exact engineering job?
4. What remains valid after the model, data, solver, runtime, or context changes?

Carbon's business model is deliberately hybrid:

```text
SERVICES
land high-value technical customers
        ↓
PRODUCTIZED SERVICES
repeatable Evidence Audits / Discovery / Qualification
        ↓
ENTERPRISE PLATFORM
recurring workflows, lifecycle, registry, APIs
        ↓
NETWORK MARKETPLACE
scale external discovery / optimizer supply
        ↓
QUALIFICATION + LIFECYCLE
attach recurring high-value evidence services
        ↓
PHYSICS INTELLIGENCE
higher-margin decision products when prospectively proven
```

The company must stand on its own without relying on speculative Alpha appreciation. The network is a differentiated scaling layer and economic marketplace for useful scientific work.

---

# 2. Problem

Fast physical models can unlock large gains in:

- repeated simulation;
- optimization;
- uncertainty studies;
- control;
- digital twins;
- agentic engineering;
- design-space exploration.

But adoption is constrained by a credibility/discovery/lifecycle bottleneck.

Traditional validation is often bespoke and internal. Model-development teams may grade their own artifacts. Public benchmarks can be overfit. High average accuracy can obscure physical failure. A model that was credible for one version or envelope can become stale after changes.

The commercial opportunity is therefore not only to build more models. It is to provide infrastructure for **independent model discovery, evidence, bounded qualification, and lifecycle management**.

# 4. Product portfolio

## 4.1 Carbon Evidence Audit — first wedge

Customer brings an existing fast physical model.

Carbon provides:
- problem/claim definition;
- independent exam authoring;
- truth/reference integration;
- protected evaluation;
- physical/robustness/accuracy evidence;
- failure/stratum report;
- evidence/provenance bundle;
- recommendation for remediation, narrowing, further qualification, or rejection of the stated claim.

A negative scientific result is still a successful Audit delivery if the agreed evidence was produced honestly.

## 4.2 Challenge Design / Feasibility

Customer wants to know whether a physical modeling problem can become a valid Carbon discovery/evidence program.

Deliverables:
- task definition;
- input/output contract;
- envelope/population proposal;
- truth-access feasibility;
- privacy/rights assessment;
- evidence/qualification path;
- program scope/budget recommendation.

## 4.3 Sponsored Discovery Program

Customer funds competitive search on a qualified physical problem.

Carbon monetizes:
- Challenge authoring;
- truth integration;
- program/platform fee;
- evaluation usage;
- reward administration;
- follow-on candidate development;
- qualification/deployment/lifecycle.

Sponsor-funded participant rewards are separately accounted.

## 4.4 Model Development Program

Carbon uses its search, reconstruction, and evidence infrastructure to deliver a strong candidate artifact/method.

Commercial delivery does not automatically imply Product Qualification.

## 4.5 Qualified Model Program

Carbon takes one exact candidate through a deeper job-shaped evidence path.

Deliverable package can include:
- exact artifact identity;
- Product Battery Record;
- bounded Qualification Record;
- operating limitations;
- deployment/runtime identity;
- answerability/escalation policy;
- requalification triggers.

## 4.6 Model Lifecycle

Recurring service/product layer for:
- change intake;
- drift detection where applicable;
- version tracking;
- requalification;
- envelope expansion;
- evidence updates;
- support/escalation.

## 4.7 Enterprise Evidence Platform

Longer-term recurring platform for internal customer teams:
- private evidence workspaces;
- model/evidence registry;
- qualified truth adapters;
- evaluation orchestration;
- qualification lifecycle;
- audit/export APIs;
- VPC/on-prem deployment;
- usage commitments and enterprise support.

## 4.8 API / OEM Evidence Rail

External engineering platforms build or deliver models; Carbon supplies independent evidence/qualification infrastructure.

Revenue:
- annual license;
- per-evaluation usage;
- OEM royalty;
- support.

## 4.9 Frontier Market

Sponsors fund qualified scientific frontiers and external researchers/agents compete for verified advances.

This is the network-marketplace end state, not a prerequisite for first revenue.

## 4.10 Physics Intelligence

Later product family only after prospective decision lift is demonstrated.

Possible products:
- method recommendations;
- transfer prediction;
- failure-risk prediction;
- experiment allocation;
- qualification prioritization.


# 15. Principal business risks

1. Carbon remains a bespoke consulting company.
2. customers can validate internally at lower cost/value loss than Carbon offers.
3. integration/privacy/IP complexity slows enterprise deployment.
4. product qualification is too expensive to scale.
5. network fails to outperform centralized search economically.
6. evidence corpus cannot compound because rights are too restrictive.
7. sales cycles are too long relative to ACV.
8. platformization arrives before customer workflows are understood.
9. token/network complexity distracts buyers/investors.
10. Physics Intelligence is marketed before earning prospective validity.

Each risk should have an explicit test in the business roadmap.

# 16. Strategic objective

> **Build the independent infrastructure layer that helps engineering organizations discover better fast physical models, establish what evidence supports, and maintain that evidence as systems change — while progressively converting expert delivery into scalable software, network supply, and recurring lifecycle revenue.**
