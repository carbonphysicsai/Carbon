## State and migration

The additive v0.10 workspace stores the observation in the same design
assessment used by the client and internal briefs. Changes to physics, goal,
inputs, outputs, units, geometry, conditions, regime, exclusions, workload,
rights, or reference scope make the observation stale while retaining the
historical bytes. A commercial/editorial change leaves it current. A child
revision keeps the parent's history and starts with no returned observation.

On workspace import, the browser reruns the accepted structural function and
recomputes the exact identity. An exact-scope altered result rejects atomically;
a changed scope is shown as stale. v0.9 placeholders migrate to
available-not-requested or still-pending states and never become returned
observations.
