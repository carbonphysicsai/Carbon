# Carbon Goal-to-Challenge Workbench v0.5 specification

## v0.5 routing, owner console, and applicability

Every design revision carries one closed planning route: `UNASSESSED`, `USE_EXISTING_CAPABILITY`, `ADAPT_SUPPORTED_CHALLENGE`, or `DEVELOP_NEW_CAPABILITY`. Route selection is a local planning assertion for that exact revision. A sealed revision cannot be changed; a child revision begins `UNASSESSED`. Existing capability may skip authoring, adaptation is limited to a source-supported template, and new capability requires a bounded feasibility question, lead, stop condition, dependency, and restart event.

The Owner Console derives one attention bucket and one primary next action from closed records. Workflow progress, evidence maturity, and customer outcome are independent axes. Status provenance is one of `WORKBENCH_DERIVED`, `LOCAL_MANUAL_ASSERTION`, `NATIVE_IMPORTED_RESULT`, or `EXTERNAL_LINKED_RECORD`; exported, sent, acknowledged, executed, and qualified remain distinct. No overall completion or fit score exists.

Evidence bindings identify exact source evidence, digest/identity, design revision, traces/cases, dependency domains, scientific applicability, rights/use state, rationale, and provenance. Closed change-impact rules selectively carry unchanged-scope evidence or mark affected evidence for review. `CARRIED_FORWARD_UNCHANGED_SCOPE` is not qualification. Source-owner confirmation/authorization requires a trusted source interface and is rejected in local workspace imports.

`carbon.goal-workbench.workspace.v0.5` migrates v0.1–v0.4 additively. Migration creates route `UNASSESSED`, empty applicability records, and a receipt; it infers no source science or authority. Historical CPES and C-05 identities remain unchanged. The parser preserves the existing duplicate-member, dangerous-key, size/depth, unsafe-number, enum, authority-field, CSP, and offline fail-closed posture.

## Economics

The immutable current baseline is one adequate fresh pack per separately admitted job under A. The old v0.1 cohort calculator is retained only as a labeled legacy hypothetical sharing scenario.

For an explicitly homogeneous fixed group:

```text
A work = b × R
B work = R + H
group saving = (b − 1) × R − H
saving/candidate = group saving ÷ b
```

For `b=1`, B overhead is charged and break-even division is not performed. Equality is not an advantage. Each quantity retains value, unit/scope, and observed/estimated/assumed/simulated/unknown status. Missing R, H, b, unit, or scope makes arithmetic unknown. Unknown demand or field-dependent evidence keeps any computed relation conditional. Different resource classes, scopes, environments, and timing boundaries cannot be automatically composed.

Aggregate accounting charges full incurred reference/overhead work to incomplete groups and divides by eligible completions. Zero completions produce undefined cost/completion. Upfront implementation/qualification fields never change recurring calculations or recommendation. Candidate reconstruction/inference/obligations and required failures/audits remain charged at their true scope.

The dynamic view consumes the pinned detached Python replay output; no scheduler is ported to JavaScript. It shows work, completed and unfinished comparisons, completed-work delay, groups, and scope-specific caveats. Membership is recomputed per overhead; the 38/39 versus 36/36 counterexample is a golden vector. C is not selectable as policy.

## Migration and exports

`carbon_workbench_draft_v0.2` and `carbon_workbench_workspace_v0.2` are closed additive schemas. v0.1 workspaces are accepted only at their exact source/schema identity. Invalid imports do not mutate the current session. Parsed derived results are never trusted; they are recomputed. Authority fields are fixed at unreviewed/not-qualified/not-sent/not-granted.

Client and Engineering JSON/Markdown exports are projections of the same analysis record. The client form omits its contact field from client output. The Engineering packet retains exact study/control/attack references, six evidence conditions, assumptions, and review questions. Both embed caveats; neither is executable or transmittable. Files are unencrypted.

## Safety and operation

JSON parsing rejects duplicate members, prototype-pollution keys, excess size/depth, nonfinite and unsafe integers, Boolean numeric coercion, unsupported fields/versions, malformed identity, and forged authority. Imported text is escaped. Bundled JSON escapes `<` and Unicode separators before insertion into script nodes. CSP disables every source by default and allows only build-hashed inline assets; connections, forms, objects, and bases are disabled.

The standalone artifact works under `file://` and an approved `127.0.0.1` static host. No analytics, URL fetch, solver, evaluator, telemetry, credentials, cloud sync, public service, send action, answer publication, or sharing activation is present.
