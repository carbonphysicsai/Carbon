Explore value under your assumptions.
Unknown values remain unknown. Cost scenarios do not predict achievable model performance or quote a Carbon engagement.
Arithmetic and limits
Monthly net = predictions × (current cost − target cost) − additional monthly cost. Twelve-month net subtracts one-time setup cost. Whole-month break-even assumes constant volume and costs. Dollar comparisons use microdollar rounding.
Include reference work, integration, failures, fallback and maintenance in the relevant costs. Taxes, financing and discounting are outside this model. Aggregate execution time is not elapsed calendar time. Compare output quality before interpreting time or cost differences as value.

Sending this draft to Carbon under an NDA

            
  1. Press Download encrypted for Carbon. The draft is encrypted in this browser to Carbon's intake key. Nothing is sent.
  2. Check that this key fingerprint matches the one in the Data Handling Statement attached to your NDA: checking the key…
  3. Attach the downloaded .carbon-sealed file, and nothing else, to an email to the intake address given with your NDA.

            
You cannot open the encrypted file yourself. Keep the reviewed draft as well if you want a copy of what you sent.
