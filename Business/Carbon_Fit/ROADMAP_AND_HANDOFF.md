# Carbon Fit roadmap and handoff

**Planning order, not an active implementation board.** [Package status](README.md). [GOV-FIT-01](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/issues/138) owns documentation delivery; [FIT-WEB-01](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/issues/139) is the website Engineering backlog item.

## 1. Evidence-acquisition order

| Order | Work | Proceed when | Stop, park or redesign when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One real client fit review and credible baseline | Job, access and consequential next question are identifiable | Missing causal inputs or rights blocks meaningful work |
| 2 | Reference feasibility and resource profiling | A bounded development probe can test accuracy, independence, new-case cost and failures | Reference cannot support the claim, or no affordable evidence program exists |
| Parallel when source arrives | Harshdeep's JAX inventory | Exact source, dependencies and permitted use become available | Source unavailable or task/representation mismatch |
| 3 | Small same-job backbone comparison | Qualified comparison path and bounded reconstruction dependencies exist | Extra information or compute confounds the comparison; variation prevents resolution |
| 4 | One simple numerical/ROM correction | Its anticipated benefit justifies a bounded experiment | Added solve/qualification cost removes client value or physical failures persist |
| Later funded profiles | Differentiable-solver closure, pretrained adaptation and richer compositions | Reference, reconstruction, security and workload economics support the scope | Unresolved provenance, gradients, custody or lifecycle cost |
| Later research | Pareto transitions, local-optima and hyperbolic methods | Enough rights-eligible transition evidence and a prospective comparison against simpler guidance | No measured decision utility or unsolved leakage/selection bias |

This ranking concerns the next evidence purchase. It is not an empirical ranking of neural architectures. Keep graph, foundation-model and hybrid directions visible with restart triggers, but do not require them for mainnet. Source availability is not integration, and integration is not qualification.

## 2. Model neutrality and modular construction

The long-term assessment remains neutral across standalone surrogates, classical/reduced models, learned corrections/closures, numerical-learning hybrids, pretrained adaptations and bounded construction programs. Preserve separate axes for family, composition, construction procedure, representation, information budget and execution freedom.

P0 can consider several pinned backbones only through its exact current assembly/compiler/resource and Challenge contracts. A field called `backbone` does not authorize an arbitrary repository zoo. Audit supported dimensions, causal inputs, outputs, physics applicability, license/provenance, environment, memory, cold/warm reconstruction, inference and scientific variability. No JAX implementation was inspected in this package.

The current master plan already places model-family neutrality in J, richer construction in K and generalized reconstruction in L. Evidence memory, product qualification and commercial engagement remain in E/F/G, with later system lifecycle/research in M/N. These are references to existing domains, not changed dependencies. Engineering must classify any new component, adapter or construction representation and select its owning ticket before implementation.

## 4. Decisions before activation

Runtime/profile owners must decide actual hardware and capacity, measured construction/inference/reference limits, queue/service windows, retry/resume rules and exposure policy. Science owns physical scope, reference adequacy, finite sampling, measurements and comparison resolution. Security owns entropy/commitment integration, custody, cache reuse/disclosure bounds and incident response. Product/rights owners approve website hosting/receipt destination, access, collection purposes, retention and permitted learning.

These are activation inputs, not reasons to stop all design work or to seek a global numerical maximum now. Development fixtures remain synthetic and cannot become production parameters. The canonical cross-domain decision queue and existing lead routes remain authoritative for formal ratification; this document is an acceptance checklist, not a second decision registry.

## 5. Evidence retention for later learning

Preserve the job/population, exact construction and environment, resource profile, reference/measurement identity, input access, selected intervention, actual cost, uncertain or failed outcome, and rights/selection provenance. Link measured parent-to-child edits where available; do not infer an observed transition from embedding similarity.

Start with task-conditioned tables and feasible Pareto views. A nondominated observed point is not a local optimum without a neighborhood and supporting search evidence. Hyperbolic geometry is a hypothesis to compare against simpler representations, not a required database or scientific authority. Intake interest, research guidance and official qualification remain separate.
