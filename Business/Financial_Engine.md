# Carbon Financial Engine v1

**Status:** OWNER-CANONICAL financial architecture.  
**Purpose:** define how Carbon should model revenue, cost, margin progression, capital needs, and investor scenarios without confusing assumptions with traction or forecasts.

---

# 1. Financial doctrine

Carbon's financial model must distinguish:

```text
ACTUALS
signed/recognized business evidence

PIPELINE
probability-weighted commercial opportunities

PLANNING ASSUMPTIONS
internal operating assumptions

SCENARIOS
illustrative strategic models
```

Never present scenario outputs as historical performance or committed forecasts.

---

# 3. Revenue-quality classes

Every revenue dollar should be classified:

```text
BESPOKE_PROJECT
PRODUCTIZED_SERVICE
USAGE
RECURRING_SUBSCRIPTION
LICENSE / ROYALTY
MARKETPLACE / ADMIN
```

The strategic goal is not simply revenue growth. It is a rising share of repeatable, recurring, software-, usage-, licensing-, and marketplace-led revenue.

# 4. Direct cost model

Track by engagement/product:

- scientific/business authoring labor;
- engineering/integration labor;
- truth-source cost;
- GPU/CPU/evaluation compute;
- external network reward cost where Carbon-funded;
- third-party software/license cost;
- security/private deployment cost;
- customer support burden;
- travel/field deployment where material.

For pass-through sponsor rewards, report separately rather than hiding them inside cost of revenue.

# 6. Margin architecture

Expected qualitative progression:

```text
EARLY
expert-heavy audits / integration
lower gross margin
        ↓
REPEATABLE SERVICES
standard templates / adapters
improving margin
        ↓
PLATFORM / USAGE
software-led recurring workflows
higher margin
        ↓
API / OEM / MARKETPLACE
scalable distribution / supply
highest structural margin potential
```

Illustrative planning bands may be used internally, but should be clearly labeled as assumptions. A reasonable architecture test is whether gross margin can improve materially as custom effort per program falls.

Business stop-ship:

> **If revenue growth requires proportional growth in bespoke technical headcount indefinitely, Carbon has not achieved the intended business model.**

# 8. Sales economics

Before enough real data exists, CAC/payback should be modeled as an assumption range rather than a KPI claim.

Once meaningful:

- CAC by motion/product;
- sales-cycle length;
- win rate;
- pilot-to-expansion rate;
- CAC payback;
- account gross contribution;
- net revenue retention;
- renewal rate;
- expansion ACV.

High-touch founder-led sales are acceptable early if they create repeatable product learning.

---

# 9. Scenario model

Use at least three explicit scenarios:

```text
CONSERVATIVE
slow sales conversion, services-heavy, limited platform/network leverage

BASE ARCHITECTURE
successful Audit wedge, meaningful expansion, recurring lifecycle/platform growth

UPSIDE
strong enterprise adoption, OEM/API distribution, network-backed discovery, higher recurring mix
```

Scenario variables should include:

- number of qualified accounts;
- Evidence Audit price and volume;
- Sponsored Discovery price/volume;
- Qualification attach rate;
- Lifecycle attach rate;
- Platform customer count / ACV;
- API/OEM revenue;
- custom labor per program;
- compute/truth cost;
- gross margin by product;
- sales headcount productivity;
- network substitution/leverage.

Physics Intelligence revenue should not be required for the base case.

---

# 10. Capital planning

Capital should buy de-risking milestones rather than broad architecture completion.

Budget categories:

- scientific/technical team;
- product/engineering;
- compute and qualification infrastructure;
- enterprise security/private deployment;
- legal/IP/commercial contracts;
- GTM/customer discovery;
- network/testnet/treasury implementation;
- working capital / runway.

Fundraise size should be bottom-up from milestone plan and runway, not selected first and justified backward.

---

# 11. Financing milestones

Preferred milestone ladder:

```text
F0 business architecture locked
F1 validated buyer pain
F2 first paid Evidence Audit
F3 repeatable second/third delivery
F4 first expansion within an account
F5 first recurring/lifecycle revenue
F6 first Sponsored Discovery with credible network economics
F7 first platform/usage commitment
F8 first OEM/API or multi-team enterprise deployment
F9 demonstrated margin/productization improvement
```

Each financing round should fund a defined subset of these proofs.

---

# 12. Investor reporting discipline

Investor materials should separate:

- booked revenue;
- recognized revenue;
- pipeline;
- signed recurring commitments;
- sponsor reward pass-through;
- scenario revenue;
- hypothetical TAM.

Do not count:

- sponsor bounty principal as ordinary product revenue without appropriate accounting basis;
- token appreciation as company revenue;
- unsigned LOIs as contracted revenue;
- architecture-specified future products as pipeline.
