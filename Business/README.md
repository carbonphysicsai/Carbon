# Carbon business

**Commercial focus updated 20 November 2026:** [custom model discovery and design](../.agent/decisions/2026-11-20-OWNER-CUSTOM-MODEL-DISCOVERY-DESIGN-01.md).

Carbon focuses on **custom model discovery and design for engineering problems**. Engineering teams need fast models that predict the quantities and conditions relevant to their design decisions. Carbon combines the search for useful methods, problem-specific model development and independent evaluation.

The primary conversation starts with the model the customer needs. **Sponsored Discovery** searches for useful approaches; **Model Development** designs and develops a reproducible candidate around the agreed requirements. Feasibility and reference integration support that program. Qualification, integration and lifecycle work follow when justified. An **Evidence Audit** is a complementary option for an existing model, not a required first engagement.

These documents describe company strategy and commercial design. They do not establish paid customers, recurring revenue, or production qualification. For implementation and research evidence, read [Project status](../docs/publications/PROJECT_STATUS.md).

## Company and network

The company owns customer relationships and delivery obligations. The subnet provides a route to external research and evaluation. Enterprise buyers can procure services in fiat; they do not need to manage tokens to commission a modeling program.

Carbon plans to pay for useful subnet output through Alpha buyback and burn. The company has not implemented that commercial mechanism; protocol rewards follow their own registered policy. Neither the business plan nor token ownership establishes a claim on company revenue.

## Commercial responsibilities

The business team defines target customers, packaging, pricing, contracts, and delivery terms. Scientific owners define the evidence needed to support a claim. A customer payment cannot lower that standard or change a result after evaluation.
