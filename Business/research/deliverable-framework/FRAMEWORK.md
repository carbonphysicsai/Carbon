# Challenge-neutral customer deliverable framework

Status: **PROPOSED / RESEARCH ONLY**. Authority: [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md), [Constitution](../../../CONSTITUTION.md), and the [common design packet](../../../docs/development/challenge_pipeline/COMMON_DESIGN_PACKET_V1.md). This framework organizes evidence; it does not change the judge, scoring rules, scientific thresholds, disclosure rules or product rights.

## One record, several renderings

The common record carries the following header: document/version; Challenge and contract versions; intended and eligible tier; development/customer mode; decision and buyer role; exact candidate artifact and runtime identity, or `HUMAN_INPUT`; source snapshot; evidence cut-off; disclosure audience; applicable standard editions; applicability and tailoring authority; qualification-record reference or `HUMAN_INPUT`; adverse-evidence review status.

Every section has coverage `EVIDENCED`, `PARTIAL` or `GAP`, a scoped statement, evidence references, gaps, and an automation class. Coverage asks whether the stated fact has support. Outcome asks what the scientific measurement concluded. Do not convert coverage into PASS. A FAIL can be EVIDENCED. Unknowns and not-run checks are GAP, not successful negatives.

Requirements have stable identifiers, context of use, units/measurement version, acceptance criterion and its approving authority, basis, outcome, uncertainty, source references and adverse-finding links. Criteria not sourced and approved remain `HUMAN_INPUT`. Preserve typed `FAILED_REFERENCE`, `FAILED_INFRA`, `UNRESOLVED`, `NOT_ASSESSED`, `PASS` and `FAIL`; non-scientific failures cannot be charged to a model. Add `NOT_APPLICABLE` only with recorded human rationale.

Evidence basis is explicit: **verification**, **reference-relative**, **physical validation**, or **decision value**. A reference solve establishes comparison to that reference, subject to its adequacy. Physical validation additionally requires appropriate real-system referents, their uncertainties and an accepted applicability argument. Runtime equivalence, hashes, hidden-exam scores and an independent scorer cannot create that missing evidence.

## Automation specification and gaps

Proposed flow: **allow-listed evidence records → identity/version checks → typed facts and findings → common record → standard profile → human-reviewed deliverable**. Renderings reorder or index the same facts; they cannot select favorable panels, change evidence basis, create acceptance criteria, or hide failures. An applicability table retains every relevant clause as mapped, gap, human-tailored exclusion, or awaiting licensed review.

Needed work, not implemented here:

1. Define public and controlled-buyer projections with approved rights. An audit's transparency is not permission to disclose official seeds, draw IDs, protected cases, reconstructable commitments or hidden-derived row data. A digest may be published only through its approved projection; a hash is not automatically safe.
2. Normalize Challenge requirement and measurement IDs, artifact/runtime identities, evidence roles, strata, common masks and reference-failure classifications. Reject a join when the versions or evidence roles differ; report a missing leg rather than manufacture an outcome.
3. Extract EV/Q1 aggregates and verified Attacker findings with source locators and preserved uncertainty/dependence. Track which evidence was not inspected. Existing challenge-specific report builders are reusable inputs, not a Challenge-neutral export service.
4. Separate measured facts, researcher assumptions, authority approvals and qualification judgments. Record standards edition and mapping-verification status. Add licensed ASME review before claiming a clause-complete rendering.
5. Produce an immutable delivered manifest and append-only corrections, with signed/manual decisions bound to the intended use and artifact. Validate references and required gaps; test missing records, stale identities, conflicting outcomes, unsafe disclosure and omitted adverse findings.

No percentage, cost saving or network advantage is estimated here. Quantitative claims in a filled deliverable require a source or `ASSUMPTION` with low/base/high. Source point values can use an equal triplet, expressly **not** an uncertainty interval; any reported confidence interval has its own method, level and dependence caveat. Unsourced unknowns use `HUMAN_INPUT`, never a guessed midpoint.

## Maturity tiers and entry criteria

These are proposed eligibility rules, not automatic certifications. Document completion and commercial eligibility are separate. `UNESTABLISHED` applies whenever the governing authority has not accepted the required evidence. A development gap assessment is preparatory work, even if titled EvidenceAudit sample.

| Tier | Customer receives | Required before claiming the tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EvidenceAudit | Requirement-specific independent assessment, reference-relative/physical distinction, adverse findings and an auditable gap/action record; an adverse conclusion is a valid deliverable | Agreed buyer use and evidence rights; identified subject artifact; qualified problem-specific exam and credible reference for the scoped claims; declared applicability, measurements and independence; adequate records and reviewed disclosure; responsible authority accepts the audit scope. A failed subject model can still receive a valid audit; a failed or unqualified exam cannot qualify it. |
| Decision tool plus dossier | Exact deployable fast model, integration/fallback contract and the audit evidence supporting its bounded use | EvidenceAudit prerequisites; artifact/runtime-specific qualification under approved policy; independent held-out requirement evidence and relevant physical evidence for any physical claim; uncertainty and boundary coverage accepted; tested deployment parity, abstention and fallback; matched-admissibility baseline/value case and customer workflow accepted; unresolved adverse findings handled without relaxing limits. A competition rank is insufficient. |
| Lifecycle re-evidence | Versioned continued-use evidence, change assessments, monitoring and renewed or withdrawn scoped claims | Eligible decision tool; agreed material-change/domain-drift triggers, access and rights; monitored use and incidents; immutable change lineage; qualified re-evaluation route with reference failure separation; accepted process for suspension, fallback, supersession and renewed approval. Renewal follows real changes and evidence, not a fabricated subscription cadence. |

For each tier retain a criterion-by-criterion eligibility table with evidence, gaps, authority and decision date. External NASA/ASME/DoD compliance, accreditation, regulatory acceptance and Carbon product qualification are distinct judgments. The standards rendering is supporting material for the customer's designated authority, never an accreditation decision generated from a score.

## Claim and evidence rules

- Separate **demonstrated**, **designed**, and **needed** on every customer-facing claim. "Independent" needs its own scope, conflict/dependence record and evidence; separate people or seeds alone do not establish it.
- Bind every conclusion to the artifact, use, measurement, population, reference route and evidence version. Preserve prior outcomes and their original interpretation.
- Mandatory failures precede soft ranking. Report all adverse findings, dissent, unresolved outcomes and uninspected evidence. Explain whether a defect is repaired and independently retested; retain the original finding.
- Reference-relative success cannot assert real-world safety, life, torque, warpage or physical compliance. Neither provenance nor reproducibility demonstrates physical truth.
- Keep protected material private. Buyer access requires an approved disclosure policy and rights; this public schema accepts public committed source references only.
- Commercial price, sponsorship and purchase cannot alter score, admission, qualification or settlement. Owner decisions remain explicit `HUMAN_INPUT` until recorded.
