# DELIVERABLE-FRAMEWORK-01 — owner brief

**Recommendation:** keep one evidence record with fixed sections, then render a buyer's chosen standards profile from that record. Start with an honest EvidenceAudit. Add a model only when the exact deployable artifact has the required evidence for its intended use; add lifecycle service when changes have defined re-evidence obligations.

This is a **research proposal**, not a new scientific contract, implementation, qualification policy, or customer commitment. It follows [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md). No solver, paid service, hidden data, or customer outreach was used. Research baseline: `915225242146be67e6de098d4a08324cc63dc684`.

- [Framework](FRAMEWORK.md): common content, Carbon inputs, automation gaps, tier entry criteria and rules.
- [Fill-in template](TEMPLATE.md): the same sections for battery, motor, warpage or a future Challenge.
- [Standards crosswalk](STANDARDS.md): verified NASA requirement identifiers, ASME V&V 20 preview anchors, DoD template anchors, and explicit licensed-review gaps for ASME V&V 10/40.
- [Machine-readable schema](deliverable.schema.json): proposed document shape; validating it proves structure only.

The deliverable has separate **coverage**, **scientific outcome**, and **tier eligibility** records. A well-evidenced failure is still a failure. A development sample can satisfy the document schema while qualifying for no commercial tier. Reference-relative agreement is never relabelled physical validation. All adverse findings accompany the conclusion, including unresolved and superseded findings with their history.

The source facts can increasingly be generated: identities, registered requirements, safe outcome aggregates, reproducibility records and finding histories. Intended use, physical-reference adequacy, acceptance criteria, risk acceptance, disclosure rights and the qualification decision remain owner or buyer responsibilities. This batch implements no exporter and makes no percentage-automation claim.

**First use:** fill the template from the battery's committed public EV4, EV5 and Q1 records. The sample should demonstrate both useful evidence and missing prerequisites, rather than imply customer readiness. Before external use, agree the decision, artifact, applicable clauses, evidence rights and acceptance authority. The ASME clause mapping needs an authorized review of licensed editions; no standards were purchased.

Bounded working contract: add only this directory, preserve all existing Challenge files, recommend rather than select scientific limits or commercial terms, and stop at a reviewable PR to PR Head. Structural JSON checks and documentation checks are appropriate; solver execution is outside scope. The Development Hub is retired under the current delivery protocol and needs no update. Maturity earned by this work: **SPECIFIED research proposal** only. Adoption, qualification, pricing and deployment remain human decisions.
