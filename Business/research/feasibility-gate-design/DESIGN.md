# Public feasibility-gate audit and Test Engineer handoff

**FEASIBILITY-GATE-DESIGN-01 — research recommendation; no scientific adoption.**

## What the measurements establish

The published EV5 verification decisions label **98 eligible real members, grouped into 78 recipes, unsafe at least once**. The remaining **one member/recipe is UNKNOWN**, with unresolved decisions, rather than verified safe. There are **zero fully resolved safe real recipes**. Graphite run 5 supplies **27 members / 8 effective recipe clusters** after its published alias correction; **24 members / 7 clusters** make an unsafe choice somewhere on development. The previously reported **23** refers specifically to `D-T24-S0.12`; another seed fails elsewhere. These are source counts, low=base=high. See `inventory`, `ev5_member_measurements` and `run5_member_measurements` in [results.json](results.json), bound to [the public inputs](SOURCES.md).

Consequently **safe-recipe false rejection is NOT_IDENTIFIABLE** in both real panels. Every curve carries a null value and zero denominator for that estimand. A no-observed-failure recipe is not certified safe when decisions are unresolved. The report separately measures rejection of resolved safe **decision events** (`SELECTED_FEASIBLE` or `CORRECT_ABSTENTION`), which can occur in otherwise unsafe recipes. That opportunity-loss measure is useful but is not specificity against safe recipes. Oracle, rank-preserving-delay and conservative synthetic controls are shown separately; their reported unresolved decisions also remain unresolved.

No measured scalar gate has a point that catches **every unsafe real recipe while retaining any eligible real member** on EV5. A reported recall of one obtained by failing the whole panel is not an adequate decision tool. This finding argues for a safer selection policy and a genuinely safe comparison panel, not a declaration that a tighter scalar cutoff has solved safety.

It remains a design needing a prospective registry extension, not a v5 candidate: current v5 accepts one gate measure per candidate. Its curves show the same trade-off between rejecting most/all real members and residual unsafe members. Do not relabel a combined maximum as the existing `feasibility` measure.

## Per-case and worst-stratum gates: measured scope and gaps

The committed EV5 H3 data are **constraint-level marginal counts on the near region**. The runtime's `feasibility` measure is instead **joint case false-feasible**: the reference resolves at least one hard constraint FAIL, and the model calls all hard constraints PASS. Summing constraint false acceptances or dividing by the total constraint calls does not recover that joint rate. Its public scoring-set recall curve is therefore **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**, including the current strict >0.05 owner rule. No host-only prediction bundle was read or reconstructed.

We can measure joint false-feasible on **EV5 development decision grids**, using published `agreement.false_acceptances` over `references.status_counts.INFEASIBLE`, and compare against **separate verification outcomes**. Those diagnostic curves are `D_grid_joint_feasibility` and `D_grid_worst_temperature`. At pooled >0.02 they catch **75/78** unsafe verification recipes, retaining **3** real members; at worst-temperature >0.05 they catch **66/78**, retaining **12**. All retained members at these points remain unsafe somewhere in verification. The temperature groups are descriptive blocks, not a newly approved scientific stratum law. These measurements **are not the existing scoring-set G-FEAS** and must never be substituted into its registry slot.

For a future worst-stratum gate, predeclare strata from P and collect **joint resolved-case counts per stratum**, with missing/zero-denominator strata unmeasured, rather than clean. Fail if any stratum exceeds its owner-set risk allowance or lacks sufficient evidence. Also compute worst constraint within each stratum; the present H3 global counts cannot measure this cross-tabulation. Hierarchical/simultaneous intervals and a minimum stratum denominator are **HUMAN_INPUT**, to be registered before confirmation.

## Margin-aware selection and conformal abstention

These should change **which actions may be selected**, in addition to any recipe-level gate. They cannot be measured on the published aggregates: the per-case paired predicted and reference margins are missing. Actual recall, safe rejection, coverage and A-Q interaction remain **NOT_IDENTIFIABLE**, rather than inferred from means.

**Margin-aware candidate design.** Express each continuous constraint in its registered safety direction and band units. For a frozen member and action slate, let the calibration residual be the maximum optimism `(predicted margin − reference margin) / registered band` across constraints **and across actions in that question**. Calibrate a one-sided bound on independent question blocks. An action is eligible only when every predicted margin exceeds the sum of its reference band and calibrated model-error allowance; the reach-CV constraint needs its own qualified handling. Absent measurements, unsupported strata or ambiguous bounds require abstention/fallback. A fallback reference solve must preserve reference-failure semantics. No reference band is reduced to gain coverage.

Using the maximum over the slate protects the later argmin selection against a misleading case-wise average. A case-level coverage statement alone does not guarantee safety of the selected best-looking action. The score/selection evaluator, not the miner, computes the calibration bound; calibration, gate selection and confirmation are separate folds, with seed/recipe aliases kept together. Question blocks and geometry/condition support must match the registered population and weighting.

**Split-conformal option.** Under an explicitly assessed exchangeability assumption, the order statistic at `ceil((n+1)*(1−alpha))` of the slate-wise optimism residuals supplies a candidate bound. If that index exceeds n, use an infinite bound and abstain. Alpha is **HUMAN_INPUT**, not 0.05 by default. This is a proposed simultaneous margin construction, not an implemented or physically validated Carbon guarantee. Persistent adaptive mining, changed P/Q and selecting among many members/calibrators can invalidate a naive exchangeability claim; use the approved split/confirmation plan and a registered multiplicity policy.

**Risk-controlled feasibility option.** Use an independent calibration fold to choose among preregistered acceptance thresholds with a one-sided risk bound and an abstain region; collect selected-action unsafe risk and lost-safe-opportunity curves on a separate evaluation fold. Define the loss before calibration. Unsafe **accepted-action** rate is a conditional ratio, while unsafe selection per offered question is unconditional; a method controlling one does not automatically control the other. Accepting fewer actions need not monotonically reduce unsafe **selection** when the argmin switches to a different action, so the monotone-loss premise must be verified or a finite-threshold simultaneous testing construction used. Conformal Risk Control addresses expected monotone loss; Risk-Controlling Prediction Sets supplies a holdout risk-control route. Neither result grants unmeasured worst-stratum, adaptive-adversary or physical safety assurance. [Angelopoulos et al., Conformal Risk Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814); [Bates et al., Risk-Controlling Prediction Sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02703).

## Interaction with A-Q and registry-v5 handoff

Actual A-Q is a weighted **geometric** combination, `sqrt(a*q)`, with `a=1/(1+E)` and q from Q3 regret. The public artifacts lack a member-wise Q3 leg; #1044 likewise reports `INCOMPLETE_LEGS`. **Actual A-Q tau, rank, top-k regret and gate/ranking interaction are not identifiable here.** No verification loss is reused as its own ranking score.

For a clearly marked diagnostic, the analyzer computes `sqrt((1/(1+E))*(1/(1+mean development decision loss)))` on **five common-resolved development questions**, and evaluates tau against loss on **eight distinct verification questions**. This is `DISJOINT_DEVELOPMENT_DECISION_LOSS_PROXY_NOT_Q3`, not a registry score or new proof. Ungated proxy tau is **0.336**; near >1 gives **0.329**, plating >0.02 **0.243**, and worst-constraint >0.005 **0.296**, yet each chosen top member is unsafe on some verification question. Better tau cannot compensate for admissibility failure. All gate failures rank below every pass using a −infinity analysis floor; if all fail, the result is **NONE_ELIGIBLE**, not a winning failed member. Passing members retain their relative A-Q order when that actual score exists.

Recommend **two candidate families for #1044's selection fold**, not two qualified gates:

1. **G-PLATE/A-Q**, with both comparisons and a low-cutoff sweep. It has measured discrimination against the localized sign-error and run-5 bundled winner which mean-near/E miss. It is expressible by the existing `plating_fa` gate and A-Q base; adding the prospective candidate rows requires no new gate semantics. It still misses other unsafe seeds/thermal failures, so it cannot become the only safety claim.
2. **Tighter G-FEAS/A-Q**, with both the registered ≥ comparator and strict > comparator, including zero-error/one-error boundary behaviour. It retains the current joint all-constraint semantics and is already expressible in v5. Its scoring-set curve must be measured by the Test Engineer; the disjoint decision-grid evidence supports investigating tighter cutoffs but does not estimate this candidate's recall. The zero-FA adversary is a mandatory challenge to coverage, not a reason to declare a lower cutoff sufficient.

[candidates.json](candidates.json) supplies parser-compatible **proposed** rows and a separate HUMAN_INPUT adoption record. Neither existing registry nor live rule was edited. Worst-constraint/stratum combinations and calibrated selection are the priority extension if these single gates cannot satisfy the owner's value/safety criterion; they require an explicit prospective contract, not a semantic change hidden inside v5.

**Required selection-fold output:** for every preregistered candidate/cutoff, joint and per-constraint counts, recipe-cluster unsafe recall and interval, verified-safe recipe rejection and interval, unknown count, retained member count, all known-bad/adversarial verdicts, actual A-Q tau/top-k regret, and NONE_ELIGIBLE rate. Add genuinely safe and over-cautious constructions, not only the present near-universally unsafe real panel. Publish permitted aggregates; protected predictions remain under evaluator custody. The owner chooses safety/coverage thresholds before confirmation. This research uses no protected material.
