## Quote model: physical cost structure first

Predict **internal consumed cost**, **elapsed delivery time** and **achievable evidence tier/decision quality** separately. Commercial price is a later owner decision reflecting integration, rights, support and value; it is not `compute × markup`. Currency/rate date and re-evidence period must match before summation.

`Cost = intake/authoring labour + rights/data work + reference setup + Σ(reference cases + controls + refinements + retries) + baseline setup/query + construction/search + independent validation/adversarial checks + packaging/integration + network overhead + retention/support + lifecycle reserve`

For one homogeneous reference tier, `T_bank = T_setup + N × t_case / (p × eta)` and `C_bank = hourly_rate × T_bank_hours`. Here p is measured safe concurrency and eta effective occupied throughput relative to that concurrency. Do not count the same retry both in N and in eta. Heterogeneous strata/refinements need separate terms; cases hitting the ceiling are censored, not successful solves at the ceiling. Fixed-rate billing, transfer, idle and teardown require their own terms when applicable. A parallel critical-path model describes elapsed delivery; summed labour and CPU times do not. Blocking rights/reference/feasibility has **unbounded** elapsed high until resolved.

For low/base/high scenarios vary explicit inputs jointly, retaining common scope and correlations. A harder mesh usually increases both case time and RAM, reducing p; these cannot be sampled independently to create implausibly cheap forecasts. Include fallback rate and baseline construction costs. Existing cached customer libraries may have sunk setup costs; compare both incremental customer use and new-build lifetime costs transparently. Cost cannot compensate for inadequate reference or infeasibility.

The quote output should say: accepted scope and exclusions; claim tier; component ranges and structural unknowns; prerequisites; revision triggers; owner-approved acceptance boundary. If a mandatory component is unknown, total cost/time remains null rather than the sum of known pieces labelled a total. Quality is a vector of requirement-level evidence with uncertainty and adverse findings. No fit-error number becomes a false-feasible guarantee, physical validation or customer acceptance.

## Learning with sparse evidence

Start with engineering decomposition and nearest scoped analogue, explicitly checking domain difference. Report prediction residuals once actual receipts exist. Repeated bank sizes/hardware/refinement tiers can estimate scaling within one physics route; they do not increase the independent Challenge count. Keep a blocked/failed cohort and leave-one-Challenge-out diagnostics, then prospectively check new briefs before selling forecast precision. No statistical coverage probability or narrow general confidence band is warranted by the current eight planned rehearsals, especially with missing cost/time labels.

Recommend separate acceptance states: INTAKE GAP REPORT, CONDITIONAL ESTIMATE, MEASURED ANALOGUE ESTIMATE, and PROSPECTIVELY CALIBRATED QUOTE. These are proposed business labels only, not deployed schema enums. Advancement requires evidence and owner approval; none follows from this research PR. Re-evidence is triggered by changes in scope, solver, requirements, hardware, data rights, material law, model or baseline. No fixed speed target, customer pilot, adoption, price, legal term or qualification is authorized here.
