Searches also found secondary subnet aggregators, model cards and social
reports. They were used to locate primary sources, not to establish technical
rules or live failure rates. Robotensor's ladder page was inaccessible on the
recheck, so no exact margin is claimed from it. Requested SN30 KOTH details and
the unpublished VALIDATOR-32 draft remain explicit gaps. No unverified story is
promoted to a measured failure.

All numerical source constants in the dossier use fixed low/base/high values;
the only transport/power numbers are labeled ASSUMPTION in RECOMMENDATION.
The survey does not estimate financial return, miner receipts or a production
false-takeover rate. Every proposed policy parameter is HUMAN_INPUT.
