# CUSTOMER-DELIVERABLE-01 — owner summary

**Recommendation:** build a decision-specific evidence product first; add a deployable fast model after its reference, customer use and product evidence are qualified. The valuable package is the model **plus an inspectable claim about one use**, its limits, evidence and revision obligations. “Standards-grade” is a target, not a qualification Carbon has earned. This follows [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md); terms and claims remain the owner's decisions.

Carbon's discovery/price/validation thesis is plausible but unproved. Competing builders might improve search; competition might lower construction cost. Neither establishes lower **delivered** cost. Protected reference-relative grading adds evidence, but cannot repair an inadequate reference or establish physical validity. Incumbents already offer physics models and validation; the difference to test is decision-level evidence and independently reconstructed competition, not exclusive ownership of validation.
