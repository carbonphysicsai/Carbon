# Deliverable design and commercial hypotheses

Ticket CUSTOMER-DELIVERABLE-01; **proposal only**. Commercial terms, prices, buyer acceptance and public claims require their owners. Source/number identities are in [evidence.json](evidence.json); the [coverage map](evidence-map.md) distinguishes actual development evidence from designed artefacts.

## What the customer would buy

**Proposed enduring product:** an exact fast-model artefact and decision interface, a context-bound credibility dossier, a requirement-level validation record, declared applicability/uncertainty/fallback behaviour and a maintained evidence lineage. The buyer's job is to make and defend a specific engineering decision. It is not to buy a high leaderboard number.

**First offer:** a bounded EvidenceAudit of the customer's present model or workflow, returning observed strengths, failures, unresolved evidence and a plan for the decision. This is commercially honest even if no surrogate passes or the cheap baseline wins. SponsoredDiscovery becomes useful when the accepted task and reference expose a real construction opportunity. The full deployable package follows its own product evidence and qualification. This sequence comes from [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md), not a new commercial policy.

## Standard-facing assembly

For an industrial/NASA-structured dossier, propose separate **capability assessment** and **use/results assessment** volumes. The former establishes what the pinned model can support; the latter documents the actual decision, inputs, uncertainty and limitations. A requirements crosswalk should identify the relevant NASA requirement IDs, such as M&S 15–18 (verification/validation and domains), 28–30 (uncertainty/sensitivity), 31/48 (assessments), 32–39 (reporting/review/records) and 41/51 (lifecycle/defects), with any unmet requirement conspicuous. This is an authoring proposal based on the public matrix, not a NASA acceptance. [S09](https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/NASA/B/1/NASA-STD-7009B-Final-3-5-2024.pdf).

For ASME solid/thermal jobs, include discipline-specific verification and comparator uncertainty, plus surrogate-to-reference fidelity. Licensed clause review still precedes any compliance claim. For DoD, assemble the separate plan/report products under the assigned accreditation authority. For medical work, agree the model's role and guidance applicability first; do not sell a generic “FDA-ready neural model”. For software-tool qualification, add the applicable lifecycle requirements and tests rather than rebranding the physics dossier. [Applicability map](standards.md).

## Package ranking

**ASSUMPTION rubric:** each axis is ordinal, with endpoint scenarios, not a statistical confidence interval. Scores run from **1/1/1 minimum to 5/5/5 maximum** by definition.

**Buyer value:** 1 = useful exploratory output; 2 = repeatable analyst assistance; 3 = a documented design/review decision; 4 = repeated decisions plus defensible evidence that reduces review/rework; 5 = evidence important to costly/high-consequence acceptance, contingent on proven adequacy. This ranks the plausible job's usefulness, not demonstrated savings or willingness to pay.

**Carbon feasibility:** 1 = new domain/evidence/regulatory path; 2 = major physical/assurance/integration gaps; 3 = an existing task basis with substantial qualification/adapter work; 4 = a bounded manual diagnostic product from existing public evidence and expert review; 5 = a repeatable, accepted delivery workflow. The high endpoint is a possible scenario, not a current earned state.

Index = value × feasibility. Low multiplies low endpoints, base multiplies base endpoints, high multiplies high endpoints. No excitement, token price, funding, customer payment or Challenge score enters it. Rankings use the base scenario; ranges overlap, so customer evidence can change the order.

## Test the owner's network thesis

| Proposed edge | Plausible mechanism | What Carbon can claim from this review | What would actually test it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Discovery** | Independent builders explore different construction hypotheses. | Open competition is a design route; development construction studies exist. Network advantage over a competent central team is **not demonstrated**. | Same frozen use/reference and budget: central team versus open builders; measure distinct useful hypotheses, admissible decision improvements, total effort and failures. Count accepted evidence, not submissions. |
| **Price** | Competition may reduce the cost of obtaining a useful construction. | Lower delivered cost is **not demonstrated**. Data, reference qualification, repeated evaluation, review, integration and liability are not removed by inexpensive inference or token incentives. | Track all payer/operator costs and time per supported decision/improvement at matched credibility, including unsuccessful candidates and recurring evidence. Report subsidy/token-funded effort separately; do not call subsidised access durable lower cost. |
| **Independent validation against specific requirements** | Evaluator reconstruction and protected fresh tests reduce dependence on builders' self-reports and tuning exposure. | Bounded protected/reference-relative evaluation is partly implemented and tested in DEVELOPMENT. It is not proof that builders “can't game” the exam or that physical/regulatory acceptance is earned. | Qualify reference, population and measurement; retain adverse attacks, exposure/retesting and confirmation; establish reviewer competence/conflicts and audit access. No assessor certifies its own unqualified grading instrument. |

Ansys provides learned simulation models; NVIDIA supplies open construction tooling; PhysicsX integrates physics AI into engineering workflows; Siemens offers historical-data reuse with high-fidelity validation. These sources do not support claiming that competitors lack validation or openness. Carbon's testable position is the **combination** of reconstruction, prospective requirement/population contracts, protected assessment and decision-value evidence from competing builders. [S24](https://ansys.synopsys.com/products/ai/simai), [S25](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo), [S27](https://www.physicsx.ai/), [S28](https://news.siemens.com/en-us/siemens-simcenter-physicsai/).

## Position and value capture — recommendation

Sell the named engineering result and its evidence scope. An initial audit can lead to discovery or expose that the customer's cached model is already sufficient. The reference-bounded tool's case is strongest when **new supported decisions recur** and it improves their admissible outcome or elapsed workflow; a fixed finite bank's answers may be better delivered as a cache.

For buyer value, estimate attributable analyst/review time, avoided repeat simulation/testing where the acceptance authority actually permits it, and measured rework/decision improvement. Do not add overlapping benefits, equate gross opportunity with WTP, or assume a fast model replaces required physical tests. Credibility evidence may make a decision defensible without improving the design; that is a separate value proposition to validate.

For price, scope authoring, evidence acquisition/qualification, construction, independent assessment, delivery and ongoing change separately, with owner-approved rights and acceptance. A failure/gap audit has a deliverable even if no model qualifies. Public sources justify neither a fixed Carbon tariff nor a promise of a regulatory certificate.
