# Credibility standards and buyer obligations

Ticket CUSTOMER-DELIVERABLE-01; research date 2026-10-09. Source IDs and retrieval limitations are in [evidence.json](evidence.json). This is an applicability and artefact map, **not an exhaustive normative clause audit or legal determination**. No standard was bought. Licensed texts, accepted editions and project-specific tailoring require the responsible buyer/assurance owner.

“Must meet” depends on the use, procurement contract and applicable regulator. A physical-model credibility assessment, software-tool qualification, laboratory competence and official accreditation address different claims. There is no universal accuracy threshold or certificate a hidden-exam score can create.

## Applicability traps that affect the product

**Medical ML boundary.** FDA's official webinar covers first-principles models and the first-principles parts of hybrid models; it excludes standalone data-driven/statistical models. A network trained on physics outputs is not automatically a first-principles model. Agree the surrogate's role and evidence route with the sponsor/regulator before promising submission support; FDA recommends early interaction for credibility plans. [S06, scope and planning slides](https://www.fda.gov/media/175363/download). The FDA reporting guidance separately supports explicit comparator conditions, instrumentation/measurement uncertainty, assumptions and applicability. [S07](https://www.fda.gov/media/87586/download).

**Two links in the evidence chain.** Evaluate emulator-to-reference fidelity and reference-to-referent credibility separately. NASA's public standard explicitly allows a higher-fidelity model to be a surrogate referent. This supports a bounded emulator assessment; it does not establish that the higher-fidelity model matches the customer's real hardware. [S09, Appendix E.3.4](https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/NASA/B/1/NASA-STD-7009B-Final-3-5-2024.pdf). Carbon's reference-tier doctrine preserves the same distinction. [R15](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/4578d20b27f3d88641a8fe06b3bdbbde8aca17f7/docs/development/challenge_pipeline/round1/reference-credibility.md).

**Tool assurance is another claim.** ISO/IEC safety tool processes and DO-330 may be relevant to a customer's use of a Carbon tool, but a numerical error score cannot discharge lifecycle, traceability, configuration or assurance obligations. Conversely, a qualified tool does not make an inadequately modelled physical system valid.

**No universal limits.** Risk, required credibility and release limits come from the customer context and acceptance owner. No percentage-error threshold, sample count, stress limit, temperature cap, reliability level or production uncertainty floor is adopted by this research.

## Review before a standards claim

The buyer and reviewer should freeze the applicable edition/clauses, intended use, consequence of a wrong decision, comparator role and risk level; assign responsibilities for each artefact; review licensed texts where needed; and agree evidence adequacy before execution. Claim wording and regulatory applicability require the appropriate owner. A completed traceability matrix may legitimately conclude **insufficient evidence**. It is still a useful diagnostic deliverable.
