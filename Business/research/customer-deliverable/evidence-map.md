## Meaning of the coverage labels

**Covered** means the narrow listed artefact is publicly evidenced in the bounded development scope stated. **Partly covered** means some implementation/results or a design exists but the intended customer artefact is incomplete. **Missing** means this review found no evidence sufficient for the named customer-use obligation. “Missing” is not a claim about undisclosed records. None of these labels establishes compliance with a whole standard.

Public summaries were read for aggregate outcomes and claim limits. Protected cases, seeds, prediction bundles, operator roots and private confirmation reports were not fetched or reconstructed. A public document's reference to private evidence is not permission to access it.

## Historical evidence is not the new map task

The dated battery status is useful historical reconciliation, but later EV5 public results supersede its “nothing dispatched” implication. The current protocol file still reports **DEFINING**, with a null lock/suite version; that protocol status does not erase individual development runs. The new battery v3 law remains a prospective specification. Neither a historical EV5 success nor failure is silently rescored as a v3 outcome. [R16, R19, R21]

Graphite's engineering readiness page permits explicit waivers in its engineering launch signal; a waiver is not a scientific pass. Readiness schema validation, a successful CI run or a well-formed dossier cannot confer physical or regulatory credibility. [R09, R10]

## Customer audit export boundary — proposal

The deliverable can expose the exact model/runtime and requirement IDs, approved aggregate per-stratum outcomes with denominators, reference/measurement versions, retained limitations/findings, commitments and reviewer conclusions. A controlled auditor may check custody under an agreed rights/security protocol. Any needed new detailed comparisons should use fresh customer qualification material, separate non-hidden task witnesses or properly authorised released cases.

It must never disclose official exam draws, protected seeds, reconstruction-sensitive case IDs, raw hidden labels or derivatives that reveal them. **If a reviewer needs evidence Carbon cannot lawfully or safely provide, the affected claim remains unestablished**; the owner must agree another evidence route. “Transparent” does not mean exporting the answer key.
