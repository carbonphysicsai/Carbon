Sources inspected 2026-10-10. Primary research and project materials only. No source supplies a universal physical novelty threshold or demonstrates a Carbon contamination audit.

|---|---|---|
| L1 | [Mirzadeh et al., GSM-Symbolic, revised paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05229v2) | Controlled template instantiations and sensitivity to numerical changes. Supports variant controls, not physical-model cheating detection. |
| L2 | [White et al., LiveBench, revised paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19314v2) | Frequently updated, objectively scored tasks. Use the revised “Contamination-Limited” title; freshness is mitigation, not proof of unseen data. |
