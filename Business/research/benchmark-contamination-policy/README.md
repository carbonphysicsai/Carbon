# Benchmark contamination policy — owner summary

**BENCHMARK-CONTAMINATION-POLICY-01 · RESEARCH RECOMMENDATION · NOT RUN · 2026-10-10**

Recommend using public engineering benchmarks as **reference-route anchors**, excluded from scored EVAL and STRESS questions. Public answers, renamed copies, re-meshed copies and physically equivalent transformations are excluded together. Knowing physics, learning the public generator and using published correlations remain legitimate competition.

Hidden seeds are necessary custody controls; they do not establish semantic independence. The proposed [policy](POLICY.md) adds a versioned public-source catalogue, physics-aware canonicalization and distances for the backward-facing step and original 3D periodic metagrating. It also requires public development witnesses showing that copying or interpolating anchor answers does not reliably reproduce the constrained design decision. All distance normalizers, exclusion thresholds, error allowances and statistical acceptance criteria remain **HUMAN_INPUT**. No registered population, generator, Score Pack or qualification gate changes here.

For CFD, compare dimensionless wall geometry and operating conditions, including Reynolds-number convention and inlet boundary layer. Uniform scaling at the same similarity parameters does not create a novel case. For optics, compare the periodic silicon mask after allowed translation/symmetry alignment and dimensionless periods, thickness, materials and excitation. Resampling a published mask or scaling geometry with wavelength is insufficient.

An anchor-versus-variant performance anomaly is an audit finding, not proof of cheating. The proposal specifies controls, reference-error checks, multiple-testing accounting and human disposition. Text canaries and language-model likelihood tests have limited applicability to physical model outputs. Audit results cannot silently change historical scores or become similarity penalties.

Public claims should identify the exact benchmark, observables, settings and measured discrepancy. Published experimental agreement is bounded to that case; numerical code agreement is not hardware validation. Both Carbon anchor reproduction and variant adequacy remain **NOT_RUN**.

The [source register](SOURCES.md) explains the literature and its limits. [policy.json](policy.json) is a non-executable proposal record with null scientific thresholds. Packets can use the metrics in the next briefs, then request scientific acceptance and implementation separately. This research accessed no protected material, ran no solver and authorized no spend. Policy adoption and launch decisions remain the owner's.
