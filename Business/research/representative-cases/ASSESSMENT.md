## Bolt first: two distinct preparation routes

**BOLT-CONTROL:** Use the CC-BY TRC revision-D assembly CAD/drawings as a named, unchanged geometry and assembly control [B2]. This is a clamped vibration benchmark, not the proposed eccentric bracket. Torque instructions are source facts; they do not supply force preload or surface friction. Keep the experimental and representative numerical interpretations separate.

**BOLT-DESIGN:** Keep the proposed three-dimensional bracket/bolt/parent contact job and its opening, slip and load outputs [C1]. Packet owners must declare a new representative geometry/action family; the published TRC assembly can test pretension/contact conventions but cannot silently become that job. Fixed elastic generic steel is a defensible *numerical input*, with strain/linear-elastic applicability declared and no inferred yield/fatigue allowance. The generic source constants are E **209/209/209 GPa**, Poisson ratio **0.30/0.30/0.30** [G1]. Do not call them calibrated TRC material properties.

Bossard's engineering guide gives dry, machined steel/steel separation-interface friction **0.10–0.30** and notes that actual joints may fall outside guidelines [B1]. A proposed representative sensitivity triple is **0.10/0.20/0.30**; endpoints sourced, midpoint ASSUMPTION. This is service-interface friction, not thread or under-head tightening friction. The same guide's torque-control category with estimated friction gives tightening factor alpha_A **1.6–2.0**; proposed **1.6/1.8/2.0**, midpoint ASSUMPTION. It is a maximum/minimum assembly-preload ratio, not a symmetric percentage standard deviation or a sampling distribution.

These ingredients permit specification of an explicitly representative elastic/contact rehearsal. They do **not** determine the absolute preload, proof strength, embedding losses or service load. Those remain HUMAN_INPUT until sourced/approved in a case contract. No mandatory safety limit is weakened or replaced by a guideline.

Open route: CalculiX contact/pretension or another independently cleared open solver, pinned source, dependencies and image. Contact pressure, opening, tangential slip, reactions and elastic stresses require a measurement contract. Compare a simplified axial case with analytical bolt/member compliance; then check load reversal, opening, contact discretization, penalty sensitivity and work/reaction balance. Solver or reference failure is never graded as candidate failure. No implementation or run is authorized here.

**Cheap-baseline test:** VDI-style compliance/load-factor and separation/slip inequalities, followed by a calibrated low-order contact model and a cached FE response surface. [VDI's publisher summary](https://www.vdi.de/en/home/vdi-standards/details/vdi-2230-blatt-1-systematische-berechnung-hochbeanspruchter-schraubenverbindungen-zylindrische-einschraubenverbindungen) establishes central/eccentric joint calculations but does not grant redistribution of the standard. Link engineering guidance; do not bundle its copyrighted tables/PDF as licensed data. Compare on the exact mandatory conditions. A narrow action grammar that a response map solves is not a useful model Challenge.

**Equal-budget hypothesis:** eccentric prying and changes in contact topology might make a model's screening valuable when direct contact solves are costly. This is an ASSUMPTION, with gain including zero or worse. Direct adaptive FE optimization gets the same geometry knowledge, physical bounds, warm starts and training-data allowance accounting as the model-assisted arm. Do not award value for predicting a fixed grid already cached by everyone.

**Preparation recommendation:** advance bolt to packet-owner definition and Data Collection's separately authorized first panel. Do not assert runnable or accepted until the missing contract and executable observations exist.

## Solenoid second: fixed-condition rehearsal, original hot job still open

GetDP's GPL-2.0-or-later library provides a generic tabulated nonlinear steel law, plus a planar electromagnet tutorial [G1/G2]. The proposed internal numerical source is the exact table and its interpolation implementation at the pinned revision; no grade, processing or temperature calibration is inferred. Use the whole source table, not a guessed constant permeability.

The original proposal is an axisymmetric non-permanent-magnet pot-yoke actuator [C2]. A planar tutorial is only a formulation control. Converting it to a pot-yoke family requires exact geometry, winding and axisymmetric observers. A declared current-density excitation can support magnetic force/coenergy outputs; it cannot uniquely determine turns, conductor dimensions, resistance or hot I-squared-R losses.

A new fixed-condition magnetic-force rehearsal is specifiable with open laws. The original hot force/loss contract remains incomplete. This narrowing loses thermal winding design and material-temperature robustness. It is not a relaxed pass of the original actuator decision. Preserve motor in the portfolio; its new owner-reported feasible design makes solenoid additional electromagnetic coverage rather than a motor replacement.

Strong shortcuts: nonlinear magnetic circuit with fringing calibration, interpolated reference library, then response surfaces and direct adaptive FE search. Bosch's published proportional-solenoid optimization uses a metamodel workflow [G3]; industry already uses this shortcut. The fast model must win complete-cost admissible design selection against it. Source-based saturation nonlinearity is a hypothesis for difficulty, not measured baseline failure.

Future panel: analytical air-core/linear controls, nonlinear solve convergence, force via independent stress/coenergy reductions, displacement/mesh sensitivity and reference failures. Temperature laws and accepted losses remain HUMAN_INPUT; no hot-material claim.

## Seal third: published parameter model with a limited control

Yenigun et al.'s CC-BY article supplies component geometry, coupon-derived and ANN-derived Ogden coefficients and figures, without a released raw-data archive [E1]. It does not independently measure the friction used in the ANN model. Prefer the named traditional silicone row as a *published parameter-model control*; do not combine it with another rubber's friction or temperature row.

Source-point tuple, low=base=high for each: alpha1 **0.6793**, mu1 **1.1441**, alpha2 **0.1117**, mu2 **1.1440**. The table does not fully specify an executable unit/compressibility contract. Dimensional units, the exact Ogden normalization, compressibility and admissible stretches are HUMAN_INPUT to reconcile. This research neither fits nor converts those coefficients. CalculiX's documented handling of zero compressibility differs from simply imposing perfect incompressibility [E2]; a keyword copy is not a validity witness.

A limited O-ring compression/control rehearsal is possible in principle after those bridges are resolved. The original supported gland under pressure needs a declared gland geometry and applicable friction/contact law, material/stability evidence, and mandatory temperature conditions [C3]. The separately cleared Zenodo tension/compression data [E3] offer a future calibration route, not an interchangeable parameter set for the silicone row.

Narrowing to compression loses supported-gland extrusion robustness and any leakage/lifetime value. Contact pressure does not certify fluid tightness. Strong competitors are squeeze/assembly charts, direct low-dimensional FE, fitted response maps and direct optimization. Hyperelastic nonlinearity alone is insufficient value. Do not revive the full seal proposal solely because typical coefficients are public.
