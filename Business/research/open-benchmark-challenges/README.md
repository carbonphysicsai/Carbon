# OPEN-BENCHMARK-CHALLENGES-01 — owner summary

Research recommendation, 2026-10-10. No solver runs, spend, hidden material or AX42 access. Adoption and scientific limits remain the owner's decisions.

**Investigate NASA's backward-facing step and the published 3D metagrating testbed first.** They offer the strongest inspected bridges to, respectively, experimental data and independent numerical reference results. Neither has passed Carbon's feasibility, cost or equal-budget value panel.

The search nominates two evidence routes per missing regime, with substantial differences in maturity:

- **CFD:** backward-facing step first; wall-mounted hump second. Published experimental fields support an honest comparison at the published operating conditions. New shapes require a separate applicability argument, and the hump's documented RANS discrepancy must remain visible.
- **Optics:** 3D periodic metagrating first; 2D mode converter second. The MIT-licensed testbed publishes geometry, constitutive constants, scripts and independent numerical comparisons. Its 2D examples support an optimization rehearsal; no example establishes fabricated-device validation.
- **Structures:** the measured friction-contact round robin is the better physical anchor; the MIT PolyFEM contact deck is an affordable numerical rehearsal. Neither presently supplies a verified, complete bolted-joint validation family. The representative-case follow-up is still needed.
- **Acoustics:** theatre impulse responses are the more relevant physical evidence route, subject to asset-level inspection; rigid-sphere scattering is a useful analytical control. Sphere interpolation is already cheap. A finite-baffle design family would need its own evidence and energy-accounting checks.

[The assessment](RESEARCH.md) distinguishes cleared repository licenses from uninspected dataset files, and benchmark agreement from qualification of a parametrized Challenge. NAFEMS membership access, an ERCOFTAC listing, and a solver's license do not by themselves clear benchmark assets.
