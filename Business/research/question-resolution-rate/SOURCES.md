## Economics and statistical assumptions

The owner supplied **150 resolved questions** as the interim sizing and **approximately 0.73 CPU-h per offered question** in the current job. The low/high cost endpoints **0.66 / 0.80**, target-yield scenarios, repeated recovery-pattern transfer, one-busy-core conversion and identity-hash diagnostic cohorts are **ASSUMPTION research choices**. They do not set a production threshold, quote a customer or spend funds. Production P, Q, evidence weights, bands, retry/recovery rules and acceptance remain **HUMAN_INPUT**.

Counts, rates, elapsed-time sums and point counterexamples calculated from the public blobs are measured or deterministic arithmetic (low=base=high). Cost tables give explicit low/base/high sensitivity results; those are not confidence bounds on future yield. True process CPU time, target-host transfer, a deployable frontier prescreen, weighted-tau power, uncertainty-resolving calibration and future safe-model coverage remain missing.

[Art Owen, Monte Carlo theory, methods and examples, chapter 9](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-is.pdf), §§9.1, 9.3, 9.11: importance weights, support and diagnostic issues for proposal changes.
