# MOTOR-BUYER-LIMITS-01

Research and recommendation only, accessed 2026-10-11. No solver execution, purchases, customer contact, protected evidence or AX42 access. Test Lead and owner retain every acceptance-limit and Challenge decision.

**Recommendation: preserve the precision-joint job while the pending skew work is assessed, and reconcile the active measurement contract before considering any limit change.** Public evidence supports demanding smooth torque from a precision servo, but does not establish a universal buyer-approved percentage or prove Carbon's proposed motor feasible. A failed exploratory panel is a reason to inspect topology, measurement and use case; it is not evidence that a buyer accepts a weaker specification.

The owner reports zero joint passes among 83 designs, and a pending 6-degree skew/bisection study. These are **OWNER_REPORT**, treated as fixed observations with low/base/high equal to the reported value; no raw panel was accessed or remeasured. The owner's latest wording specifies a 0.30 peak-to-peak fraction. The committed prospective precision-joint packet instead specifies a 0.05 fraction **and** a 0.30 N·m absolute holding cap. The former is 30%; the latter is an absolute limit alongside 5%. These must not be silently interchanged. The active panel's immutable manifest and cogging cutoff are **HUMAN_INPUT**. See [definitions](DEFINITIONS.md).

The most useful public evidence is explicit about its limits:

- [Tecnotion's cogging guide](https://www.tecnotion.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/WhitePaper_Cogging_2023.pdf) gives family-specific, unpowered peak-to-peak guidelines relative to **continuous rated torque**, not holding-command mean torque.
- [STXI's PRHD2 specification](https://stxim.com/product/prhd2-highly-dynamic-servo-motors/) supplies a servo cogging ceiling but leaves the waveform convention unspecified on the product page.
- [Qiu et al.'s pump-motor study](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/17/21/5379) uses the same pole/slot combination and a loaded-ripple design constraint; it has a different envelope and operating job.
- E-bike and aerial papers show that application, commutation, rating and measurement definition matter. An achieved simulation result is not an OEM procurement limit.
