# Public source register and comparators

Accessed 2026-10-11. Primary supplier guidance/specifications and original research only. **PRODUCT_SPEC** means a manufacturer's public product claim, not an independently verified acceptance test. **VENDOR_GUIDELINE** is weaker. **STUDY_CONSTRAINT** is a research project's chosen requirement; **FEA_RESULT** is an achieved calculation. None automatically establishes a Carbon customer's requirement.

Numerical convention: a fixed sourced value or threshold has degenerate low/base/high equal to that value, preserving `<` or `≤`; this is not an uncertainty interval. A published range has its observed endpoints, with base **HUMAN_INPUT** unless expressly reported. Different families and operating points are not pooled into a market distribution. Missing definitions, buyer limits and physical uncertainty bands remain HUMAN_INPUT. Dates, identifiers, page numbers and topology labels are bibliographic/structural identifiers, not estimates.

In particular, a named product family is not proof of an integrator's accepted limit. Manufacturer confirmation of ambiguous metrics and a buyer-relevant motion/load requirement remain HUMAN_INPUT; research makes no contact.
