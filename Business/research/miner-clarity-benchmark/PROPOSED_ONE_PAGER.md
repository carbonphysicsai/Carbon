# Build a better battery model. Compete for the crown.

**PROPOSED PUBLIC WORDING — DEVELOPMENT / BATTERY TESTNET.** This describes the owner's announced championship design; operation begins only when the linked deployed rule says so. It is not a mainnet or engineering-qualification claim.

Develop a recipe on public practice material, then freeze it and use the available submission path. **Current setup, example, hardware/cost, intake and My status links: HUMAN_INPUT.** Practice is separate from the exam.

## First, become eligible

Carbon rebuilds your model from the frozen recipe under its posted reproducibility rules. It must have a screening score and pass the reference-based feasibility and important-region checks. These checks make it eligible for the championship; they do not certify real-world safety.

Your status should give the result and next step. The draft describes about **18 hours** before screening feedback becomes public; the deployed card controls. Eligibility alone earns no championship reward.

## The battery championship

The planned round is **16:00 UTC daily**, with entry closing **14:00 UTC**. The king and the **top 10 eligible contenders** enter on the posted screening ranking. Entries after cutoff wait for a later round under the published entry policy.

Everyone uses their stored rebuilt model on the same fresh batch of **4,000 hidden cases**. Nothing is retrained. All scored cases are retired; they are not offered again to later submissions.

To take the crown, you must pass the model checks and clear **both** improvement bars:

- **The posted margin:** more than **2.25%** improvement over the king on the round's registered metric.
- **The noise guard:** enough paired improvement to clear the uncertainty test measured on that batch's declared draw units.

The target card shows both bars, metric and version. Noise can require a larger gain. Ties keep the king; the registered tie/commit-priority rule controls multiple successful contenders.

The rule targets **at most 1% false takeovers per round under its registered statistical assumptions**. Daily risk accumulates: the stated bound over **30 rounds with no better contender is about 26%** (about **7% over a week**). This does not guarantee that luck can never produce a takeover. See the [owner's current statistical rule](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/ca71b8ecf500250f57c9dfd731950e1ff5e1f731/.agent/decisions/2026-10-11-OWNER-BATTERY-CHAMPIONSHIP-02.md).

## Results, rewards and failures

After a completed round, the feed publishes rounded entrant scores, the king and the next posted target. **Result/target page and exact scoring definitions: HUMAN_INPUT.**

The king's reward share decays daily; a new king starts fresh under the posted policy. **Amount/decay and testnet payment status: HUMAN_INPUT.** There is no earnings promise.

Batch/validator failure voids the round; the king stays. A miner-side infrastructure failure needs a reason and retry path. **Binding failure policy: HUMAN_INPUT.**

The public leaderboard does not publish your recipe. Authorized use/access follows the operative miner terms. **Terms link: HUMAN_INPUT.** Rule changes are announced prospectively, preserving scheduled rounds and historical results.

Other Challenges post their own cadence, measured-noise guard and cost. Use their current card; battery numbers do not transfer.
