# Miner clarity benchmark — owner summary

**Research and proposed wording only, 2026-10-11.** The strongest pattern is a short answer to what to build, an exact entry checklist, a public preflight, and a status page that explains the next action. Carbon can adopt that structure without disclosing the exam or changing its championship policy.

The comparison covers [Chutes](https://github.com/chutesai/chutes-miner/blob/44c6603ced87c5108359a30fa1e1e6c12f864557/README.md), [Gradients](https://github.com/gradients-ai/G.O.D/blob/de5ea9cd90bfff32d59a8403cb62433a8ce46bab/docs/miner.md), [Crusades](https://github.com/one-covenant/crusades/blob/5b9a0c25a705afcd70bfbf135cd79095ba7bebe2/README.md), [Distil](https://github.com/unarbos/distil/blob/88751532bea283d812df98c0b8e304465344eed0/docs/MINER_FAQ.md) and [Affine](https://github.com/AffineFoundation/affine/blob/d59a6bde8bc88c975b3b2523732b2d2af9a857a0/docs/MINER_QUICKSTART.md), with dormant [Templar](https://github.com/one-covenant/templar/blob/0fefc2ad763cd4cfda080e58267fe562455f3b45/README.md) as a migration example. These are visible public implementations with useful contrasting workflows, **not an audited ranking of the most successful subnets**. No comparable miner retention, profit or measured onboarding cohort was identified. Public repo activity and product/docs surfaces justify the sample; they do not prove documentation caused success.

Carbon's actual draft clearly explains entry, the round clock and winner reward. The committed [owner amendment](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/ca71b8ecf500250f57c9dfd731950e1ff5e1f731/.agent/decisions/2026-10-11-OWNER-BATTERY-CHAMPIONSHIP-02.md) is newer: use draw-unit paired SE and a t-quantile, collapse duplicate pairs, and treat any design-question leg separately. The draft's fixed **3.09** is only the approximate large-sample limit. Preserve the owner's **1% per-round target**, disclosure of **about 26% over 30 all-null rounds**, and retirement of all scored cases; do not convert these into an empirically certified guarantee.

Recommended changes, in priority order:

1. Say **eligible for the championship** instead of scientifically ambiguous “qualified”; describe feasibility checks against the reference, not guaranteed real-world safety.
2. Show **both bars**: the posted **2.25%** improvement floor and each contender's measured noise guard. A single “beat 2.25%” banner is incomplete.
3. Replace “a lucky draw can't crown you” with the declared false-takeover target and conditions. Retain the monthly figure as the owner requires, linked to the approved error contract.
4. Add **Start → Practice → Submit → My status**, with current Challenge/version, hardware/budget, an example recipe and exact public preflight. Carbon's [MCP guide](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/ca71b8ecf500250f57c9dfd731950e1ff5e1f731/carbon/miner_mcp/README.md) supplies reusable routes but distinguishes development submission from official intake.
5. Publish a rule/version card and private status reasons; include the next deadline, effective target, failure/retry semantics, reward decay link and recipe terms. Avoid a blanket “never published” promise that ignores authorized submitted-work use.
6. Generate repeated facts from one approved public projection. A rule change must update the one-pager, leaderboard and agent-readable card together.

[Comparison](SUBNET_COMPARISON.md), [draft review](CARBON_REVIEW.md), [replacement one-pager](PROPOSED_ONE_PAGER.md) and [source ledger](SOURCES.md) make these concrete. The owner-supplied input was read-only, hash-recorded and excluded from the PR.

Proposed usability targets are **ASSUMPTION**, not measurements: understand what to build/win in **2/3/5 minutes**, and complete a small public preflight on an already configured supported host in **15/30/60 minutes**, low/base/high. Research installs and mining were not run. A later observed walkthrough should record failures and elapsed time separately from registration, official evaluation and publication lag.

This work changes no score, gate, payouts, custody or runtime API. Publication needs the current deployed rule/intake, safe diagnostic projection and operative miner terms; missing links and readiness remain **HUMAN_INPUT**.
