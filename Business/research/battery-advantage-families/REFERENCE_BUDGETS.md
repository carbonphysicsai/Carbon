not the validator's chosen V2 bank. Low/base/high offered questions are

For a finite grammar, complete acquisition is

`reference condition-solves = unique (action, physical condition, history, solver-policy) keys`.

Without reuse, the conservative offered-panel count is
`N_offered × A_actions × M_conditions`. A refinement/witness reference is an
additional route/cost, not free replacement. Combinations of the same solved
conditions share acquisition but also share statistical errors. Only genuinely
new histories/conditions/cases count as fresh evidence.

preference β values reuse the same physical references; do not multiply
acquisition by the number of scalarizations. Conversely, do not call the

## What can be examined cheaply before a large bank

For the original full-35 shifted family, the five fallback-feasible V
conditions contain **11** unresolved action-condition records. They identify
a public reference-triage list, not 11 guaranteed successful refinements.
Finer solves may remain within the original bands. The existing public
refinement overlay covers D, not a complete V settlement. Reference bands
must not be dropped or narrowed without new credibility evidence and owner
authority. No refinement was run in this task.

All 50 warm compositions reuse only **210** physical action-condition cases
(35 actions × six conditions). The 25 priced compositions use five of those
conditions: **175** unique cases. Acquiring more compositions from them costs
no new base solves here but cannot supply independent fresh-bank statistics.
For a new robust panel, use the no-reuse count above unless a prospective
reuse/dependence policy explicitly says otherwise.

The strict 20-action comparison uses **60** reference cases for three public
conditions, and the strict 9-action comparison **36** for four. The map's D
source acquisition is separate (420 requested D cases, five failed). Restricting
the grammar only to obtain a favourable comparison is not a cost-saving receipt
or a justified buyer problem. These counts cannot bypass the intended job's
reference-completeness requirement.
