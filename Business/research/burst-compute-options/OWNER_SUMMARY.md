[Order guidance](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/order-processing/), [Robot billing](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/billing-and-account-management/cancellation/cancellations-robot/).

[Hetzner tariff](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/), [Scaleway tariff](https://www.scaleway.com/en/pricing/elastic-metal/), [Scaleway quota](https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/organizations-and-projects/additional-content/organization-quotas.mdx).

AWS C7a and Azure Fasv6 Spot offer promising observed rates: approximately $0.021/full-core-hour and $0.014/full-core-hour in the sampled US regions. A 5,000-hour job's illustrative low/base/high compute costs are $55.56/$153.54/$718.20 and $55.56/$105.58/$956.16 respectively, before storage/transfer/idle provisioning. These combine observed prices with explicit price, occupancy and replay assumptions; they are not quotes. AWS Standard defaults are only five vCPUs per purchasing class and may be smaller on a new account; Azure family/regional/Spot approval is also required. No fresh account is confirmed usable today. [Price observations](price-observations.json), [cost assumptions](COST_MODEL.md), [AWS quota policy](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ec2/latest/instancetypes/ec2-instance-quotas.html), [Azure quota process](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/quotas/regional-quota-requests).

**Hours, not days, has a capacity boundary.** Even perfect scaling needs 25 hours for 5,000 comparable CPU-hours at 200 workers. With 192 workers our ASSUMPTION low/base/high occupancy/replay scenarios give 28.94/38.19/72.92 hours. Two AX162s have 192 threads but only 96 physical cores: do not call that the equivalent of a 192-full-core AWS host. At the small 200-hour end, the same 192-worker scenarios take 1.16/1.53/2.92 compute hours, plus provisioning. [Derivation](COST_MODEL.md).

**Custody direction:** Azure SNP/TDX or GCP N2D-SNP/C3-TDX reduce hypervisor access to guest memory. GCP C3D's documented confidential route is SEV, not SNP. Hardware memory protection needs attestation, controlled key release, guest trust, encrypted disks/swap and a complete deletion record; it does not qualify Carbon's sealed workflow by itself. Recommend a public-only acceptance exercise under the security owner before any protected use. Ordinary VM host SSH/sudo can administer a non-root worker without installing sshd inside the worker pod. [Configuration distinction](https://docs.cloud.google.com/confidential-computing/confidential-vm/docs/supported-configurations), [Azure protections and limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/confidential-computing/confidential-vm-faq).
