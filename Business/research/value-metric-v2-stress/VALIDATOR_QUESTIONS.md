# Questions for Carbon Validator before V2 registration

**Review against the unchanged owner draft, SHA-256 `973594923121ad177bbbd764ee94bae1d5b59b50aebe6ed0d67667250b0ffa4c`. DEVELOPMENT; no answer or parameter is adopted here.** Measured examples and curves are in REPORT/results. These questions identify the smallest definition choices needed for a future implementer; they do not request access to protected material.

1. **Which questions enter the mean when the declared fallback is not FEASIBLE?** §1 calls for a mean over P; §1.1 gives those questions no gain baseline and leaves rescue credit open. Must bank admission require a FEASIBLE fallback, or is a separate benefit definition needed for rescues? If the denominator changes, how is that change bound to the question law/P/Q/w? Our all-offered no-rescue-credit proxy and paired safe-fallback subset answer different questions. Neither is silently registered.

2. **Should the no-op placement requirement refer to positive net U rather than positive gross G?** For example, G = 0.25 and one verification costing kappa = 0.5 gives U = -0.25, so a zero-utility abstainer correctly outranks it under §1. The blanket §4.1 requirement cannot hold for every positive gross gain at arbitrary costs. On settled NONE, abstention is also the correct behavior. Is the intended acceptance criterion positive net gain and a positive cheap-arm margin, with no-op ranks treated diagnostically?

3. **What exactly does the token charge represent?** §1.2 says sealed lookup, not a real solve. Kappa is therefore in benefit units per complete-action token, not a measured fraction of solver cost. How is it calibrated to a buyer workflow? Does one robust action with three/four mandatory conditions cost the same token as a single-condition action? Are startup, bank acquisition, inference, actual cache hits and refinement charged separately, or intentionally outside this score? Token count cannot establish a measured whole-query VALUE-BAR-V1 budget.

4. **What happens to a question containing failed/unavailable reference actions?** The public input has five failed records. §1.2 needs a pre-solved action bank, while §1 says unavailable attempts consume slots. Is such a question inadmissible until reference completion, or does it receive a typed reference-invalid result for every model? Charging it can measure wasted workflow cost, but reference failure cannot become scientific candidate failure. The scored bank contract must state this before confirmation.

5. **How do unknown fallback, known-infeasible fallback and settled NONE differ?** §1.1 describes rescue when fallback is infeasible or unresolved; the §2 rescue row names only infeasible fallback. What is reported/graded when fallback truth is unavailable? Can the buyer actually “keep” a fallback already known infeasible? Rescues need an independent benefit unit if credited; eta in this stress is only an illustrative extra term, never G. A gain denominator of zero cannot produce a ceiling-capture fraction.

6. **What distinguishes unsafe-heavy and safe shortlists with the same gain and total attempts?** U has no unsafe-count term. Equal G and n_verify imply equal U, regardless of which rejected attempts were unsafe. §4.7 cannot promise strict scalar ordering in that case; the mandatory gate must do the safety work. Which immutable G-FEAS definition/version, scope, denominator and threshold is bound to V2? This audit reports all 100 real members without inventing gate eligibility.

7. **What proposal grammar and stopping policy is frozen?** Does the model submit at most k actions, may it stop at a shorter list, and may it include predicted-infeasible actions? RES-07 orders predicted-feasible actions and evaluates the entire frozen prefix. A cap is not a requirement to spend every slot. Duplicate/refrozen lists require intake rejection; arbitrary ordering or adaptive verification would be a different policy. How is actual attempt count independently recorded rather than trusted from a miner?

8. **What physical claim does the benefit unit support?** These arrays measure the old 4.19 V CV-onset proxy, not target-SOC charge time, safety or lifetime. How does current battery-v3's protocol/cooling-map question law define objective, unit and fallback? If u changes, does kappa rescale consistently to preserve the same cost tradeoff, or is the owner changing the utility preference? The stress shows both numerical sensitivity and coherent-unit invariance, not a buyer-calibrated objective map.

9. **How is reuse represented without double-counting shared physical cases?** The interleaved/edge/robust families here reuse public EV4 outputs and have different baseline availability. Rho sensitivity does not establish transfer. What independent prospective shifted families and weights will be registered? Is rho = 0 intended only for this candidate, and what evidence would support a later nonzero value? B-probes/stretch remain unavailable; no unknown family is silently assigned zero economic value.

10. **What prospective statistical/takeover contract applies to U?** A relative 2.25% margin is unstable near zero or negative U. What absolute minimum useful gain, paired guard, error budget, multiple-comparison policy and tiebreak receipt will be registered? Public parameter sweeps and pointwise bootstrap intervals cannot select a winner and certify that choice on the same data. No chain commitment times were provided; this report preserves ties as intervals.

11. **What is the cheap-arm acceptance criterion and whose decision is it?** Beating an always-abstaining or weak fixed-order arm does not prove model value. Is onboarding based on held-out ceiling capture, positive paired net margin over a preselected strongest cheap arm, or both? Where no model beats the arm, does the Challenge hold? The payout condition is economic authority, separately owner-owned. Our threshold/margin grids are descriptive options and create no crown, payment or qualification.

12. **Where does exact replay source live?** At initial measurement the executable was retained locally, hash-bound and reused without rewriting its measurement functions. **Source-publication request addressed:** the [original executable](../value-metric-audit/res07-analyze.py.txt) and [staging contract](../value-metric-audit/REPLAY.md) are now committed in this PR with the original digest. A clean checkout supplies the code; pinned dependencies and explicitly public inputs remain required. The Validator's rev-2 answers in [the review](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/pull/1218#issuecomment-6133571737) are separate from these original measurements; this addition does not adopt or measure rev 2.
