# VALUE-METRIC-V2 public stress — RES-08

**DEVELOPMENT / NOT REGISTERED. Review target: the unchanged owner draft, SHA-256 `973594923121ad177bbbd764ee94bae1d5b59b50aebe6ed0d67667250b0ffa4c`. Reviewer: Carbon Validator.**

V2 assigns no positive utility to pure abstention on these public single-condition panels: abstention has U exactly zero and ranks last against all real members throughout the primary grid. Cheap maps remain strong, the declared fallback is not feasible across the whole offered family, and the draft contains two scalar-ordering claims that its formula cannot guarantee.

Finite-panel arithmetic/counts are MEASURED: low = base = high. Confidence intervals are separately reported, pointwise and exploratory. ## Evidence and interpretation

The unchanged RES-07 functions read the same 148 unique public prediction bundles, excluding 21 identical copies. The primary panel comprises all 100 real CPU rebuilds / 80 recipe labels. Other public cohorts are inventoried but do not enter the real-member ranks. All returned new actions are reference-FEASIBLE under the unchanged historical measurement/bands. This is not a mandatory-gate eligibility receipt.

The historical action bank has 35 CC protocol actions. Nominal D has 12 condition questions; shifted V has 12 interleaved questions within the old envelope. Edge reuses six V questions. Robust-SOC has four common-action questions over three SOC conditions each; robust-temperature has three common-action questions over four temperatures each. Those composites reuse cases and are not independent new solves. Only 840 authorized D/V reference keys enter; 835 are OK and five failed solves occur in one D question. Thus 11/12 D questions and all 12 V questions have reference data for every action. Band-unresolved decisions remain separate from missing reference data.

The objective is old EV4 CV-onset time at 4.19 V, with historical unit 120 seconds. It is not target-SOC charge time, cell safety or lifetime, and does not qualify current battery-v3. The fixed primary fallback is c1=0.75, c2=0.6. It is reference-FEASIBLE on 7/12 D, 5/12 V, 1/6 edge and zero robust questions. A public representative family with an infeasible default is not a fully defined V2 scoring bank.

For priced questions, G = max(0, (fallback time − best verified-return time)/u); with no feasible return, improvement is zero. Every complete frozen unique action attempt consumes a token, including unsafe, band-unresolved and unavailable attempts. U = G − κn; always-abstain has no attempts. A robust action costs one token here despite requiring three/four underlying conditions. Tokens are not measured CPU-hours or money.

For unpriced questions the published all-offered tables use an explicit zero-rescue-credit bookkeeping proxy. Physical gain remains unknown when the fallback is not FEASIBLE; zero credited gain is not zero buyer benefit. Utility on the fixed safe-fallback subset and on a complete-reference-bank subset is separately published for every primary member. They change the diagnostic denominator; neither mask silently changes P/Q/w. Rescue credit remains a Validator definition gap. Reference failure never becomes a new scientific candidate verdict.

## Where real members beat cheap methods

At the display anchor, the D-only bilinear map captures 100% of the finite priced nominal gain ceiling by k=3. Three real members beat its all-offered U, one beats it on the known-safe-fallback subset, and zero beat it on the complete-reference-bank subset. The maximum all-offered point advantage is 0.00833 benefit units; it comes from attempt accounting, not extra nominal gross gain. No nominal paired lower CI against the map is above zero at this anchor. The missing-reference mask changes the claim materially.

On shifted V, 89/100 beat the fixed k=3 map and 88/100 beat the retrospective cheap budget envelope. The map captures 83.34% of the finite priced gain ceiling. The corresponding safe-fallback subset also has 89/100 map wins, but only five priced questions. No shifted paired lower CI against the map is above zero at this anchor. Thus there is a shifted-value hypothesis, not a confirmed model advantage. Map source acquisition and model construction costs are not matched; strongest cheap alternatives and a prospective independent fold are still required by VALUE-BAR-V1.

On edge, every real member beats the map at k=3, κ=0.1, but only one of six questions has a FEASIBLE declared fallback; the map captures none of that priced gain. The interval includes omission of that temperature block and does not confirm broad edge transfer. Robust-SOC’s “100 real members beat the map” is purely a verification-cost comparison under zero rescue credit: the priced gain ceiling is undefined as a physical value benchmark, and abstention wins. Its two reference-feasible common-action opportunities require a separate rescue-benefit definition.

## Gaming and robustness

- **No-op and fallback:** zero-gain controls never have positive U for positive κ. They can outrank costly proposals on edge or unpriced families without adding design benefit. This is economically coherent only if the score’s benefit coverage matches the intended buyer decision. An all-questions zero baseline can hide that mismatch.
- **Extra verifications:** a best-first oracle and a unique padded oracle have identical gross gains. At k=3, κ=0.1, padding reduces nominal U from 3.6666 to 3.5166 and shifted from 3.8827 to 3.7577. Duplicate proposals, reordering and free unavailable attempts are prevented by unique frozen-prefix/cap checks. Higher fixed caps can still make a cheap arm weaker; the retrospective within-cap envelope exposes this.
- **Priced-region masking:** the oracle-priced mask raises the bilinear map’s nominal U from 3.5083 to 3.5666 and shifted from 3.0732 to 3.1732 with unchanged credited gain, while refusing unpriced questions. Its nominal rank becomes 1/101. This is an answer-informed diagnostic, not an implemented miner attack. The implementable D-fitted guard loses too much gain at the anchor, but at k=5, κ=0.5 ranks 2/101 nominally with U=2.2263, versus unguarded map U=2.0333, while delivering less gross gain (3.3513 vs 3.7416). Saving costs is not itself fraud; the missing rescue term can reward abandoning valuable buyer coverage.
- **Safety is not encoded by attempt count:** at the anchor there are 87 nominal and 10 shifted pairs of real members with equal aggregate gain and total attempts but different rejected unsafe counts. Their U ties. Draft §4.7’s strict ordering is therefore not assured; the unchanged mandatory gate must reject scientific failure independently. Verification feasibility is reference-relative, not guaranteed physical safety.
- **Raw-member visibility:** `mlp_raw-s0` achieves shifted U=3.7410 and rank 17–20/100 at the anchor, with two rejected unsafe and three unresolved/unavailable attempts. Its public RES-06 record also has 241 voltage-ceiling violations over the authorized case grid. This is a raw-cohort diagnostic, not permission to bypass its historical or future gate. No current gate eligibility is reconstructed from U.
- **Copying and exact caches:** a scorer using public finite action outcomes is intentionally answer-informed; the oracle/free-lookup ceilings expose available headroom. This stress cannot demonstrate resistance on new hidden variants, detect independent construction, or price cache acquisition. Digest deduplication prevents duplicate bundles becoming extra member evidence; it does not establish anti-copy security.
