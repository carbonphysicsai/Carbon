## Readable proposed terms

**What you submit.** A submission is the recipe and supporting files you deliberately send through Carbon's designated submission process, identified in a file manifest and receipt tied to this terms version. It includes the code, configuration and instructions you identify as necessary for reconstruction. It does not include your local drafts, experiments, unsubmitted improvements, unrelated repositories, personal files, conversations or general know-how. Referenced assets must be listed with their owners and license terms; identifying an asset does not give Carbon rights you do not hold. Incomplete transport and accidental uploads need the counsel-approved receipt/withdrawal rule below.

**What you keep.** You keep ownership of your submitted recipe and your background know-how. This license is non-exclusive: you may use your work elsewhere, improve it, and license it to others. You do not transfer your entire research practice or future work to Carbon. The license is limited to what you submit, including any identified background component that you own and deliberately include. Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

**What Carbon may do.** On your deliberate submission, you grant Carbon a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive license to copy, run, inspect, reproduce, modify and make derivative versions of the submitted recipe; use it to train and reconstruct models; and use, deploy and commercially provide the recipe and resulting models and services. Carbon may sublicense the licensed recipe rights to customers and to providers needed to perform this work. Customer sublicenses must comply with the approved confidentiality and third-party schedules. The grant applies whether or not your submission wins, advances the frontier or passes scientific gates. It does not mean Carbon has validated your work. The copyright grant and any necessary patent/know-how permissions must be expressed in counsel's final legal text; undisclosed unrelated patents are not automatically swept in.

**Models Carbon trains.** As between you and Carbon, Carbon owns the resulting model artifacts it trains using its data, seed and compute, to the extent ownership rights exist and may be allocated. This does not transfer your recipe ownership. It does not give Carbon ownership of third-party dependencies, customer data or models you have not submitted. If a delivered model embeds licensed code or other protected assets, the applicable license obligations continue. Legal allocation does not prove copyrightability or scientific qualification.

**Confidential recipes.** Carbon will treat submitted recipes as confidential under the final approved confidentiality terms. Only a hash of the recipe goes on chain. The agreed evidence/score disclosure can be published according to its separate allow-list; publishing an allowed result does not authorize publishing confidential recipe source. Authorized personnel, validators/service providers and customer sublicensees must receive only the access permitted by the agreed confidentiality schedule. Exceptions, required disclosures, retention, breach handling and customer source access are HUMAN_INPUT. This draft is not a claim that Carbon's technical security controls have been qualified.

**Authority and dependencies.** You must have the rights and authority needed to grant this license, including permission from your employer, coauthors or team when they hold rights. Identify third-party code, weights, data and patent/license restrictions in the manifest and preserve required notices. Do not submit other people's secrets, credentials or data without authorization. If a dependency cannot support the intended commercial use or confidentiality promise, the affected delivery remains blocked until a compatible route is agreed. You do not have to assign third-party rights you do not own.

**Reward and support.** Rights and scientific grading are separate. A license does not guarantee a reward, passing grade, customer contract, attribution waiver or token entitlement. Applicable reward rules remain separately identified and versioned. Whether the recipe license is royalty-free/fully paid-up, what consideration supports every submission's license, and what optional support duties apply are **HUMAN_INPUT**. Recommended commercial direction for review: no unagreed per-customer royalty or compulsory ongoing miner support; this is not an adopted payment term.

**Duration and leaving.** The intended license for a completed deliberate submission survives leaving the subnet, loss of registration, account closure and subsequent rule changes, subject to mandatory law and the final legal terms. It does not force you to submit future work. Carbon cannot broaden an old submission's grant by changing future terms. IP disputes, infringement remediation, patent exceptions, legally required deletion and survival of customer sublicenses need counsel's approved rules. “Irrevocable” does not override statutory rights that cannot be waived.

## Decisions and questions for counsel

| Question / decision | Why it matters / proposed boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Contracting entity, governing law, forum, countries and capacity; HUMAN_INPUT | Identify OpCo and enforceability against individuals/entities; assess minors, consumer rules, export/privacy and employer authority without inventing blanket jurisdiction restrictions. |
| Is the bespoke non-exclusive license sufficient, or are narrow confirmatory assignments needed for Carbon-trained artifacts? | Preserve miner recipe title. Address actual authorship, employees/contractors, joint creation, derivative code, weights and database rights separately; do not assign all research by default. |
| Exact receipt/acceptance event, accidental or malformed uploads, scientific failures and withdrawals | Make intentional submission and terms consent provable. The owner wants rights on submitted work, not winner-only rights; no silent grant from ambient workspace access or discussion. |
| Consideration and royalty-free/fully paid-up treatment; HUMAN_INPUT | Is access/prize opportunity or a stated obligation adequate in relevant jurisdictions? Avoid inventing fixed payments, minimum emissions or continuing royalties; do not assert an unpaid grant is enforceable everywhere. |
| Necessary patent grant, sublicensing tiers, continuation rights and patent retaliation; HUMAN_INPUT | Copyright alone may not permit running a patented method. Scope to rights the submitter can license and necessary submitted uses; reconcile any exception with customer continuity and irrevocability wording. |
| Know-how/confidentiality, disclosure exceptions and security obligations; HUMAN_INPUT | Owner requires confidential recipes/hash only on chain. Define lawful customer/provider access, leakage response, source escrow/retention if any and remedies without guaranteeing nonexistent controls. |
| Third-party/open-source/pretrained asset schedule and compatibility | Which combinations permit hosted use, recipe delivery, weights distribution and private sublicensing? Preserve notices and any source obligations; don't require assignment of dependencies or infer all output is unrestricted. |
| Corporate/team authorization and internal Graphite/Level 5 chain of title | A hotkey is not rights authority. Internal employee/contractor instruments are separate from external miner terms and outside this draft's implementation scope. |
| Customer sublicenses, OEM resale, transfer on acquisition and insolvency | Ensure downstream rights do not exceed incoming grant; define confidentiality, further sublicensing, ownership statements and survival. Non-exclusive recipe rights cannot be resold as exclusive title. |
| Mandatory termination, moral rights and nonwaivable law | Review territorial exceptions and surviving derivative/customer rights. Avoid universal irrevocability claims or a blanket moral-rights waiver copied without analysis. |
| Attribution, reasonable representations, liability/indemnity, dispute/appeal and takedown; HUMAN_INPUT | Miner-friendly terms should not hide uncapped individual exposure or scientific warranties. Separate provenance breach, scientific failure, infrastructure failure and reward disputes; owner selects risk allocation with counsel. |
| Historical submissions and amendments | Obtain appropriate affirmative consent for any new grant; bind receipts to old versions, preserve evidence history and avoid unilateral retroactive expansion. |
