# IP-LICENSE-OPTIONS-01 — owner brief

**RESEARCH / RECOMMENDATION ONLY — NOT OPERATIVE MINER TERMS.** Based on the owner's direct instruction and [OWNER-LAUNCH-STRATEGY-01 #978, exact record](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/27aecf8977fc11f34d66229d8a38169d514305a6/.agent/decisions/2026-10-10-OWNER-LAUNCH-STRATEGY-01.md), read with [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md). Legal terms are the owner's decision with counsel. No acceptance mechanism, rights transfer, runtime policy, payment, spend or external outreach is implemented.

**Recommend a bespoke, submission-bound non-exclusive contributor license**, with a corporate authorization path and an asset-level rights manifest. Carbon needs durable rights to run, modify and commercially use the submitted recipe and sublicense it to customers. Miners retain their recipe ownership, ability to reuse it and unsubmitted know-how. Carbon owns the model it trains, as between the parties and subject to underlying rights; paying for compute alone cannot establish all copyright, patent or third-party rights.

[OPTIONS.md](OPTIONS.md) compares assignment, exclusive licensing, commercial non-exclusive licensing, open-source contributor agreements, public permissive licenses and evaluation-only terms. Apache's CLA is a useful rights-grant pattern, but its broad communication definition and nonprofit/public-release context should not be copied. Python's CLA preserves contributor ownership but constrains relicensing to open-source licenses. A provenance sign-off alone does not grant Carbon the commercial rights it needs.

Competition precedents vary. Kaggle's published rules distinguish contest-specific winner licenses; DrivenData supplies licensing/assignment alternatives but the inspected Parkinson's contest selects MIT. Among Bittensor subnets, Enigma assigns authored submission contributions, ORO retains code ownership with licensed evaluation/training uses, and Sundae Bar licenses SN121 contributions toward a commercial generalist agent. None establishes a network-wide IP rule or proves top-miner preference. Carbon's proposed confidential recipe and customer sublicense differ from public-release or evaluation-only precedents.

[MINER_TERMS_DRAFT.md](MINER_TERMS_DRAFT.md) offers readable terms with explicit HUMAN_INPUT decisions: legal entity/jurisdiction, consideration and royalty treatment, necessary patent rights, confidentiality/access safeguards, employer/team authority, third-party dependencies, customer sublicenses, mandatory-law exceptions and dispute/liability rules. It binds the license to deliberate, identified submissions regardless of scientific success, excludes local drafts and future improvements, and preserves historical evidence and acceptance versions. Recipes remain confidential; only their hash goes on chain, per the owner's direction. This is a requested contractual design, not a claim that secrecy controls are already qualified.

Commercial strength/miner attractiveness are **qualitative researcher inferences**, not survey results, numeric scores or recruitment forecasts. Legal/recruitment costs are UNKNOWN with null low/base/high. Assignment could provide stronger control but conflicts with the owner's retained-rights direction; no change to that direction is recommended.
