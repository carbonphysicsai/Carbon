## Boundary the agreement must preserve

The [owner record, decision 4](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/27aecf8977fc11f34d66229d8a38169d514305a6/.agent/decisions/2026-10-10-OWNER-LAUNCH-STRATEGY-01.md) specifies submitted work only; Carbon ownership of models trained from submissions with Carbon's data, seed and compute; perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, sublicensable **non-exclusive** recipe rights; retained miner know-how; confidential recipes with only a hash on chain; and Carbon-owned internal Graphite/Level 5 work. Counsel must establish the legal instruments. External miner licensing does not establish employees'/contractors' chain of title for internal work.

Treat the submitted recipe, incorporated background components, third-party assets, Carbon-trained model, customer data, evidence records and unsubmitted know-how as different assets. A contract should identify each one's rights and restrictions. Non-exclusivity means Carbon can commercially use the recipe while its owner can license it elsewhere. It does not permit Carbon to promise a customer exclusive ownership of that recipe. Customer exclusivity, if offered, must concern rights Carbon can actually supply and remain separate from scientific judgment. [Business Canon §§9,13,17](../../Business_Canon.md).

## Forms compared

Commercial strength means reliable ability to reconstruct, modify, deploy, sell and sublicense the intended product, with defensible title/provenance. Miner attractiveness means retained reuse, bounded disclosure, understandable rights and proportionate obligations. **Every assessment in the last two columns is INFERENCE**, conditional on enforceability and actual miner preferences. No numeric rubric, recruitment effect, cost, revenue or market size is invented. Monetary and recruitment estimates have L/B/H=null, status UNKNOWN.

## Primary precedents and what they actually license

**Apache ICLA v2.2 §§1–7.** Contributor retains title; copyright grant is perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free and irrevocable, including sublicensing. Necessary patent claims are licensed with a litigation-triggered termination exception. Employer authority and third-party disclosures are addressed. Its contribution definition includes intentional communications through several channels, and its consideration includes nonprofit/public-benefit language. Carbon should borrow the structure, not those scope or institutional promises. [Primary ICLA](https://www.apache.org/licenses/icla.pdf). This is a license, not assignment; confidentiality is not supplied by that form.

**Python Software Foundation agreement, December 2021 form.** Contributor retains copyright and other licensing freedom. PSF receives perpetual, irrevocable copying/derivative rights under the initial license or another open-source license approved unanimously by its board. The signing workflow distinguishes organization authorization. It illustrates retained ownership but is not evidence of a private proprietary relicensing right. [Primary agreement](https://policies.python.org/python.org/Contributor-License-Agreement-V12_2021.pdf), [signing guidance](https://www.python.org/psf/contrib/contrib-form/).

**Developer Certificate of Origin v1.1.** Sign-off certifies creation or lawful reuse under the indicated open-source license, with a public contribution/sign-off record. It is not an ownership assignment or Carbon-specific commercial license. Public sign-off conventions need separate privacy treatment in a confidential recipe channel. [Primary DCO](https://developercertificate.org/).

**Kaggle: distinguish the chosen rule from template alternatives.** The ANRF AISEHack Polymer Property Prediction competition's indexed official rules specify a non-exclusive, sublicensable, perpetual/irrevocable commercial winning-submission/source-code grant. The Google Cloud & YouTube-8M competition instead selects open-source winner licensing despite containing a conditional non-exclusive alternative in its general rules. These are **winner obligations**, not proof that every ordinary entry transfers commercial rights. Fresh direct opens returned no text; indexed official-page text supports the limited comparison, and counsel should retain a full authenticated rule copy before relying on it. [AISEHack rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/aisehack-2-0/rules), [YouTube-8M rules](https://www.kaggle.com/c/youtube8m/rules). No universal Kaggle license or current adoption claim.

**DrivenData DaT Parkinson's Challenge, Permissions/Guidelines.** All entrants grant a limited administration license. Winner terms offer non-exclusive licensing, open-source licensing, assignment or an original/proprietary-elements distinction depending on the selected contest rule. This particular contest selects MIT release of winning solutions and requires rights for external data. Useful lesson: distinguish evaluation rights, winner rights, background assets and data rights. Carbon's intended commercial grant on every deliberate submission differs from that winning-only trigger. [Primary rules](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/311/dat-parkinsons-challenge/rules/). Published options are not all simultaneously operative in that contest.

**Enigma, qBitTensor Labs SN63, rules §5.** Authored/modified contributions in any submission, winning or otherwise, are assigned; genuine third-party components are excluded and licensed separately. Unassignable rights receive an exclusive license fallback; winning submissions are released under AGPL with possible additional commercial licensing. This is a clear submitted-work assignment precedent, but conflicts with Carbon's non-exclusive direction and confidential recipe channel. Its asserted consideration is participation/prize opportunity; that assertion is not independent proof of enforceability. [Primary competition rules](https://www.qbittensorlabs.com/enigma/rules).

**ORO SN15, Your submissions.** Miners retain code ownership. A perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free non-exclusive grant covers running/evaluating/auditing/publishing code and using evaluation trajectories for model training/research; sublicensing names service providers and validators. Code may become public. This is closer to retained-title licensing but does not expressly supply Carbon's desired broad customer recipe sublicense or secrecy commitment. Do not infer those rights from training permission. [Primary terms](https://app.oroagents.com/privacy-policy).

**Sundae Bar SN121, §§9,13.** SN121 submissions contribute to development/commercial offering of its generalist agent under a worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable royalty-free license; marketplace agents have a distinct rights channel. The terms address derived material and exclude contributor ownership interest in the generalist agent, and require contributor chain of title. This is a relevant commercial aggregation example, not a legal determination that its agent is never derivative. Carbon needs its own express trained-model allocation and customer sublicense. [Primary terms](https://www.sundaebar.ai/terms-of-service).

The [official Bittensor mining guide](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/mining) assigns the work definition to each subnet's codebase. Chain registration, scoring/emissions, a public model upload, or an MIT license on a validator repository does not by itself establish a commercial grant over every miner asset. No network-wide IP agreement or representative survey is claimed. The examples above are purposively selected public terms, not proof that stronger rights recruit better miners.

## Rights counsel must validate beyond the headline license

Under US copyright law, copyright protects expression but excludes ideas/processes/methods as such; preexisting material remains distinct from new authorship. A recipe's implementation, patent claims and confidential know-how require separate analysis. [17 USC §§102(b),103](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html). Owning a file or paying for compute is distinct from ownership of its copyright. Transfers require the applicable written/signature formalities; certain author grants, including non-exclusive licenses, can be terminated under mandatory statutory conditions notwithstanding contrary agreement. [17 USC §§202–205](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html). “Irrevocable” should express contractual intent subject to mandatory law, not promise universal immunity from it. These US examples do not establish worldwide enforceability.

The Copyright Office's generative-output report requires sufficient human expressive authorship for copyright protection. It is **not a ruling on Carbon's neural weights**, nor evidence that Carbon owns every possible statutory right in a trained artifact. Counsel should assess weights, inference code, embedded miner code, patent use, trade secrets and dataset restrictions separately. Carbon-model ownership must be an allocation between parties **to the extent rights exist and can be allocated**, backed by actual rights receipts. [Copyright Office summary](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html).

Third-party licenses are not washed away by the miner's license. A permitted recipe can still depend on code, pretrained weights or data that restrict redistribution, hosted use, patents or attribution. GPLv3's output rule turns on whether the output constitutes covered work, not merely use of a GPL tool; assess the actual assets, combination and delivery. [GPLv3 §2](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl.en.html). No categorical claim that using an open solver forces every trained model open, or that trained models are always exempt, is made.

## Recommended operational shape, subject to counsel

Use an explicit terms acceptance version plus an intentional submission receipt containing recipe/file manifest, immutable digest, submitting entity/authorized signer and permitted dependencies. A hotkey proves control of a key, not employer authorization or ownership. Keep identity/contract records private where appropriate; only the allowed hash goes on chain. Bind the grant at submission, regardless of winning or scientific admission, with malformed/incomplete transport distinguished from a completed deliberate submission. No grant over unsubmitted drafts, private workspace content, conversations, later ideas or future versions without submission.

Use a separate customer sublicense and data/evidence schedule; specify whether it permits hosted execution, distribution of recipe code under confidentiality, model delivery, OEM redistribution and further sublicensing. Customer rights must not exceed Carbon's incoming rights. Preserve dependency notices and use limitations. If the recipe cannot remain confidential under an incorporated component's obligations, stop that commercial route pending a compatible replacement or separately accepted scope, rather than quietly breaching either promise.

Retain access to a reproducible licensed submission and legal receipt without asserting technical confidentiality or arbitrary-code security is already qualified. A later takedown/IP dispute may require suspension or remediation; continued commercial use is not automatically lawful merely because the license says irrevocable. Subsequent rule versions cannot retrospectively broaden old grants. Reconstruction/scoring remain independently governed; ownership and payment never supply scientific admissibility.

The companion draft is intentionally incomplete on consideration/royalty treatment, patent language, liability and dispute jurisdiction. The owner has decided non-exclusivity and commercial ability, **not** a new royalty, guaranteed reward, uncapped miner indemnity, attribution waiver or customer exclusivity. Recommend counsel resolve these before any operative acceptance or submission collection under the new form. No changes to contracts, canon, submissions, optimizer or payments are included in this PR.
