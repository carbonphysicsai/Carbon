# Customer pricing benchmarks — owner summary

**Recommendation only, 2026-10-11.** Public evidence supports paying for bounded engineering analysis and independent evidence. It does **not** establish a market price for Carbon's model-plus-dossier offer. Price, scope, rights and delivery commitments remain the owner's decisions under the [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md).

Published anchors: [8020 Engineering](https://8020engineering.com/cfd-consulting-packages-and-pricing-us/) advertises CFD packages at **USD 2,490–6,990**; [Joseph Webb](https://josephwebb.net/pricing.html) publishes **USD 75/125/165 per hour**, with nonlinear studies taking **5–12 days**; [ARKE](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/403132573695999) lists independent V&V at **GBP 546.36–1,326.57 per unit/day**. These are supplier offers, not verified invoices or equivalent surrogate programmes. [CoolSim](https://coolsimsoftware.com/pricing/) publishes project/annual software access separately from services.

For Ansys, Altair/Siemens, PhysicsX, Monolith and Neural Concept, the reviewed primary material provides no verified numerical enterprise transaction price. Autodesk NavPack's official buying page was not retrievable; its current price is **HUMAN_INPUT**, rather than evidence that no public price exists. Funding, claimed savings, token counts and training time cannot fill this gap.

The following **ASSUMPTION low/base/high** budgets use explicit expert hours, a transferred advertised hourly-rate proxy, and a nonlabor allowance. They are conditional planning envelopes for one bounded design family with usable data and reference route already available; they are **not quotes, confidence intervals, market medians or promised qualification**.

[Job offers](JOB_OFFERS.md) defines scope, exclusions, input tests and failure outcomes. [In-house cost](IN_HOUSE_COST.md) gives an independent US labor scenario. [Market evidence](MARKET_EVIDENCE.md) separates software, consulting, reported buyer spend, avoided cost and elapsed time. [Sources](SOURCES.md) and [machine-readable evidence](evidence.json) retain units, provenance and unknowns.

Start with a priced intake/evidence-gap assessment, then quote model development only after feasibility, reference adequacy and cheap-baseline checks. A negative finding is a valid deliverable. An EvidenceAudit requires its own accepted evidence prerequisites; an unqualified exam yields a development gap report. Capture actual buyer spend, decision volume, staff effort, data preparation, acceptance scope and calendar waits before calibrating a quote model.

Warpage remains outside internal launch: missing process/material calibration makes price and elapsed completion **unknown**, not merely the high scenario. None of these commercial examples changes the launch portfolio. Carbon's public rehearsal evidence is useful development evidence; this work demonstrates neither paid traction nor a deployable qualified customer tool.
