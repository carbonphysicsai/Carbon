## Opening draft

Carbon is building discovery and evidence infrastructure for fast physical models. The intended customer output is an evidence record about whether an exact model supports an exact engineering decision, where it fails, and what stronger evidence is still needed. EvidenceAudit is the preferred first commercial product; sponsored competitive discovery is the next network-differentiated direction. These are product plans, not paid traction. [I01: Business Canon](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/567dae6491a187253d463390225e019835de33f7/Business/Business_Canon.md)

We are rehearsing that process internally: define the brief and hard requirements, make the open reference route credible for the stated claim, test the strongest affordable shortcuts, compare design search at equal time and compute, and return a versioned evidence dossier. The reference simulator settles the selected numerical outputs. A model's proposed benefit is a better admissible design within the total budget; fitting a solver quickly is insufficient by itself. The current equal-budget tooling states that it has no real battery-v3, motor-S3 or f02 curve yet. [I10: equal-budget evidence boundary](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/567dae6491a187253d463390225e019835de33f7/docs/development/challenge_pipeline/equal-budget-design.md)

The public development record contains failures as well as implementation progress. It has exposed false-feasible choices and a score that reconstructed reproducibly while failing an adversarial decision test. Those findings show what the process detected under its historical scope. They do not establish that newer rules have repaired every failure.
