# PRICING-EVIDENCE-01 — owner brief

**Recommendation:** price an agreed evidence package by its decision value, scope and full delivery cost. Use the published comparators below to check plausibility; do not turn a compute bill or a general consulting day rate into Carbon's product price. This follows [Business Canon](../../Business_Canon.md) and #956's [tier criteria](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/b0244360dbe49d534b0346779e1cebe5b21de085/Business/research/deliverable-framework/FRAMEWORK.md).

Public evidence is strongest for consulting rate cards and bounded simulation work. ARKEG's published modelling/simulation V&V rate is £546.36–£1,326.57 per unit/day. A small FEA supplier publishes $1,200–$2,000 quick assessments and $2,800–$5,500 standard projects. CoolSim publishes a $4,500 project pass and optional modelling services from $5,000/project. These are advertised offers, not observed invoices or proof of standards-grade audit pricing. [Sources and limitations](RESEARCH.md).

**Per-tier planning ranges**, low/base/high, all **ASSUMPTION**, in GBP: EvidenceAudit **£2,731.80 / £15,000 / £39,797.10**; decision tool plus dossier **£16,390.80 / £90,000 / £238,782.60**; lifecycle re-evidence **£2,731.80 / £20,000 / £79,594.20 per bounded material-change event**. These are supplier-rate-equivalent effort envelopes, not proposed Carbon quotes, typical V&V costs, willingness-to-pay or total delivery budgets. Physical testing, model construction, integration, rights, compute and support may sit outside them. The arithmetic and assumed effort are in [evidence.json](evidence.json).

PhysicsX and Ansys SimAI have **no usable public list price in the reviewed pages**. Their low/base/high are **HUMAN_INPUT**, not a range inferred from fundraising or third-party pricing sites. Generic AI-audit services are adjacent benchmarks; they do not establish physics credibility or accreditation.

The battery sample qualifies for no commercial tier yet. The next pricing evidence should be a buyer-approved scope with its incumbent cost, error consequences and acceptance criteria, plus an independently costed delivery plan. A failed subject model can still produce a valuable audit, but the exam/reference must be adequate for that audit. Owner decisions: offer scope, actual price, liability/rights/support and any lifecycle commercial terms.
