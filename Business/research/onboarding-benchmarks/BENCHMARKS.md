## Failure evidence worth copying into the ledger

[ImageNet-V2's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10811) found accuracy drops on newly collected test data, while attributing the shift mainly to harder examples rather than establishing adaptive overfitting as the cause. Lesson: stable public benchmark ranking does not prove reliability on the intended population. Carbon must keep P separate from enriched witness Q and score weighting w; a deliberately hard panel is not an unbiased deployment sample.

[Current MLPerf Endpoints compliance documentation](https://docs.mlcommons.org/endpoints-submission-docs/reference/compliance-checks/) explicitly warns that its automated accuracy-presence check lags the developing rule's point coverage. Lesson: a passing script can miss a required evidence condition. This is a documented development-branch limitation, not an allegation about all ratified MLPerf results. Freeze rule, checker and evidence versions separately.

SN37's cited release notes document a dataloader bug, external dataset/API instability, increased sample size and validator-state resets. Carbon should bind dataset/reference and scorer versions; a reset can repair future evaluation without silently rescaling historical claims. Its own [#970 blocker taxonomy](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/blob/6b60cdd235b2ccda38339a72899c9c7f262b8bf5/docs/development/challenge_pipeline/onboarding/blocker_taxonomy.json) records routing, runtime/distro, controller, pod, token, reboot, permissions and silent-failure classes. Time lost remains UNKNOWN; no invented delay estimates are assigned.

## What best in class should mean for Carbon

Recommendation: compare repeatable **evidence-complete** throughput, not a declaration-to-announcement stopwatch. For each stage record challenge/brief/reference/contract versions; entered, work-started, evidence-complete and accepted timestamps; work hours; queue intervals and blockers; owner/lane; attempted/completed/failed/censored work; setup and compute/retention cash; expected and actual deliverables; cheap-baseline receipt; rights; and rework parent. Inputs, process, outputs and network receipts should use the same immutable brief identity.
