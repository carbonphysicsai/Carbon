# Carbon Business Canon v1

**Status:** OWNER-CANONICAL business section of the Carbon canon.  
**Purpose:** preserve the durable business principles learned through the commercial, revenue, pricing, GTM, investor, and market-positioning gauntlets.  
**Scope:** company/business architecture outside the scientific loop.  
**Does not override:** scientific specifications, Challenge qualification, Score Packs, validator rules, Product Qualification Packs, or treasury protocol specifications.

---

# 1. Business identity

> **Carbon is building the discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models.**

Investor/company expression:

> **Carbon lands through high-value independent evidence and sponsored R&D programs, converts repeated workflows into enterprise software and APIs, uses an open research network to scale discovery, and monetizes qualification and model lifecycle as recurring infrastructure.**

Category:

> **Discovery + Evidence Infrastructure for Physics AI**

Investor shorthand:

> **The evidence and research-market layer for Physics AI.**

Carbon is not required to own the customer's CAE stack, high-fidelity solver, model architecture, or engineering workflow. It can sit across those systems as an independent discovery/evidence layer.

---

# 2. Durable business model

The hybrid approach is locked:

```text
SERVICES
        ↓
ENTERPRISE PLATFORM
        ↓
NETWORK MARKETPLACE
        ↓
QUALIFICATION + LIFECYCLE
        ↓
PHYSICS INTELLIGENCE
```

Canon law:

> **Services are the customer-entry wedge, not the terminal margin structure.**

> **Every repeated expert workflow should be evaluated for productization into reusable templates, adapters, software, APIs, or platform primitives.**

> **Recurring qualification, lifecycle, support, usage, and platform revenue are first-class business objectives.**

---

# 3. Standalone-company doctrine

Carbon OpCo must be commercially defensible even if an investor assigns zero value to speculative Alpha appreciation.

A credible standalone company should be able to build toward:

- high-value enterprise contracts;
- repeatable productized services;
- recurring software and usage revenue;
- qualification and lifecycle attachment revenue;
- improving gross margin as workflows standardize;
- API/OEM distribution;
- rights-aware reusable knowledge assets;
- network supply-side leverage;
- multi-industry applicability without serving every vertical at once.

Canon law:

> **Build a real company, not a token-dependent story.**

---

# 4. Customer problem doctrine

As fast physical models become easier to build, the bottleneck increasingly shifts toward:

```text
DISCOVERY
Which method should we try?

EVIDENCE
Does the model actually survive?

QUALIFICATION
Can this exact artifact support this exact job?

LIFECYCLE
What remains valid after the model, data, solver, hardware, or use changes?
```

Carbon should sell into these problems rather than forcing customers to buy a generic "Physics AI platform."

# 6. First-product doctrine

Preferred first wedge:

> **Carbon Evidence Audit** — independently evaluate a customer's existing fast physical model against a qualified, problem-specific exam and return evidence about what survives, where it fails, and what stronger evidence or remediation would be required.

Why:

- monetizes the strongest near-term Carbon capability: the judge;
- does not require a mature open discovery market;
- can produce value even when the model fails;
- naturally expands into remediation, discovery, qualification, deployment, and lifecycle;
- creates reusable evidence workflows.

Preferred first network-differentiated product:

> **Carbon Sponsored Discovery Program** — qualify a customer problem, open competitive search, independently determine whether the frontier moved, and deliver candidate/evidence outputs.

---

# 7. Commercial outcome doctrine

Scientific success and commercial delivery are distinct.

Examples:

```text
Customer model fails the Audit
→ commercially successful delivery if the agreed evidence program was delivered honestly.

No contender advances a Sponsored Discovery frontier
→ commercially successful program if the qualified search/evidence program was delivered.
```

Canon law:

> **Sales must never depend on manufacturing a favorable scientific answer.**

# 9. Pricing doctrine

Pricing may reflect:

- authoring labor;
- truth integration burden;
- evaluation compute;
- security/deployment requirements;
- rights/exclusivity;
- support SLA;
- urgency and customer value;
- software/platform usage.

Pricing may not change the scientific standard for the same registered claim.

Avoid as primary business models:

- generic consulting day rates;
- success-only pricing;
- unlimited evaluation bundles;
- compute-only pricing;
- mandatory token purchase for enterprise customers.

Canon law:

> **Compute is a cost and usage rail, not the complete value proposition.**

# 11. Recurrence doctrine

Recurring revenue should arise from real engineering change, not artificial subscription packaging.

Re-evidence may be required when:

- model version changes;
- training/data changes;
- solver changes;
- hardware/runtime changes;
- operating envelope expands;
- system composition changes;
- deployment context changes;
- customer program requirements change.

Therefore Carbon can become persistent evidence infrastructure for a changing portfolio of physical models.

---

# 12. Margin doctrine

Early Carbon may contain meaningful expert services. The business only becomes attractive at scale if delivery becomes increasingly reusable and software-led.

Track:

- custom expert hours per engagement;
- reusable adapter/template share;
- software/usage share of revenue;
- recurring revenue share;
- gross contribution by revenue rail;
- revenue per technical FTE;
- platform/OEM adoption;
- network-executed share of eligible work.

Canon law:

> **If every new customer requires rebuilding a consulting company inside Carbon, the business architecture has failed.**

---

# 13. Rights and privacy doctrine

Carbon may compound customer-derived evidence or method knowledge only where rights and confidentiality permit it.

Commercial contracts should explicitly address rights in:

- customer data;
- customer solver access;
- Challenge semantics;
- submitted strategies/methods;
- reconstructed artifacts;
- model weights/source/recipes;
- ExperimentRecords/evidence metadata;
- aggregated evidence;
- Landscape/Physics-Intelligence derivatives;
- publications;
- cross-customer reuse;
- exclusivity.

Canon law:

> **Commercial optimization does not imply ownership.**

Private/customer-controlled/air-gapped modes are legitimate product directions, but must be technically qualified before being sold as implemented capabilities.

---

# 14. Go-to-market doctrine

Do not lead enterprise sales with Bittensor or Alpha.

Lead with the customer's engineering problem:

> **Do you already build or use fast physical models, and what prevents you from relying on them more broadly?**

Primary early direct-selling verticals to validate:

1. aerospace / space / defense;
2. energy / turbomachinery / industrial physics.

Strategic partner track:

- CAE / engineering software vendors;
- engineering-AI platforms;
- simulation infrastructure providers.

These can be competitors, suppliers, customers, or OEM/integration partners depending on the engagement.

# 15. Category and competitive doctrine

Carbon should not position itself as a smaller generic AI-native engineering platform.

Preferred stack position:

```text
HIGH-FIDELITY PHYSICS / CAE / EXPERIMENTS
        ↓
MODEL BUILDERS / ENGINEERING-AI SYSTEMS
        ↓
CARBON
independent discovery + evidence + qualification
        ↓
ENGINEERING DEPLOYMENT / DECISION WORKFLOWS
```

Differentiation is the combination of:

```text
qualified exam
+ producer-independent evaluation
+ competitive construction-method search
+ frontier economics
+ bounded qualification
+ lifecycle evidence
```

Do not claim that no one else validates models; claim the differentiated institutional combination only where accurate.

# 16. Moat doctrine

The durable moat should compound across:

- qualified evidence workflows;
- truth/solver/representation adapters;
- proprietary evaluation and qualification infrastructure;
- independent experiment records;
- rights-permitted method knowledge;
- customer workflow integrations;
- external research liquidity;
- qualification/lifecycle registry;
- later Physics Intelligence that demonstrates prospective decision lift.

Canon law:

> **Bittensor alone is not the moat. Scientific rigor alone is not the moat. The moat is the compounding system of evidence, workflow, supply, rights, and integration.**

---

# 17. Network doctrine

The subnet is Carbon's scalable external research/optimizer and verification market.

Its strategic role must be earned empirically through one or more of:

- more useful hypotheses per dollar;
- greater method diversity;
- faster verified frontier improvement;
- lower marginal discovery cost;
- access to specialist external capability.

Canon law:

> **Decentralization is a falsifiable business advantage, not an article of faith.**

If centralized search outperforms the network on relevant cost/performance measures, Carbon should treat that as evidence against the network thesis for that workload.

---

# 18. OpCo / Alpha doctrine

```text
CARBON OPCO
enterprise revenue
services / software / licenses / support
        ↕ explicit reviewed bridges
CARBON NETWORK
miners / validators / frontier rewards / Alpha / treasury
```

Rules:

1. OpCo revenue does not automatically create Alpha value.
2. Enterprise buying may remain fiat-first.
3. Network value should come from genuine economically useful network work.
4. Sponsored rewards can create additional participant incentives where appropriate.
5. Network-native service utility may be added where it improves the product rather than customer friction.
6. Buyback/burn/revenue-share or similar financial mechanisms require separate legal/economic review before adoption.

Canon law:

> **Create Alpha value by making the subnet economically necessary to valuable scientific work, not by allowing token economics to become scientific authority.**

# 20. Market-size doctrine

Do not construct TAM by adding overlapping top-down markets.

Use:

```text
TAM
adjacent economic pools and category evidence, clearly avoiding double count

SAM
bottom-up target enterprises × programs/model portfolios × addressable annual spend

SOM
named accounts × qualified-opportunity rate × close rate × ACV × expansion
```

Hard market figures belong in a separately sourced investor model, not in this canon unless independently validated and dated.
