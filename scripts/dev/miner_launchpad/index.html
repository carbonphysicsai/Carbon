      Wallet & identity

      Wallet & Identity

      Register your hotkey on the subnet. You sign in your own wallet.

      Register on the subnet

      00 / Join

      Registration is how you reach Carbon's research environment. You execute it in your own wallet — Carbon never asks for, holds or transmits a private key, seed phrase or mnemonic, and nothing here signs on your behalf.

      The recycle amount is spent from your COLDKEY, not your hotkey. Funding the hotkey is the most common way a first registration fails: the transaction is signed for the hotkey you are registering, and the balance is taken from the coldkey that owns it.

      Paste an address only. This field rejects anything phrase-shaped before it is read, and no request body records what you typed.
