Mine Carbon

From a clean Linux machine to your own Carbon Control Center, then choose the Challenge you want to mine.

Testnet (subnet 567), DEVELOPMENT. Nothing here is qualified, paid or on chain. Your keys never leave your own wallet.

What you need

  1. A Linux x86-64 machine (Windows: WSL2) with git, curl and Docker. For GPU practice, an NVIDIA GPU with the NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
  2. A hotkey in your own Bittensor wallet. The Control Center prepares the registration call; you sign it in your own wallet.
  3. An inference key of your own (Engy or Chutes) for an autonomous agent, if you use one.

1. Install and open the Control Center

git clone https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon.git ~/carbon
~/carbon/scripts/install_miner.sh        # add --gpu for GPU practice

The script checks your machine, installs Carbon's locked environment, builds the pinned images locally and starts the Control Center on 127.0.0.1. It prints the address and a session token.

2. Start your signer

~/carbon/.venv/bin/carbon-miner-signer --wallet <wallet> --hotkey <hotkey>

Carbon never holds or asks for your key. Every request is signed by this process, on your machine.

3. In the Control Center

  1. Wallet & Identity: confirm your hotkey is registered on subnet 567.
  2. Set up your environment: inference, compute (this machine's CPU or GPU, or a GPU rented on your own RunPod, Lium or Targon account) and agent (Carbon's or Hermes).
  3. Challenges: read each Challenge's description and research environment, choose one and launch.
