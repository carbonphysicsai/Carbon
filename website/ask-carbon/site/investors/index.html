04 / The Evidence Landscape

The Exponential Asset.

Every evaluation is recorded: method, conditions, reference, cost, where it failed, how sure the result is. The records compound into a living brain of what works in physics AI, and every model it absorbs makes it more valuable.

It cannot be bought, scraped or copied. It’s built only by running the evaluations Carbon runs.

What Landscape becomes

Stage one

The map

Every model Carbon evaluates adds a record: what was built, what it was tested against, where it broke and at what cost. The map of what works in physics AI, and what does not.

Stage two

The research copilot

Open Landscape on a turbine problem and see everything known about it in one place: what is proven, what is disputed, where the evidence is weak and what nobody has tested yet.

Stage three

The decision-value engine

Ask where the next $100,000, or $10 million, of R&D should go. Landscape ranks every possible experiment, simulation and model evaluation by how much uncertainty it removes per dollar.

Example

“Increase turbine efficiency by 3% without exceeding the existing thermal envelope.”

  1. Landscape breaks the objective into research questions
  2. Surfaces what is known, and which assumptions are shaky
  3. Proposes candidate approaches
  4. Commissions simulations and tests physics AI models through Carbon
  5. Recommends the single next experiment worth running
  6. The result comes back. The map improves. The next call is better.

A $10B aerospace, energy or industrial company spends billions deciding what to investigate next. Landscape is built to answer that question. You could build a company on that capability alone.
