01 / The selection thesis

We invest in repeated decisions.
Not expensive simulations for their own sake.

A slow solver is not enough. A valuable industry is not enough. Each program needs a bounded physical job, a credible alternative, a path to measurements and a reason a customer would pay again.

01

Choose a real engineering job

Geometry, schedules and operating conditions are decisions. Raw field prediction is a tool. We invest where a better model could change an actual design or operating choice.

02

Beat the cheapest competent route

Compare against optimized solvers, reduced-order models, analytical methods and caching. Include data, training, calibration, verification and fallback—not just inference.

03

Build assets that can be reused

A reference adapter, evidence workflow and rights-cleared method can support new variants. Reuse must reduce measured delivery effort; eight bespoke consulting projects are not the end state.

Portfolio planning lens

Commercial merit first.
Network conviction matters.

The selection gives Bittensor investor fit a 25% planning weight: recognizable frontier problems, visible proof and a credible role for external research. The remaining 75% is commercial and technical judgment. These are qualitative planning weights, not a measured ranking or emissions formula.

01 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Cold-plate manufacturers, server OEMs and accelerator thermal teams. Thermal engineering is the technical entry point; an R&D or product owner must establish a real budget.

First commercial engagement

A finite Evidence Audit or feasibility study of a channel-design workflow, followed by sponsored model development if the current method has a costly, measurable gap.

Where the economics could work

The addressable job is screening and refining a family of designs, not replacing every CFD calculation. Potential value comes from less engineering wait time, more verified design iterations and, only if a design is tested, lower pumping burden. Better component predictions alone do not establish data-center energy savings.

Why the work could recur

New heat maps, package layouts, coolant conditions and product variants could reuse an adapter and trigger model updates or re-evaluation. That is the recurring-work hypothesis; it is not contracted demand.

The strong alternative to beat

Physics-aware channel correlations, thermal-resistance networks, cached calculations and an optimized conjugate heat-transfer design workflow.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a manufacturable geometry and flow setting under the same pumping, temperature and geometry constraints. Independently check the chosen design for feasibility and hydraulic objective; count the full search and checking costs.

Physical scope and exclusions

Begin with bounded single-phase laminar flow and solid conduction. The existing straight periodic microchannel model excludes headers and manifolds. Coupled manifold design needs a new, verified reference contract before it can enforce temperature constraints.

Open-source reference path

OpenFOAM. Reuse the existing channel work, then qualify any new geometry, flow distribution and thermal coupling. Generic flow-only predictions cannot certify a hotspot temperature.

Evidence today

The current cold-plate development study supports a channel examination, not a commercial manifold model. Its learned baseline showed only a small aggregate advantage over a closed form and a worse temperature-profile prediction. No decisive design-workflow win is established.

Next evidence milestone

Measure complete channel-design economics against the strong closed form; verify the coupled reference before widening geometry.

Path to physical reality

Calibrated inlet temperature, heat input, pressure, flow and temperature measurements on an instrumented plate; predict new operating conditions before observing them.

When we would pause

Pause acceleration investment if the inexpensive baseline already reaches the same verified design at lower total cost, or if realistic coupling and manufacturability remove the advantage.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Headers and manifold distribution
  • Tolerance-aware coolant routing
  • Customer-specific plate families
  • System integration with new evidence

02 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Accelerator, server and power-management engineering teams. This shares a customer conversation with cooling, but is a separate dynamic physical task.

First commercial engagement

A scoped thermal-model audit or an instrumented heater/plate feasibility program, followed by device-specific model development where measurements support it.

Where the economics could work

The value hypothesis is less conservative operation within an evidenced thermal envelope and faster evaluation of power schedules. We must compare the complete decision workflow, not claim throughput gains from a temperature model alone. Cheap calibrated dynamics may already solve the problem.

Why the work could recur

Power histories, package/contact changes, cooling boundaries and runtime revisions could create recurring calibration and lifecycle work. Expansion into scheduling or control needs additional system evidence.

The strong alternative to beat

Calibrated thermal RC networks, analytical conduction models and reduced-order dynamics. These can be extremely fast and are mandatory competitors.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a complete power schedule on an unseen waveform family. Verify its temperature trajectory, time to limit and recovery independently; measure useful work at the same registered constraints and resource budget.

Physical scope and exclusions

Layered-solid transient conduction with specified contact and boundary conditions. No boiling, thermal runaway or whole-data-center scheduler claim. All causal inputs and the initial state must be available.

Open-source reference path

Proposed Elmer transient thermal reference, with an appropriate independent analytical or numerical witness on selected cases. Solver availability is not reference qualification.

Evidence today

This is a proposed extension, not an implemented or experimentally validated Carbon challenge. It is the preferred first owned experiment because the input power and measured temperature can be controlled and recorded directly.

Next evidence milestone

Define and verify a layered-plate task, benchmark calibrated RC models, then price and approve a prospective instrumented pilot.

Path to physical reality

An owned heater/plate rig. Commit forecasts before a held-out power schedule runs; separate frozen-model forecasting from any explicitly versioned online adaptation.

When we would pause

Do not fund a larger learned model if calibrated RC dynamics meet the decision requirement more cheaply. Do not infer identifiable material or contact parameters from an ambiguous sensor fit.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Device-specific thermal dynamics
  • Contact and boundary calibration
  • Power-envelope lifecycle updates
  • Control support after separate qualification

03 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Silicon-photonics, optical-packaging and interconnect engineering teams. A component design program is the entry, not a claim to model an entire co-packaged optics system.

First commercial engagement

A reference/feasibility study for one material stack and port definition, then sponsored robust-design research and a rights-cleared fabrication/measurement program.

Where the economics could work

The prospective advantage is more verified tolerance-aware design options per research budget. Full-wave cost can make repeated search attractive, but adjoint optimization may drastically reduce the solver burden. Fabrication, metrology and process-distribution acquisition can dominate the real economics.

Why the work could recur

New stacks, alignment envelopes, bands and packaging variants may reuse methods and evidence workflows. Commercial re-use depends on stack access, rights and actual process measurements.

The strong alternative to beat

Adjoint optimization, lower-dimensional screening, interpolation and optimized full-wave verification. Do not compare only with brute-force parameter sweeps.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a grating geometry for a declared tolerance distribution and wavelength band. Verify selected devices with converged 3D calculations. Synthetic robustness becomes a manufacturing claim only after actual process and optical evidence.

Physical scope and exclusions

Fixed material stack, polarization, band and port conventions; bounded manufacturable geometry. Numerical loss and coupling require qualified normalization, phase, boundaries and convergence.

Open-source reference path

Proposed Meep full-wave FDTD; an existing fdtdx GPU path is a feasibility candidate, not automatic truth. Pin CPU/GPU profiles separately and qualify any primary/witness role.

Evidence today

Carbon's existing symmetric coupler is a cheap, lower-dimensional internal test. It does not substantiate the commercial 3D grating task. Retain it as a regression asset; replace it as the headline use case.

Next evidence milestone

Demonstrate converged full-wave outputs and realistic fabrication bounds, then measure a robust-design workflow against an adjoint baseline.

Path to physical reality

Measured geometry and optical transmission on fabricated devices, with held-out devices and declared alignment conditions. Do not label a synthetic tolerance score as manufacturing yield.

When we would pause

Pause if an optimized adjoint workflow wins on verified design quality and cost, or if fabrication access and rights prevent a credible experimental path.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Packaging alignment robustness
  • New registered material stacks
  • Other grating and coupling families
  • Optical-I/O component workflows

04 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Cell manufacturers, battery R&D teams and OEM battery/BMS engineers. The hypothesis is a bounded cell-development program, not universal charging software.

First commercial engagement

Audit a charging/ageing decision workflow or integrate an available cell reference, then sponsor cell-specific model and experiment development.

Where the economics could work

Model-guided protocol selection could reduce simulation and experimental search effort. That is valuable only when selected schedules survive independent feasibility checks. Real-cell cycling, replication and diagnostic costs must be included; an optimistic boundary prediction can erase a nominal compute saving.

Why the work could recur

Chemistry, batch, temperature, geometry and protocol changes could create recurring calibration and re-evaluation. Full lifetime and pack operation require new contracts and evidence.

The strong alternative to beat

CCCV rules, calibrated reduced battery models, interpolation, direct schedule optimization and established experimental-design methods.

The fixed-resource design test

Commit a charging protocol before fresh independent assessment. Compare verified charge time at the same constraints, including false-feasible selections, capacity outcomes and complete search/verification costs.

Physical scope and exclusions

Retain the existing PyBaMM charging-plus-ageing scope: 30 simulated cycles, voltage/temperature trajectories, plating margin and specified capacity checkpoints. It is not full lifetime, real-cell safety or measured plating truth.

Open-source reference path

Existing PyBaMM DFN with the pinned ageing and thermal formulation. A future experimental contract must define cell identity, diagnostics, replication and its assessment horizon.

Evidence today

Battery is the protocol-development lead. The decision-value studies retain feasibility-boundary and localized prediction concerns; no commercial or experimentally grounded charging superiority is established.

Next evidence milestone

Complete the battery-led challenge protocol and resolve decision-value findings before admitting broader tasks or expanding the charging claim.

Path to physical reality

Supervised, replicated cell tests with held-out protocols and batches, rights-cleared diagnostics and predictions committed before outcomes. This page authorizes no charging or hardware control.

When we would pause

Do not promote a faster protocol when independent checks find constraint violations. Voltage and temperature alone cannot establish absence of lithium plating.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Measured cell-specific calibration
  • Batch and temperature variation
  • Richer thermal geometry
  • Lifetime or pack tasks only with new evidence

05 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Precision actuator, robotics and motor-design teams. Electromagnetic design is the initial scope; full robot motion quality requires system evidence.

First commercial engagement

A torque-prediction or design-workflow audit, followed by sponsored bounded motor optimization and measured validation of shortlisted designs.

Where the economics could work

The opportunity is faster exploration of acceptable geometry/current combinations and fewer poor designs sent to prototype. A torque-ripple improvement must survive independent analysis and tolerances; it cannot be priced as an efficiency, acoustic or robot-throughput improvement without those measurements.

Why the work could recur

New geometric families, current conditions and measured material curves could support repeat development and lifecycle work. Reusable meshing/evaluation reduces delivery effort only if demonstrated across actual programs.

The strong alternative to beat

Analytical harmonic models, interpolation, reduced magnetic models and optimized multi-angle electromagnetic analysis.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a geometry/current combination from the same bounds. Independently verify the full torque-angle curve, mean torque and ripple objective, accounting for saturation and critical mesh regions.

Physical scope and exclusions

Existing bounded 2D 8-pole/24-slot motor with a 60-angle torque curve and declared iron law. No automatic 3D end-effect, thermal, efficiency-map or assembly-level precision claim.

Open-source reference path

Existing pinned GetDP and Gmsh path with refinement checks. Measured material laws and any new 3D or loss model require separate reference adequacy.

Evidence today

The full-curve development study showed a learned baseline improving aggregate error against its analytical comparator, but retained substantial ripple error and a reference-invalid case. This is not commercial precision qualification.

Next evidence milestone

Measure complete design-search value, close ripple/tail concerns and test independently checked selected designs.

Path to physical reality

Measured steel response and calibrated current/torque-angle tests, with unseen geometries or devices and explicit fixture uncertainty.

When we would pause

Pause if curve-level improvements fail to select better feasible motors, or if a cheap reduced model provides the same decision at lower cost.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Measured material curves
  • Tolerance-aware torque design
  • 3D end effects as a new task
  • Loss and thermal coupling with new qualification

06 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Precision-automation, motion-stage and robotics engineering teams. This can expand the motor conversation without treating motor and structural evidence as interchangeable.

First commercial engagement

A dynamic-response model audit or bounded variable-geometry feasibility study, then a design program tied to a customer's measured excitation and supports.

Where the economics could work

Potential value is less redesign and faster selection of structures that avoid critical resonances. A fixed-geometry modal sweep is often inexpensive: the investment case depends on variable geometry, reusable evidence or a demonstrated improvement against measured behavior, not deliberately slow solver settings.

Why the work could recur

Payload, support, damping and assembly changes can create new evidence work. Repeated product-family studies could justify lifecycle tools after delivery effort and demand are measured.

The strong alternative to beat

Modal superposition, efficient factorization reuse, analytical beams and reduced-order models across geometry changes.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a geometry under common mass, stiffness and excitation constraints. Independently verify peak complex response and near-resonance behavior; compare against a tuned modal workflow.

Physical scope and exclusions

Linear elasticity with explicitly defined excitation, support and damping. No nonlinear impact, fatigue, full-machine settling-time or throughput claim.

Open-source reference path

Proposed CalculiX modal/harmonic analysis with mesh and frequency refinement, supported by analytical controls. Static stress and modal families are foundations, not additional headline slots.

Evidence today

A roadmap family and an available solver do not constitute an implemented Carbon challenge. The variable-geometry decision and reference adequacy still need to be defined and tested.

Next evidence milestone

Demonstrate a meaningful variable-geometry job and benchmark a strong modal baseline before building a large learned-model study.

Path to physical reality

Calibrated force and accelerometer measurements on a structure, held out by geometry or assembly. Fit and forecast data must remain separate.

When we would pause

Pause if fast modal methods already answer the job, or if unobserved damping/support behavior makes the proposed model underdetermined.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Measured joints and damping
  • Payload and support variants
  • Precision-machine assemblies
  • Broader dynamics with new reference scope

07 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Industrial compressor and equipment acoustic-design teams. A component silencer study is the entry; the model does not generate the compressor's noise source.

First commercial engagement

A linear-acoustic feasibility or model-evidence study, followed by component design and a measured duct-rig program if the baseline leaves a valuable gap.

Where the economics could work

A reusable design workflow could shorten acoustic iteration and reduce failed prototypes. The first cost test is against transfer matrices and modal methods. Backpressure and operating energy are separate checks; a linear acoustic score cannot establish those savings.

Why the work could recur

Source spectra, packaging, termination and product variants may yield repeat programs. Operating-flow acoustics is a later scope, not an included feature.

The strong alternative to beat

Transfer-matrix methods, modal acoustics and optimized 3D acoustic verification on designs where the simpler representation is inadequate.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a chamber/duct geometry within the same package constraints. Independently verify band attenuation and complex response, with pinned source and termination assumptions.

Physical scope and exclusions

Linear passive acoustics under declared boundary impedances. No turbulent noise generation, flow-induced effects or pressure-loss prediction.

Open-source reference path

Proposed Elmer Helmholtz reference, with qualified analytical controls and a possible independent GetDP witness on difficult cases.

Evidence today

The challenge remains a proposal. No Carbon silencer reference examination, experimentally verified design or customer ROI is claimed.

Next evidence milestone

Show that a realistic compact geometry creates a valuable decision beyond the transfer-matrix baseline, then verify the acoustic reference.

Path to physical reality

A calibrated duct rig measuring transfer response with declared source and termination conditions, with unseen geometries and repeat measurements.

When we would pause

Use the cheap method if it already solves the job. Do not sell an acoustic-only optimum as a viable compressor component without separate flow and integration checks.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • Source and termination variants
  • Customer packaging families
  • Operating-flow acoustics as a new task
  • Coupled pressure-loss/integration checks

08 / Research investment case

Who could buy

Microfluidic cartridge, lab-automation and process-equipment developers. This is a component-design opportunity, not a clinical or drug-development claim.

First commercial engagement

A passive-mixing reference or model audit, followed by a bounded cartridge-design program and tracer measurements where the economics justify it.

Where the economics could work

Potential value is quicker cartridge iteration and fewer physical design trials. The full cost includes the velocity-field solve and transport accuracy; artificial numerical diffusion cannot count as better mixing. Simple channels may favor analytical or reduced models.

Why the work could recur

Flow rates, diffusivities and cartridge families may create repeat evaluation and adaptation. Reuse across customers depends on geometry rights and experimental comparability.

The strong alternative to beat

Analytical diffusion/channel estimates, network transport models, reduced-order models and optimized flow-plus-transport simulation.

The fixed-resource design test

Select a manufacturable cartridge and flow setting. Independently check outlet concentration uniformity at the same residence/length constraints, including the cost and uncertainty of the velocity solution.

Physical scope and exclusions

Laminar incompressible flow with passive scalar transport and declared diffusivity. No reaction yield, cell viability, clinical performance or chemical kinetics claim.

Open-source reference path

Proposed OpenFOAM flow and scalar-transport path. Qualify Peclet behavior, conservation, positivity and numerical diffusion; count flow solving rather than hiding it as free input.

Evidence today

This is a design proposal, not a verified Carbon micromixer model. The flow, transport and measurement contracts require their own feasibility and reference work.

Next evidence milestone

Verify flow-plus-transport controls, compare an inexpensive mixing baseline and define an observable cartridge task before scaling research.

Path to physical reality

Calibrated fluorescence or tracer observations, with an explicit sensor/observation model and whole cartridges or experiments held out.

When we would pause

Pause if low-cost transport estimates solve the decision or if measurement/solver diffusion obscures the actual physical mixing advantage.

Potential expansions · not current capabilities

  • New passive cartridge families
  • Measured transport calibration
  • Lab automation integration
  • Reaction tasks only under new contracts

03 / The economic discipline

The win has to survive
the whole cost stack.

A model can predict in milliseconds and still be the more expensive choice. Research is justified by the total cost of reaching the same acceptable engineering decision, over a useful life.

04 / The proof investors should expect

Not “AI vs. solver.”
A fair design competition.

Once the references and models are ready, we propose testing the engineering decisions they produce. No matched-resource portfolio competition results are published here yet.

Proposed comparison contract

  1. 01

    Freeze the job

    Same geometry or schedule bounds, constraints, allowed information and fresh held-out cases. Register the decision and failure rules before outcomes.

  2. 02

    Match the resources

    Declare hardware, elapsed-time and total-dollar budgets. For a model-only test, also match optimizer, initialization, query allowance and stopping rules. For a full-workflow test, allow each route its best method inside the same budget.

  3. 03

    Commit the selected design

    Each route submits its choice before independent reference checks. Include all data, training, search, retries, coordination, verification and fallback costs.

  4. 04

    Judge the actual decision

    Report verified feasible designs, objective quality, time/cost to a correct decision, false-feasible outcomes, missed feasible options and tail failures. Best known is not proof of a global optimum.

05 / Capital, time and miner emissions

Fund useful progress.
Not a permanent benchmark treadmill.

The network thesis is access to useful external research that a small internal team cannot supply as efficiently. More submissions are not proof. Better verified outcomes per dollar would be.

Company capital + engineering time

Build the exam and the customer wedge.

Reference adequacy, scientific review, adapters, cost studies, instruments and customer discovery are investments. Scope and approve each program; measure custom effort and reuse rather than opening eight simultaneous builds.

Miner research + network rewards

Earn a role in the research budget.

Miners need complete public research provisions. Independent reconstruction and protected evaluation test useful advances. This page proposes research priorities; it does not set emissions, activate paying challenges or promise equal rewards across eight programs.

Proposed investment checkpoints

Continue, narrow or pause on evidence. These are planning checkpoints, not new qualification rules or automatic spend approvals.

  1. 01 / Reference

    Can we measure the job?

    Verify outputs, convergence, controls and failure handling; price the research kit and reference volume.

  2. 02 / Decision value

    Can the method beat a strong alternative?

    Run the cost study and fresh decision test. Complete the required challenge-specific training-budget study before limits or rewards are set.

  3. 03 / Physical + customer evidence

    Does it help in the field?

    Run approved prospective measurements, establish buyer pain and procurement, and deliver a rights-cleared finite pilot.

  4. 04 / Repeat use

    Does the work compound?

    Measure repeat purchases, delivery effort, lifecycle needs and external-research contribution before scaling.

The Bittensor test

Compare network-led construction with competent centralized search under matched tasks, allowed data and full research budgets. Useful method diversity, faster verified improvement and lower discovery cost are the relevant signals. A centralized win is evidence we must act on, not hide.

Company revenue, miner rewards and Alpha value are different quantities. Carbon's planned buyback-and-burn approach for useful subnet research still requires operating terms and legal/economic review. It creates no revenue share, token-holder entitlement or guaranteed return.

06 / The capability we want to build

From learning a solver
to learning the physical system.

The ambitious end state: fast models that learn measured behavior, predict unseen interventions within a defined envelope and help choose the next useful experiment. Faster verified engineering decisions—not a universal replacement for physics or simulation.

01

Start with known physics

Qualified references and inexpensive models establish controls, causal inputs and a bounded job.

02

Learn what the instrument reveals

Use calibrated sensor records to estimate supported parameters or corrections. Sensor fit alone does not identify hidden physics.

03

Predict before observing

Commit forecasts before held-out experiments. Separate permitted online adaptation from a frozen forecast test.

04

Use, monitor, re-evidence

Abstain or fall back outside the supported envelope. Changes to model, hardware, data or conditions trigger new evidence.

First prospective pilot

A heater. A plate.
A prediction made in advance.

Burst-power thermal envelopes offer the first owned experimental path: known power input, measured temperature and an unseen power history. Price and approve the pilot, qualify the observations and compare against a calibrated RC model before expanding.

Harder to game, not declared ungameable.

Sensor time, calibration, location, uncertainty, actuator history and raw-data custody must be recorded. Hold out whole experiments, devices or geometry families—not random neighboring samples. Independent future tests, not a producer's dashboard, establish success.

Use cheap research screens to prioritize work, independent numerical checks where appropriate and selected prospective experiments. No requirement to run four complete high-cost panels for every candidate; no screen replaces the complete evidence required by a scientific claim. Private sensor streams need approved rights and security arrangements.
