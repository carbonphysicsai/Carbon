The Team

We Combine Aerospace Verification, Scientific ML and Commercial Execution.

Co-founder & CEO

Ryan Bequette

Technical direction and subnet design

Previously

“Over a decade in engineering showed me how many bottlenecks stand between brilliant minds and real-world progress. When AI models started learning the physical world, I got chills about what that could unlock. We built Carbon to keep engineers at the bleeding edge of Physics AI, with models they trust for their job, so they stay focused on better ideas, better designs and building better things. That’s how we create abundance.”

Ryan is a mechanical and aerospace engineer. He spent five years as a United States Air Force test engineer, then joined Virgin Galactic’s design and verification team on the Iron Bird, and most recently the test team for Boeing’s T-7A training simulator, turning requirements into verification plans. He has been part of the Bittensor network since 2022.
