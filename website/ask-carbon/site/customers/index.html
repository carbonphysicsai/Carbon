01 / What a Job Looks Like

Every Job Starts With Your Conditions.

Before any model is built or tested, we write down the decision it has to support, the range it has to cover, the reference it’s judged against and what a wrong answer costs. Every candidate is scored on exactly that.

Decision
The engineering call this model has to support, stated so a result can be checked against it.
Candidate
The model you already have, or every model the network builds for your problem.
Operating range
The conditions the answer must hold across: loads, geometries, inlet states, materials.
Reference
Your high-fidelity runs or measurements for that range, held back and used only to score.
The bar
What counts as passing, and what a wrong answer costs you if it gets through.

02 / Two Valuable Commodities

Bring a Model. Or Bring a Problem.

Evidence Audit

You have a model. You need to know where it holds.

Bring an existing physics AI model and the decision it should support. We agree feasibility, reference quality and scope before committing to evaluation, then test the model independently and report what survives, where it fails, and what stronger evidence would take.

  • Starts from your model and your reference
  • Fixed scope, agreed before any evaluation runs
  • Returns a decision package, not a score

Model Discovery

You have a problem. You need a better model for it.

Bring an engineering problem and defined success criteria. Carbon turns it into a competitive research challenge across its network, with independent evaluators testing every candidate against your reference and reporting whether the frontier moved.

  • Starts from your problem and success criteria
  • Research runs across Carbon’s network, funded by you
  • Every candidate independently evaluated

How we charge

  • Evidence Audits are fixed-scope and fixed-price, agreed at stage two.
  • Model Discovery is funded by the customer: you set the reward and the success criteria.

You define the engineering need. Payment does not determine the scientific result.

04 / What You Get

Evidence You Can Sign Off On.

The output of a Carbon job is a decision package: reproducible, scoped to your conditions, and explicit about where the model holds and where it stops.

Record
The model or method, versions, tested conditions and the reference it was judged against.
Results
Accuracy, the physics requirements that matter to your decision, and robustness under changed conditions, with uncertainty and known failures.
Scope
What the evidence supports, what remains open, and the next tests we would recommend.
Qualification
A separate, bounded conclusion, issued only where the evidence supports it.
