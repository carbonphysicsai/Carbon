## Required downstream contracts

NET-2 owns canonical domain-separated hotkey-authenticated messages, contextual
identity, bounded requests, durable replay protection and failure classification.
NET-3 binds canonical artifact digests, authenticated original receipt order,
availability, Challenge/ScorePack/evaluation pins and admitted requests through
existing stores/evaluator authority. A commitment is never accepted science.

C-REWARD owns the pure reward engine, accepted-event adapter, durable state,
closed batches and A6 public projection. Preserve canonical scientific score
bytes, registered comparator/uncertainty and geometric component composition.
For registered S0 < U, opening credit is zero and each strictly accepted record
gain contributes gain/(U-S0), decaying with a development 86,400-second half-life
from immutable finalized-chain activation. No clipping cap or opening gift.
Current holders receive current earned targets; self-improvement adds credit,
takeover transfers the current share, standing ties preserve incumbents, and
closed-batch ties use authenticated original receipt order. Copying or identity
changes cannot renew credit. Q12 floor rounding leaves all remainder unearned.
Challenge ledgers remain independent; shared winners aggregate before UID
mapping. No funding/score parameter is invented for a real Challenge.

DIRECT_WINNER_PLUS_BURN is the persistent default with treasury absent. Complete
vectors preserve all unearned allocation and unused capacity as explicit burn;
no earned-subset normalization, loser floor or cross-Challenge redistribution.
Verify actual runtime burn semantics and quantization capability before signing.
The optional reserve interface remains disabled and has a separate follow-up;
it cannot gate direct rewards. Future admission accounts for settled and all
pending/unrevealed liabilities with conservative bounds and verified receiver
health; unknown state burns new unearned allocation while preserving liabilities.

Weekly development reviews are anchored to opening and use the preceding
complete healthy 72 hours. Earned/allocated < 10% triggers PLATEAU_REVIEW;
missing, duplicate, unhealthy and zero-denominator evidence is indeterminate.
This never retires or reprices a Challenge. Publish coefficients before
competition and accepted combined/aggregate leg scores through A6's versioned
allow-list, aliases, cadence and budget; three-decimal display never compares.

NET-4A owns nominal stage-specific intents; NET-4B owns complete-vector compiler,
execution-time final integer checks before signing/encryption, durable intent
and dispatch reconciliation, inclusion/finality/reveal/row/settlement receipts,
and publication heartbeat/recovery. NET-5 exercises multi-Challenge/shared winner,
all-burn, UID recycling, replay/restarts, outages and adversarial reward scenarios
against a pinned disposable runtime using explicitly synthetic C0 science.
NET-6 owns reproducible configuration, secret inputs, process management,
health, recovery, backup/restore and the concrete C1/C2 operator handoff.

Finite credit and constant allocation imply lifetime target at most 1/ln(2)
days of full allocation. This is not guaranteed receipts or value, strategy
proofness, participation evidence, or a proven economic optimum. Preserve

reward policy. Bittensor owns network identity/metagraph, consensus and
settlement. Dependencies flow through Carbon-owned types into carbon.chain;
