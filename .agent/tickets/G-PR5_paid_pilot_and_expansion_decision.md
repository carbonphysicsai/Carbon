# G-PR5 — Paid pilot and evidence-based expansion decision

**Wave:** G commercial/private/sponsored plane
**Status:** `future_reserved`; unselected and unstarted
**Goal:** Run the exact preregistered pilot and decide stop, iterate, or expand from measured utility, demand, economics, operations, and safety.

**Prerequisites/owners:** G-PR0 through G-PR4 implemented and independently accepted; product, commercial, finance, scientific utility, security/privacy, support, free-path and owner decision authority.

**Scope and reuse:** Execute the frozen sample and baselines; reconcile query/quote/accepted/paid/repeat facts from their owners; measure matched-spend utility, external versus miner demand, contribution, support burden, leakage, reliability and free-path health. Record exclusions/attrition and confidence.

**Interfaces/failure/limits:** Do not redefine metrics after observation, equate interest with revenue, infer independence from accounts, or present a designed product as traction. Evidence Audit remains the established entry point unless separate commercial evidence changes that decision.

**Acceptance tests:** preregistration fidelity, source-ledger reconciliation, sampling/attrition, metric reproducibility, safety incidents, free-path comparison, contribution completeness, and explicit stop/indeterminate result.

**Definition of Done:** [ ] Owner decision cites the frozen scorecard and exact evidence. [ ] Expansion is bounded or stopped according to criteria. [ ] No scientific/product qualification transfers from commercial success.

**Handoff:** Any expansion or advanced Research Scientist capability requires its own future board/ticket; this ticket creates no open-ended mandate.
