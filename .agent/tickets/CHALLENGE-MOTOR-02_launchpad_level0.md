## Outcome

Make the existing electric-motor magnetics Challenge reachable as a bounded,
declarative Level-0 construction target from Carbon's Challenge registry and
Graphite admission study. The ticket adds:

1. a Challenge-specific construction contract over the existing public
   Gaussian kernel-ridge reconstruction;
2. strict compilation and reconstruction recipes bound to the pinned 150-case
   public TRAIN artifact;
3. a discovery document and Challenge registry entry;
4. a registered campaign/research composition and CPU worker declaration;
5. a Challenge-specific admission-study adapter with every construction
   dimension placed and one attack/control specimen for every Track A check;
6. focused regression tests proving cross-Challenge refusal and preserving the
   battery and cooling paths.

Shared code remains Challenge-parameterized. A Motor value belongs in the
Motor adapter, never as a new default in shared code.

## Scope

KEEP the existing two-dimensional periodic motor cross-section, eight-input
public DEVELOPMENT population, 60-angle torque-curve prediction contract,
public TRAIN/PRACTICE artifacts, registered gates and Gaussian kernel-ridge
baseline. WRAP them with the existing Challenge construction, research and
study seams.

The only rebuildable family in this ticket is the existing Gaussian
kernel-ridge model. Its `length` and `ridge` choices are the already published
calibration grid. The defaults are the already selected public calibration
result `(4.0, 1e-4)`. These are reconstruction settings, not new physical or
acceptance values.

Out of scope:

- changing or executing the frozen eight-design by six-condition decision

  or design-selection rule in OWNER-GRAPHITE-TEST-WAVE-01 section 6;

- constructed Track B controls or a fresh sealed confirmation set;

## Working decisions

- **MOTOR-L0-D1 — identity.** The construction token is the existing registry
  id `electric-motor-magnetics`, version `1.0`.
- **MOTOR-L0-D2 — bounded vocabulary.** `kernel_ridge`, `length` and `ridge`
  are the only rebuildable construction choices. Unsupported families and
  executable/pretrained submissions remain fail closed.
- **MOTOR-L0-D3 — public material.** Reconstruction reads only the
  digest-pinned public 150-record TRAIN artifact. Practice reads only the
  public 30-record PRACTICE artifact. The private 60-case pool, decision-study
  evidence and future confirmation material have no construction loader.
- **MOTOR-L0-D4 — worker.** Reconstruction/practice uses the existing pinned
  Linux CPU isolated carrier and exact NumPy environment. This ticket does not
  build or authorize a new image and offers no GPU lane.
- **MOTOR-L0-D5 — admission freeze.** The study adapter intentionally leaves
  the attack budget and evaluator-held fresh confirmation population as
  `HUMAN_INPUT`, so the sheet is not freezable and no Graphite attack wave is
  authorized by this engineering integration.
- **MOTOR-L0-D6 — downstream split.** Track B controls, fresh confirmation
  material, Motor validator/scoring and counted decision-reference work remain
  separate tickets and PRs.
