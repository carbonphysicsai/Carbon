# G-PR0 — Paid-pilot preregistration

**Wave:** G commercial/private/sponsored plane
**Status:** `future_reserved`; unselected and unstarted
**Goal:** Freeze the paid research pilot's baseline, sample/measurement plan, success and stop criteria, and utility/demand/economics/safety scorecard before workflow implementation.

**Prerequisites/owners:** Business Canon, Evidence Audit entry product, E-D12 opportunity evidence where available, product, finance, scientific evaluation, security/privacy, support and owner authority. Human inputs: thresholds, budget, target cohort, acceptable risk.

**Scope and reuse:** Define matched-total-spend utility, time/cost to useful decisions, quote/accept/repeat measures, miner versus external demand, support burden, free-path health, safety/leakage, and contribution including execution/reference/storage/inference/curation/payment/refund/support costs.

**Interfaces/failure/limits:** No unsourced market target, post-hoc success criterion, pricing, product activation, spending, or claim of traction. Self-reported outcomes are separate from Carbon measurements.

**Acceptance tests:** preregistration timestamp/identity, metric provenance, sampling/attrition handling, ledger source mapping, stop/indeterminate paths, and change-control before observations.

**Definition of Done:** [ ] Owners approve/freeze an evaluable scorecard. [ ] G-PR1..5 cannot weaken it after results. [ ] Paid workflow remains unavailable.

**Handoff:** G-PR1/2/3/4 instrument the frozen measures; G-PR5 executes the decision.
