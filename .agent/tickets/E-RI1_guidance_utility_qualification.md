# E-RI1 — Prospective guidance utility and predictive qualification

**Wave:** E Landscape and evidence memory
**Status:** `future_reserved`; unselected and unstarted
**Goal:** Test learned-release guidance and predictive tools prospectively before external activation.

**Prerequisites/owners:** E-EA5, candidate E-EB1/E-EA6 artifacts, E-EA7 correction readiness; scientific/statistical, product-utility, leakage/security, rights and release owners. Human inputs: prospective thresholds, held-out population, baselines, acceptable roles.

**Scope and reuse:** Freeze private release candidates; evaluate on future/held-out representative research tasks and evidence with lineage/dependence control. Measure grounding, abstention, counterevidence, matched-cost decision usefulness, latency/cost, calibration/transfer and leakage.

**Interfaces/failure/limits:** An unapproved public release is not required to gather approval evidence. Offline fit or retrospective pattern mining cannot grant external/predictive/scientific authority. No hidden future evidence, post-hoc threshold, or current Challenge mutation.

**Acceptance tests:** time-forward splits, frozen candidate identity, dependence/leakage audits, negative and abstention cases, correction invalidation, baseline comparison, cost/latency, and reproducible decision record.

**Definition of Done:** [ ] Human owners accept/reject a precisely scoped permitted role prospectively. [ ] Failed/indeterminate candidates remain inactive. [ ] Qualification does not transfer to other artifacts/models/contexts.

**Handoff:** Approved scope may feed Concierge/Ports; advanced Research Scientist functions require later explicit tickets and selection.
