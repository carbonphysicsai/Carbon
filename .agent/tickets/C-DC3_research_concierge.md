# C-DC3 — Launch Research Concierge

**Wave:** C authenticated launch communication
**Status:** `future_reserved`; unselected and unstarted
**Goal:** Implement useful approved-source research assistance with one replaceable reasoning component and truthful gaps/failures.

**Prerequisites/owners:** C-DC2; approved public contracts/catalogues, practice/scaffold resources, authorized PriorPacks, public-science curation, disclosure/security, product, and operational-triage owners.

**Scope and reuse:** Implement authenticate-to-demand-projection pipeline from the companion contract: classify, retrieve approved sources, reason, check claims/actions, recheck availability, persist lineage, deliver, capture gaps/feedback/subscriptions. Bootstrap sources avoid a circular Landscape dependency.

**Interfaces/failure/limits:** Responses label sourced evidence, user context, inference, uncertainty, unsupported scope, and unavailable service. Retrieval failure cannot claim no evidence. No canned unsupported answer after model/provider failure. No protected/private-source existence leak, official prediction, spending, official submission, or unrestricted tools.

**Acceptance tests:** held-out research tasks; grounding/claim support; abstention/gaps; counterevidence; stale/withdrawn sources; prompt/document/tool-output injection; budgets/timeouts/provider failure; lineage completeness; duplicate delivery; operational triage and kill switches.

**Definition of Done:** [ ] Focused deterministic and bounded integration tests pass for approved-source references, groundedness/abstention, truthful failures, suggestion/official-result separation, access/session isolation, resource limits, feedback, monitoring, and kill switches. [ ] Human replies obey identical boundaries. [ ] Empirical effectiveness remains `UNMEASURED`; no paid campaign, statistically significant lift, or separate research qualification is required. [ ] Learned EvidenceBriefs remain unavailable until E-EB1/E-EA6.

**Handoff:** C-DC4 evaluates external launch isolation. The network path may ship with Concierge disabled and enable it after these checks pass. E-D12 and optional separately budgeted E-RI1 work may later assess aggregate demand and guidance utility without becoming a retroactive launch gate.
