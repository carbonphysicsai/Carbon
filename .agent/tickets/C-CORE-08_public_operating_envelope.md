## C-CORE-08-D1: atomic sequence admission in the existing ledger

Problem: existing numerical reservations correctly cap each worker at 720,000
milliseconds and prohibit multiple active numerical operations. Two ordinary
sequential admissions could spend the first allowance before discovering the
second cannot be admitted. A parent charged in addition to children would also
double-count usage.

Working decision approved by the integration owner: add a narrow prospective
two-child sequence to the same SQLite CampaignLedger. One immediate transaction
binds a zero-resource parent and exactly two immutable ordered child identities,
request fingerprints and full reservations. Children begin `HELD`; existing
usage calculation counts their reservations immediately, preserving final-work
headroom. A held child is an allowance reservation, not a dispatched worker.
The parent has no independent execution allowance and its state is a projection
of child records. This adds no second ledger, scheduler or research loop.

Each child's reservation retains the existing per-worker ceiling: 720,000
numerical milliseconds, two trajectories/invocations and three OUTPUT_BYTES
retained-byte allowances. Aggregate admission checks the sum atomically against
the grant, campaign time and all resource caps before either worker is claimed.
The unchanged numerical lease permits only one actual controller invocation.

An atomic claim rechecks grant expiry, principal, generation and dispatch
controls, verifies the previous child's terminal state and observed cleanup,
then transitions only the next `HELD` child to existing `RESERVED`. Claiming
consumes no additional allowance. Normal `finish` reconciles dispatched children
only; a held child cannot be finished/refunded as dispatched. After expiry,
authenticated cleanup may release only proven never-claimed `HELD` children.
Unknown dispatched consumption retains its reservation until reconciliation.
Retries, reconnects and new output paths cannot reinsert children, reorder a
sequence, change its fingerprint or increase allowances.

Audit all status, recovery, usage, stop and completion consumers for `HELD`.
Neither an unknown dispatch nor a held second child may be reported as completed,
refunded or available for unbounded replay. Preserve partial completed artifacts
and child costs. A restart may inspect them; it cannot automatically rerun an
ambiguous worker. Cancellation stops future claims and requests observed cleanup
through the existing controller for dispatched work.

Rejected alternatives: independent child admission lacks aggregate reservation;
an extra parent charge double-counts; a separate sweep ledger/scheduler changes
authority; increasing the existing per-worker limit weakens the accepted profile.
Supersession is a prospective change to this ticket and the narrowly owned
ledger sequence API, leaving serialized historical grants/operations intact.
