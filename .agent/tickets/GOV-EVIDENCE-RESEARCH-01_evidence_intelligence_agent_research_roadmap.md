It does not select a Wave C/E/G runtime ticket, modify the active Wave B order, grant LIVE/security/scientific/commercial authority, or establish that any runtime exists.

## Goal

Ratify one implementation path that makes Carbon structurally ready to:

- retain high-value official evidence from real competition;
- open intelligent two-way miner/agent communication at launch;
- learn from research demand without confusing demand with science;
- route value through Landscape Ports A/B/C/D;
- monetize permitted research assistance and non-official evidence when commercially validated;
- mature toward a qualified Research Scientist without allowing an agent to become the scientific judge.

## Hard boundaries

1. The Research Concierge never receives protected evidence-vault credentials.
2. Dynamic request-time queries against private official evidence are forbidden.
3. Official evidence is retained even when Landscape cannot ingest or use it yet.
4. D12 demand is not scientific evidence and never enters `S_combined` or emissions.
5. Port B cannot vary candidate scientific evidence.
6. Port C proposes; authorized governance decides.
7. Port D still requires its own qualification contracts.
8. Payment cannot change the official exam, score, queue entitlement, or official-derived release class.
9. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is a valid agent response and must not be filled with fabricated scientific guidance.
10. Historical responses and evidence remain immutable; corrections append and propagate impact state.

## Commercial maturity discipline

The roadmap supports a potential recurring/high-value research product. It does not claim demand or revenue.

Before paid execution is built, freeze the pilot scorecard. Before expansion, measure repeat paid campaigns, external-customer demand separately from miner-funded demand, research utility at matched spend, direct contribution, support burden, leakage/security, and free-path health.

## Must not

Do not start C/E/G implementation from this ticket; change the current Wave B selected ticket; invent scientific thresholds, security acceptance, retention/legal terms, public/paid release policy, pricing, or customer reuse rights; imply that a question is scientific evidence; expose raw official evidence; or treat business design as paid traction.
