# E-EB1 — Approved EvidenceBrief pipeline

**Wave:** E Landscape and evidence memory
**Status:** `future_reserved`; unselected and unstarted
**Goal:** Create an offline, source-grounded, reviewable `EvidenceBrief` candidate/approval lifecycle.

**Prerequisites/owners:** E-EA4 and, where learned synthesis is used, E-EA5; evidence, scientific, disclosure, security, rights/IP, publication/PriorPack, and product owners. Human inputs: epistemic/release criteria and exact artifact approval.

**Scope and reuse:** Bind applicability, comparator/intervention, outcome/estimand, evidence origin, epistemic state, support, uncertainty, counterevidence, resources, transfer limits, falsification options, dependencies, audiences and release refs. Preserve contradictions and abstention.

**Interfaces/failure/limits:** Draft, review, approval, activation, withdrawal and supersession are separate. Offline private analysis is not approval; approval is not serving. No request-time vault access, raw export, scientific-status inflation, or private-source leak.

**Acceptance tests:** source/claim completeness, counterevidence, missingness, rights/permission, leakage/cumulative disclosure, version immutability, withdrawal, unavailable source, unsupported claim rejection, and synthetic examples not treated as measured Carbon results.

**Definition of Done:** [ ] Exact approved artifact is content/version bound with owner record. [ ] No serving occurs before E-EA6. [ ] Corrections are addressable by E-EA7.

**Handoff:** E-EA6 integrates release/serving; C-DC3 may then retrieve only activated versions.
