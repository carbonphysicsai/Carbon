# CHALLENGE-READINESS-RELAY-01 — relay three readiness records in one change

**Status:** in progress.
**Primary Hub map_ref:** `SYSTEM/AGENT-EXECUTION`, `HUB_UPDATE_REQUIRED`.
**Authority:** OWNER-CHALLENGE-DESIGN-01 (2026-10-01), OWNER-DX-03.
**Tracking:** carbonphysicsai/Carbon#342, #344, #345.

## Outcome

One change publishes three readiness records, each written from its own
ticket's evidence:
- `chip-cold-plate` v5, from CHALLENGE-COLD-PLATE-01;
- `electric-motor-magnetics` v3, from CHALLENGE-MOTOR-01;
- `photonic-coupler` v4, from CHALLENGE-PHOTONIC-01.

With them come the readiness schema's added units (`L/min/kW`, `deg`,
`A/mm^2`), the regenerated readiness table, the tests that recount each
pilot from its records, and the Workbench's family relay rebuilt by its own
tools.

## Decision RELAY-D1: batch the records, and reconcile Ask Carbon once

1. **Decision and ticket.** RELAY-D1, this ticket.
2. **The problem.**
   - The Pilot Designer relays each Challenge's latest readiness record
     (`Business/Carbon_Fit/workbench/tools/build_challenge_families.py`). Its
     tests require the relay to be current.
   - The owner-approved Ask Carbon release (WEB-QA-11-D2, bundle `86f51385`)
     pins the Pilot Designer's bytes in
     `website/ask-carbon/PUBLIC_RELEASE_CANDIDATE.json`. Its canonical guard
     fails when they differ.
   - So every record bump needs a new Ask Carbon candidate. The recorded
     precedent (CHALLENGE-ADMISSION-01) is never to edit the digest alone.
3. **The recommended approach.**
   - Each Challenge's PR lands its code, evidence and tests without the
     record bump.
   - This ticket carries all three bumps and one Workbench rebuild.
   - It merges after one Ask Carbon candidate reconciliation that certifies
     the rebuilt Pilot Designer.
4. **Where.**
   - This ticket.
   - The saved record v5 and schema change (taken from PR #489 at
     `cdb007f3e`).
   - The motor and photonic records, written from their pilots.
5. **Alternatives rejected.**
   - Reconcile Ask Carbon in each Challenge's PR: three candidates in a day,
     each superseding one the owner approved on 2026-10-01.
   - Edit the pinned digest alone: forbidden by the precedent. It would
     describe a bundle nobody built.
   - Leave the record bumps in the Challenges' PRs: each would fail canonical
     until a reconciliation, holding back code and evidence that do not
     depend on it.
6. **What it affects.**
   - Readiness records and their table.
   - The Workbench family relay, its previews, release zip and manifest.
   - Ask Carbon's release candidate.
   - #477 (the schema v3 migration) must migrate these versions when it is
     rebased.
7. **Reversibility.** Fully reversible. The records are additive versions, and
   the Workbench and Ask Carbon artifacts are regenerated by their own tools.
8. **To change it,** move the record bumps back into the Challenges' PRs.
9. **The smallest superseding change** is a decision that the Ask Carbon
   release stop pinning the Workbench's working copy. For example, the
   candidate could carry its own snapshot of the Pilot Designer. That
   decouples record bumps from releases.
10. **Human-reserved input.**
    - Publishing a new Ask Carbon candidate is the owner's act (WEB-QA
      decisions).
    - Re-deriving it is the Ask Carbon lane's release work.
    - Nothing else here is reserved.

## Definition of done

- The three records validate (`python -m carbon.challenge_readiness
  validate`). The table is regenerated, and each pilot's counts are recounted
  from its committed records by a test.
- The Workbench relay is rebuilt, its freshness gate passes, and its proposal
  and source tests pass.
- The Ask Carbon candidate is reconciled for the rebuilt Pilot Designer by
  that lane, and canonical passes.

## Out of scope

Publishing or deploying Ask Carbon, any record's approval, and any
scientific, security or launch acceptance.
