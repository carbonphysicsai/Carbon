### Proposed design and design-analysis results

The recommendation is five deterministic fixture-policy profiles by four
matched arms, with 264 complete blocks/profile and 26 pre-numbered reserve
blocks. This is 5,280 planned and at most 5,800 agent-arm runs, consuming
997,920 planned and at most 1,096,200 B-07E fixture units under the proposed
eight-attempt, current 189-unit per-run cap. That cap must be recomputed after
fixture extension. Profile-specific wall-time and normalized total-compute
caps await the exact drivers/meter and are determined
prospectively as `ceil(1.25 × arm-neutral dry-run p99)`.

Held-out quality uses current loss anchors 36.5 and 90.0. The smallest current
semantic step is `0.23534629881254276`; the proposed practical floor is half,
`0.11767314940627138`. Equal-profile v2 contrasts must separately clear every
no-prior, generic-prior, and v1 baseline under paired profile-stratified
simultaneous one-sided 95% bounds. Transfer is a required non-inferiority
support endpoint; candidate invalid/no-result is retained at zero quality;
typed infrastructure/reference failure replaces the complete block. Broad
profile regressions, missing blocks, or bounds that do not resolve remain
indeterminate.

Canonical intervention diversity ignores raw labels, counts a family at most
once per v2 run, collapses duplicate lineage globally, and rejects conflicting
lineage/bucket identities. A family needs at least 10% prevalence in at least
four profiles. Unsupported mass is conservatively assigned to the largest
supported family; inverse Simpson counts only when at least three families are
supported and maximum share is at most one half. The floor is `2.0`; the
current fixture scores zero by construction.

Conditional leakage is the signed cross-fitted normalized proper-log-loss gain
from adding transcript features to shadow-only physics controls across four
frozen protected targets. The proposed estimator freezes whole-transcript
folds and probability clipping and requires a positive shadow-only denominator
with a simultaneous lower bound above zero. Proposed limit is `0.05`: all
simultaneous one-sided 95% upper bounds at or below the limit is the pass
condition, any lower bound above it is the fail condition, and overlap is
indeterminate.
The raw leakage carrier now retains signed finite statistics so a negative
estimate is not silently truncated; its other information measures remain
non-negative. It is still caller-supplied engineering data, not executed or
qualifying evidence.
