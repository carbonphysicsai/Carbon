## Fourth structural slice

Slice 4 implements one closed, separately canonicalized campaign evidence
domain with exact `CampaignAcquisitionManifest`, externally supplied
`CampaignResultManifest`, and combined `CampaignEvidenceManifest` identities.
The required family matrices cover:

- manufactured-solution/refinement/observed-order studies;
- planted-defect/mutation campaigns;
- analytic or limiting-case anchors;
- primary/witness convergence and reference disagreement;
- generator-oracle adversarial campaigns;
- measurement-floor studies;
- decision-resolution evidence; and
- residual limitations.

Each family requires exact applicable Challenge, upstream subject,
measurement-definition, acquisition, provenance, result, failure/exclusion,
uncertainty, limitation, scope, version, and digest bindings. The
primary/witness family preserves distinct convergence and disagreement
evidence classes. Prospective specified, attempted, and partial acquisitions
remain representable without a result. Blocked, invalid, inapplicable,
deferred, generator-failure, and reference-failure outcomes remain exact
records but cannot be projected into dossier evidence.

The existing claim/evidence matrix is the only projection path. It permits
explicit one-to-many support where already authorized and rejects silent
cross-section or cross-campaign substitution. The focused synthetic D7 proof
retains primary reference-adequacy evidence while adding a separately mapped
reference-disagreement campaign digest; neither replaces the other.

Decision-resolution fields are structural only and remain
`OWNER_RATIFICATION_PENDING`. The implementation contains no acquisition
engine, generator/oracle execution, mutation runner, statistical calculation,
bootstrap, covariance or interval computation, power calculation, threshold,
winner selection, promotion, registry operation, or LIVE transition. All
outcome labels and result artifacts are externally supplied observations, not
