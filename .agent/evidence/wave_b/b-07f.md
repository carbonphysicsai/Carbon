## Implemented behavior

`ResolvedPlanFixtureTrainEvalService` is a separate, fixture-only provider
behind A7's exact `FixtureExecutionEnvelope` and A8's existing
`CompletedFixtureRun`. It does not alter the frozen A8 stub or the v1 package
surface. The provider recompiles the submitted Strategy through B-02B under
constructor-pinned Challenge, assembly, catalog, compiler, training-support,
and `R_strategy` identities; it delegates static admissibility to B-02C.

The registered `fixture_sampling_level` binding is consumed from the canonical
resolved policy. Its two allowed values select one or two fixed synthetic
training observations, measurably changing a deterministic through-origin toy
fit and its held-out fixture error. The only seed source is
`FixtureOfficialEntropy`; fixed B-03, B-04, and B-05 fixture refs bind the
generator, reference, and measurement meanings. Measurement inputs enter only
the unchanged A5 fixture ScorePack and `ScoreEngine`.

Private content-addressed reconstruction and result receipts bind the exact
attempt, Strategy hash, plan/policy/catalog/assembly/compiler/resource,
environment, generator/reference/measurement, ScorePack, and result
identities. They omit entropy and seed bytes, cases, paths, and raw
measurements. Compilation, construction, resource, reference, measurement,
and infrastructure failures remain distinct. All new values are immutable,
non-serializable, non-emission-capable, and permanently marked
`TEST_ONLY_FIXTURE_NOT_QUALIFIED`.
