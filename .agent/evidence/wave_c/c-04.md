## Implemented flow

The trusted controller builds one path-free, closed request from an exact
public Burgers case and fixed role. Its complete digest binds every material
case/query/method/environment setting. A fresh C-03-profile container executes
exactly one Cole–Hopf primary, conservative finite-volume witness or ETDRK4
development cross-check. It has no reference-selection callback, candidate
artifact, training state, truth store, scorer, archive credential or network.

The worker writes at most one bounded raw float64 array plus its closed
manifest to disposable scratch. The controller streams a provisional snapshot,
records cgroup and output observations, terminates the exact container and
descendants, closes intake, then invokes a separately limited validator. Only
exact, finite, identity-matching bytes become a DEVELOPMENT association. Exact
replay revalidates the retained snapshot and creates no second container effect.
