### Direction

Lead with a customer's engineering problem and the fast model it needs. Custom model discovery and design uses the existing Sponsored Discovery and Model Development product families. Feasibility and reference integration establish a supportable program; independent reconstruction and evaluation are integral to candidate development and comparison.

The intended customer handoff is the agreed candidate model or construction method, reproducibility material and evaluation findings, with limitations and rights stated. Scope and acceptance must address the possibility that no suitable candidate is found. Qualification for an exact use, integration and lifecycle work follow where justified.

This supersedes the audit-first entry sequence in the Business Canon, product architecture, GTM, business plan, investor narrative and financial planning examples. Evidence Audit remains a complementary option for an existing model; the instruction does not discontinue it or make it a prerequisite for discovery.

### Scope and boundaries

- Model design refers to the model's architecture, construction method and behavior for a defined problem. This is not an unrestricted physical-product design or engineering-certification promise.
- Preserve P1–P11 product identifiers, revenue identifiers, rights policies, scientific rules and network economics. The product list's order does not define the customer journey.
- The decision establishes emphasis, not customer traction, universal runtime support, private deployment readiness, prices, delivery dates or scientific success guarantees.
- Technical feasibility, scientific qualification and specific delivery commitments retain their existing owners. Customer payment never influences scoring or scientific conclusions.
- Historical studies and decisions retain their original meaning. This change reconciles current business documents and the repository introduction; it does not deploy website changes or alter runtime behavior.
