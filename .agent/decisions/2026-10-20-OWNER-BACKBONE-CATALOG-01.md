# OWNER-BACKBONE-CATALOG-01: every Carbon-tested backbone is selectable by catalog id

Date: 2026-10-20. Recorded on 2026-10-21; it was cited 16 times with no record file.
Status: APPROVED by the owner.
- **First:** relayed by the Test Lead on 2026-10-20.
- **Then:** confirmed directly in the Carbon Validator's session on 2026-10-21 ("Yes, record it").

## Owner, verbatim

- "I don't want to limit the backbone selection if it's been run and tested by us"
- "get that catalogue as wide as we can and then add them all to launchpad"

## Decided

- **Every backbone Carbon has run and tested is selectable by miners** by catalog id plus settings (the `catalog` source kind, option C), on each Challenge's next version.
- **New miner-authored architectures stay graph-only.** Miner bundles stay closed until they're tested.
- **An entry is offered only where it was tested** (`tested_on`, bound with its image digest and pair-identity receipt). See OWNER-CATALOG-VENDORED-LICENCES-01 for what may be published.
