## Decided

**TRAIN40-v2 is a new F02 evaluation version.** It has 40 cases in the same `c30` rectangular-pulse context and observer:
- **S1 near-limit:** 24 cases, U[70, 100] W, U[4, 6] s.
- **S2 context:** 16 cases, U[80, 150] W, U[5, 20] s.
- **The draw is seeded, and the freeze checks it stays disjoint from the eight evaluation actions.**

**Why:** the registered TRAIN40 peaks (95.6–142.1 °C) all sit above the limit, while the exam's decision region (5 s, 80–87 W) crosses it near 85.7 W.

## Conditions

- **MMS first:** the solving build passes its own MMS before solving (RR-PACKS-05).
- **Both sides populated:** if under a third of S1 lands on either side of 95 °C, S1 is re-drawn before acceptance.
- **Convergence:** spot-checked on 4 S1 cases.
- **`train_peak_std_c`:** recomputed from TRAIN40-v2 with ddof 0. It tightens A in the decision region by design.

**History:** TRAIN40 and its results stay as recorded and aren't re-scored. Estimated cost is about 4.3 CPU-h.

## Revision: the scale (owner, 2026-10-19, in the Carbon Validator's session)

- **The finding:** RR-08 measured `train_peak_std_c` (ddof 0) at 16.704 °C on all 40 TRAIN40-v2 rows. That loosens A by about 18% against v1's 13.633, the opposite of the "tightens A" effect stated above. The 16 S2 context rows, which reach 151 °C, widen it.
- **The options put to the owner:**
  - all 40 rows, 16.704 °C;
  - the 24 S1 near-limit rows only, 6.701 °C (about 2× tighter A);
  - with the Validator recommending the S1-only figure.
- **The owner, verbatim:** "your recommendation".
- **Decided:** `train_peak_std_c` for TRAIN40-v2 = **6.701 °C**, computed with ddof 0 over the 24 S1 rows of TRAIN40-v2 (sha b6875185…).
- **What it supersedes:** the "recomputed from TRAIN40-v2" rule above, which is replaced prospectively for this version. v1 results keep 13.633 and aren't re-scored.
