## 2026-10-10 — OWNER-BENCHMARK-CONTAMINATION-01: adopt the benchmark contamination policy

**Authority.** The owner's answer, as relayed. The question was put to the owner

> Adopt the benchmark policy? Published benchmarks validate our solver and never become hidden scored questions, and close copies (re-meshed, resampled, rescaled) are excluded too. I recommend yes.

The owner's answer:

> Yes

The owner confirmed it again on 2026-10-10 to the coordinating session, which relayed
the confirmation. This record is written by the Graphite Testing Manager at the
coordinator's request; the answer is the owner's as relayed, not a separate
statement made in this session. The proposal is
`Business/research/benchmark-contamination-policy/` (BENCHMARK-CONTAMINATION-POLICY-01,
PR #1022): its `POLICY.md`, `SOURCES.md` and `policy.json`.

**Adopted.**
1. **Anchor-only rule.** A published benchmark is a reference-route anchor: public
   reference verification and validation, reproducibility, and a separate diagnostic
   control. It is never a scored hidden question, an EVAL or STRESS action, or a hidden
   bank item.
2. **Equivalent-copy exclusion.** Close copies are excluded with the anchor:
   re-meshed, resampled, rescaled and physically equivalent transformations.
3. **Family novelty metrics are the method.** Novelty is a physics and decision
   contract, measured with the policy's per-family metrics.
4. **Published answers, correlations and cached maps are legitimate competitors.**
   Published formulas, similarity laws, cached simulations, response surfaces and
   direct solver-based optimizers are admitted competitors where the output and
   acceptance contract allow.
5. **An anomaly triggers investigation, not a cheating verdict.** An
   anchor-versus-variant performance anomaly is an audit finding with human
   disposition.

**Still HUMAN_INPUT.** Not decided by this record, and nothing may fill them in:
- novelty acceptance thresholds;
- buyer limits;
- P, Q and w for each benchmark Challenge.

The policy text also leaves HUMAN_INPUT, per family, the novelty weights, normalizers,
the exact equivalence group and the "far enough" acceptance predicate.

**Not decided here.** Whether any benchmark Challenge is admitted, any spend, any
scientific qualification, and any public claim about benchmark agreement. The policy's
claim discipline stands: numerical code agreement is not hardware validation. The
policy's `status` stays PROPOSED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED_NOT_RUN: adopted as the method, not
yet implemented or run.
