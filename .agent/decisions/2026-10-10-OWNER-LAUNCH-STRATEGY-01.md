**Decided.**

1. **Launch Challenges are internal rehearsals of the commercial pipeline.**
   - Each launch Challenge runs end to end inside Carbon: brief → packet →
     reference → panel → question law → bank → readiness → Graphite stages →
     tested.
   - Each uses open solvers and published, rights-clear data. A launch
     Challenge never depends on a customer pilot or on customer data.
   - Credibility and value claims are sized to that. A representative
     reference case is claimed as representative, not as validated for any
     buyer.
   - Each Challenge records the brief-to-product ledger (ONBOARDING-PIPELINE-01,
     #970, #975): its inputs and their sources, the time and cost of each
     stage, its outputs, and the network's behaviour.
   - Across the eight, the goal is a customer intake specification (what a
     customer must supply) and a quote model (predicted cost, time and
     quality for a new brief).
2. **"Tested" requires the cheap-baseline comparison.** A Challenge is not
   *tested* until its models' decisions are compared, at matched
   admissibility, with the strongest cheap method a buyer would otherwise use
   (value criterion V4). This is added to the working definition of "tested
   Challenge" in #970.
3. **Speed: no fixed targets.** Each onboarding is executed as efficiently as
   possible, and its time and cost per stage are recorded in the ledger.
4. **IP direction: submitted work, commercially usable, miner-friendly.**
   Carbon's commercial rights attach to what miners **submit**, not to local
   work Carbon cannot see. The working direction for legal review is:
   - Carbon owns the model it trains from a submission (Carbon's data, seed
     and compute);
   - on submission, the miner grants Carbon a perpetual, irrevocable,
     worldwide licence to the submitted recipe, with the right to sublicense
     to customers;
   - the licence is non-exclusive, so miners keep their own know-how;
   - Graphite and internal Level 5 work belongs to Carbon;
   - recipes stay confidential, and only a hash goes on chain.

   The exact licence form (licence or assignment, and enforceability) is HUMAN_INPUT
   pending legal review. The constraints are that Carbon must keep commercial
   ability, and that the terms must not scare off strong miners.
5. **Revenue supports Alpha.** Carbon's miner workforce is paid in Alpha.
   - Carbon intends to use a share of company revenue to support Alpha, as the
     mechanism that sustains that workforce. Buyback and burn is the current
     direction (founder direction, 23 September 2026).
   - The share, timing and mechanism are owner and board decisions, set after
     legal review. The amount is not yet known. This creates no revenue-share
     or token-holder entitlement.
   - Customer payments never influence scoring, ranking or qualification.
   - Agents do not implement or change a revenue-to-Alpha mechanism without an
     owner decision.
   - Public wording of this intent is reviewed by counsel before publication
     ("approve with that context for now").
