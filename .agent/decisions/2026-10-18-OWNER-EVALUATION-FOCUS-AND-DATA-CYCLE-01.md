## 1. Main evaluation focus

Carbon's evaluation should reward models that support better engineering decisions and accurately predict the physics that makes those decisions reliable. Evaluation should also retain the evidence needed to tell a customer where an exact model is useful, unreliable, unsupported or in need of solver verification.

The agreed prospective development structure is:

```text
mandatory gates first
eligible score = 0.50 * Physics + 0.50 * Design
Design = min(verified achieved-value credit, decision-critical prediction credit)
```

Physics measures broad accuracy, robustness and supported generalization. Design measures continuous verified buyer-unit regret plus the correctness of signed limit margins, supported finite-difference responses, learned objective gaps and other Challenge-relevant difficult predictions. Include unselected alternatives. A correct action ID alone cannot earn full Design credit. Analytically known input-derived quantities do not earn learned-prediction credit.

Both score halves remain continuous; native mandatory failures remain noncompensable. Reference uncertainty/equivalence and incomplete evidence remain explicit. A partial point-estimate diagnostic is not a complete exam or product qualification. Challenge-specific outputs, limits, normalization, reference resolution, coverage and sample sufficiency remain versioned inputs under their existing authority. This owner direction fixes the main development focus, not every candidate numerical scale or an already-deployed production formula.

Immediate design scope: Motor's existing 8p/24s study, Cooling's existing periodic-cell study, the f02 cold eight-action pilot, and the f08 eight-geometry summary pilot. Do not relabel these as revised Motor 10p/12s, revised Cooling, f02 menu-v2 or battery-v3 evidence. Apply the process to later Challenge onboarding through each Challenge's own brief and physical/reference scope.

## 2. Evaluation and product learning form one evidence cycle

```text
registered buyer job and evaluation contract
    -> qualified, protected evaluation under a frozen batch policy
    -> model outcomes + regional errors + decision margins + workflow costs
    -> common coverage / failure / reference-resolution gaps
    -> prospective, bounded next-data plan
    -> accepted new reference tranche and future sealed batch
    -> exposure-limited use and retirement
    -> eligible public training release
    -> better construction methods and new independent evaluation
```

Retain the same source-owned underlying facts for scoring and later intelligence: full joint context, exact model/runtime and reference identities, outputs and predictions, native-unit errors and signed margins, local pairs, decision gaps, adverse outcomes, applicability, missing evidence, reference resolution, dependence/role/exposure, and measured complete workflow cost. Preserve failures; separate infrastructure/reference failures from candidate physics failures. Reuse existing evidence and storage rather than create a second fact store.

Use the existing Evidence Intelligence/Research Concierge, Product Spec v2 Trust Map/Envelope Guard/Decision Assistant, Landscape and product-qualification designs. Internal records and approved product guidance have different disclosure/authority. A score or a map does not itself issue TRUST or a qualification claim.

## 3. How evidence may guide future acquisition and rotation

Aggregate, eligible historical evidence may identify useful next regions: poorly covered buyer conditions, missing accessible limit sides, design comparisons, local responses, supported-domain edges, recurring adverse decisions and insufficient reference resolution. Prefer data that improves a named customer-use statement and the exam's ability to distinguish useful physical models. Record why each acquisition is needed and which existing data it reuses.

Before future scored use, freeze and version the source-owned acquisition/SamplingPlan, case law, acceptance and use contract. State the proposed region or stratum, reason, output/measurement, reference route, independent unit, count/precision target, cost cap, dependence, exposure/retirement and effective future window. Product operates bounded jobs under the actual resource and grant controls; a priority signal alone cannot commission work or spend.

Preserve these fairness controls:

1. Keep representative buyer-distribution evidence and deliberate difficult/stress evidence separate. Preserve P, proposal Q and score weights w. Filling stress gaps must not silently redefine the buyer population or upweight a refined region by raw node count.
2. Evidence may guide the NEXT prospectively registered policy/tranche; it cannot mutate an active committed batch, change scoring after outcomes, target a named miner or select per-submission questions. Within a comparison, candidates receive the same registered evidence and query/compute rules.
3. A material change to population, envelope, score, observable or weighting is a new prospective version with the required scientific acceptance and comparability treatment. Merely rotating new cases from an unchanged registered law does not redefine the task. Historical scores remain attached to their original identity; raw scores from different rulers are not silently comparable.
4. Keep a representative component and common comparison design under the applicable registered policy. New refinements, repeated conditions or shared-bank questions are not new independent evidence merely because their IDs differ.
5. A map's diagnostic hole proposes future work; it never directly selects an active official case or grants a model scientific success. Public/product guidance requires independent validation where a predictive/use claim is made; calibration evidence cannot validate its own resulting guidance.

**Authority seam:** earlier product-map language forbidding maps from selecting official scored cases remains in force for direct/runtime manipulation. This owner decision permits aggregate evidence to inform a separately registered future acquisition/SamplingPlan. It grants no runtime map-to-exam mutation, automatic P/Q/w change or hidden-vault access. Domain owners must reference this decision when prospectively reconciling that wording.

## 4. Retirement becomes public learning, with custody intact

Continue the existing bank lifecycle; do not invent a second publication service:

- Count every use against the registered case/bank/question exposure limit. Different requirements asked of the same underlying solved bank do not reset exposure. Source/image/refinement variants and renamed/reordered questions do not create freshness when the published answers remain inferable.
- Retire on the registered schedule/exposure or earlier when disclosure compromises protection. Never use a published case again as hidden evidence. Do not backfill a short bank with overexposed cases.
- Retired means removed from future hidden draws, not immediately safe to publish. Release only after every active window and unresolved comparison that legitimately depends on the material has closed under its governing lifecycle and the required reveal/journal conditions are met.
- Check dependency disclosure: publishing one action/reference must not reveal the answers to a still-protected design question or another active bank. Hold the affected shared material until those dependencies are safely retired/closed. Do not invent an all-Challenges release barrier for unrelated cases.
- Publish eligible retired bank cases automatically through the existing signed per-Challenge public training-pool route, with source/reference/contract/version lineage and verifiable integrity. Public release remains allow-listed and rights-eligible. Never publish protected future seed/root material, private metadata or material outside that release authority.
- Reference failures/unresolved labels do not become trusted training truth. Preserve their status and keep them out of an accepted-label projection until resolved; scientifically meaningful candidate failures can still be retained as evidence.
- Publish the same versioned release to all eligible miners; no privileged early feed. A growing public pool produces new immutable manifests/releases. Each construction/training/evaluation job remains pinned to its declared TRAIN/pool version and effective period; do not mutate an in-flight fixed TRAIN dataset.
- Tuning, calibration, confirmation, sealed holdout and customer/private evidence have separate custody and are not auto-published merely because a bank case retires. Training-pool release does not authorize hidden-holdout revelation or customer-data reuse.
