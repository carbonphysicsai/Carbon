## 2026-10-15 — F13-SCIENCE-VALUES-01: f13 silencer reference-path and bank values (DEVELOPMENT)

**Authority.** OWNER-SOLVER-READINESS-SCIENCE-DELEGATION-01 (2026-10-15). These
are DEVELOPMENT values for the f13 Elmer Helmholtz reference path and its
public development panel. They grant no qualification, Tier 2/3 claim, LIVE
status, reward or spend. Hidden and sealed material is untouched; the bank
freeze path below takes its seed from the operator and none is recorded here.
The owner may change any value by a later record. Values are prospective:
the v1 triage (image 8bcd864d, 10 mm stubs), the v1 feature proof and the
v2 power probe keep their original records.

**Buyer and what flips the decision** (packet `round1/f13-compressor-silencer.md`
§1, §3, §5, §6; `reference-routes.md` f13). A compressor-skid acoustic engineer
picks a two-chamber passive silencer inside a diameter/length package that
maximises the interval-weighted band p10 of transmission loss over 500–2500 Hz,
with p10 ≥ 5 dB as the buyer target. The p10 is a lower quantile: it is set by
the low-TL intervals (pass bands, where transmitted power is 30–100 % of
incident), not by the transmission-zero peaks. The decision flips when a
design's p10 is mis-stated across the floor, when a low-TL interval is missed
by the frequency grid, or when a chamber mode moves enough to reorder two
contenders. The value-check spread recommendation for f13 is 0.5 dB of p10
(`question-laws/value-check-v1.md`), so the numerical bands below keep every
reference error at or under half of that (0.25 dB on p10/mean).

Sections A–E were set **before** the local-image (`61be1cb7`) runs they judge;
the commit order on this branch is the record. Where an older-image result
already existed, the table says so. No value was loosened after a result.
