## 2026-10-08 — BATTERY-IMPL-3-01: battery implementation 3.0, every PyTorch family follows its device

**Authority.**
- **The ruling.** The Test Lead's priority assignment (2026-10-08) under the
  owner's backend-parity rule: PyTorch GPU must work the way JAX GPU does. Fix
  it in the shared torch device path, not as an FNO-only patch.
- **The precedent.** TORCH-GPU-01 (the owner's option A): a change to the
  battery implementation modules is a new implementation version. Earlier
  versions stay read-only snapshots and keep verifying from main
  (invariant 10).

**The failure.** worker-images-v2's torch-gpu image rebuilt `fno_defaults`
on an RTX 3060 and failed both repeats after about 640 s:
`RuntimeError: Expected all tensors to be on the same device`, at
neuraloperator's `embeddings.py:163`. The MLP rebuilt bit-identically, 4 of 4.
- **The cause.** `fit` trains on the rebuild device (CUDA), and `predict`
  runs on the CPU. neuraloperator's `GridEmbedding` caches its grid as a plain
  attribute, built on the first call's device and reused afterwards.
  `Module.to`, which the FNO's `apply` called, moves parameters and buffers
  only, so the cached CUDA grid met CPU features.
- **The reproduction.** On a CPU-only host: call the 2.0 FNO on the CPU, then
  on the `meta` device. The result: "Tensor on device cpu is not on the
  expected device meta!"

**Decision.**
- **Implementation 3.0.** `CURRENT` is 3.0. The 2.0 bytes are a read-only
  snapshot (`implementation_snapshots/v2`, its manifest and digest pinned in
  code).
- **What changed: `torch_training` and `torch_families` only.**
  - `Network` takes the library `modules` its `apply` calls.
  - Before every call (training and prediction alike), `Network` places those
    modules on the inputs' device with `follow_device`. That moves parameters,
    buffers, and the tensors a library caches as plain attributes.
  - The FNO passes its module and drops its own `module.to`.
- **CPU numerics are unchanged.** On one device, `follow_device` is the
  identity. A CPU rebuild is byte-identical under 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0, and the
  torch test in CI holds this.
- **Prediction stays on the CPU,** as 2.0 designed it.
- **Development variants are untouched.** They pin implementation 1.0.

**Tests.**
- `tests/cpu/test_battery_torch_device_follow.py`, for every family (mlp,
  deeponet, fno):
  - each family is called on the CPU, then on a second device (CUDA, else
    `meta`). Every tensor its modules hold, and its output, must follow.
  - with CUDA, a CPU call follows and must equal the first CPU output exactly.
  - the FNO's cached grid is shown to exist, and `Module.to` alone is shown to
    leave it behind.
  - every registered family is covered.
- `test_battery_implementation_versions.py`:
  - the 2.0 snapshot is its pinned bytes;
  - 3.0 changed only the PyTorch modules;
  - the CPU rebuild is byte-identical under every version.
- `test_battery_level1.py`: `LEVEL0_PINS_V3`.

**Runbook.** `docs/development/images/RELEASED_WORKER_IMAGES.md` records the
executor's two notes for running a GPU image by hand:
- WSL2's driver mount and library path;
- a writable `/scratch/tmp`.

**Not claimed.**
- **The GPU path is not yet verified here.** GPU verification of the FNO
  round trip rides on worker-images-v3's GPU run.
- **Ranking is unchanged.** No score or ranking changes, and CPU and CUDA
  rebuilds remain separate evidence.
