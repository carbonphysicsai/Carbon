# Benchmark Challenge decisions — Test Lead under owner delegation (2026-10-11)

**Authority.** The owner, verbatim, in the Test Lead session: "You make optimal decisions on those 17 questions focused on carbon value prop."

**Scope.** This answers the 17 items in each of #1053's owner-decision inventories: backward-facing step (BFS) and full 3D metagrating (MG). Every decision is `DEVELOPMENT`.
- Qualification, LIVE, mainnet and spend above the bank rule stay the owner's.
- No value here claims physical validity.
- Where a number can only come from measurement, the decision is the **rule that sets it**. The value stays closed until that rule has been applied and recorded.

**Value-prop lens.** Every choice serves the claim we must be able to prove: a Carbon model beats the strongest cheap shortcut, and beats solver-alone search at equal budget, on a real buyer decision, with the solver as referee. Choices that would make that claim easy rather than true were rejected.

## Decisions (both families unless noted)

1. **Buyer decision and limits.**
   - **BFS:** choose one recovery contour that minimises duty-weighted mean total-pressure loss over a service brief of operating points. It must satisfy the separation limits (reattachment length at or below the requirement; no reverse flow at the exit plane) at **every** point.
   - **MG:** choose one device that maximises the **worst-case** desired-order efficiency across the operating brief (wavelengths, incidences, polarisations). Unwanted-order and reflection limits must hold at every condition.
   - Requirement levels are drawn per question from P (decision 4), not fixed at one number.
   - Value, volume and latency are reported as sourced or labelled ASSUMPTION ranges, with at least 2 sources (VALUE-BAR-V1).
2. **Geometry and action grammar.**
   - **BFS:** the monotone recovery-contour spline family that research proposed.
   - **MG:** binary x/y masks on a fixed physical period, with finite thickness and the testbed's minimum-feature rule.
   - For both, approve the proposed DOFs and precision. Envelope endpoints are DEVELOPMENT assumptions, registered before any truth.
   - No silent narrowing: no stripe-only MG and no 2D reframe.
3. **Conditions and material support.**
   - **BFS:** anchor at the NASA case's stated conditions and conventions (Re_H basis, Mach, developed inlet profile). The service brief spans the research-proposed Re_H and Mach ranges, as DEVELOPMENT.
   - **MG:** the source's ideal, nondispersive, lossless silicon is the declared support. Each action's period stays fixed across its scenarios. No broadband hardware claim.
4. **P (population).** Launch is internal-only (OWNER-LAUNCH-STRATEGY-01), so P is a **representative** joint distribution, uniform over the accepted envelope and labelled REPRESENTATIVE.
   - Requirement levels are drawn so that every stratum shows a 20–80% pass rate, a margin spread, at least one feasible design, and changing answers (the owner's value-check rule).
   - All catalogue anchors and their equivalents are excluded.
   - The independent question unit is one complete service brief.
5. **Q (sampling).** Registered before any truth: 40% nominal, 20% corners, 30% frontier, 10% transition. For MG, half of the transition share goes to cases where the cheap library is expected to fail.
   - Rejections are tracked.
   - Settled NONE_FEASIBLE cases are never redrawn.
   - Weights never repair absent coverage.
6. **w (weights).** Score weight equals buyer frequency under P. Diagnostic sampling weights are kept separate and never enter the score.
   - The objective is BFS duty-weighted mean (energy cost accumulates) and MG worst-case (spec compliance).
   - A failed mandatory limit at any condition is never compensated.
7. **Measurement and objective.** Adopt #1053's recommendations as written.
   - **BFS:** mass-flow-weighted total-pressure loss in Pa, with dynamic-pressure normalisation. A shifted Cp is never a substitute.
   - **MG:** flux-normalised order efficiencies, covering all propagating orders including cross-polarisation, with an energy residual check and percentage-point units.
   - Tie-equivalence is half the minimum useful improvement.
8. **Reference package.** Approve the proposed pins: SU2 with the NASA SSTm option mapping; Meep, with S4 as the independent cross-tool. Each is used only after a complete dependency, image, deck and observer pin **and** passing code verification (MMS, or analytic where MMS is impractical), per CODE-VERIFICATION-MMS-01.
9. **Reference acceptance.** These are rules, not guessed numbers:
   - **Code:** the observed order of accuracy is inside the registered band.
   - **Solution:** the decision-relevant observable changes by less than **half the minimum useful improvement** between the last two refinement rungs, and the picked design and verdict are unchanged.
   - **Cross-tool:** the picks agree and the regret difference is below the tie-equivalence.
   - **Validation, Tier 2:** BFS agrees with NASA reattachment within the published experimental uncertainty, for that observable only. MG agrees with the testbed's numerical results, which is not a claim about reality.
   - Witnesses never use hidden or tuning cases.
10. **Novelty catalogue.** Freeze the full audited catalogue before any outcomes: every reconstructable public anchor, tutorial and derivative, with rights. No cherry-picking.
11. **Novelty equivalence and normalisers.** Adopt #1053's descriptors and symmetry groups. Remeshing, resampling and valid rescaling are never new physics. A mirror that swaps the desired orders is not automatically equivalent.
12. **Novelty acceptance.** It is decided by decisions, not distance alone (#1022). A variant counts as novel only if geometry or mask novelty is present (mandatory) **and**, on the public development set, the anchor's published answer or cached map no longer makes the same admissible decision. Changing only Re, thickness or angle never rescues a copy. Component thresholds are set at the smallest distance where that decision test passes, and recorded.
13. **Frontier, power and ties.**
    - 5 feasible and 5 infeasible designs within two accepted refinement bands.
    - The band width equals the minimum useful improvement.
    - Add frontier actions; never widen bands.
    - Power and false-alarm rates follow #1045's design.
14. **Question and bank counts.** E = 1, matching battery testnet. k, m and n come from #1045-style power analysis on the public development set, and B comes from the per-Challenge cost cadence rule. They stay closed until those measurements are recorded.
15. **Panel registration and reuse.** Adopt as written. Every solve is audited, and an unknown index counts as unknown, not zero.
16. **Construction, TRAIN and rights.** Only rights-clear sources are used. TRAIN is generated publicly from the same envelope and kept disjoint from the panel. A kit domain check must show 100% panel coverage before evidence (the lesson from battery v3 and motor). There is no arbitrary evaluator execution.
17. **Execution and adoption.**
    - The 7-item readiness check must be all YES before evidence spend.
    - First, a capped measurement pilot of at most **€10 each**, so the actual costs replace the ASSUMPTION ranges.
    - Then decide under the owner's bank rule (team cost-benefit, €500 maximum).
    - Admission happens only if the value bar (VALUE-BAR-V1) passes against the strongest cheap baseline and at equal budget. Admission is never automatic.
    - **Priority:** after battery v3, motor and f02. These two add the CFD and optics regimes for the investor evidence.
