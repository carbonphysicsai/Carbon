## The plan (mainnet)

1. **Practice first, no wallet.** Install, practise and improve with no wallet; the wallet comes up only when submitting.
2. **One-click wallet.** A Setup button opens the existing `carbon-miner-identity` helper on its own loopback page; passwords never pass through the Control Center. Plain-language recovery-phrase guidance; signer started after one password entry; Setup confirms registration from the chain.
3. **Registration made visible.** Live registration cost shown in USD before commit; a short guide to acquiring TAO with links out (Carbon never handles miner money); a plain payout guide.
4. **Carbon-sponsored registration**, gated on earned practice results (for example beating the starting model). This reverses the built-off state from the 10-21 onboarding decision only once its values are set.

Stage 5 (a partner or custodial route) is NOT adopted; it needs a separate decision and legal advice.

## Bounds

- Planning only: no work is assigned by this record (owner's instruction).
- Open owner values: the sponsored-registration on/off switch, budget and eligibility rule; which exchanges to link, if any.
- Sets no economics, threshold or score. Keys and passwords stay on the miner's machine, typed only into the helper's or signer's own prompt.
