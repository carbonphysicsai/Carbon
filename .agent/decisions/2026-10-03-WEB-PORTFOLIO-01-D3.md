page implementation plus a solver-side visual now, with a future leading-model

before each conditional scale illustration. No model latency or achieved savings
is promised. Shared arithmetic explains energy cost, usable capacity, pass rate,
charging dwell, unit manufacturing cost and fixed-volume processing time. Motor

construction, truth/evidence, integration, serving and upkeep; operational claims
need their own validation. Customer targets are not clients. No canon, economic
allocation, qualification threshold, real-cell safety or scientific scope changes.

2D heat equation with manufactured analytical control and refinement/residual
checks. No official/private material or physics-library dependency is accessed.

optimizer run, physical experiment, matched-resource contest or qualified result.
The future model panel is empty. No score, timer, winner or fictitious model field.

same design bounds, physical constraints and declared compute/wall-clock budgets;
independently checked final designs; elapsed search time and total costs including
preparation/build. Exact scientific contracts remain domain-owned, not selected by
this webpage. Models shipping does not itself qualify them for operational use.

as product impact; fabricated side-by-side results; pretending an illustrative
heat field is an OpenFOAM/cold-plate optimization; a new runtime/backend or live
experiment. Static inline SVG preserves local/offline preview and existing site.
