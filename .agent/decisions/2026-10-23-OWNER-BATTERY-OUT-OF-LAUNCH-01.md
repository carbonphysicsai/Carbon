Amends: OWNER-LAUNCH-PORTFOLIO-02 (launch scope) and OWNER-BATTERY-LAUNCH-VERSION-01. The Battery version decision stands for any later return.

## Decided

- Battery is not a launch Challenge. Stop all Battery work: sealed batches, its receiver, catalog v2 builds and its recipe v2. Recorded results stay as recorded; nothing is deleted or rescored.
- Launch readiness (Phase Z) now counts 4 Challenges: ≥2 QA-passed sealed exams each, end to end on both routes, and the testnet cycle.
- Battery may return after launch, once practice shows a graded result clearly above Carbon's baseline. Returning needs a new owner decision.

## Why

- The owner judged that Battery had not yet proved useful. It was the most expensive Challenge (memory, a full redesign, about 10-hour batches, and a receiver that did not fit the C-03 defaults), with no graded result.
