# MOTOR-VAL-D4 — Register Motor candidate-fault classification

**Ticket:** CHALLENGE-MOTOR-07. **Status:** implemented working engineering
decision for public DEVELOPMENT evaluation; not science qualification.

**Problem:** An exception during a candidate-triggered Motor Level-0 rebuild or
prediction, or a non-finite score, otherwise reaches dispatch's generic
`adapter_failure` path with no registered policy identity. The selective-crash
Track A test needs an inspectable, versioned interpretation of retry/refund.

**Recommendation and location:** Register `motor-candidate-fault-v1.json`
through the digest-pinned Graphite attribution registry. The Motor adapter
loads it at construction and supplies it on typed `CandidateFault` exceptions.
The existing validator returns non-scientific `FAILED_INFRA/adapter_failure`;
it performs no retry, charge or refund. The registered submission lifecycle
and fee service retain those responsibilities, subject to their actual attempt
budget. A later change requires a new registered policy version.

**Alternatives rejected:** An implicit code-only classification cannot be
attacked or versioned. Marking a numerical crash as a scientific candidate
failure would invent an unapproved gate. Automatic retry/refund in this
adapter would usurp the registered lifecycle.

**Affected boundaries:** `carbon/challenge_validator/motor.py`,
`carbon/challenge_validator/candidate_fault.py`, and the registered policy
JSON/registry; infrastructure/science separation, no hidden data disclosure,
and historical policy identity. Cooling's policy and Battery's live contract
are unchanged. VALIDATOR-19 and any future protected Score Pack remain separate.

**Reversibility:** Prospective registry version migration, not an edit to v1 or
a rescore of historical attempts. If the lead disagrees, add a new policy
document and registry current pointer after a new owner-approved decision;
the smallest superseding change is that registered version plus affected
adapter tests. No human-reserved scientific input is needed for the current
public DEVELOPMENT classification. A production attribution/security decision
remains unmade.
