# BOLTED-JOINT-REHEARSAL-BUILD-01 — representative, not customer substitution

WRAP retained pins and neutral analysis with a representative preparation
module. Do not use TRC's differently scoped vibration assembly, noncommercial
tightening datasets or a copied contributed deck with unresolved rights.
Original formulas/draft text and explicit synthetic geometry avoid those assets.

customer law. NIST generic elastic constants do not supply yielding or bolt
grade allowables. Open limits remain None. The code cannot issue an official
result, dispatch, grant or customer acceptance. A VDI-style stiffness calculation
is a real cheap baseline but does not qualify nonlinear opening/slip; preserve
contact-aware cached FE as the strongest incumbent still needing measurement.

carbon/design_search/bolted_budget.py proposal and bolted-rehearsal/ docs.

its identity, tying faying surfaces to make a solve easy, force-maintaining a
locked bolt in service, or filling missing safety limits. Reversible additive
route; supersede those files prospectively if the owner changes the rehearsal.

owns adequacy and question limits; business/customer owns replacement rights
and measured value. No domain authority or implementation maturity is inferred
