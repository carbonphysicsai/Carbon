# OWNER-STANDING-GPU-TEST-RENTALS-01: standing approval for short GPU test rentals

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: APPROVED by the owner, directly in the Lead session (local_b7032708-92d1-4a7c-a1d3-9ad008ba9cae). Asked to give a standing OK for "test GPU rentals up to $5 each, deleted right after, inside the weekly test budget", the owner replied: "yes".

## Decided

- Test Lead may rent a GPU machine for a test without asking each time, when:
  - the expected cost of that rental is at most **$5**, priced before renting;
  - it is used only for practice or test runs on public data, never for sealed exams and never as the validator;
  - it is deleted as soon as its results are fetched and verified;
  - the week's total stays inside the standing weekly test grant.
- Anything above $5, any monthly or dedicated host (for example the validator's 4090), and anything beyond the weekly grant still needs the owner's own figure-specific approval.
- Each rental is logged with its provider, price per hour, hours and total.
