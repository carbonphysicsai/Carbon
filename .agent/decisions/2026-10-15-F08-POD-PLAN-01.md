# 2026-10-15 — F08-POD-PLAN-01: f08 triage and TRAIN-bank pod plan

**Decision.** `docs/development/evidence/reference-solvers-2026-10-15/plans/f08-triage-bank.json`
runs the 17-launch f08 triage (primaries, witnesses, controls, cold repeat)
and then the 1× TRAIN rung (256 public geometries) on one runpod-cpu5c-16vcpu
pod. Engineering choices:

1. A bulk step may name an `observe` command (`pod_phase.py`), run on the pod
   over the complete bulk output before the work dir is deleted, so verdicts
   do not depend on the 2 MB `keep` limit (calculix-smoke-3 lost the beam
   control's 2.19 MB case.dat). `f08_freeze.py --observe` now reads the
   bulk/pod layout and takes native costs from the bulk ledger.
2. Bank launches use `f08_deck.BANK_COMMAND`: the same solve, then the
   ~150 MB `case.eig` is removed (the observer reads case.dat only); 256
   launches must fit the 20 GB pod disk.
3. The bank step's freeze first runs `f08_bank.py gate`: it launches only if
   the triage observation picked V9 = h 3. Otherwise nothing runs and the
   TRAIN manifest is refrozen at the picked mesh.
4. TRAIN public seed 20261015 (public role; nested prefixes of one 2,048 draw).
   `structures-readiness/registration.json` is committed so freezes reproduce.

**Alternatives rejected.** Raising `keep` limits (puts MB-scale solver output
into git); a separate observe script step (the bulk work dir is already
deleted); freezing all 2,048 TRAIN decks locally (≈ 2 h CPU and 8 GB disk).

**Reversible.** Plan and command are versioned files; nothing launched.
