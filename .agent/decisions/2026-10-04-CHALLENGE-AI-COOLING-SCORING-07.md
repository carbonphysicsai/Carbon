# CHALLENGE-AI-COOLING-SCORING-07 — public-practice scoring decisions

**Date:** 2026-10-04

**Authority:** delegated engineering choices within
OWNER-GRAPHITE-TEST-WAVE-01 section 3 and the already registered Cooling
DEVELOPMENT construction/practice contracts. No scientific, security, spend,
qualification, customer, production or LIVE authority is exercised here.

## Decision

Register the existing cold-plate public-practice computation as Cooling's
`ChallengeScoring`. Reuse its exact pinned public material, deterministic
kernel-ridge compiler, isolated CPU practice program, prediction gates and
TRAIN-normalized aggregate.

For Graphite's baseline display, calculate only the paired mean difference over
common scorable public cases. Report it as descriptive, report no confidence
interval, and always set `promotable` false. Missing comparability or candidate
ineligibility remains explicit. A future scientific owner may register a
promotion/comparison rule under a new decision; this engineering ticket does
not anticipate its threshold or sampling design.

## Rationale

The Challenge already has a fixed public practice rule suitable for adaptive
DEVELOPMENT feedback. Reusing it answers whether a constructed model is
rebuildable, physically well formed on the public cases and lower-error than
the scaffold on those same cases. The shared cases are dependent and
adaptively visible, so they cannot support population reliability or a new
confidence interval. A non-promoting descriptive comparison preserves that
distinction while making the pod path operational.

## Consequences

- Cooling constructions can be built on a pod and scored on Carbon's host.
- Battery and Cooling must be selected by name; no shared default chooses one.
- Protected/counting/confirmation evidence remains absent from construction.
- The result is public adaptive DEVELOPMENT evidence only.
- Cooling's general validator, PB-ADV, Track B and confirmation work remain
  separate tickets.
