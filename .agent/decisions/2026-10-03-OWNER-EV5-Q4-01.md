**Authority.** The owner, 2026-10-03, on #536 Q4: "yes on Q2 and Q4 if

1. **Kept.** A percentile interval at 95 %, B = 10000 and seed 20261001, as
   in EV4's paired comparison. Any fixed seed is equally valid; what matters is
   fixing it before any solve.
2. **Added: stratified resampling.** H2 compares two groups: the real members
   the gate FAILs and those it PASSes. Resampling all members together lets
   the group sizes vary, and with few failures it can draw no FAIL member at
   all (EV2: 2 of 14 fail, so about 1 replicate in 9). Those replicates would
   be skipped, which biases the interval. Each
   replicate therefore resamples the FAIL and PASS members separately, each to
   its own size, and the conditions jointly. If either group is empty, the
   difference is undefined and reported, never zero.

   (`group_difference_bootstrap`), `ev5.H2_BOOTSTRAP` in the freeze manifest,
   and `BATTERY_ENGINEERING_VALUE_EV5.md` §4.
