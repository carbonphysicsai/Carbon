## Decision and reason

Treat the **buyer job** as the outer unit for a future design-task study. The current condition panels mostly describe one fixed job. Battery temperatures and cycles, Motor angles/commands, Cooling heat maps, f02 package scenarios, f06 wavelengths/offsets, f08 damping/frequencies, f13 frequency nodes and f17 flow/diffusivity points are within-job or shared-platform observations. Changing only a seed does not create a new buyer need. f02 may make a separate schedule choice for each scenario, but those choices stay clustered within the same package job.

Each packet §3 now sketches buyer-recognizable job variation, how a buyer would identify a distinct brief, and a small recommended *requirements inventory* size. The proposed axes may exceed the current reference or action support; they are not silently admitted. `P_job`, protected `Q_job`, job-level weights, eligible support, exclusions, clustering, reference adequacy, hidden batch size and power remain `HUMAN_INPUT`. The Test Lead must derive a powered sample from behavior-defined good/bad controls and measured cost after the authoring/reference owners approve supported jobs. One approved job may contain a full condition panel; mandatory failures cannot be averaged away.

## Practical boundary

The six/eight/four-brief packet recommendations are scoping inventories, not test counts or funded solver campaigns. Existing optimizer pilot counts are conditional and require that many eligible jobs; f02's former 24-condition coverage pass is explicitly not a 24-job pilot. The five new Challenges' solver cost remains unmeasured. f13's proposed 16-design full-curve bank implies at least 3,216 separate-frequency launches for one job before refinement, so a multi-job bank needs a new measured route and grant. Current `carbon/design_search/tasks.py` is absent on the inspected main; when it lands, reconcile immutable outer job identity and inner condition panel with its actual fields before code integration. Adjacent design-space and reference-credibility work may change packet support and must be reconciled prospectively.

No scientific qualification, production population, customer acceptance, security acceptance, LIVE state or paid demand follows. KEEP the existing packet condition panels and optimizer commitments; WRAP them later in an approved, versioned outer job/task contract. Do not reinterpret prior studies or scores as job-level evidence.
