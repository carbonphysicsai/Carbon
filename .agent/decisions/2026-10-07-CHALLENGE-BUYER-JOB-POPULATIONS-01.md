**Date:** 2026-10-07. **Origin:** owner's follow-up on draft PR #759. **Status:** DEVELOPMENT buyer-requirements proposal, `SPECIFIED` only. **Scope:** eight round-one customer packets and their linked Carbon-owned optimizer proposals. ## Decision and reason

Treat the **fresh buyer design question** as the outer draw for a future design-task study. One Challenge quiz still runs per batch. A question may vary supported service conditions or buyer requirements while retaining the same physical cell, joint, module or solved design bank. Battery temperatures/cycles, Motor angle curves, Cooling mesh/heat samples, f06 wavelength points, f08 frequencies, f13 frequency nodes and f17 outlet/flow probes within an unchanged question are correlated measurements. Changing only a seed does not create a new buyer need. f02 may choose a schedule separately for each scenario, but related choices remain clustered by physical package/bank.

Each packet §3 now sketches buyer-recognizable condition and requirement variation and a small recommended question inventory. Requirement variation can change the best admissible design at no new solver cost when a bank already stores all needed quantitative outputs; answer diversity must be verified. Physical-context variation is optional and may need new reference work. `P_job`, protected `Q_job`, weights, eligible support, exclusions, clustering, exposure, reference adequacy, hidden batch size and power remain `HUMAN_INPUT`. The Test Lead must derive a powered sample from behavior-defined controls after the authoring/reference owners approve supported questions. Mandatory failures cannot be averaged away.

## Practical boundary

The four/eight/twelve-question recommendations are scoping inventories, not test counts or funded solver campaigns. Existing optimizer pilots are conditional and need that many eligible, unexposed questions; f02's 24-condition coverage pass is not a 24-independent-question power pilot. Shared-bank questions must retain bank clustering and consume underlying case exposure `E` under [OWNER-BANK-ARCHITECTURE-01 / #756](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/pull/756). At `E`, cases retire into the release queue; publication remains a separate release decision, and retired/published cases never return to hidden use. If a batch lacks enough unexposed questions for Test Lead power, its design leg is diagnostic. The five new Challenges' solver cost remains unmeasured. f13's proposed 16-design full-curve bank implies at least 3,216 separate-frequency launches once before refinement; re-asking supported questions saves solves but cannot reset exposure. `carbon/design_search/tasks.py` is proposed in [#764](https://github.com/carbonphysicsai/Carbon/pull/764) but absent on inspected main; reconcile its actual identity fields before code integration. Adjacent design-space and reference-credibility work may change packet support and must be reconciled prospectively.

No scientific qualification, production population, customer acceptance, security acceptance, LIVE state or paid demand follows.
