# OWNER-KOTH-MARGIN-AND-PRIVACY-01: one fixed margin per Challenge, measured at kings' level; recipes stay private

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: APPROVED by the owner. Confirmed in the Test Lead's session and relayed to the Carbon Validator, who records it.

## Owner, verbatim

> We have to keep scoring simple. Protect what's valuable and encourage discovery. Need to find the right balance. Tempted to protect and reward the models designs that really get adopted so miners want to submit.

## Decided

1. **One fixed absolute rebuild-noise margin per Challenge.** There's no re-measurement as a king's score moves.
   - The margin's measurement panel includes Carbon's best agent-tested recipe (OWNER-LEG-MINIMUM-BEST-AGENT-01), so it's measured near the score level kings will hold.
   - The published record states the panel's recipes and its score level.
   - This applies the frozen KOTH rule (KOTH-TAKEOVER-RULE-01) unchanged.
2. **Submitted constructions and the king's recipe stay private to Carbon.** No miner surface discloses them. Miners see only the Ladder document (rounded totals, legs and registry gate names) and the public practice and kit material.

## Not decided here

**Rewarding the model designs that get adopted** is a future business direction, not something to build now. It belongs under `Business/`.
