## 2026-10-10 — SCORE-PROOF-01: a one-command, off-chain proof of battery's scoring rules at every construction level

**Authority.**
- **The direction.** The owner, relayed by the Test Lead: "do all score
  tuning off chain asap … at every level … we have to get this scoring right
  and prove it".
- **The scope.** The development scoring work runs under
  OWNER-DEV-AUTONOMY-01. Adopting a rule and choosing its thresholds stay the
  owner's (OWNER-BATTERY-SCORE-RULE-01 is the rule in force). No spend, chain
  write, training or new solve.

**Decision.**
- **Built on what is on main, not rebuilt.**
  - The panel is `tuning_rescore`'s: stored predictions, the sealed
    references through `challenge_validator.tuning`, development decision
    values, and the registry with its commit check. It is extracted as
    `tuning_rescore.assemble`, so the rescore and the proof share one panel.
  - The rescore's output is unchanged.
- **`carbon.battery.value.score_proof`** reports, for each registered
  candidate in registry v5, at each level and pooled:
  - **τ** with a recipe-cluster bootstrap 95% interval, and the paired Δτ
    against the rule in force (`G-FEAS/A-Q>@0.05`);
  - **known-bad and adversarial members in the top half;**
  - **top-1 regret;**
  - **gate recall on the unsafe members**, with their ranks;
  - **fold stability:** τ over 5 folds of the decision scenarios and 5 folds
    of the scoring cases;
  - **adversarial divergence:** an adversarial member scoring at or above a
    member of better value, beyond the value noise band.

  The targets are recorded (no known-bad member in the top half, gate recall
  1.0, no divergence). The cutoffs stay HUMAN_INPUT.
- **The τ.** A vectorized τ-b, tested equal to `score_value.kendall_tau_b`,
  makes the bootstrap affordable: about 6 minutes for 142 members and every
  registry v5 candidate.
- **`proof-members-v1.json`** assigns each member a level by registered name
  rules. The controls are level-agnostic anchors that join every pool. The
  roles are:
  - **known-bad:** the boundary-optimist and localized-sign-error controls;
  - **benign:** the oracle and the rank-preserving delay, whose ranks are
    reported and should stay high;
  - **adversarial:** EV5's 10 members;
  - **unsafe:** run 5's member.

  It lists what each level is missing and the cheapest fill. Nothing is
  spent here.
- **`SCORE_PROOF_OWNER_SHEET.md`** is the owner's one command on the AX42 and
  what to send back (`proof.md`, aggregates only).

**Evidence (plumbing only, not proof).** `--public-standin` ran on the PC:
142 members, all Level 0, in about 6 minutes, aggregates only.
- **The rule in force is withheld.** The stand-in has no per-member Q3
  regret, so its `q` leg is empty. Every A-Q rule is withheld as
  `INCOMPLETE_LEGS`, and so is the paired Δτ against the rule in force. A
  first run showed why the guard is needed: with the leg missing, only
  gate-failed members carried a (floor) score, which gave a meaningless
  τ = 1.0.
- **Complete rules report normally.** For example, CE: τ 0.144
  [0.015, 0.281], 11 known-bad or adversarial members in the top half,
  regret 0.
- **For the sealed run to check:** on the public set, G-FEAS@0.05 does not
  gate run 5's unsafe member (recall 0.0).
- **The real run** passes `--q3-regret` (the owner sheet).

**Missing members (owner decision; not spent).**
- **Level 0:** stage A's 5 bundles (stage A has not run; #991), and
  rehearsal miners H and I (free CPU rebuilds on the AX42).
- **Levels 1 to 3:** no member with tuning-set predictions and decision
  values. The fill is free CPU rebuilds of the dev ladder's submissions,
  operator-side.
- **Level 4:** blocked until the security owner accepts G5.

**Amendment (2026-10-11): selection apart from proof, the registered Level 0 members, one sitting.**
- **Selection is kept apart from proof** (Test Lead). `proof-split-v1.json`,
  registered before any run, hashes the scoring cases, and each level's
  recipes where a level holds at least 20, into a selection fold and a
  confirmation fold. Anchors (controls and the registered roles) are in
  both.
  - `--phase selection` compares every rule on the selection fold, and the
    owner picks there.
  - `--phase confirmation --rule ID` locks the candidates to the pick and
    the rule in force. It gives PROVEN or UNPROVEN against the registered
    criteria, naming each failure.
- **The gate check is pre-registered.** A gate that misses an unsafe member
  is FAIL and names the cutoff range that would catch it, never picked.
- **The decision tooling is ported to main.** `tuning_dev_panel` and the
  `ev4-dev-tuning-v1` contract from #671, which merged into a branch that
  never reached main.
- **The Level 0 members are registered before any value:**
  - `panels/proof-panel-l0-v1.json` holds stage A's 5 delivered bundles,
    keyed by (lane, run), with the strategy and seed from each run's
    `recipe.json`;
  - minerH's and minerI's 6 submissions, with strategies copied by
    Launchpad and Carbon's registered rebuild seed 0 (a miner supplies no
    seed);
  - the `ev4-dev-proof-v1` contract is ev4-dev-tuning-v1 with that panel.
- **The decision values are computed off the sealed set.** They come from
  public EV4 references on the PC (`tuning_dev_panel --contract
  ev4-dev-proof-v1`).
- **The owner's sitting.** `score_proof_run` prints its CPU estimate, rebuilds
  the registered members on free host CPU against the sealed set and the
  quiz, rescores, and runs selection. Confirmation is one more command
  after the pick (`SCORE_PROOF_OWNER_SHEET.md`).
- **Levels 1 to 3** come in the level-aware ticket, which is required.
  **Level 4** waits for G5.

**Amendment 2 (2026-10-11): SCORE-PROOF-DESIGN-01's statistics (#1045).**
- **The primary pool.** Tau, its intervals, the folds and top-1 regret use
  the real recipes only. The purposive probes (the controls, the known-bad
  members and the adversarial constructions) are placed in the full ranking
  and analysed apart, never resampled as recipes.
- **Crossed bootstrap.** Recipe clusters and decision questions are
  resampled independently and jointly, with the paired delta on the same
  draws. Recipe-only and question-only intervals are reported as
  sensitivity. The scoring cases stay fixed, so the intervals are
  conditional on the scoring set. Undefined replicates are counted, never
  set to 0.
- **Tie accounting and no forced deployment.** Tie accounting is reported
  (score, value and double ties). When every real recipe fails a gate,
  deployment is `NONE_ELIGIBLE`, never the floor's arbitrary top member.
- **The design's later items stay open:** a public planning audit, interval
  coverage checks, and multiplicity control if all rules are confirmed. The
  confirmation phase already follows its recommended route: one frozen
  pick against the rule in force.

**Finding (stand-in plumbing; not proof).** The development decision mask
holds 7 questions. On the stand-in selection fold (74 members, 59 of them
real recipes, 809 scoring cases), CE's joint interval is
[-0.116, 0.428], recipe-only [-0.030, 0.365] and question-only
[-0.027, 0.334]. At this question count no rule can be proven to the
owner's requested precision. SCORE-PROOF-DESIGN-01's base planning is 628
independent questions. More resolved decision questions is the binding
input, and it is the owner's to fund.

**Amendment 3 (2026-10-11): the owner's precision target, a group-sequential confirmation on sealed questions.**
- **The owner's decision.** Relayed by the Test Lead, verbatim: "Go with
  ±0.15 and the early check at 150". It is registered prospectively in
  `proof-sequential-v1.json`:
  - the target is a pooled τ interval half-width of 0.15 at about 280
    questions;
  - there is an interim look at 150 questions;
  - two-sided α is 0.05, spent by Lan-DeMets O'Brien–Fleming. At 150/280
    that is z 2.678, nominal α 0.0074; the final is z 1.985, nominal α
    0.0471. The interim uses its actual resolved fraction.
- **Stopping.** The run stops on a clear PROVEN (every screen passes and the
  interval lies above 0) or a clear FAIL (a screen fails, or the interval
  lies below 0). A non-binding futility stop is offered at the interim.
  Otherwise the run continues to about 280 (CONTINUE), and at the final it
  is UNPROVEN.
- **Per-level screens, all FAIL-able** (the Test Lead): known-bad and
  adversarial placement, gate recall, a deployable real recipe with top-1
  regret within the value noise band, and adversarial divergence. They are
  checked pooled and at each level with at least 10 members. Per-level τ is
  descriptive, and a level that fails a screen gets its own powered proof.
- **Registry v5 is authoritative:** 130 expanded rules, which is registry
  v4's 129 (as #1045 counts) plus the rule in force. Confirmation tests one
  frozen pick against the rule in force.
- **Sealed questions** (`score_proof_questions`). The AX42 draws its own
  seed and the conditions. These are disjoint from EV4's public conditions
  and the quiz's Q3. It writes the sealed contract (EV4's decision contract)
  and the 35 × N reference jobs. Then:
  - the startup-host shards solve them;
  - a REF-RESOLVE-01 refine round follows;
  - the AX42 rebuilds every member and evaluates.

  Only the contract digest is public.
- **The first 150 questions** are solved on one hourly CCX63 under
  OWNER-SCORE-PROOF-CCX63-GRANT-01 (EUR 5, 3.6 server-hours; the owner
  confirmed directly in the Test Engineer's session) and
  OWNER-STARTUP-HOST-CUSTODY-02 (`SCORE_PROOF_CCX63_SHEET.md`).
- **Fixes.**
  - `Experiment.import_predictions` now accepts a bundle made under any
    registered implementation version, as `evaluate` already did. The EV4
    bundles were made under 1.0.
  - The proof panel moved out of `contracts/`, because a loader reads every
    file there as a contract. That was the cause of CI shards 4 and 5.
