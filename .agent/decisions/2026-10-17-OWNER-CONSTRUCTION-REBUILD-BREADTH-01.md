# 2026-10-17 — OWNER-CONSTRUCTION-REBUILD-BREADTH-01

**Authority.** The owner clarified the intended construction boundary in the
Launchpad session: S1–S5 were meant to let Carbon independently rebuild any
bounded construction design within the agreed contract, including designs
drawn from open-source backbones/capabilities and new pure mathematical graph
operations. A finite list of library names or levels is not the policy for what
miners may discover. The owner also required a clean rebuild before evaluation,
no submitted trained weights or fitted state, and no participant executable
code in the validator's grading/evaluator runtime.

**TRAIN and practice.** Every independent reconstruction of a candidate for a
given Challenge uses that Challenge's same frozen TRAIN material within its
registered resource envelope. Miners may practice with their own data or data
generated with the provided solver. Practice data and fitted state are not
substitutes for the Challenge-owned reconstruction input.

**Correction to the 2026-10-13 plan.**
`OWNER-CONSTRUCTION-ANYTHING-01` and the S1–S5 plan framed the target as a
finite set of then-tested L0–L4 Battery fields and their combinations. That set
was coverage at the time,
not the final breadth of the owner's intended construction contract. A derived
level describes a design; it does not grant or deny admission. Serving the
combined digest in S5 does not by itself admit or rebuild a new backbone or
operation outside the finite registered vocabulary.

**Implementation rule.** A submitted design must identify its complete
reproducible construction procedure, source/graph materials, and resource use.
Carbon may admit a design only when trusted reconstruction implementations can
realize all of its operations within the fixed bounds and produce the full
numeric state, including buffers. Participant-authored construction material,
where supported, remains in a separate bounded lower-trust construction
domain. The grading/evaluator runtime receives only checked inert graph/state
and runs source-free inference and the actual Challenge scoring contract.
Unsupported operations, missing trusted lowering, provenance mismatch, or
unpriceable resource use receive typed refusals naming the specific missing
capability or bound; a library or level label alone is not a refusal reason.

**Maturity and acceptance.** This is a prospective scope correction, not a
claim that every open-source capability is implemented, secure or qualified.
The existing Level-4 operation allowlist, registered reconstructions, resource
checks, isolation, hidden-data boundary, pinned historical contracts, and
human science/security authority stay in force until versioned replacements
are implemented and tested. A full model-case PASS requires normal Launchpad
submission, fresh independent reconstruction on fixed TRAIN, source-free
frozen inference, actual Challenge gates/score, and model/result lineage in
the existing ScoreLab bank. Source tests, a staged recipe, or an S1–S5 slice
alone do not earn that PASS.

No production Challenge threshold, security acceptance, score, deployment,
settlement or LIVE status is decided by this record.
