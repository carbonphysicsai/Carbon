# EQUAL-BUDGET-REGISTRATION-01-D1 — sourced anchors, assumed buyer budgets

**Status:** proposed working DEVELOPMENT registration. **Authority:** Test
Lead's value-bar delegation relayed 2026-10-10. The numeric budget is a
prospective comparison input, not a measured industry spend or an adopted
commercial/scientific threshold.

Research's [#1014 specification](../../Business/research/equal-budget-realism/SPECIFICATION.md)
says exact Carbon-job budgets remain HUMAN_INPUT. It supplies method classes,
generic 0.5/2/8-hour illustrative tiers, historical battery/f02 costs and an
unrelated 100-point motor example. The Challenge cheap-baseline/value-cost
files supply f13's original-route full-curve cost context. None measures the
buyer's exact workflow budget. We therefore register labelled assumptions:

| Challenge | Complete-panel evaluations half/base/double | Wall hours | CPU core-hours | Reason for base |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| battery-v3 | 12/24/48 | 4/8/16 | 12/24/48 | Five-band, 30-cycle map makes the older 91 CPU-second single-action context a lower planning anchor; 24 maps leave room for multi-start and final checks. |
| motor 10p/12s | 6/12/24 | 12/24/48 | 48/96/192 | Whole command/angle/skew panels are unmeasured; 12 panels are a constrained first design search, with a wide sensitivity range. |
| f02 nine-action | 5/9/18 | 1/2/4 | 1/2/4 | A competent direct route enumerates all nine discrete actions; 2–3 CPU-minute historical waveform costs leave setup/refinement allowance. The double tier can saturate the finite menu. |
| f13 original offset | 4/8/16 | 8/16/32 | 16/32/64 | Original complete curves are owner-reported above 2 CPU-hours; eight curves fit a plausible multi-core review window, with full 16-member bank only at the double tier. |

The half evaluation count for odd nine uses ceiling, since 4.5 complete
evaluations are impossible. The current f13 coaxial reframe remains conditional;
these numbers do not adopt it. No other Challenge budget or source measurement
is inferred. Future measured cost may show any tier cannot finish; that is a
result, not permission to move the cap after seeing it.

**Engineering choice:** one digest-bound registration supplies a typed shared
resource cap. Existing fixed-order replay must reject a different ladder and
charge every arm against the same cap. Future adaptive/ordinary-surrogate
policies consume the same cap object; they cannot silently pick a larger
allowance. VALUE-BAR-V1 gates on the base tier and reports half/double outcomes.
Reversible by a prospective versioned rule; past reports retain their original
registration digest.

**Alternative rejected:** selecting a budget where Carbon's observed curve
crosses zero, or transplanting Attia's 224-protocol space / TEAM 25's 100
points / #1014's generic 0.5/2/8 hours as measured Carbon buyer budgets.
That would replace evidence with a favourable post-hoc policy.
