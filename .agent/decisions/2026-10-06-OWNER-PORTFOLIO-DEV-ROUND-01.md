## Working interpretation and choices

This prospectively fills the first-round DEVELOPMENT requirements left open
by OWNER-LAUNCH-PORTFOLIO-02 §3 for **f02, f06, f08, f13 and f17 only**.
Codex selects customer-shaped synthetic populations, material fixtures,
decision limits, numerical verification acceptance criteria and bounded
feasibility allowances in [the round-one package](../../docs/development/challenge_pipeline/round1/README.md).
Core limits and grant ceilings are in `round1/requirements.json`; packets
complete the geometry, observation and sampling conventions. They are approved working
choices under this delegation, not five actual customer specifications.

The recommendation is **KEEP** this first pass, then revise prospectively
where public DEVELOPMENT evidence exposes a problem. No repeated approval
prompt is needed for an in-envelope, reversible first-round engineering
choice. Record each material revision and notify the responsible lead.

**Reference adequacy is an outcome, not an approval flag.** The owner authorizes
selection of the checks and their numerical limits; no solver has passed those
checks for these new tasks. Images, complete case/deck manifests, extraction,
resource enforcement and evidence custody must be implemented and pinned
before the feasibility allowance can be dispatched. All five reference
adequacy states remain NOT_DEMONSTRATED. Failure or exhausted budget yields
unresolved evidence, not a looser criterion or inferred success.

non-transferable allowances**, 46 hours of allocation on one 16-vCPU CPU node
at a time (736 allocated vCPU-hours); per-task caps are in the numeric sheet.

baselines. No GPU grant, automatic retries, counted model-comparison campaign, fresh
protected confirmation, rig purchase, network write or reward is covered.
All attempts, setup, refinement, normalization and failed work consume the
allowance. Retain artifacts and the ledger; uncertain attempts remain charged.
Do not dispatch without a price quote and enforced limits that fit every cap.
If the required pinned profile cannot fit, stop that execution sub-scope and
present the specific alternative; do not silently buy a larger instance.

caps are ceilings, not a claim that this profile supplies the requested RAM.
In particular f06's 256-GiB ceiling requires a capacity/price check before
dispatch; a different CPU profile would be a recorded prospective engineering
choice under this round, never comparable protocol timing evidence by default.

## Boundaries retained

- The customer personas are role-play, not paid traction, customer acceptance,
  real-device safety limits, process yield or manufacturing qualification.
- The population is a synthetic DEVELOPMENT experiment law, not a deployment
  population. Feasibility sampling is not an independent confirmation set.
- Numerical checks do not establish physical truth. Scientific qualification,
  security acceptance, rights, process lock, deployment and LIVE remain with
  the applicable owners. No actual reference adequacy is asserted by decree.
- The Battery-led protocol remains DEFINING. This decision authorizes parallel
  design preparation; it does not move a queued family, activate runtime IDs,
  lock the protocol or duplicate the validator/scoring pipeline.
- Battery, Cooling and Motor contracts and historical evidence are unchanged;
  EV5 and its sealed journal sequence 14 are untouched.
- No protected cases, seeds or labels enter research/pods. TRAIN provenance,
  public practice and protected evaluation stay separate. No score weights or
  promotion rule are adopted; 45/30/25 remains a candidate to test.
- No training limit is set: a per-Challenge training-budget study is still
  required before limits/rewards. No experimental data or rig grant is implied.

with cheap analytical screening before solver packaging. Rejected: leaving
every numeric field OPEN after the owner's explicit instruction; pretending
the role-play establishes demand or empirical adequacy; copying the prior
three Challenges' numbers; and building five competing evaluation frameworks.

Implementation is documentation plus an offline screening utility. It does
not register an executable campaign. The PR belongs to this ticket alone;
PR Lead takes over delivery after handoff. Science visibility goes to #42;
PR Lead/Carbon Validator coordination goes to #643.

If a lead disagrees, supersede this decision and the affected packet/sheet
prospectively. Before any reference observation, corrections are cheap and
reversible. After an execution, retain its exact version and evidence and use
a new version for changed requirements. A reserved qualification decision
cannot be resolved by editing a Boolean in this package.
