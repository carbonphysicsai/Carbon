## 2026-10-14 — IDENTITY-RECOVERY-01-D1: freeze observed winner coldkey prospectively

**Ticket and base:** `IDENTITY-RECOVERY-01`, current main `408552f39bbcb90ed553d6575e267abc2800eb64`.
**Classification:** `IMPLEMENTATION_LAG` in the registered testnet winner rule. `OWNER-TESTNET-WEIGHTS-01` §3 says the same miner is measured by hotkey or coldkey, and `OWNER-VALV2-WEIGHTS-01` §5 says a same-hotkey or same-coldkey promotion never resets the clock. `VALIDATOR-14` records the same behavior. None supplies a new payout or historical backfill rule.

**Working decision:** New owner-only winner ledger records use schema v2 and freeze the winner coldkey observed in the finalized snapshot. A later final may use that field only when the record matches the exact predecessor model and hotkey. A new deployment's `FIRST_INCUMBENT` with an existing Challenge ledger winner also compares against that prior recorded winner, even though the new promotion has no `previous` model. Its challenger coldkey comes from the current finalized snapshot. The same-miner no-reset and different-miner first/improvement clocks are the existing registered decisions. A v1 predecessor can use its still-present hotkey's current snapshot coldkey under the prior behavior. If the predecessor has disappeared and v1 has no frozen identity, or a v2 frozen identity is missing or malformed, preview and issue refuse with `PREDECESSOR_IDENTITY_UNAVAILABLE`; they do not append a new ledger record or create an intent. Missing challenger identity also refuses. The existing ledger bytes and old eligibility decisions are never changed.

**Location and interfaces:** `carbon/rewards/winner_eligibility.py` owns the private v2 ledger record and `WinnerIdentityFailure`; `carbon/rewards/testnet_winner_publication.py` maps that failure to a typed publication refusal. The publisher's public weight policy, score, Challenge shares, decay arithmetic, and chain dispatch are unchanged. Focused tests live in `tests/cpu/test_reward_winner_eligibility.py` and `tests/cpu/test_reward_testnet_winner_publication.py`. This is a prospective private schema migration; mixed v1/v2 append-only records remain readable.

**Alternatives rejected:** Using only the current snapshot can turn predecessor deregistration into a fresh clock. Backfilling a v1 coldkey without historical proof would reinterpret evidence. Treating unknown identity as a new winner, same miner, or burned share would invent a reward disposition. Keeping an untyped generic failure would hide the exact operator stop.

**Reversibility and supersession:** The code and v2 writer can be superseded prospectively, but recorded v2 evidence remains historical. A lead change would update this decision, the ticket, the two reward modules, and the focused tests; it would not rewrite old ledger lines. A different historical-recovery method requires authoritative identity evidence and a prospective owner disposition for any affected real record. No scientific, security, economic, mainnet, or deployment acceptance is made here.

**Lead visibility:** Test Lead has the bounded working contract and PR head. Any required notification to a separate lead inbox should cite this decision and the PR; no notification is recorded as completed here.
