## 1. Executive Summary

Carbon is a Bittensor subnet that operates a **decentralized verification layer for physics-informed neural operator surrogates**. Miners submit training strategies; validators retrain on hidden procedural data under hard physics gates; lean scores drive emissions. Verified **Model Cards** can feed a Landscape Agent that compounds symbolic and causal insight across four ports — only after the lean exam is honest enough to treat as labels.

**Lead capability:** trustless verification + dual threshold (cheap search exams / honest commercial battery).  
**Compounding architecture:** Landscape four-port flywheel (build-ordered; not a pre-launch live brain).  
**Product lens:** solution maps for inverse design, plant-style response, exploration & UQ, hybrid truth — see `Use_Cases_by_Phase.md`.

**Dual threshold (non-negotiable)**

| Path | What it grades | Outcome |
|------|----------------|--------|
| **Miner → validator (lean)** | Physics gates, stress, short rollout, Model Card | Emissions / leaderboard — **search stays cheap** |
| **Landscape → Specialist Bank** | Effect-based recipe → controlled retrain → **product battery** | **Commercial SKU** — shelf credibility |

Leaderboard rank ≠ shelf product. No pay-to-compete.

**Knowledge flywheel (Landscape)** — architecture; public L0 priors only after Launch Bar green

```text
Model Cards (lean verified)
        → private graph
        → Port A: noisy priors (search)
        → Port B: eval routing (validators; floor rules)
        → Port C: challenge weight proposals
        → Port D: gauntlet-gated specialists
```

**Epistemic line:** Hard gates (when Launch Bar green) are protocol truth. Causal bands are observational estimates — never spoken with gate-level certainty.

Export law for Port D: ***Ground truth in. Verified knowledge out.***

## 8. Validation Strategy (Lean Path)

### Scoring (canonical detail)

**Formulas, Score Pack schema, validator load path, and per-challenge bank:** [`appendices/Scoring.md`](./appendices/Scoring.md).

Lean labels are the **trust root** for Landscape ingest. Unfinished gates/scores → do not claim verified compounding (`Launch_Bar.md`).

| Component | Weight | Composition |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Physics Fidelity** | 45% | Weighted gate **margins** (residual, conservation, short rollout, …) |
| **Robustness** | 30% | Stress categories: mean/tail blend + weakest-category pressure |
| **Accuracy** | 25% | Normalized held-out field error |

**Hard Gate Rule:** Any mandatory FAIL → `S_combined = 0`.

**Not in lean score:** training loss, product battery, Landscape similarity, prior distance.

**Challenge binding:** Validator loads Score Pack + Generator Pack by registered `(challenge_id, scoring_version, generator_version)` hashes — no silent default exam.

### Physics Gates (Hard)

Mass/energy/boundary, short rollout, plus regime gates by phase — as Score Pack declares. fp32 mandatory.

**UQ policy:** lean path does not require universal conformal hard gates in Phase 0–1A; product/UQ claims use specialist PB where declared.

### ChallengeWinnerTracker

```text
weight = lean_score * exp(-blocks_since_win / half_life)
```

Emissions follow **lean** outcomes only.
