## 1. Editorial decision

Whitepaper v3.1 remains a scientific/system-design paper.

The business canon is introduced as a **separate authority plane** around the scientific infrastructure, primarily in the value-accrual/commercial section and publication-status language.

It is intentionally **not** rewritten as an investor pitch.

---

## 2. Material v3.1 additions

### Abstract / opening posture

Adds a compact statement that the same scientific infrastructure admits a separate commercial loop:

- independent evidence;
- sponsored discovery;
- bounded qualification;
- deployment integration;
- lifecycle/requalification;
- reusable enterprise evidence infrastructure.

The paragraph explicitly states that commercial terms may choose the problem, privacy mode, deliverable, rights, and funding model but do not create scientific truth or weaken the evidentiary burden for a stated claim.

### Partner modes

Clarifies that scientific partner modes can map onto distinct products while the commercial contract remains outside the scientific judge.

### New subsection — Commercial architecture outside the scientific loop

Adds the owner-canonical hybrid model:

```text
expert-led services
→ reusable/productized workflows
→ enterprise platform/API
→ network-scaled external discovery/evaluation supply
→ qualification/lifecycle recurrence
→ Physics Intelligence only after prospective validation
```

Introduces the representative commercial ladder:

```text
Evidence Audit / Challenge Feasibility
→ Sponsored Discovery or Remediation
→ Model Development
→ Product Qualification
→ Deployment
→ Lifecycle / Requalification
→ Enterprise Evidence Platform / API / OEM
→ prospectively validated Physics Intelligence
```

The section explicitly labels this as business design rather than implementation or traction.

### New subsection — Standalone company and network economy

Separates:

```text
CARBON OPCO
services / software / licenses / qualification / support
        ↕ explicit mechanisms
CARBON NETWORK
miners / validators / sponsor rewards / Alpha / treasury
```

Adds the following publication rules:

- enterprise customers may use conventional procurement;
- sponsor reward principal is economically distinct from ordinary service/platform revenue;
- off-chain company revenue does not automatically create Alpha value;
- Alpha/network utility must arise through explicit economically useful network work or separately reviewed mechanisms;
- a successful OpCo and a successful network economy are distinct hypotheses.

### New subsection — Business flywheel and rights-aware evidence reuse

Adds the business flywheel:

```text
paid customer problem
→ evidence/discovery program
→ revenue + experiment records
→ reusable adapters/templates/workflows
→ lower marginal delivery cost / more platformization
→ more network eligibility / research supply
→ better outcomes
→ qualification/lifecycle expansion
```

Adds the condition that cross-customer evidence compounding occurs only where privacy/IP/contractual rights permit it.

### Commercial burden of proof

Separates scientific maturity from commercial maturity. The paper now states that Carbon must separately demonstrate:

- willingness to pay;
- paid pilots;
- repeatable delivery;
- expansion;
- recurring usage;
- falling bespoke effort;
- improving gross contribution;
- measurable network leverage.

### Non-claims

Adds that Carbon does not currently claim:

- commercial validation of the owner-recommended business model;
- design-stage products as contracted offerings;
- illustrative revenue/margin scenarios as forecasts;
- automatic Alpha value accrual from OpCo revenue.

### Appendix / authority map

Adds explicit business authority references to the canonical `Business/` folder while preserving scientific/runtime authority separation.

The whitepaper must keep those maturity states distinct.
