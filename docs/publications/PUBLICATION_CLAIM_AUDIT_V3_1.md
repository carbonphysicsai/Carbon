# Carbon Publication Claim Audit v3.1

**Status:** pre-release claim-control checklist.  
**Applies to:** Whitepaper v3.1, Academic Litepaper v3.1, and investor/stage presentation guidance derived from them.  
**Purpose:** make scientific, implementation, business, and network maturity boundaries explicit before release.

---

# 1. Scientific claims that may be stated as Carbon design

Appropriate when clearly framed as architecture/design:

- Carbon separates producer from official examiner.
- The intended Challenge architecture qualifies the exam before using it to qualify candidates.
- Target population, finite sampling/proposal distribution, and score/evidence weighting are distinct semantics.
- Validation Dossier qualifies the scientific exam chain.
- Score Pack is a versioned Evidence Use Contract.
- Mandatory scientific admissibility precedes soft ranking.
- Challenge scores are Challenge-bound and not automatically comparable across different Challenges.
- Frontier promotion is a distinct evidence step from ordinary candidate scoring.
- Performance reward is intended for verified frontier advance rather than permanent incumbency.
- Treasury settlement is a distinct authority from scientific winner determination.
- Product qualification is a stronger, separate claim from winning a discovery competition.
- Bittensor supplies the economic/optimizer substrate; it does not establish physical truth.

These statements must not be phrased as production deployment unless the relevant implementation and qualification evidence exists.

---

# 2. Scientific evidence Carbon may currently discuss carefully

Appropriate as design-gauntlet / methodological evidence, not production validation:

- the Burgers task audit found a missing causal input when viscosity varied but was not supplied to the one-input candidate;
- independent truth comparison exposed low-viscosity generator-resolution concerns in tested cases;
- a generator-matching oracle could be favored over a physical-truth-matching oracle under the flawed comparison path;
- the old final-state spatial-balance quantity omitted `u_t` and should not be described as a full Burgers PDE residual;
- reconstruction variability was material in pilot work.

Permissible synthesis:

> **The design gauntlets demonstrated that Carbon's own exam can contain defects that must be found and repaired before authoritative use.**

Do not turn these findings into a claim that the repaired Challenge is production-qualified.

---

# 3. Business claims that may be stated as Carbon strategy

Appropriate as current owner-canonical business design:

- Carbon is building discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models.
- The business model is hybrid: services → productized/enterprise platform → network marketplace → qualification/lifecycle → later Physics Intelligence.
- Evidence Audit is the preferred first commercial wedge.
- Sponsored Discovery is the preferred first strongly network-differentiated product.
- lifecycle/requalification is designed as a recurring revenue layer.
- enterprise platform, API/OEM, marketplace, and rights-permitted licensing are scale layers.
- enterprise purchasing may remain fiat-first.
- OpCo and network economics are separate systems connected by explicit mechanisms.
- the network's commercial advantage is falsifiable and must be demonstrated empirically.

These are business-design claims, not evidence of market acceptance.

---

# 4. Claims that require commercial evidence before being stated as facts

Do not state as achieved without evidence:

- Carbon has paying customers;
- Carbon has a validated price point;
- Evidence Audit has repeatable demand;
- Sponsored Discovery has repeatable demand;
- Carbon has recurring revenue;
- qualification/lifecycle has demonstrated attach or renewal rates;
- Carbon has a proven sales cycle, CAC, payback period, NRR, or ACV;
- Carbon has achieved a stated gross margin;
- the Enterprise Evidence Platform has customer adoption;
- API/OEM is a current revenue channel;
- a Frontier Market has commercial liquidity;
- Physics Intelligence is a validated product;
- rights-permitted evidence compounding has created a measurable commercial moat;
- the subnet has reduced customer R&D cost or improved outcomes relative to centralized alternatives.

---

# 5. Financial-language rules

If an illustrative scenario is discussed, label it explicitly as:

- planning assumption;
- scenario;
- architecture test;
- not a forecast;
- not traction;
- not contracted revenue.

Do not count or describe as ordinary Carbon revenue without appropriate basis:

- sponsor bounty/reward principal;
- Alpha price appreciation;
- unsigned LOIs;
- hypothetical future licenses;
- design-stage products.

---

# 6. Alpha/network claim rules

Permitted:

> **Carbon aims to create network utility by routing economically valuable scientific work through miners, validators, sponsor-funded frontier rewards, and other useful network services where the network improves the product.**

> **OpCo revenue does not automatically create Alpha value.**

> **The network earns a stronger business role only if it demonstrates useful economic/scientific leverage.**

Do not claim without separately adopted and reviewed mechanisms:

- OpCo profits accrue to Alpha holders;
- company revenue is automatically used for buybacks/burn;
- Alpha holders receive revenue share;
- token price proves product-market fit;
- every enterprise customer must buy Alpha;
- treasury settlement is production-secure.

---

# 7. Qualification claim rules

Use:

- bounded evidence-backed qualification;
- exact artifact/system identity;
- defined context of use;
- explicit limitations;
- requalification triggers;
- answerability/escalation where applicable.

Avoid:

- universally safe;
- certified for all use;
- regulator-approved;
- guaranteed trustworthy;
- qualification of a component automatically qualifies a composed system.

---

# 8. Competitive/market claim rules

Appropriate:

- Carbon positions itself as independent discovery/evidence/qualification infrastructure across CAE, model builders, engineering-AI, and deployment workflows;
- adjacent companies may be competitors, suppliers, customers, or partners depending on engagement;
- Carbon's differentiation is the integrated institutional combination rather than the claim that every component is unique.

Before public use, externally sourced statements about named competitors, market sizes, funding, revenue, recurring mix, or industry growth should be re-verified with dated primary/high-quality sources.

Do not embed unsourced market figures into scientific papers merely to strengthen investor rhetoric.

---

# 9. Publication-specific posture

## Whitepaper

Primary job: scientific/system architecture and falsifiable hypotheses. Business architecture should remain bounded to value-accrual, commercial use, and authority separation.

## Academic Litepaper

Primary job: concise scientific thesis. Business section should remain one compact explanatory layer.

## Investor/stage deck

May lead more strongly with company category, commercial wedge, land-and-expand, and network advantage, but must use business maturity language and keep unresolved business-lead questions unresolved.

---

# 10. Release checklist

Before release, verify:

- [ ] exact publication source is version-controlled;
- [ ] rendered artifact was generated from that exact source;
- [ ] no unresolved citations or broken internal references;
- [ ] no owner-internal status jargon remains where public wording is clearer;
- [ ] no business design is phrased as traction;
- [ ] no scientific design is phrased as production qualification;
- [ ] no automatic OpCo-to-Alpha value claim;
- [ ] no universal qualification/safety claim;
- [ ] no stale `score drives emissions` terminal-architecture language;
- [ ] no stale `generator = truth` or universal Julia-truth language;
- [ ] no stale incumbency-rent language;
- [ ] no raw cross-Challenge score pooling claim;
- [ ] final PDF visually inspected end-to-end;
- [ ] business lead has reviewed business-facing sections;
- [ ] scientific/technical lead has reviewed scientific-facing sections before formal release.
