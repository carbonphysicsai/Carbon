## 1. Editorial decision

Commercial material is concentrated in one new section rather than dispersed throughout the scientific argument.

---

## 2. Material v3.1 additions

### Abstract

Adds a short statement that Carbon's scientific infrastructure can support distinct commercial products outside the scientific judge:

- independent evidence around an existing model;
- sponsored discovery;
- bounded qualification and lifecycle support;
- repeated enterprise evidence infrastructure.

The abstract explicitly says those are business designs rather than current traction and that customer payment does not alter the evidence required for a scientific claim.

### New section — Commercial architecture: land with evidence, expand into infrastructure

Adds five layers:

1. **Services / evidence wedge** — Evidence Audit or Challenge Feasibility, with truth/solver/data integration where required.
2. **Discovery and qualification expansion** — Sponsored Discovery, Model Development, Product Qualification, and deployment.
3. **Recurring lifecycle** — requalification, support, model-change intake, and repeated evidence programs.
4. **Enterprise platform and distribution** — private workspaces, usage, APIs, OEM, VPC/on-prem where qualified.
5. **Network and later intelligence** — subnet as scalable external research/evaluation supply; Physics Intelligence only after prospective decision lift.

Includes the locked synthesis:

> **Services get Carbon into the customer. Platform makes the relationship recurring. The network makes discovery scalable. Qualification increases contract value. Lifecycle increases retention. Physics intelligence compounds the moat only if it earns prospective value.**

### OpCo / network separation

Adds that:

- enterprise customers may use conventional procurement;
- OpCo revenue does not automatically create Alpha value;
- network economic relevance is earned through useful miner/validator/sponsor activity;
- token economics are not scientific authority or a substitute for a viable company.

### First commercial wedge

Adds the proposed `Carbon Evidence Audit` as the initial commercial wedge and `Sponsored Discovery Program` as the first strongly network-differentiated product.

### Commercial maturity statement

Explicitly states that the business architecture remains unvalidated until Carbon demonstrates buyer demand, paid pilots, repeatable delivery, expansion, recurring revenue, increasing productization, and measurable network leverage.

### Non-claims

Adds that Carbon does not claim:

- the proposed business model is already commercially validated;
- design-stage products have customers merely because they are specified;
- illustrative financial scenarios are forecasts;
- OpCo revenue automatically creates Alpha value.

## 3. Scientific material deliberately preserved

The business section does not replace or dilute the litepaper's core scientific lines:

> **First prove the exam. Then run the exam.**

> **Fail mandatory physics, and soft accuracy cannot rescue you.**

> **Carbon pays for verified progress, not incumbency.**

The qualified-exam, protected-evaluation, frontier-promotion, product-qualification, and Bittensor-authority boundaries remain intact.

## 4. Citation discipline

Business architecture is Carbon-authored strategy and should not be decorated with scientific citations as if academic papers validated Carbon's go-to-market model.

External market/company facts, TAM figures, competitor revenue, or investor-market claims should be sourced separately in investor materials if used.

The litepaper's academic citations should continue to support external scientific premises only.
