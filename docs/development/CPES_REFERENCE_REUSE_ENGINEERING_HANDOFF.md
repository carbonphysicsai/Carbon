# Minimal later Engineering contract for CPES reference reuse

**Status:** research handoff only; no implementation ticket selected

**Source:** EXAM-PROTECT-01 continuation at issue #142
**Current executable baseline:** retain Variant A

## Conditional entry criteria

A later Variant-B DEVELOPMENT proposal should be selected only when all of the
following are available for one named Challenge/profile:

- a source-owned compatibility identity covering physical inputs, requested
  outputs, units/scaling, generator/reference configuration, environment,
  policy, evidence depth, custody and disclosure permissions;
- eligible arrival/admission observations showing at least two compatible jobs
  coexist at actual dispatch opportunities;
- a qualified or explicitly bounded research reference-cost observation for
  that exact identity;
- a bounded estimate or measured proxy for membership, cache, recovery, audit
  and closure overhead;
- scientific ownership of field-size, common-case comparison, repetitions and
  promotion meaning; and
- an owner-supplied acceptable feedback-delay/service envelope.

The investment screen is conditional, not an activation threshold. For fixed
membership and unchanged evidence, recurring work can decrease only when
`(b - 1)R > H`. Dynamic replay must still recompute membership and completion
for every overhead assumption.

## Required lifecycle

```text
durable candidate admission
-> predefined dispatch opportunity
-> atomic compatibility validation and immutable membership lock
-> persist draw/dispatch intent
-> bind fresh common case/reference identity
-> candidate-owned reconstruction and replica obligations
-> exact private prediction/reference evidence
-> terminal disposition of every answer-dependent opportunity
-> pack closure
-> idempotent permitted summaries
```

Singleton fallback is the only legal underfilled behavior in the first slice.
No intentional wait, late admission, cross-Challenge sharing, adaptive
membership, early summary, answer archive, new reward effect or production
provider belongs in that slice.

## Durable acceptance cases

1. Two independent connections race membership against exposure; only the
   pre-exposure transaction wins.
2. Copies, retries and another identity retain the original CandidateRef and
   do not create a pack or draw.
3. Compatibility mismatch in any exact cache field rejects before reference or
   candidate execution.
4. Incomplete, malformed, stale, cross-pack and cross-attempt evidence rejects.
5. Crash before/after membership, draw intent, reference dispatch, result,
   closure and summary intent preserves identity or enters reconciliation.
6. Infrastructure retry retains the pack; replicas remain distinct obligations
   and are never best-of-many proposals.
7. Reference failure remains reference failure and closes no positive result.
8. A slow, cancelled, failed or unresolved member has an explicit terminal or
   blocked disposition; no caller flag infers closure.
9. Closure races with late prediction, retry, replica and stale callback reject
   after restart.
10. A blocked shared pack does not stop unrelated Challenges on the same
    bounded resource.
11. Summary delivery is idempotent across dispatch/acknowledgement crashes and
    reveals no case-selection lineage.
12. No answer publication, accepted-result, frontier, reward, network,
    production or LIVE route is reachable, including through direct internal
    interfaces.
13. Vulnerable controls prove late admission, premature closure and forged
    authority attacks succeed when their guard is removed.
14. A feedback-channel probe proves the chosen summary precision/rate cannot
    be adaptively reused beyond its registered evidence policy.

## Still separate

Production entropy and event finality, protected custody, privileged-host
integrity, authoritative asset lineage, scientific reference adequacy,
field-size resolution, public rights and retirement remain owned decisions.
The first B implementation must not pretend to solve or route around them.
