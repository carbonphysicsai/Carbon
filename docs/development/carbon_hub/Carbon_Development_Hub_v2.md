## Change routes

- **Add a new Challenge** (`new-challenge`): Route a new physical job from authored semantics through real integration and human qualification.
- **Add or change a model architecture** (`model-architecture`): Place architecture work according to whether it changes a bounded catalog component, a reconstruction adapter, or the permitted search space.
- **Add or change a miner prior** (`miner-prior`): Route prior work through immutable schema, publication controls, deterministic provision, and leakage testing.
- **Change a reference or truth path** (`reference-truth`): Route truth assets, primary and witness roles, uncertainty, access, typed failure, operations, and fail-closed behavior through their owning records.
- **Change measurement or scoring** (`measurement-scoring`): Keep measurement definition, uncertainty, Score Pack evidence use, admissibility, ranking, frontier policy, and settlement in separate owning records.
- **Repair a protocol defect or invariant regression** (`protocol-defect`): Attach the defect to the ticket that owns the violated contract, then preserve the repair and evidence trail.
- **Add a commercial or private deployment mode** (`commercial-private`): Route customer-facing capability through rights, privacy, truth access, delivery, acceptance, qualification, and lifecycle.
