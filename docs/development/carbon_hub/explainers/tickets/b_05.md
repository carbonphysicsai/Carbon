# B-05: Measurement and Score Pack authoring

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-05`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2

## What and why

Define MeasurementContract, ReconstructionEvidencePolicy, dependence-aware UncertaintyPolicy, and Score Pack authoring bindings.

Reference outputs become scientific evidence only after Carbon defines what to measure, where the measurement applies, how uncertainty enters, and how admissibility and ranking use the result.

## What it adds

A working measurement/evidence-use contract plus the first Challenge-bound definition, evidence-role, canonical identity, and fixture-store slice.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02C, B-04, B-01H
- **Feeds:** B-06, B-07A, B-07C, B-07F, B-E1, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex + SciML
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + SRE
- **Master questions:** MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008

## Explicit non-goals

It does not allow the scoring engine to invent thresholds, weights, stopping rules, or evidence policy at runtime.
