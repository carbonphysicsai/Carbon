# C-01: Durable execution state and queue

**Wave:** C

**Map ref:** `WAVE-C/C-01`

**Status:** DONE

**Target phase:** C1

## What and why

Own a private durable queue around source-issued A7 attempt identities, exact scientific/environment policies, partial work and opaque result associations.

A crash between intent, dispatch and result recording must not silently rerun a Strategy, change protected cases or lose consumed work.

## What it adds

Atomic admission and claiming, explicit restart reconciliation, verified resumable partial references, exact retry continuity and requester-safe status.

## Current stage
