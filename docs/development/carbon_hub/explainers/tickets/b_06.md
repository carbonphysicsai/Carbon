# B-06: Validation Dossier and qualification manifest

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-06`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2/3

## What and why

Build D1-D12 Dossier structure, interval-coverage evidence, cross-section consistency checks, and qualification-manifest machinery.

The team needs one place to assemble generator, reference, measurement, uncertainty, attack, and limitation evidence without confusing a complete file structure with a qualified exam.

## What it adds

A working contract and first structural slice with exact Challenge-bound D1-D12 identities, typed evidence/signer refs, explicit completeness, version/supersession, fixture propagation, and deterministic Dossier serialization.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02A, B-03, B-04, B-05, A3
- **Feeds:** B-E3, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + security + independent reviewer
- **Master questions:** MQ-003, MQ-004, MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008, MQ-018

## Explicit non-goals

It does not treat schema completeness, PASS labels, signer names, signature presence, fixtures, or tests as verified authorization, scientific qualification, or LIVE authority; manifest issuance and active-registry comparison remain later work.

## Current stage

Selected under B-06-D0 from exact merged main 2500e510 and tree 89763523. Slice 1 implements the D1-D12 structural identity/evidence/signature foundation with focused native tests; Slice 2 typed evidence manifests and cross-section completeness are next.

## Maturity ceiling

The working contract and first implementation slice are a branch-local candidate. Ticket-level SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, and TESTED remain unearned until delivery completes; scientific, security, network, commercial, production, qualification, review, merge, and LIVE authority remain unearned.
