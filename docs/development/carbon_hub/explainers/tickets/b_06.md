# B-06: Validation Dossier and qualification manifest

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-06`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2/3

## What and why

Build D1-D12 Dossier structure, interval-coverage evidence, cross-section consistency checks, and qualification-manifest machinery.

The team needs one place to assemble generator, reference, measurement, uncertainty, attack, and limitation evidence without confusing a complete file structure with a qualified exam.

## What it adds

Three structural slices: exact Challenge-bound D1-D12 identity/signers; typed subject, evidence, claim, statistical, accounting, secrecy, and limitation manifests; and a deterministic qualification candidate with a closed artifact set, external signer-result seam, and pure exact A3 snapshot comparison.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02A, B-03, B-04, B-05, A3
- **Feeds:** B-E3, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + security + independent reviewer
- **Master questions:** MQ-003, MQ-004, MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008, MQ-018

## Explicit non-goals

It does not treat schema completeness, PASS labels, populated dependence fields, component uncertainty, signer names, external authorization flags, a matching A3 snapshot, fixtures, or tests as verified scientific approval, production qualification, registry authority, or LIVE readiness.

## Current stage

Slices 1-3 are local structural checkpoints. Exact D1-D12 identity and typed evidence remain intact; qualification-candidate construction, closed artifact sets, external signer-result inputs, and pure exact A3 snapshot comparison are now implemented and locally tested. Campaign-specific evidence schemas are next.

## Maturity ceiling

The working contract and three implementation slices are a branch-local candidate. Ticket-level SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, and TESTED remain unearned until delivery completes; scientific, security, network, commercial, production, qualification, review, merge, and LIVE authority remain unearned.
