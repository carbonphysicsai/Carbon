# B-06: Validation Dossier and qualification manifest

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-06`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2/3

## What and why

Build D1-D12 Dossier structure, interval-coverage evidence, cross-section consistency checks, and qualification-manifest machinery.

The team needs one place to assemble generator, reference, measurement, uncertainty, attack, and limitation evidence without confusing a complete file structure with a qualified exam.

## What it adds

Two structural slices: exact Challenge-bound D1-D12 identity/signers plus typed subject, evidence, claim, statistical, accounting, secrecy, and limitation manifests with deterministic origin-preserving history.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02A, B-03, B-04, B-05, A3
- **Feeds:** B-E3, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + security + independent reviewer
- **Master questions:** MQ-003, MQ-004, MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008, MQ-018

## Explicit non-goals

It does not treat schema completeness, PASS labels, populated dependence fields, component uncertainty, signer names, signature presence, fixtures, or tests as verified authorization, scientific qualification, or LIVE authority; manifest issuance and active-registry comparison remain later work.

## Current stage

Slices 1-2 are local structural checkpoints. Exact D1-D12 identity, claim/evidence non-substitution, subject versions/digests, pending dependence scope, intended/realized accounting, secrecy, limitations, fixture propagation, and canonical identity are implemented and locally tested; qualification-manifest comparison is next.

## Maturity ceiling

The working contract and two implementation slices are a branch-local candidate. Ticket-level SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, and TESTED remain unearned until delivery completes; scientific, security, network, commercial, production, qualification, review, merge, and LIVE authority remain unearned.
