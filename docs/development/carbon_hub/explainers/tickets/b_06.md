# B-06: Validation Dossier and qualification manifest

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-06`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2/3

## What and why

Build D1-D12 Dossier structure, interval-coverage evidence, cross-section consistency checks, and qualification-manifest machinery.

The team needs one place to assemble generator, reference, measurement, uncertainty, attack, and limitation evidence without confusing a complete file structure with a qualified exam.

## What it adds

Four structural slices: exact Challenge-bound D1-D12 identity/signers; typed subject, evidence, claim, statistical, accounting, secrecy, and limitation manifests; a deterministic qualification candidate with closed artifact set, external signer-result seam, and pure exact A3 snapshot comparison; and typed campaign acquisition/result manifests for every ticket-named evidence family.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02A, B-03, B-04, B-05, A3
- **Feeds:** B-E3, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + security + independent reviewer
- **Master questions:** MQ-003, MQ-004, MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008, MQ-018

## Explicit non-goals

It does not treat schema completeness, PASS labels, dependence fields, component uncertainty, signer names, external authorization flags, matching A3 structure, fixtures, or tests as verified scientific approval, production qualification, registry authority, or LIVE readiness.

## Current stage

B-06's bounded structural implementation is reconciled with current main and OWNER-DX-03. All nine historical review findings are repaired; their reviews and approvals are historical evidence only. The integrated ready revision requires applicable automated acceptance and normal expected-head merge. B-05 stays in_progress with no active work.

## Maturity ceiling

The reconciled contract, four implementation slices, and nine bounded review repairs form a tested structural delivery candidate. Ticket-level SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, and TESTED remain unearned until delivery completes; scientific, security, network, commercial, production, qualification, and LIVE authority remain unearned.
