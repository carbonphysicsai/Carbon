# B-06: Validation Dossier and qualification manifest

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-06`

**Status:** IN_PROGRESS

**Target phase:** WB-2/3

## What and why

Build D1-D12 Dossier structure, interval-coverage evidence, cross-section consistency checks, and qualification-manifest machinery.

The team needs one place to assemble generator, reference, measurement, uncertainty, attack, and limitation evidence without confusing a complete file structure with a qualified exam.

## What it adds

Four structural slices: exact Challenge-bound D1-D12 identity/signers; typed subject, evidence, claim, statistical, accounting, secrecy, and limitation manifests; a deterministic qualification candidate with a closed artifact set, external signer-result seam, and pure exact A3 snapshot comparison; and typed campaign acquisition/result manifests for all ticket-named evidence families.

## Placement and handoff

- **Depends on:** B-02A, B-03, B-04, B-05, A3
- **Feeds:** B-E3, B-GATE
- **Driver:** Codex
- **Review route:** SciML + statistics + protocol + security + independent reviewer
- **Master questions:** MQ-003, MQ-004, MQ-005, MQ-006, MQ-007, MQ-008, MQ-018

## Explicit non-goals

It does not treat schema completeness, PASS labels, populated dependence fields, component uncertainty, signer names, external authorization flags, a matching A3 snapshot, fixtures, or tests as verified scientific approval, production qualification, registry authority, or LIVE readiness.

B-06's fourth complete-diff review returned B06-CR-009, now repaired while B06-CR-001 through B06-CR-008 remain verified repaired. All predecessor CI/reviews are stale; fresh exact-head CI and a fifth completely fresh review remain before approval, merge, or closeout. B-05 stays in_progress with no active review loop.

The reconciled contract, four implementation slices, and bounded repairs for all four findings-bearing reviews form a native-tested repair candidate. Ticket-level SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, and TESTED remain unearned until delivery completes; scientific, security, network, commercial, production, qualification, merge, and LIVE authority remain unearned.
