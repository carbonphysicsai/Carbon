# B-07B: Research tasks, records, receipts, and lineage

**Wave:** B

**Map ref:** `WAVE-B/B-07B`

## What and why

Implement ResearchTask, ExperimentRecord, ResearchReceipt, evidence classes, failure retention, and lineage.

Research results need durable identity and provenance so later practice, priors, utility studies, and rights controls can distinguish what happened and what the evidence may support.

## What it adds

A constructor-bound linearizable local task provider, immutable exact input pins, private ExperimentRecords with resolved-plan-derived lineage and typed evidence/failures, bounded terminal receipts, provider-owned retry, cancellation and poll replay, and rights-fail-closed retention.
