## What and why

Wave M handles deployment adapters, portfolios, routers, fallback and abstention, system composition, drift, requalification, envelope expansion, and customer or auditor evidence views.

Real engineering systems change across models, data, solvers, hardware, operating envelopes, and deployment context. Qualification must track the exact system identity and change history.

## Success and unlocks

Carbon can preserve bounded evidence across a product lifecycle and trigger requalification when a material change breaks the previous identity or claim.

Recurring lifecycle, enterprise portfolio, and system-level qualification services.
