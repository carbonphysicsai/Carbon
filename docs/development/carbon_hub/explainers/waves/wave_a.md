# Wave A: Bounded protocol skeleton

**Status:** CLOSED

**Map ref:** `WAVE-A`

**Purpose:** Prove Carbon's software authority boundaries before real scientific execution.

## Sequence

- **Predecessor:** Constitution and repository orientation
- **Successor:** Wave B

## What and why

Wave A creates the smallest bounded protocol skeleton that can express a Challenge, a declarative strategy, protected randomness, scoring, submission lifecycle, public-safe disclosure, miner-facing operations, observability, and invariant checks.

Carbon cannot trust later scientific or network behavior until the software keeps producers, evaluators, public views, protected data, and economic state in separate authority domains.

## Success and unlocks

The A-1 and A0-A12 tickets close in their recorded engineering scopes, the invariant suite passes, and the repository can demonstrate the intended boundaries with fixture-only behavior.

Wave B can build science-ready authoring and research contracts on a stable substrate instead of inventing basic lifecycle and authority semantics again.

## Authority ceiling

Closed only in the wave's recorded bounded engineering scope. No later qualification or production authority follows.

## Still unavailable

Wave A closure does not create scientific, security, network, commercial, production, LIVE, launch, frontier, settlement, weight, or emission authority.
