# Wave H: Frontier promotion and finality

**Status:** PLANNED

**Map ref:** `WAVE-H`

**Purpose:** Separate ordinary Challenge ranking from verified frontier advance.

## Sequence

- **Predecessor:** Wave D
- **Successor:** Wave I

## What and why

Wave H introduces FrontierBaseline, FrontierRecord, LeaderReplacementPolicy, FrontierPromotionExam, FrontierAdvanceEvent, ChallengeSetEpoch, appeal, and finality while preserving explicit SUPERIOR, NOT_SUPERIOR, and INDETERMINATE outcomes.

A local ranking or floating-point improvement does not prove that a contender moved the scientific frontier on comparable fresh evidence.

## Success and unlocks

Carbon promotes a contender only through the registered fresh promotion policy, records one exact finalizable frontier event when the evidence resolves it, and preserves the registered one-event-per-Challenge-settlement-window direction.

Event-bound economic settlement and a governed portfolio of Challenges.

## Authority ceiling

Planning and compatibility context only; this wave is not active implementation authority.

## Still unavailable

Incumbency does not earn perpetual rewards, unresolved evidence does not create a winner, and scores remain non-comparable across Challenges by default.
