# Wave G: Commercial, private, and sponsored engagement plane

**Status:** PLANNED

**Map ref:** `WAVE-G`

**Purpose:** Serve real enterprises without moving business authority into the judge.

## Sequence

- **Predecessor:** Wave D
- **Successor:** Only a prospectively authorized successor

## What and why

Wave G retains its engagement/rights/privacy/deliverable authorities. Passive planned work G-PR0, G-PR1, G-PR2, G-PR3, G-PR4 and G-PR5 adds a staged paid-research lane: preregistration, exact entitlement and quotes, tenant campaign memory, isolated non-official execution, billing/spend control, and an evidence-based expansion decision.

Customers need private deployment, clear rights, bounded deliverables, and commercial acceptance without gaining the power to change scientific evidence rules.

## Success and unlocks

The frozen pilot measures research utility, demand, full contribution, support, safety and free-path health while every job remains rights-bound, tenant-isolated, non-official, explicitly authorized and retry-safe financially.

Evidence Audits, Sponsored Discovery, qualification, lifecycle, private/VPC delivery, and later enterprise platform workflows. This post-D lane does not gate H or I.

## Authority ceiling

Planning and compatibility context only; this wave is not active implementation authority.

## Still unavailable

A designed product or stated budget is not traction or spend authority. Payment cannot unlock protected official information, change official treatment, or qualify non-official research.
