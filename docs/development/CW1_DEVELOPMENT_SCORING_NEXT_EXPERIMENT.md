# Next bounded real-agent experiment — request, not execution authority

## Question and decision

Can one agent, shown the explicit balanced-v2 objective and its own allowed
historical feedback, produce an admissible trajectory within the existing
32..64-step construction surface? This targets the observed mean/energy failure,
not a larger model or a broader population. A new admissible baseline is needed
before real accepted-improvement credit can open; failed historical FNO-40 is
not an eligible reward baseline. Even an admissible challenger against an
inadmissible baseline is reported as an opening-baseline candidate, not a paid
win or an accepted scalar improvement in v2.

## One requested envelope

- One fresh authenticated baseline using the retained FNO-40 construction and
  one model-proposed challenger; three fresh replicas each, no best-replica
  selection. At most one new agent proposal/evaluation, including invalid
  proposals. No automatic replacement or forced second proposal.
- Same pinned existing gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07 provider adapter; at most 12 calls
  including failures/retries, 65,536 input and 2,048 output tokens per call,
  at most USD 0.25 new provider charges. Existing conservative per-call
  reservations total USD 0.24576 for 12 maximum requests; verify current
  configured rates before dispatch and stop if the cap cannot be guaranteed.
  An ambiguous request retains its reservation and is not duplicated.
- Six reconstructions maximum, 384 optimizer updates maximum, six predictions,
  144 C-05 measurements, plus one derived comparison/reference-refinement
  carrier. Reuse the authenticated TRAIN/reference cohort artifacts where
  version compatible. No fresh cohort selection based on outcomes.
- One isolated 2-CPU/4-GiB/no-swap worker; 600-second productive deadline per
  carrier, 1,800 cumulative worker wall seconds, 3,600 session wall seconds,
  at most 10 GiB new artifacts. Reserve the existing worst-case operation
  allowance before each worker; stop rather than overrun these totals.
- Valid for six hours from a future explicit approval. This document records
  no approval timestamp and consumes no old authorization. No chain write,
  registration, activation, weight change, credit purchase or billing change.

## Prospective setup and permitted information

Freeze balanced-v2, measurement v3, exact v2 challenge/cohort/reference/resource
bindings and trusted session roots before either construction starts. Prepare
the authenticated service and its public verification configuration before
registration. Fresh private reconstruction seeds are evaluator-owned; bind and
retain them before dispatch through the existing repeat plan. C-06/C-07/C-08
provide the actual source handoffs. The operator must not author receipts.

The 12/12/12 cohort remains seen adaptive development evidence, never prospective
population confirmation. Prior FNO results and this task's analytic controls
are calibration; the next fresh reconstructions test the frozen rule's behavior
on new construction randomness, not its generalization to unseen cases.
No evaluator cases/labels, controller filesystem, wallet or signing key enters
miner capabilities. Use the private provider-key file and existing restricted
miner tools. Publish the objective through challenge discovery; attach only
the miner's own checked comparison feedback through C-07.

## Costs, stop rules and informative outcomes

The previous one-challenger session used eight calls, USD 0.00348135 priced
usage (USD 0.00745575 conservative accounting), 271.79 worker-wall seconds and
162.45 CPU seconds for three reconstructions/predictions and 72 measurements.
Two constructions at similar cost project roughly 9 worker minutes; the
30-minute cumulative cap leaves bounded headroom without widening per-worker
resources. This estimate assumes similar JIT/training cost and is not a quote.
The provider ceiling covers possible long contexts/reasoning above prior use;
no larger model is proposed. Ask Carbon's website budget is separate.

Stop on budget exhaustion, ambiguity, missing/reference/infra failure, quarantine,
revocation, cancellation or incompatible identity. Preserve stopped attempts;
do not resend or tune the rule after observing results. Source signatures,
complete-cohort admission and C-10 are rechecked before any simulation.

Informative outcomes include: both inadmissible (construction surface/budget is
the bottleneck); one admissible opening-baseline candidate; admissible robust
improvement/regression/equivalence/trade-off; or unresolved replica/reference
resolution. If both are admissible and a robust improvement meets v2, perform
only the non-paying simulation with explicit clock/allocation. If no admissible
baseline emerges, recommend the smallest construction-surface change from the
observed failure rather than weakening the gate or claiming a winner.
