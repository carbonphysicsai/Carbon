## Durable return and consumer interface

Each returned raw record is appended, flushed and fsynced before assessment.
Partial receipts retain all nine offered job states, including submitted,
failed-infrastructure, missing and not-dispatched identities. Completed capture
is called `T9_CAPTURED_NOT_TRAIN_QUALIFIED`; it is not a successful training or
scientific-value check. CAL/EVAL/N1 release and qualification remain false.

`validate_delivery(binding, manifest, raw_chunks, *, expected_binding_digest,
expected_manifest_digest, g0_binding, g0_raw, g0_receipt)` authenticates the
complete offered set and every returned source-bound raw line. It constructs
`ValidatedT9Evidence` through a private token, not a caller-controlled PASS.
`validate_for(evidence, *, expected_binding_digest, expected_manifest_digest,
scope)` refuses raw dictionaries and foreign/context-mismatched evidence and
returns a fresh snapshot. This is internal provenance typing, not a security
guarantee against arbitrary hostile Python.

A foreign raw schema or contradictory observer is a contract refusal before
evidence is issued, even when its chunk has been rehashed. A typed pre-solve
or infrastructure failure records the requested T9 context without claiming
execution or tracking success. Correct-context bad telemetry remains retained
and unadmitted; foreign raw bytes remain retained with INVALID_DELIVERY.

The snapshot contains scope, binding/manifest digests, protocol/method/observer/
dataset IDs, exact source commit/files, canonical G0 receipt digest, nine jobs,
returned records, source-row byte/line proofs, tracking reports and every-job
states. Missing records have no fabricated source mapping. Nonfinite output
numbers become null only in the JSON-safe snapshot, after original-raw tracking
and extraction; the exact raw bytes remain retained and hash-mapped. Null does
not create eligibility or repair a label.
