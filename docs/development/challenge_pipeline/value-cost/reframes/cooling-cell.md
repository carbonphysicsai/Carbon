## Buyer job and independent practice evidence

Mock cold-plate thermal engineer asks which candidate interior cells are
worth detailed verification for the **stated** post-spreader map/coolant
service; the screen reports pass/fail/unknown and local margins rather than
promising a hydraulic optimum. [CoolIT's custom design workflow](https://www.coolitsystems.com/coldplate-technology/)
starts from a processor thermal profile and uses CFD, geometry development,
prototyping and validation. Independent
[Boyd's cooling design workflow](https://www.boydcorp.com/thermal/liquid-cooling-systems.html?hss_channel=lcp-72747)
describes cold-plate/heat-exchanger performance simulation and design
optimization. Neither proves our periodic cell represents an assembly.

The independent [Mat et al. microchannel study](https://www.tj.kyushu-u.ac.jp/evergreen/contents/EG2024-11_2_content/p1426-1434.html)
uses a segmented symmetric microchannel CFD model and response-surface
thermal/pressure optimization. Its 2500 response-surface design points are
not 2500 CFD acquisitions. This supports an early reduced component-design
step, not our vapour-chamber properties or transferable performance.

## Candidate, preserved limits and sacrifice

Keep the current geometry/flow action support, all mandatory service strata,
materials, property ranges and peak **lid-side TIM2 case temperature <=85 C**.
Do not move the plane to the die, flatten a map arbitrarily, change inlet,
TIM or lid, relax 85 C, or drop a hard hydraulic limit. Owner must first
select final lid/service inputs and **cell** hydraulic constraints. Assembly
50 kPa/2.5 W/3 L/min numbers are not silently adopted cell requirements.
Missing allocation/pinned physical scaling HOLDS the relevant classification.

Proposed output is a feasible-set/margin **screen**, retaining detailed cell
verification before use. The buyer gives up a certified best thermal/
hydraulic trade and any full-plate/pump/die-junction conclusion. Local margin
is evaluated at matched coordinates with the post-spreader TIM2 heat flux;
no mean-flux correction to an unrelated maximum. Every stratum is mandatory.
Unknown map/reference/cell support remains UNRESOLVED. No algorithmically
chosen 'buyer' inlet or lid moves a safety boundary here.
