## Candidate question and what it gives up

Keep the original valid ribbed-plate geometry, linear material, clamp, load
and all three damping conditions. Screen static compliance/mass and modal
features; return a shortlist **requiring** full FRF verification, not a
certified resonance-safe best support. Screening must not call an unknown
harmonic peak safe. A natural frequency outside a band alone does not prove
tip compliance, omitted-mode contributions or off-resonant safety.

Original mass <=0.45 kg, static <=0.03 mm/N and dynamic peak <=0.15 mm/N over
80–600 Hz remain binding before final original-task acceptance. Do not raise
damping, omit resonances, change clamps, add contact/nonlinearity, or discard
bad designs to manufacture a viable learned task. New screening shortlist
rules, proxy objective and acceptance remain HUMAN_INPUT; no new question law.

Buyer gives up direct full-response optimality and claimed 10/30/50-micrometre
peak-motion improvement. Retain full harmonic verification of deciding
contenders, including low damping, mode crossings and static/mass boundaries.
This **loses** part of the original value, not a price increase for complexity.
