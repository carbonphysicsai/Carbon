## Buyer problem and two independent sources

Mock buyer: PIC layout engineer needs an early pitch/duty/etch/longitudinal
alignment shortlist before expensive finite-width verification. The
[Ansys grating workflow](https://optics.ansys.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042305334-Grating-coupler)
optimizes pitch/duty/longitudinal position in 2D and then refines in 3D. Its
example reports 10–20 min for the initial optimization, not our C1 or a buyer
latency requirement. [Westerveld, Urbach and Yousefi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02964)
independently combine rigorous 2D simulation, effective-index reduction and
diffraction for a grating coupler. Their geometry/normalization is not ours;
their example error does not certify our reduction. These support a real
design step, not a measured Carbon customer or a transferable accuracy tier.
