## C1/C2 estimates and the EUR100 target

**All C1 values are unmeasured hypotheses**, low/base/high complete-case process
CPU-hours, not p50/p95. They reflect small local nonlinear FE or bounded steady
CFD decks; no existing family timing is borrowed as a measurement. Memory is
also a hypothesis. Define complete cases in each card, including every load,
stroke, current, meshing and extraction step. C3/C4 NOT_DEMONSTRATED.

Common illustrative CCX63 scenario:

`U = primary cases + 2 × refined cases + charged failed attempts + 8 × 2 witnesses`

`bill = 1.37 × (U × complete-case CPU-h × elapsed/CPU ratio + 4) × 1.19 + 10 EUR`.

The 1.37 EUR/node-h is the framework's working rate, not a CPU-hour tariff.
[Hetzner's published table](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/)
lists Germany/Finland CCX63 at 1.3678 EUR/h excluding VAT/IPv4; location and
invoice matter. Assume serial one-core cases, elapsed/CPU = 1, **no core-count
speedup**. This ratio must be measured; idle/IO can make it false. Four node-h
cover setup/idle/meshing comparison-fit overhead, not a measured allowance.
1.19 is a tax stress scenario, not tax advice; EUR10 covers hypothetical other
charges. Failed attempts are charged, not permission to retry. Refined runs
are assumed twice primary cost. Eight public witness pairs charge both tools
at primary-equivalent cost; existing buyer licence has assumed zero incremental
licence fee. That assumption and EUR10 reserve may fail. Measure actual witness,
licence, fitting, storage, tax, transfer, censored/failed work and scheduling;
reduce allowed work or reject C2 if the complete bill exceeds EUR100.

High scenarios fall below EUR100 **only under these assumptions**. A small
panel is not a population bank or power proof; if T2 requires more designs,
reprice rather than shrinking the menu or weakening limits. Package/image,
deck/material/observer/panel pins and free-CPU triage need separate authority.
No recommendation here orders a CCX63 or allocates even free solver work.
