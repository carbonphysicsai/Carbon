## Evidence states and credibility

OBSERVED_PUBLIC means a named public development report at an immutable head,
not an independent rerun or accepted truth. OWNER_REPORTED means the framework
or owner message supplies the number without a complete ledger in this ticket.
ASSUMPTION means an elicited scenario for sensitivity, not a measured fact.
HUMAN_INPUT means adoption/acceptance remains pending. NOT_DEMONSTRATED means
required evidence is missing. Historical estimates, hypotheses and old fidelity
timings cannot supply current p50/p95 or a credibility tier.

All eight **target** Tier 2 within their stated simulation-design step;
**earned Tier 2 is NOT_DEMONSTRATED** here. Tier 1 requires the buyer's exact
tool/settings; Tier 3 is required for physical-use claims. A reduced scope
narrows the claim even when the target tier number remains 2. Until accepted
matched-tool evidence, say only that a development route is proposed or tested,
not that it agrees with an industry simulator. After accepted Tier 2 evidence:
“matches the reference simulator” in the named scope, never “matches reality”.

Reuse [#767 reference custody/decision agreement](../round1/reference-credibility.md):
independently held buyer-tool witnesses from a separate non-hidden draw of
the same task distribution or retired published cases, biased diagnostically
toward near-limit/decision-flipping regions. No EVAL/STRESS/quiz/tuning cases.
Compare pointwise errors AND feasibility verdicts, best picks/equivalent sets
or buyer-unit regret. Acceptance thresholds remain HUMAN_INPUT. A systematic
reference disagreement causes a prospective reference revision; sealed results
keep their original identity and are never silently rescored or blamed on a candidate.

These are **break-even targets, not timings**. Serial CPU=wall is an explicit
scenario, not a conversion valid for every package. CPU user+system sums all
ranks/children; node-hours include idle/setup/failure/refinement. Measure
effective throughput and memory-limited concurrency on the priced host. Never
divide CPU-h by core count and call the result a measured bill. A >2-CPU-h f13
curve alone does NOT prove a 16-design bank exceeds €100; its p95, wall,
witnesses and overhead decide that. Full finite 3D f06 with billion-cell fine
rungs is a likely cost/capacity failure, not a cost measured in this ticket.

C3 unit: if R is question windows/week and E permits windows per physical bank,
refresh work is `(R/E) × complete-bank cost`, not `(R/E) × one frequency/node`.
If the registered law instead defines R as physical case draws/week, use its
per-case cost. Label the law and denominator. Idle/retirement publication and
training rights do not make acquisition free. Reusing threshold questions
does not renew E. Include witnesses/reference revision amortization.
15% AX42 capacity uses measured AX42-equivalent process CPU and its usable
weekly capacity with availability/headroom; no vCPU/logical-thread equivalence
is presumed. Across eight, 8×15%=120%, so a per-row pass does NOT prove one
server fit. Separately sum bank startup, ongoing and validator capacity.

Each C1 receipt needs package/source/build/deck/observer/panel pins, hardware,
all attempted rows, complete-case definition, p50/p95/peak RAM, CPU/wall/
allocated time, failure/censoring, and controls/refinement/witness ledger.
