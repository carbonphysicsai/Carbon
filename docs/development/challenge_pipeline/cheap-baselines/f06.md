## Buyer decision and incumbent

Original decision: maximize finite-panel p10 coupling, retaining coupling>=0.30
and guided reflection<=0.10 for every required 9-vector x5-wavelength condition.
The conditional early-2D step sacrifices finite-width/lateral-alignment claims
and has lower stated buyer value. Compare original versus original, or an
owner-adopted early-2D version versus that version; never mix their outcomes.

Strong incumbent: **2D FDTD parameter optimizer**, using a declared ordinary
derivative-free search (or a separately pinned applicable adjoint route),
followed by charged independent 3D checks/optimization for the original job.
Use exact spectral/tolerance lookup for a fully covered finite mask menu.
A Bragg/effective-index estimate alone is a useful initializer, not the best
existing design workflow. No fine-3D grid replaced by a favorable scalar curve.

## Method basis and reuse

[Ansys's grating-coupler workflow](https://optics.ansys.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042305334-Grating-coupler)
explicitly uses initial 2D parameter optimization then 3D refinement and
S-parameter extraction. Its example says 10–20 min for the 2D optimization;
that is **vendor-example context**, not our measured cost or customer latency
requirement. Compact S-parameter reuse is available after its acquisition.
This is a stronger comparator than thousands of fresh 3D calls by assumption.

Meep/grating package, stack, PML, source, fiber overlap, geometry/tolerance,
normalization and convergence pins come from the acquisition owner. Exact
2D implementation, search and retained 3D package pins remain HUMAN_INPUT.
The old supermode coupler is not this baseline. Baseline and Carbon use the
same frozen action variables/query allowance; no arm secretly optimizes extra
fiber offsets or changes the material law.

## Witness and decision checks

Independent non-hidden whole-mask witnesses include band edges, pitch/etch
perturbations, low coupling, reflection boundaries and finite width/dy offsets
where 2D can misrank designs. Compare matched 3D pointwise coupling/reflection
AND feasible sets/best/equivalent picks and coupling-fraction regret. Check
port power and adaptive spectral resolution; no 2D pass certifies all 45
original conditions. Check all potentially deciding contenders, not just the
2D winner. Missing 3D evidence remains UNRESOLVED, not a candidate failure.

## Cost, stop condition and credibility

Complete current-task C1 and baseline p50/p95 are NOT_MEASURED here. Record
2D setup/search/spectrum and retained 3D witness/final-verification cost and
RAM separately. Fine 10-nm 3D bounds remain **~0.6–1.2 billion cells before
padding/PML** in the packet; no assumed fitting into available memory or
