## Method basis and reuse

[Chen's transfer-matrix/GA study](https://www.extrica.com/article/15234)
demonstrates inexpensive geometry optimization checked with FE/measurements;
its geometry/flow settings and measured attenuation do not transfer here.
[Dinex's industrial study](https://www.comsol.com/paper/download/36822/Andersen.pdf)
compares transfer/FE/measurement methods and motivates retaining chamber modes.
Select the applicable comparator on calibration data and documented support,
not a witness-tuned switch between cheap and full predictions.
