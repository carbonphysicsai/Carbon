## Method basis and reuse

[AMD supplies package thermal models](https://www.amd.com/en/support/downloads/adaptive-socs-and-fpgas/device-models/thermal-and-mechanical.html)
for device thermal workflows. This anchors an existing buyer-tool workflow,
not compact-ROM adequacy, coefficients or chip validity for Carbon's stack.
[ORNL's LAREL work](https://impact.ornl.gov/en/publications/larel-a-reliability-aware-framework-for-power-module-layout-optim/)
uses reduced thermal simulation/surrogates for transient power-module design;
its speed/accuracy findings are not Carbon measurements or a certified RC.
KEEP packet waveforms, energy integration and analytic controls.

Data Collection's REFERENCE-PACKAGES-01 owns exact Elmer/mesher/environment,
layer/contact/source/Robin, timestep and peak/crossing/steady-baseline pins.
Use that acquisition manifest, not a new solver build guessed in this spec.

remain HUMAN_INPUT until the comparator is separately implemented.

## Witness and decision checks

Hold out whole waveform/action panels, not sampled timesteps. Cover every
registered initial/cooling/split/family stratum, source events, second-pulse
tails, near-95-C contenders and supported off-grid durations/powers. A ROM
needs the top-surface peak, not just two patch-center temperatures. Reconstruct
event-aligned internal maxima/crossings; retain recovery against the correct
20-W steady baseline and non-recovery, rather than treating 0.5-s sample
spacing as a certified peak bound.

Report K/s pointwise/extremum/crossing errors, energy conservation, safety
verdicts, best/equivalent schedules and **joules foregone**. A warm/imbalanced
schedule incorrectly passing 95 C cannot be compensated by more joules.
The packet's 1-C unresolved-edge rule is preserved; no coarse-ROM self-check
becomes independently refined truth. New material/geometry/cooling laws refuse.

## Cost, stop condition and credibility

Owner-reported **2–3 CPU-min** is existing Elmer C1 context, not whole-bank
quantiles or ROM latency. Count impulse/acquisition/steady-baseline work,
reduction, schedule integration, optimizer and retained spatial/time checks.
One supported waveform response is an evaluation; each output time is not.
If the fixed-stack ROM already answers schedule questions adequately cheaply,
report no demonstrated V4 advantage rather than changing safety or stack.
Tier 2 against matched Icepak/Mechanical remains a target; hardware thermal
control/real-silicon safety requires separate experimental/deployment evidence.
