## Buyer decision and incumbent

Maximize worst-condition outlet mixing M, with M>=0.8, pressure drop<=250 Pa
and hydraulic residence<=20 s on the required 3-flow x3-diffusivity panel.
Keep finite inlet/outlet concentration history, raw flux-weighted variance
and the actual grooved fluid volume. No chemistry, reaction yield or assay
claim. The existing grammar already repeats; periodic-flow reuse does not
give the buyer new design freedom or higher assumed V2.

Strong ordinary comparator: **CFD-trained RBF/response surface** for full-panel
mixing and pressure/residence outputs, selected/frozen on fit data and paired
with the same geometric optimizer. CLOSED_BANK is exact complete-panel lookup.
Additional physical comparator: cache each geometry/flow velocity field and
reuse it across diffusivities; a supported finite advection-diffusion ROM or
transfer operator may reuse the repeated flow unit. Preserve entry/exit and
finite scalar evolution. Periodic velocity never means periodic concentration
or a perfectly remixed inlet at each cell.

## Method basis and reuse

fits RBF response surfaces to CFD and optimizes mixing/pressure. It is an
ordinary existing competitor, not thousands of separately solved genetic
algorithm individuals or our finite-panel qualification. KEEP the packet's
smooth-channel/zero-D controls and outlet observer. The

baseline spec. Acquisition owns OpenFOAM/mesher/flow/scalar/extractor pins;

## Witness and decision checks

Hold out entire groove geometries and all nine conditions together. Separate
public/retired witnesses cover maximum Pe, groove/reversal extremes, pressure/
residence edges and near-M=0.8 contenders. Evaluate positive axial-flux weighted
mean/variance, raw c, solute/volume conservation, backflow refusal and spatial
refinement. An interpolator cannot certify concentration boundedness just by
predicting M. A scalar ROM must not add artificial diffusion to improve it.
Compare against a separately supported resolved finite scalar route; smooth
fields and coincidentally matching M alone do not establish transport adequacy.

Within covered laminar conditions, test rather than assume Stokes flow scaling;
finite inertia/entry effects can invalidate it. Preserve all missing flow and
scalar evidence. Report field/observable errors, feasible sets and selected
geometry, **M regret**, pressure/residence margins and unresolved coverage.
No clamp of negative M or favorable removal of high-Pe failures.
