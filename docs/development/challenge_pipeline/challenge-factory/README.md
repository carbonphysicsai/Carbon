# Challenge factory — proposed family reuse and early winnability

CHALLENGE-FACTORY-PLAN-01 / PKT-08, **SPECIFIED; design only**. The factory
should reduce repeated engineering, not lower the evidence bar or manufacture
a harder job just so a surrogate wins. Supply is already ample: discovery
screened 100 ideas and admitted **zero**. The costly mistake is fully onboarding
a buyer question that a cheap method already answers.

## Proposed order

Source-confirmed buyer job and rights → smallest applicable reference/observer
proof → **public winnability pilot** → full ten-section packet and the existing
S0–S10 onboarding stages. Existing public packets/panels may be reused as pilot
inputs; do not throw them away or repeat their solves. The pilot is an early
portfolio filter, **not a new accepted stage**, not a hidden bank and not S10's
independent value evidence. No stage map, runtime ID or family queue is changed.

The [pilot specification](winnability-pilot.md) compares the strongest cheap
method plus a few reference verifications, a plain L0 MLP, and solver alone.
It asks separately: useful learnability, verified decision advantage over the
cheap method, and room for miners to improve. Failed learnability or advantage
parks a **proposal**, subject to owner-set thresholds. Missing evidence is
indeterminate, not failure. [Current-portfolio audit](retrospective.md): none
of the four has demonstrated a pass on this complete matched-budget pilot.

## Seven kits, not seven verified foundations

| Family page | First reusable basis | What cannot be transferred by name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Heat conduction / Elmer](families/heat.md) | Source-locked build, narrowly accepted transient MMS | Layer/interface/peak observers and any new material or nonlinear model |
| [Electrochemistry / PyBaMM](families/electrochemistry.md) | Locked overlay, accepted particle/heat checks | Full DFN ageing/plating and customer cell calibration |

| [Structures / CalculiX](families/structures.md) | Source-locked modal build, prepared verification routes | Nonlinear contact, hyperelasticity, shell buckling, cure and process history |
| [Acoustics / Elmer Helmholtz](families/acoustics.md) | Same Elmer build, saved-integral/port checks | Heat MMS does not verify Helmholtz, radiation or all-mode power |
| [CFD / OpenFOAM](families/cfd.md) | Cell package and scalar/flow draft routes | Different releases/PDEs, turbulence, wall treatment or conjugate transfer |
| [Photonics / Meep](families/photonics.md) | Pin/build specification and analytic routes | No blanket verified image, 2D→3D or vacuum→dielectric transfer |

A kit release is a **proposed versioned bundle**: image/source/compiler pins,
native code-verification receipt and applicability, material/closure policy,
law→minimal-output template, verified observers, complete public research kit,
strong cheap comparator and equal-budget adapter. Cache only exact applicable
identities. Image-store availability is UNKNOWN until an operator inspects it;
a stored Dockerfile or old receipt does not prove the image is present today.
Rebuilds/re-pins require new applicable checks and acceptance.

The minimal-output design follows the **proposal** in PHYSICS_CONTRACT_01
§3.3: laws first; independently predicted balance terms, compact integrals and
registered probes; no full-field shipping by default. Solver MMS does not verify
an observer. More outputs are not free: extraction, storage, miner prediction
and any extra excitation/verification launches go in the ledger. The source
draft's output migration remains HUMAN_INPUT; it is not adopted here.

## Candidate priorities and speed measurement

[Ten candidates](candidates.md) are conditional **pilot priorities**, not an
admitted or launch portfolio. The planning index is winnability hypothesis ×
conditional buyer decision value ÷ estimated active engineering days. All
advantage hypotheses remain unproved; their honest incremental-value floor is
zero. Ranking is not a cross-Challenge scientific score, revenue or ROI.

[Timing baseline and target](timing.md) distinguishes measured artifact arrival
gaps from the recorded subsecond **TOOL** rehearsal and missing human effort.
There is no measured full-onboarding labour baseline or earned speed-up yet.
Use #1127's append-only stage/effort records; no second timing implementation.

## Work allocation, without opening work or spending

1. Packets prepares reusable kit templates and pilot registrations, one bounded
   authorized ticket at a time. This PR only plans them.
2. Data Collection owns pinned-image availability, native applicability,
   reference/observer proofs, acquisition and complete measured C1–C4 costs.
3. Optimizer owns equal-budget scoring/power implementation and arm execution.
4. Carbon Validator/science owns validity, law applicability and reference
   acceptance. Test Lead proposes decision-value/competition thresholds.
5. Owner chooses pilot thresholds/caps, customer limits and rights, any later
   dispatch, adoption and launch. A plan is not an execution grant.

Independent inputs can be prepared in parallel when separately assigned, but
there is one source of truth per family/observer and one PR per ticket. Respect
open lane ownership; do not duplicate pending acceptance or physics-contract
work. Full onboarding retains training-budget, security, contamination,
exposure, cheap-baseline, power and independent confirmation requirements.

No solver or training runs, spend, private data, full cold plate, protected
cases, EV5, sealed journal 14 or live contract changes. Current safety limits
stay hard. All scientific, security, commercial and production qualification
states are unchanged. Sources and exact reuse cautions: [sources.md](sources.md).
