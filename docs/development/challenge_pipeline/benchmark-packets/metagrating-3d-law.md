## P: one fabricated device for one buyer brief

Draw a nominal design specification, complete service scenarios and buyer
requirement values within approved material/reference support. One question
asks for **one fixed full 3D mask and thickness** satisfying all conditions,
with maximum desired-order efficiency, or settled NONE_FEASIBLE for its bank.
Choose Px/Py from the nominal brief once; keep physical periods unchanged for
all wavelength/incidence scenarios. Normal source incidence, 50° outgoing angle
and 1050 nm are public-anchor constants, not P. Dispersion/loss/manufacturing
support requires a material/process law; an ideal source index is insufficient.

Recommend a continuous-requirement primary proposal and endpoint/midpoint grid
as an audit baseline after supported efficiency, reflection, parasitic-order
and feature intervals are accepted. Their ranges/densities/correlations remain
HUMAN_INPUT. Reusing solved curves for new thresholds adds no solves only
inside recorded physical support; new wavelength/bias/incidence needs truth.
Published masks and equivalents are excluded from P, not merely Q.

## Q: diagnostic proposal, separate from P

Recommend supported fabrication/operating corners, order-cutoff regimes,
near-limit desired efficiency/reflection/leakage, mask-library failures and
rank/tie transitions. Stratum allocation, Q density and rejection rates stay
open. A physically unsupported grazing-order setting is not a harder exam
case; it is reference inadequacy/UNRESOLVED. Published/copy controls are
non-scoring reference diagnostics. No redraw of valid NONE_FEASIBLE outcomes.

## w: use and evidence weighting

Buyer optical usage or robust-service priorities determine w; mean versus
worst desired efficiency remains HUMAN_INPUT. Every mandatory condition's
limits are enforced before ranking, regardless of weight. Efficiency-point
regret is proposed, not euros fabricated from power. Distinguish target P,
diagnostic Q and estimation weights; a balanced witness set does not establish
a uniformly used device population.

## Full 3D action grammar and neutral tasks

Actions are accepted physical binary x/y patterns plus finite thickness,
fixed periods and material laws. Register minimum features, connectivity if
required, fabrication offsets, physical precision and boundary designs.
Pixel resampling is not a new design; scalar mask DOFs do not make fields 2D.
Stripes are a legitimate comparator/control, not a silent replacement family.
The action count m, questions per batch k, independent units n, exposure E
and bank size B all remain null. Source pixel dimensions are not m or k.

## Two-band frontier, diversity and refinement

T2(a) working rule is five distinct feasible and five distinct infeasible
designs, each within **two accepted refinement bands**, per mandatory stratum/
question family. Widths and applicable quantities remain HUMAN_INPUT. Resolve
more frontier actions, not a wider band or a narrowed menu to raise pass rate.
Full-service feasibility, meaningful buyer-unit margins and changing best
answers are required in addition; overall pass rate is reported, not gated.

Report each law variant's distinct winners, best-pick frequency, feasible/
NONE_FEASIBLE mix, unresolved/abstention rate and fine-grid/refinement rate.
Expected winner diversity is NOT_DEMONSTRATED. Under an explicitly independent
draw law, estimate sum_i [1-(1-p_i)^k] from approved held-out winner probabilities;
it is bounded by min(k, feasible winner designs). A large continuous interval
does not create more answers than the bank supports. NONE_FEASIBLE is a separate
verdict, not a physical winner. Close calls require interval/decision checks,
not arbitrary precision in requirement numbers.

Refine spatial resolution, decay/time/PML and RCWA harmonics independently.
If desired/parasitic power intervals touch a limit or a ranking tie, use a
registered full 3D witness/rung or report UNRESOLVED. Order-cutoff and energy-
balance uncertainty cannot be clipped into feasibility. Refined truth for
edges stays separate from normal-screen estimates; no automatic accepted tier.

NONE_FEASIBLE is valuable when a fully resolved bank proves no offered design
meets the complete brief. Forward it without redraw. One unresolved action,
missing reference/novelty support or infrastructure failure blocks that verdict.
Never lower a buyer safety/manufacturing limit just to create a feasible pick.

E/B/n and B = 10n planning applicability are HUMAN_INPUT. Count complete bank
units and all normalization/refinement/witness launches separately. Threshold
variation does not renew E. Cross-window power uses actual reuse/dependence,
not k times windows as independent fresh truth by declaration.

published-mask/RCWA/direct-search workflow. Report (a) valid-domain coverage,
(b) near-limit decisions, (c) best pick/regret and false-feasible rate,
(d) strata/diversity/unresolved coverage, (e) held-out cross-tool, energy and
cheap-baseline robustness. Score use, power and acceptance belong to Test Lead.
