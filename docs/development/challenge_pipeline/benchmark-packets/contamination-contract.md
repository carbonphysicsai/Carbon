## Catalogue and role separation

Freeze a versioned, rights-audited source catalogue before candidate outcomes:
source revision, original geometry/material/conditions, measured outputs,
known transformations and reconstructability/coverage gaps. The complete
catalogue includes all publicly reconstructable derivatives, tutorial cases
and designs, not only a hand-picked anchor. Incomplete entries are coverage
gaps, not proof that a proposed case is clean. Declare catalogue coverage and
the resulting restricted support; do not claim universal decontamination.

Separate four roles in manifests and stores:

1. **PUBLIC_REFERENCE_ANCHOR:** published benchmark; solver verification,
   validation or diagnostic only. Never a scored question, EVAL/STRESS action
   or hidden bank item. Numerical anchor agreement does not validate an entire
   buyer family or hardware.
2. **PUBLIC_EQUIVALENCE_CONTROL:** deliberately remeshed/resampled/scaled copies
   used to test the exclusion implementation. Also never scored; must be
   recognized as excluded rather than counted as novel.
3. **PUBLIC_NOVEL_WITNESS:** separately drawn non-hidden task witness after
   approved support, catalogue and novelty rules exist; matched independent
   tool comparison outside producer custody. Alternatively use retired,
   published cases with a verified release receipt, never active hidden cases.
4. **FUTURE_PROTECTED_TASK:** operator-only, later authorized role. This ticket
   creates none. The exclusion must be applied before bank construction.

Retirement alone is not release. No hidden EVAL, STRESS, quiz or tuning case,
seed, label, fingerprint, realized distance or reconstruction-sensitive ID
belongs in public witnesses, miners, pods or these documents. Publish metric
definitions and support/exclusion rules; keep future realized hidden values
within the registered disclosure boundary.

## Family-specific comparison, not byte distance

Step flow: nondimensional wall contour on an approved x/H window; expansion
ratio and geometric features; Re_H/Mach and developed inlet-profile descriptors.
Metagrating: periodic binary occupancy on physical normalized coordinates;
periods/wavelength, thickness/wavelength, complex material law, incidence,
polarization and order conventions. Details are in each packet §4.

Compare each proposed scored case to **every complete catalogue entry** under
the accepted physical equivalence group. Register extraction, quadrature,
descriptor normalization, minimum-distance reduction, group action and
acceptance predicate. Report components separately. Recommend a mandatory
geometry-novelty component as well as supported condition novelty: moving only
Re, wavelength or thickness must not hide a copied published design. Whether
any particular variant is sufficiently far remains HUMAN_INPUT.

Remeshing, resampling, coordinate relabeling or physically equivalent scaling
cannot supply novelty. Scaling is equivalent only with the same nondimensional
physics: changing a dispersive material or Reynolds law may break it. Symmetry
is allowed only if it preserves the excitation and desired output; a mirror
that swaps the wanted diffraction order is not automatically equivalent.
These are recommendations for a frozen group, not a selected acceptance rule.

Freeze catalogue, law, generator, descriptors, group, thresholds and reference/
measurement identity before the study. An unknown catalogue comparison or
unaccepted rule is **UNRESOLVED**, never an exclusion pass. Invalid geometry,
unsupported physics, reference failure and infrastructure failure stay typed.
They do not become candidate failure or NONE_FEASIBLE.

## Population, exposure and decision value

Define the exclusion in P's support explicitly. Q's diagnostic enrichment and
rejection rates must be recorded separately; importance weighting cannot
repair zero Q support. Distinct seeds or file hashes prove neither physical
novelty nor independent evidence. Threshold-only questions reuse bank evidence
and consume the same exposure E; they are not fresh cases.

Novelty alone does not prove a valuable task. On an independent public witness
bank, report per-stratum contested boundaries (five feasible and five infeasible
within two accepted refinement bands, Test Lead working rule), full-scenario
feasibility, buyer-unit margin spread, changing picks and strongest-cheap-method
agreement/regret. Freeze limit, objective, aggregation, tie and uncertainty
rules before truth access. Pass-rate is descriptive, not the old 20–80% gate.

Hold out complete designs/geometry families and complete scenario bundles.
Random rows from a single solved design are not independent held-out evidence.
Baseline setup, queries, reference calls, refinements, failed attempts, training
and final verification all count under the same registered budget. Lookup and
published formulas remain legitimate; do not weaken competitors to win V4.

Report both pointwise and decision agreement, resolved denominators, abstention
and unresolved coverage. Compare picks in buyer units under each reference;
an infeasible pick is a feasibility disagreement, not a finite regret. Empty
or two-unresolved comparisons do not count as agreement. A confidence interval
requires a registered independent unit/dependence model, still HUMAN_INPUT.

## Findings and prospective revisions

Predeclare an anchor-versus-novel-variant diagnostic, controlling difficulty,
reference/model-form and representation differences; power, false-alarm and
disposition thresholds are HUMAN_INPUT. An anomaly means investigate, **not
misconduct**, and cannot introduce an unregistered score penalty.

A systematic cross-tool gap is a reference finding, not candidate failure.
Revise the reference for future batches with a new identity and supported scope.
Already-sealed evidence keeps its original reference and is never silently
rescored. Catalogue/law revisions are prospective too. General PDE or method
knowledge is legitimate learning, not inherently contamination.
