## 1. Buyer decision and task semantics

The action is one cell-specific, two-stage `(c1, c2)` charge protocol selected **before** independent reference verification. `c1` and `c2` are C-rates. The current packet bounds are `c1 ∈ [0.5, 2.0] C` and `c2 ∈ [0.2, 1.0] C`; its pinned programme begins with 120 s rest, applies c1 until 4.0 V, c2 until 4.2 V, then CV to C/20, with the specified rest/discharge/rest and 30-cycle sequence. These are the **current prospective packet values**, not a frozen optimizer grid. A revised action range or thermal action must be represented as an explicit, prospectively versioned variable and permission, not silently encoded as a new lattice point. Discrete lattice membership, any continuous parameter, step sizes, and final count are `HUMAN_INPUT` pending the revision. [Packet §§1–2, 4](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

One protocol must work across the packet's five mock service strata at initial SOC 0.10: winter start 5 °C, cool morning 15 °C, conditioned depot 25 °C, warm repeat use 35 °C, and heatwave parking 40 °C. Cell identity, initial state, and ageing history are pinned conditions, never optimizer actions. The 25/35 °C strata carry the 10–80% session-time goal; **all five** carry thermal, plating, voltage, and capacity limits. Initial SOC 0.30/0.50 are diagnostic cases, not substitutes for the 0.10 turnaround decision. Synthetic equal stratum mass is not a measured fleet frequency or an official scoring weight. Any additional cell, ambient, history, or SOC task population is `HUMAN_INPUT` and requires a separate qualified measurement/decision contract. [Packet §§2–4](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

For a candidate protocol, define `T25` and `T35` as cycle-1 10–80% SOC elapsed minutes **from session start**, including the 120 s rest. The primary buyer objective is minimize `max(T25, T35)` among protocols satisfying every mandatory limit. The packet's acceptance order then uses lower diagnostic 30-cycle capacity loss `(Q1−Q30)/Q1`, followed by frozen protocol order. The session-time ceiling is 30 min in each warm stratum. An 80% crossing must be observed from the pinned current integral and capacity basis; terminal-voltage crossing or a clipped first-hour trace cannot stand in for it. `NOT_REACHED` is an infeasible time outcome only when the complete first-charge horizon is validly observed. [Packet §§1, 6](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

Hard limits are: maximum model cell temperature ≤45 °C throughout the **whole** declared programme; minimum model lithium-plating reaction overpotential ≥0 V over **every** charging episode; terminal voltage ≤4.2 V under the pinned termination semantics; `Q30/Q1 ≥0.99`; and the warm 30-min time goal. A failure of any mandatory condition makes a protocol infeasible irrespective of faster time or lower capacity loss. The 0-V model criterion is not experimental proof that a real cell does not plate. If no candidate is independently verified feasible, the buyer-facing result is `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_PROTOCOL` with the unresolved/failed reasons and the declared search space; the optimizer must not pick a merely least-violating protocol and call it safe. A slower cold-specific alternative may be reported as a separate investigation, never as evidence that the one-protocol five-stratum decision passed. [Packet §§1, 3, 5–6](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

The packet sets **successive-refinement comparison targets**, not certified physical uncertainty bands: ≤10 mV voltage, ≤0.5 °C temperature, ≤2 mV plating minimum, ≤0.25% capacity relative to Q1, and ≤30 s SOC-crossing time. `HUMAN_INPUT`: the scientific owner must register actual uncertainty intervals/band semantics for each prospective observable, including spatial/time coverage, before a near-boundary reference can be called feasible. Recommendation: construct the interval from a pinned paired-refinement and observer study and use its conservative endpoint at each hard limit; do not substitute the comparison target as a universal uncertainty bound. Reference intervals intersecting a limit remain `UNRESOLVED` until refined; reference solver/observer failure is `REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, never a candidate's physical failure. [Packet §§5–6](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [constitutional measurement and reference doctrine](../../../../docs/context/SCIENTIFIC_REFERENCE_CANON_V4_MASTER.md).

This is a constrained worst-warm-time decision, with capacity loss as a lexicographic secondary objective, **not** a price-weighted thermal/plating Pareto trade. No soft objective may compensate for a mandatory limit. `HUMAN_INPUT`: specify the numerical tie equality/resolution after the SOC-time and capacity observers are qualified; recommendation is to compare the reported values at their registered measurement resolution, then use increasing `c1`, increasing `c2`, and a stable candidate ID, extending this order explicitly for any new action. This preserves the packet's frozen protocol order without asserting precision the measurement does not have. [Packet §6](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

## 2. Fixed optimizer and budget — conditional registration

**Working class (a): exhaustive lattice.** This is justified if the revised action space remains a modest, discrete, fully enumerable protocol set: a complete model sweep makes abstention meaningful and the same rule can be replayed without initialisation sensitivity. The prior Q3 diagnostic used `13 × 9 = 117` points (`c1` increments 0.125 C, `c2` increments 0.1 C), but that grid was chosen for an earlier decision and is **not** adopted here. Its reference verdict changed in 2 of 52 judged model decisions under grid refinement, both at a band edge; this establishes a sensitivity to resolution, not adequacy for the new buyer job. [Grid diagnostic](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/grid-resolution-v1/README.md).

The proposed deterministic procedure is: enumerate every registered candidate in frozen order; query the same model for each candidate at all five pinned conditions, with the prospective 30-cycle outputs and timing observer; reject invalid/nonfinite/incomplete predictions; retain candidates predicted to satisfy every mandatory condition; minimize the stated lexicographic objective; return a committed pick or `NONE_FEASIBLE_PREDICTED`. The producer fixes the candidate list, condition order, code/configuration digests, model/runtime identity, tie rule, and any random material before model predictions or reference outcomes. Exhaustive enumeration uses no seed; any hidden task selection, secondary randomized-start audit, or randomized ordering draws from Carbon's producer, never from miner input. The same frozen optimizer and winning model may be used for an **offline** customer shortlist, subject to independent verification. The existing Challenge-neutral [SearchAdapter/freezing and commitment pattern](../../../../carbon/design_search/experiment.py) and [Battery adapter](../../../../carbon/battery/value/design_search_adapter.py) are KEEP/WRAP references; their present objective is **time to CV onset**, so they do not implement this 10–80% SOC buyer decision. The task's cited `carbon/design_search/tasks.py` is absent at this checkout; any future interface extension must be Challenge-neutral and versioned.

**Budget field to register:** `B_model = 5 × N_final` full trajectory/observer calls per task, where `N_final` is the approved unique candidate count after the action revision. No early stop may claim exhaustive coverage. `HUMAN_INPUT`: `N_final` and validator inference cap. Recommendation: first benchmark a 100-protocol, 500-call panel because the packet separately states unmeasured targets of 100 protocols × 5 strata ≤50 s and warm-trajectory p95 ≤0.10 s. This is a **resource planning benchmark**, not approval to cap or coarsen the physical action set or claim a feasible protocol. The historical 117-point panel would take 585 model calls; 585 × 0.10 s = 58.5 s is only a nominal per-call illustration. A p95 latency is not a per-call ceiling or a batch-time proof, and overhead still matters. If the final grid is larger or slower, revise the compute/latency target with evidence or select a newly registered optimizer class. A revised thermal or other action may make the space mixed/continuous, in which case class (a), `N_final`, and the budget must be revisited before registration. The bounded alternative to study is class (b), producer-seeded multi-start local search snapped to a versioned scoreable bank; no such switch is authorized by this draft. [Packet §§2, 8](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [grid diagnostic](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/grid-resolution-v1/README.md).

## 3. Independent verification and reference cost

For each hidden task identity, the producer should pre-solve the **entire approved candidate × condition bank**, settle band-edge points with pinned refined solves, and commit the bank before any model pick is judged. This permits reference-defined feasible best-in-bank regret in minutes, including the opportunity cost of over-caution and correct abstention when truly none is feasible. The chosen protocol's five conditions are independently verified from that same committed bank. The candidate's pick, the bank optimum, and `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_PROTOCOL` are all relative to the registered finite space; none is a global physical optimum. Producer reference results, official seeds, and sensitive IDs remain out of miner/public surfaces. [Packet §§4–6](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [quiz registry v8](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-registry-v8.json); [invariants](../../../../.agent/INVARIANTS.md).

The historical Q3 stand-in cost was 35 reference solves at median 82 CPU-s, about 0.8 CPU-h per scenario. Its later 117-point, `k=8` Q3 cost ruling was approximately 21 CPU-h per batch on the then-current VM. Both used the earlier output/decision semantics. A simple 117 × 5 × 82 s = **13.3 CPU-h** is arithmetic for that old median only; it is **not** a measured cost for a revised protocol, the needed SOC-time observer, all-cycle extrema, or new near-limit refinements. `HUMAN_INPUT`: measure `C_standard` and `C_refined` CPU-s, refinement fraction, cache reuse, parallel wall time, and producer task-bank capacity on the exact pinned 30-cycle/reference/observer configuration. Recommended cost model: `N_final × 5 × C_standard + N_refined × C_refined_incremental` CPU-s for a five-condition bank, amortized across models only when task/reference identities truly match. Do not spend on an unbounded hidden bank to support a feasibility claim before reference adequacy is demonstrated. [Quiz evidence](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/README.md); [Q3 cost ruling](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-diagnostics-v1.json); [packet §5](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

`NOT_DEMONSTRATED`: a measured feasible protocol for the new 30-min/all-five-limit decision; full-programme temperature/voltage and every-charge plating observers; SOC-crossing measurement qualification; real-cell parameter adequacy; decisive reference uncertainty at all hard limits; validator cadence fit. Current checkpoint diagnostics and first-cycle plating extraction cannot silently satisfy the stronger buyer scope. If every bank candidate is reference infeasible, the correct answer is abstention, and a design-regret separator for feasible opportunities cannot be claimed from that bank. [Packet §§5–6, 10](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

## 4. Gaming-resistance and power handoff to Test Lead

The Test Lead owns the scoring rule, attack construction, hidden bank, statistical separation target, and acceptance threshold. This buyer specification supplies the required **behavioural threats** and observables: (1) an edge optimist predicting a genuinely infeasible thermal/plating boundary point as just feasible; (2) an over-cautious model rejecting genuinely feasible fast protocols and incurring extra minutes or abstention; (3) a sign-error model that flips or smooths the localized negative plating minimum; and (4) an optimizer-aware model accurate on the frozen search path but wrong in independent task/optimizer probes. Report hard-limit failures separately from feasible regret in **minutes** and capacity-loss tie outcomes. Do not turn the packet's hypothetical dollar values into scientific score. [Packet §§1, 6, 9](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [historical Q3 findings](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/README.md); [v8 corrected edge control](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-registry-v8.json).

A producer-seeded, different registered optimizer can audit agreement on a precommitted subset, but it is a sensitivity diagnostic until Test Lead registers its role and budget. Historical OPT-B was an eight-restart continuous local search snapped to the old lattice with producer-controlled seed variants; copying its five historical seeds or exposing protected starts would make the audit predictable. Judge any pick only by independent reference outcome, never by optimizer agreement or model prediction alone. The existing [diagnostics registry](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-diagnostics-v1.json) says the optimizer-stability, grid-resolution, power, decision-value agreement, and unresolved-rate diagnostics are reported, not gates. The [v8 rule](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-registry-v8.json) refines near-limit DEVELOPMENT truth; residual `UNRESOLVED` is reported and gets no clean-feasible credit, with Test Lead's pessimistic backstop where the registered scoring contract requires it. This draft does not set a new pessimistic penalty.

`HUMAN_INPUT` / **blocked:** hidden design-task count per batch and a defensible separation claim after the revised space is frozen. Recommendation: begin a stratified pilot at `k=8` tasks only as a historical Q3 comparability point, with predeclared warm/cold, SOC/history and near-limit strata where scientifically legitimate; then have Test Lead size `k` from behaviour-defined bad/good controls, power/confidence criteria, unresolved rates, and the **new** reference-cost measurement. The old Q3 `k=8` result and 21 CPU-h cost do not transfer to this stronger five-condition decision. Under the packet's current single fixed cell, SOC 0.10 and five-stratum panel there may be only one distinct buyer task; extra seeds of the same panel are not independent examples. A varied hidden bank therefore needs a prospective population/condition decision rather than silently treating correlated cycles, time probes, or lattice points as tasks. [Packet §§3, 9](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [Q3 evidence](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/README.md); [diagnostics registry](../../evidence/battery-quiz-designs/quiz-diagnostics-v1.json).

## 5. Customer-facing offline tool and remaining decisions

Inputs: pinned simulated-cell/model identity, allowed protocol/action version, initial SOC and five service conditions, full 30-cycle programme, objective/limit contract, reference/observer version, and explicit uncertainty/provenance. Outputs: ordered model-predicted protocol shortlist, each stratum's predicted 10–80% time and hard-limit margins, independently verified reference status for selected protocols, capacity-loss diagnostic, unresolved reasons, and `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_PROTOCOL` if necessary. It supports a cell-calibration engineer's offline investigation and replaces first-pass DFN protocol sweeps only. Independent DFN confirmation, physical cycling, pack integration, charger/BMS control and release acceptance remain separate. The packet's **unmeasured** targets are p95 ≤0.10 s per warm trajectory on a pinned CPU, 100 protocols ×5 strata ≤50 s inference, and shortlist within 10 min including overhead. A final grid >100 protocols or a new action requires remeasuring these targets, not claiming the old timing. [Packet §§7–10](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md).

Before registration, obtain: (a) approved revised action variables, bounds, types and finite candidate set or a newly justified class; (b) qualified SOC crossing and full-programme observers with reference intervals; (c) a measured feasible/infeasible bank and cost/cadence study; (d) the Test Lead's separate scoring/attack/power contract and legitimate hidden task population; and (e) explicit scientific/product qualification before any real-cell or operational claim. All are `HUMAN_INPUT` or `NOT_DEMONSTRATED` as stated above. The existing [battery design-search adapter](../../../../carbon/battery/value/design_search_adapter.py) is a useful commitment and neutrality pattern, not an implementation of this buyer requirement. [Packet §10](../round1/battery-ev-fast-charge.md); [constitution](../../../../CONSTITUTION.md).
