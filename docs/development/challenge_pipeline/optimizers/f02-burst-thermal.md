# f02 — Carbon-owned burst schedule optimizer (DEVELOPMENT proposal)

**Source of selected requirements:** [f02 customer packet](../round1/f02-burst-thermal.md), [round-one sheet](../round1/requirements.json), and [round-one authority](../../../../.agent/decisions/2026-10-06-OWNER-PORTFOLIO-DEV-ROUND-01.md). These describe a synthetic development job, not a qualified chip or a deployment population. This file specifies the buyer's decision and proposes a fixed optimizer. It registers no grader, protected pool, reference campaign, or customer product. Elmer reference adequacy is **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**.

## Buyer decision and task

For a known cooling pair, initial temperature, heat split, and waveform family, choose **one** peak-power/on-time schedule, or return `NONE_FEASIBLE`. The action is discrete: peak power ∈ {80, 110, 140} W and duration ∈ {5, 10, 20} s, nine actions per scenario. The family is an exogenous condition, not a variable Carbon may change. The 24 scenario strata are the Cartesian product of coolant/convection {(30 °C, 2,500 W/m²K), (40 °C, 1,500 W/m²K)}, initial temperature {40, 55} °C, patch split {50/50, 80/20}, and waveform {rectangular, ramp, two-pulse}. `P_dev,decision` weights those strata equally; that is an experimental law, not customer frequency. The fixed stack, 20 W base, 20 s burst start, two-pulse gap, and 120 s horizon are as in the packet.

Maximize **extra delivered energy in joules**, the integral of declared power minus the 20 W base: `(peak − 20) × D` for rectangle and two-pulse, `(peak − 20) × D / 2` for ramp. This is the selected buyer objective; no throughput or reliability proxy is substituted. A design is feasible only if the reference top-die **spatial and temporal maximum** is ≤95 °C throughout 120 s. The packet's ±1 °C boundary region is `UNRESOLVED` pending refined truth; sample-only peaks are insufficient. There is no secondary objective in the packet. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended exact-tie order: greater energy, then lower predicted peak temperature, then lower peak power, then shorter duration. The latter terms break only equal-energy ties and do not change the primary objective. The optimizer may provisionally return `NONE_FEASIBLE` when all nine model predictions violate the limit; the test judge can confirm that answer only if every action has adequate reference evidence of infeasibility. Missing or unresolved reference rows instead make the judged task `UNRESOLVED`.

## Fixed optimizer and cost envelope

**Class (a), exhaustive lattice.** The nine declared actions are small enough to evaluate without search luck. Carbon fixes the nine-action manifest, model-call order, feasibility rule, exact-tie order, and version digest before inspecting model outputs. Each model receives **nine schedule predictions per task** (model evaluation budget: nine trajectory calls, each covering the required time/space observer). A Carbon producer derives any randomized hidden scenario ordering or audit starts; miners supply neither seeds nor candidate ordering. The optimizer ranks only predicted-feasible actions by extra joules and commits the action or abstention before any protected reference is read. The Test Lead may preregister a second, different optimizer for an agreement diagnostic; that diagnostic cannot silently replace this fixed buyer rule.

Truth verification can use a pre-solved nine-action bank per scenario, with independent mesh/time refinement on every action close to 95 °C or whose uncertainty could change the optimum or the `NONE_FEASIBLE` decision. The selected first feasibility allowance is **50 launches, six allocated node-hours, USD 20**, including controls, baselines, and refinements; it is not a measured per-design cost. One complete 24-stratum bank needs at least **216 primary transient solves** before controls/refinements, so this allowance cannot produce it. **Reference cost per evaluated design: NOT_MEASURED; producer cost per complete nine-action task: NOT_MEASURED and at least nine primary transient solves plus necessary refinements.** `HUMAN_INPUT` is required for a timed, quoted, separately authorized bank-production budget and a task cadence after the Elmer deck passes its controls. A proposal to cache identical pinned references is permissible; charging must include mesh, setup, failed runs, extraction, storage, and refinement. No interpolation or one-node RC screen may turn into truth.

## Verification and gaming study for the Test Lead

The Test Lead owns scoring and attack registration. Proposed decision report: reference-feasible optimum energy, committed-design reference peak, extra joules, feasibility state, and physical regret `optimum joules − chosen joules` for a feasible choice. An infeasible choice reports its temperature excess in °C separately; over-cautious abstention or a lower-energy feasible choice retains lost joules. When no action is genuinely feasible, a correct `NONE_FEASIBLE` has no opportunity regret. Do not invent a compensating scalar between joules and °C, and do not count a reference failure as model failure.

Precommit the bank and refine limit-adjacent/crossing cases using the packet's mesh/time checks. An optimizer-aware control should be accurate on the nine expected actions but wrong on held-out condition/family combinations or Carbon-derived audit orderings; evaluate its *committed decision* on independent reference truth. Include edge-optimist, over-cautious, localized sign-error, and optimizer-aware constructions. A second preregistered optimizer or alternate candidate order can expose search-path dependence, but exhaustive evaluation makes candidate coverage identical. For close calls, refine truth first; if still unresolved, the Test Lead's prospective pessimistic rule must refuse a favorable safe/zero-regret conclusion rather than manufacture a failure or success.

**Power/sizing proposal, not a qualification result:** the Test Lead should define behaviour-based good/bad models before selecting hidden tasks. The 24 exogenous strata are correlated conditions of the packet's current single package, **not 24 independent buyer jobs**. One pass over those 24 conditions is a within-job coverage diagnostic, not a 24-task power pilot. The [packet's prospective job population](../round1/f02-burst-thermal.md) sketches different package/cooling/workload briefs and an eight-brief scoping inventory; its `P_job`, protected `Q_job`, eligible support and hidden `n` remain `HUMAN_INPUT`. On independently specified, reference-covered jobs, estimate separation of infeasible picks, missed-feasible outcomes and joule regret by job-level analysis with shared packages clustered. The smallest batch must meet a separately approved discrimination target. Budget and power remain `NOT_DEMONSTRATED` until job-level reference banks, cost and model panels exist. Keep warm/55 °C, 80/20 and all three waveforms inside each applicable job; the six RC-selected boundary probes remain diagnostics.

## Customer-facing form and open values

Inputs: cooling regime, initial temperature, patch split, and permitted waveform family under the exact synthetic stack. Outputs: selected peak, duration, extra joules, predicted spatial peak and time, margin/uncertainty, and `NONE_FEASIBLE` or `UNRESOLVED` with reasons. This could replace manually trying the nine schedules in a design study; it is **not** an actuator or real-chip safety controller. The packet supplies no deployment latency or measured model runtime. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended **≤1 minute per nine-action proposal** on the buyer's agreed hardware, because a nine-point offline engineering choice should be interactive; benchmark it rather than treating it as achieved. Human acceptance of this latency, real thermal stack, operating population, and physical limit remains open. The first feasibility grant authorizes none of those claims.

Shared implementation target: express conditions, nine discrete actions, joule objective, 95 °C constraint/band, and `NONE_FEASIBLE` in the Challenge-neutral design-task contract; keep optimizer policy and Test Lead outcome logic separate from a f02 adapter. The user task references `carbon/design_search/tasks.py`, which is absent at this checkout revision; interface integration must wait for that file or an explicit prospective interface change.
