## Buyer decision and task

Choose **one support geometry** that works under all declared damping conditions, or `NONE_FEASIBLE`. The six design variables are continuous nominal dimensions: plate length 180–260 mm, width 60–100 mm, thickness 4–8 mm, common height of both ribs 8–16 mm, rib thickness 3–5 mm, and centered cable-relief length 20–60 mm. The two ribs share height/thickness; the relief width is fixed at 20 mm, its corners at 3 mm radius, and the shape must preserve ≥6 mm ligament and avoid rib intersection. Reject an invalid CAD geometry rather than repairing it. The root clamp, 1-N tip-pad force and observation points follow the packet.

Minimize the **worst 80–600 Hz tip dynamic compliance, in mm/N**, over modal damping {0.005, 0.01, 0.02}; frequency is an adversarial peak search, not a sampled customer state. Hard limits are mass ≤0.45 kg, static tip compliance ≤0.03 mm/N, and the band peak ≤0.15 mm/N at **every damping** value. The geometry must be one common design, not a different design per damping. No packet trade-off makes lower mass a soft objective once under its hard cap. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended exact-tie order: lower predicted worst peak, then lower exact CAD mass, then lower predicted static compliance, then lexicographic geometry coordinates; only exact equal peaks invoke the later terms. The optimizer may provisionally return `NONE_FEASIBLE` when it predicts no feasible evaluated geometry. A test judge can confirm this only if every member of the *registered scoreable bank* has adequate reference evidence of infeasibility; it is not proof that the entire continuous shape space is impossible. Incomplete peak search or missing reference rows yield `UNRESOLVED`.

## Fixed optimizer and cost envelope

**Class (b), multi-start local search snapped to a scoreable lattice.** Six continuous dimensions and topology validity make nine-point exhaustive search inapplicable; differentiating a learned modal curve would not by itself certify that its peaks or CAD gradients are correct. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended development snap: 10-mm length, 5-mm width, 0.5-mm plate thickness, 1-mm rib height, 0.5-mm rib thickness, and 5-mm relief length, filtered by the exact CAD validity rules. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended **16 Carbon-derived starts and at most 256 distinct geometry evaluations per task**. Before any model output, Carbon generates a fixed, scoreable bank of 256 valid snapped geometries, its 16 start nodes and its coordinate-neighbor graph; no proposal may leave that bank. For each start use a fixed neighbor order, best-improvement rule on the model's *full adaptive band peak at all three damping values*, and round-robin budget. Deduplicate; fill remaining slots from the same bank if search stalls. Thus the ceiling is **256 geometry predictions / 768 damping-response curves per task**. Freeze lattice, starts, frequency-peak procedure, stopping and tie rules before any model data; Carbon produces seeds and the miner does not. Commit the chosen geometry before independent truth. A different registered optimizer may be run as a Test Lead agreement diagnostic, without switching the buyer rule after seeing outcomes.

Verification needs a pre-solved or committed candidate bank with **static, eigen and harmonic** reference for each judged geometry. Static/eigen solves may be shared among the three damping curves; there are still at least **five solver-process launches per geometry** under the packet's accounting (one static, one eigen, three harmonic) before controls and refinements. Bracket every resonance and refine the frequency grid to at most one tenth of its half-power bandwidth. Independently check mesh, retained modes (12/24/48 where supported), peak amplitude and location. A fixed coarse frequency sample is not truth for this objective. **Reference cost per evaluated geometry: NOT_MEASURED; producer cost per complete bank task: NOT_MEASURED.** The first feasibility grant is **36 launches, 12 allocated node-hours, USD 30**, of which the packet explicitly budgets controls and convergence; it cannot produce a 256-geometry truth bank (at least 1,280 primary launches). `HUMAN_INPUT` requires measured per-stage timing/cost, bank size, actual capacity and a separately authorized producer budget. Reuse static/eigen factorization and cached exact references only with pinned identities and full cost accounting.

## Verification and gaming study for the Test Lead

The Test Lead owns scoring and adversarial qualification. Proposed outcome report: chosen geometry, mass, static compliance, all three full complex response curves/peak locations, worst reference peak, hard-limit excess in kg or mm/N, and regret in **mm/N** versus the best reference-feasible member of the registered bank. A lighter but peak-unsafe support is not compensated by mass. Over-caution means selecting a higher-peak feasible structure or abstaining when a verified lower-peak feasible structure exists; regret remains visible. A reference process failure is missing evidence, not candidate failure.

Include edge-optimist, over-cautious, localized sign/phase-error and optimizer-aware controls. In particular, an optimizer-aware model may fit the sampled frequency grid or known starts and suppress a narrow unsampled resonance; full adaptive reference peak search and Carbon-derived starts/second optimizer diagnose that attack. Near a hard limit, run the packet's independent mesh/mode/grid checks before a verdict. Its ≤5% peak-change and ≤1% eigenfrequency-change criteria are numerical adequacy targets, **not adopted classification bands**. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended provisional decision bands of ±0.0075 mm/N about the 0.15 dynamic limit and ±0.0015 mm/N about the 0.03 static limit (5% of each), to be replaced by measured uncertainty; exact mass comes from the pinned CAD/material law. If refinement remains inconclusive, a pessimistic report withholds favorable feasibility/zero-regret evidence instead of counting a fabricated failure.

**Power/sizing proposal, not a qualified sample size:** compare behavior-defined good and four known-bad families on fresh whole geometries and complete response curves, not frequency samples from one curve. Record false-feasible picks, missed feasible opportunity, worst-peak regret and unresolved rate separately at each damping value. The three damping ratios are correlated conditions on **one** support-design task, so they do not furnish three independent hidden tasks. A multi-task hidden batch needs `HUMAN_INPUT` buyer-recognizable operating jobs (for example separately justified load or packaging requirements), a task population/weighting law and more reference production; the current packet authorizes none of those variations. Independent geometry banks can probe optimizer stability but cannot establish population power. Task count, good/bad separation and sufficient batch size are **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**.

## Customer-facing form and open values

Inputs: allowed plate/rib/relief grammar, exact clamp/load/material and declared damping panel. Outputs: CAD dimensions, mass and static compliance, worst-band response and peak frequency per damping, uncertainty/provenance, and `NONE_FEASIBLE` or `UNRESOLVED` where appropriate. This could replace manual rib/relief sweeps and modal-analysis triage, not a full-machine modal test. The packet has **no deployment latency target**. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended **≤10 minutes per geometry proposal** on agreed hardware to support iterative engineering review; benchmark the full 768 curve-prediction budget before claiming it. Real joint damping, fatigue/strength, assembly effects, customer acceptance and hardware validation remain outside this synthetic task.

Shared implementation target: express continuous bounds and snapping, exact geometry validity, a common design over a three-condition panel, worst-band objective, per-condition constraints, reference bank identity and abstention in the Challenge-neutral task interface. A structural adapter supplies complex-response/peak extraction. The requested `carbon/design_search/tasks.py` is absent at this checkout revision; code integration requires that interface or an explicit prospective substitute.
