# Cooling accelerator manifold — Carbon-owned design optimizer (DEVELOPMENT proposal)

**Decision source:** [round-1 cooling buyer packet](../round1/cooling-accelerator-manifold.md) and its [numeric companion](../round1/first-three-requirements.json). **Status:** decision specification only. The revised thermal design space and cell/manifold composition validation are in progress. The full-plate CFD cost of ~60–180 CPU-hours per case is a planning input in the optimizer task brief, not a measured cost in this packet. No full-assembly reference adequacy is demonstrated.

## 1. Buyer decision and exact outcome

The buyer chooses **one manufacturable copper cold plate including inlet/outlet manifolds, plus one shared flow coefficient** for a module. Present periodic-cell geometry has continuous channel and fin widths 0.2–0.5 mm and depth 1–3 mm, a 30×30-mm heated footprint, 1-mm base, 0.5-mm lid, C11000 copper, PG25 and a declared TIM convention. Those bounds are reuse inputs, **not** an approved new full-plate design grammar or proof of feasibility. Header cross-section, channel count/end margins, port positions/diameters, wetted topology, machining rules, and any newly proposed deeper/narrower channels or base changes are **HUMAN_INPUT pending the revised design space**. Flow action is discrete `a ∈ {1.25,1.5,2.0} L/min/kW`, with total nominal flow `a × heat_load_kW`; the flow-loss scenario uses `0.625 × a`. Do not clamp unsupported low flows to present support.

Every proposed geometry/flow is paired with **all six** packet scenarios: cold uniform 30 °C/500 W, typical 40 °C/1,000 W, warm uniform 45 °C/1,500 W, warm central hotspot, warm outlet-side hotspot, and reduced supply at that outlet-side hotspot. The latter three use the packet's heat-map ratios, centers and widths, not a substituted uniform load. Geometry and flow are actions; inlet, heat map and supply loss are conditions. `P_dev` has equal synthetic mass over these six for development description, but every hard limit is checked per scenario. Deployment frequencies and official evidence weights are **HUMAN_INPUT**. Channels and streamwise samples within one assembly are correlated.

Hard limits for each service case are local die-side TIM-interface maximum ≤85 °C, full-assembly inlet-to-outlet total-pressure drop ≤50,000 Pa, hydraulic allocation `Δp × Q_in` ≤2.5 W, and inlet flow ≤3 L/min. Use the packet's local `max[T_heated_face(x,y) + R_TIM q(x,y)]` observation and pinned flow-weighted total-pressure planes; the periodic mean-flux and static-pressure proxies are not interchangeable. Unsupported header flow, port backflow under the current observer, or out-of-support low flow is `UNRESOLVED` reference scope, not a favorable pass. The objective among designs independently verified feasible in **all six** cases is **minimum worst-case hydraulic power in W**. Tie: lower worst-case die-side temperature in °C, then frozen design order. Temperature is a hard limit and tie metric, not a soft exchange for pump burden. If no design is verified feasible, answer `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_DESIGN`; if unresolved coverage prevents exclusion, answer `UNRESOLVED`.

**HUMAN_INPUT recommended close-call triggers, not verdict bands:** flag a thermal result within 1.0 °C of 85 °C and pressure or hydraulic power within 5% of their respective limits for the packet's mesh/refinement review. These reuse its selected numerical comparison scales; they are not demonstrated uncertainty bounds. Inlet-flow uncertainty and pressure-to-power propagation also require a pinned observer study. A resolved interval, not proximity alone, determines whether a hard limit passes.

## 2. Fixed optimizer proposal and budget

**Class (b), deterministic multi-start local search snapped to a scoreable lattice**, is provisional for a mixed geometry/flow space. Enumerate registered discrete topology and the three flow choices; Carbon's producer supplies hidden starts and a frozen lattice, neighbor order, deduplication, stop rule and tie order. Evaluate a candidate on the complete six-condition panel before selection. A differentiable construction gets no extra search budget or custom method. A separately registered optimizer can audit agreement under equal query rules; it does not silently replace the primary class. If the revised design space is small and fully finite, class (a) exhaustive may be selected only **before** any relevant data. Miners provide predictions, never official starts or verification.

**HUMAN_INPUT recommended model-evaluation budget:** 200 unique geometry/flow actions × six conditions = at most 1,200 model case queries. This mirrors the packet's unmeasured 200 × 6 ≤300-s inference target at warm p95 ≤0.25 s/case; it is a capacity proposal, not demonstrated validator performance. Freeze how candidates are allocated among topology and flow, whether one geometry at multiple flows counts as multiple actions, exact lattice spacing and early termination after the revised grammar is approved. Invalid actions and failures are accounted for; no free replacement draws.

## 3. Verification and cost

Reference truth for the buyer decision is **full plate plus manifolds**, not the periodic cell. Its prospective adapter must resolve local thermal maxima, flow distribution, total port pressure, inlet flow and all packet controls. The packet demands three spatial-resolution levels on difficult cases, ≤1.0 °C successive peak difference, ≤5% pressure/power change, and 1% mass/energy balance; these are demands, **NOT_DEMONSTRATED** reference error bounds. Any interval crossing a buyer limit goes to refined truth and otherwise remains `UNRESOLVED`. Reference invalidity/failed infrastructure never becomes a candidate physics penalty.

At the task brief's ~60–180 CPU-hours per full-plate case, six base cases for **one** committed design cost roughly **360–1,080 CPU-hours** before controls and refinement. Eight isolated full-plate cases would cost ~480–1,440 CPU-hours, yet cannot alone verify all six conditions for even two designs. A 200-action × six-case complete truth lattice would be ~72,000–216,000 CPU-hours before refinement. These are arithmetic planning ranges, not an execution grant or measured price. The one-time reference bank size, reuse count and incremental per-task reference cost are **HUMAN_INPUT**.

Cheapest potentially decisive path: use a Carbon-owned cell-plus-flow-network composition or other cheap physics model **only as a screening/proposal route** until its residual intervals against full-plate truth are independently qualified for every required output and stratum. Freeze a finite, scoreable design bank and solve/refine its decisive cases once. Reference regret is identifiable only against a bank with a verified best feasible member and common resolved mask; partial full-plate checks cannot establish a global optimum. If composition uncertainty overlaps 85 °C, 50 kPa or 2.5 W, purchase/refine the specific full-plate cases under a separate grant or leave the task unresolved. The same applies to the low-flow support extension. No present study demonstrates that the cheaper composition is adequate to judge this customer decision.

## 4. Resistance, sizing and customer use

The Test Lead owns grading, attacks and statistical acceptance. Development controls must include a **hotspot/pressure edge-optimist**, **over-cautious** high-flow or rejected-design behavior, a **sign-error** in pressure or hotspot response, and an **optimizer-aware** model accurate only near public search starts. Carbon hides randomized starts and protected scenarios, commits the selected action before reference access, and audits agreement with a second preregistered optimizer under equal query accounting. Only reference outcome judges the committed design. The proposed physical regret is excess worst-case hydraulic W relative to a verified feasible bank optimum, reported beside any hard-limit violation. A hard-only gate misses over-caution; a best-temperature proxy cannot erase pump regret. The Test Lead must preregister how unresolved scientific intervals are treated after refinement; a conservative backstop must never turn absent/failed reference work into candidate failure.

**Hidden-batch power:** `HUMAN_INPUT / NOT_DEMONSTRATED`. Six scenarios of one fixed module question are a coupled feasibility panel, not six fresh questions. The [packet's prospective population](../round1/cooling-accelerator-manifold.md) permits supported temperature/pressure/pump requirement variation on one solved full-manifold bank; no `P_job` or protected `Q_job` is adopted. **Recommended conditional pilot, not an adopted hidden-batch size:** 12 eligible, unexposed questions with verified answer diversity after the revised grammar has feasible examples. Reuse saves solves, but draws consume bank exposure `E` and remain clustered by module/bank in power analysis. Simulate behavior-defined controls and known-good models, then choose the smallest batch meeting the Test Lead's owner-approved separation target. No power conclusion follows from periodic-cell evidence or eight full-plate spot checks. Each question still needs its complete applicable service panel.

The proposed buyer tool accepts the full assembly grammar, heat-map service panel, inlet and allowed flow choices; it returns a geometry/flow shortlist, local maximum and location, port pressure and hydraulic W by scenario, uncertainty/reference status, and `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_DESIGN` when appropriate. Deployment is an **offline module-design workstation**. The packet targets warm p95 ≤0.25 s per full service prediction and a shortlist ≤15 min, both unmeasured. It aims to replace first-pass full-assembly CFD sweeps, while final coupled-reference confirmation, rig testing and live loop control remain separate.
