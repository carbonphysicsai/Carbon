# f06 — Carbon-owned grating-mask optimizer (DEVELOPMENT proposal)

**Selected buyer requirements:** [f06 customer packet](../round1/f06-grating-coupler.md), [round-one numeric sheet](../round1/requirements.json), and [owner's development delegation](../../../../.agent/decisions/2026-10-06-OWNER-PORTFOLIO-DEV-ROUND-01.md). The fixed material law and nine tolerance vectors are synthetic. Meep 3D reference adequacy, fine-mesh memory feasibility, process yield, and fabrication performance are **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**. This is a buyer-decision proposal, not a Score Pack or permission to run a full-wave campaign.

## Buyer decision and task

Choose **one mask geometry** for the declared stack and fiber assembly, or `NONE_FEASIBLE`. The design space is mixed: integer periods 12–20; continuous nominal pitch 580–680 nm, unetched duty 0.35–0.65, etch depth 70–130 nm, and width 8–12 µm. The 500-nm TE0 input guide, 10-µm taper, fiber MFD 10.4 µm, 10° axis, and stack/materials are fixed. Apply each tolerance perturbation before checking line/space ≥180 nm, positive residual Si, and all other geometry validity; clipping is forbidden. The nine declared vectors of correlated pitch/etch/duty and x/y alignment offsets, each with five wavelengths {1530, 1540, 1550, 1560, 1570} nm, are **one finite robustness panel**, not 45 independent customers or a fabrication-yield distribution. The same mask is judged across the full panel.

Maximize the **nearest-rank 10th percentile of coupled fiber power / incident guided power**, a dimensionless fraction: sort the 45 panel values and take the fifth. Hard limits hold at **every** panel condition: coupling ≥0.30 and reflected guided power ≤0.10. A strong percentile cannot compensate for a failed condition. There is no adopted secondary trade-off in the packet. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended exact-tie order: larger predicted panel minimum coupling, smaller predicted panel maximum reflection, fewer periods, then lexicographic geometry coordinates. These terms break exact primary-objective ties only; any Pareto front for size or reflectance would be a separately registered buyer decision. The optimizer may provisionally return `NONE_FEASIBLE` when no evaluated mask is predicted feasible. A test judge can confirm this only when adequate reference evidence rules out every design in the *registered scoreable bank*; it is not proof that the continuous physical design space has no solution. Missing or unresolved bank rows yield `UNRESOLVED`.

## Fixed optimizer and cost envelope

**Class (b), multi-start local search snapped to a scoreable lattice.** The mixed five-variable space is much larger than f02's nine actions, while full-wave reference is expensive. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended development lattice: integer period; 10-nm pitch, 0.025 duty, 10-nm etch, and 0.5-µm width steps, intersected with valid perturbed geometries. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended **16 Carbon-seeded starts and at most 256 distinct mask evaluations per task**. Before any model output, Carbon generates a fixed, scoreable bank of 256 valid snapped masks, its 16 start nodes and its coordinate-neighbor graph; no search proposal may leave that bank. At each start, use a fixed neighbor order and best-improvement rule on the model's full 45-point panel, with a fixed round-robin allocation; deduplicate proposals and fill unused slots from the same bank. A mask evaluation is nine spectral predictions (45 scalar wavelength/condition outputs), so the ceiling is **2,304 spectral calls / 11,520 scalar panel predictions per task**. Freeze the snapping, starts, neighbor order, stopping, validity filters, and tie rule before model data. Carbon's producer derives hidden starts; miners choose no seeds. The selected mask is committed before operator-side truth is opened. A second registered optimizer on an independently generated bank is an attack diagnostic owned by the Test Lead, not an adaptive replacement for the buyer optimizer.

Truth must come from a pre-solved mask bank or a committed candidate-bank verification, with all nine tolerance vectors and five wavelengths for each judged mask, independently normalized. One broadband vector solve might report all five wavelengths, but that is an implementation hypothesis, **NOT_MEASURED**. Boundary masks need the packet's 30/20/10-nm, PML, duration and energy-closure checks; a 30-nm screening solve cannot give a decisive 0.30/0.10 verdict when fine evidence is unresolved. **Reference cost per mask: NOT_MEASURED**, with at least nine vector evaluations if one solve covers five wavelengths, plus normalization, controls, and refinements. **Producer cost per complete design task: NOT_MEASURED**; it depends on how many frozen bank masks and refinements are needed. The first feasibility grant is **36 total launches, 12 allocated node-hours, USD 60**, not a production-bank budget. Even four masks × nine vectors consume all 36 launches before any controls/refinements; the packet's 10-nm cell-count screen raises an additional RAM blocker. `HUMAN_INPUT`: a measured launch/resource ledger, exact memory/price quote, bank size, and separately authorized producer budget before promising a task cadence.

## Verification and gaming study for the Test Lead

The Test Lead owns how reference outcomes become a score and which attacks are required. Report the committed mask's 45 reference coupling/reflection pairs, p10 and minimum coupling, maximum reflection, violations in dimensionless coupling/reflection fractions, and p10 regret against the best *reference-feasible mask in the same registered bank*. Over-caution means choosing a feasible but lower-p10 mask or abstaining despite a verified feasible mask; it retains the lost p10. Do not label an unresolved reference as zero coupling, and do not make a weighted average hide any mandatory condition.

Include edge-optimist, over-cautious, localized wavelength/offset sign-error, and optimizer-aware controls. The optimizer-aware control can be accurate on the visible search path while wrong on Carbon-derived starts or a second frozen search bank; judge only the committed mask by full-wave reference. Refine every near-limit design whose uncertainty can change feasible status or the winning bank mask. The packet's convergence targets (coupling difference ≤0.02 and reflection ≤0.01) are **adequacy tests**, not automatically an uncertainty band. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended provisional close-call bands of ±0.02 coupling and ±0.01 reflection around the buyer limits for Test Lead study, subject to measured refinement evidence. Until resolved, a pessimistic report refuses favorable pass/zero-regret claims; it does not invent model failure.

**Power/sizing proposal, not a qualified sample size:** preregister good/bad behavior on fully referenced buyer jobs and estimate separation for false-feasible picks, p10 regret, and missed feasible opportunity. Stratify diagnostics by wavelength edge/center and tolerance vector, but do **not** count 45 correlated points as 45 independent design tasks. The [packet's prospective job population](../round1/f06-grating-coupler.md) sketches PDK/package/interface variation and an eight-brief scoping inventory; the present reference still defines one fixed customer panel. A many-task hidden-batch calculation requires `HUMAN_INPUT` adoption of eligible job support, `P_job`, protected `Q_job`, weights, clustering and reference budget. Repeated mask banks for one PDK/package are a *search robustness diagnostic*, not a buyer-population sample. Hidden `n` and power are **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**.

## Customer-facing form and open values

Inputs: fixed stack/TE guide/fiber conventions and the permitted mask grammar; the declared tolerance panel stays attached to the design request. Outputs: nominal mask dimensions, predicted spectral/tolerance table, p10 and worst-condition margins, provenance and uncertainty, or explicit `NONE_FEASIBLE` / `UNRESOLVED`. This would replace repeated manual mask sweeps before fabrication, not fabrication/metrology or a yield claim. The packet has **no deployment latency target**. `HUMAN_INPUT` recommended **≤30 minutes per mask proposal** on agreed hardware for an offline design loop; it must be benchmarked, including all 2,304 possible spectral model calls. Real process distributions, product acceptance and manufacturing rights remain separate owner/customer decisions.

Shared implementation target: a Challenge-neutral task holds mixed bounded variables, correlated condition panels, p10 objective, per-condition constraints, validity rules, bank identity and abstention. The f06 adapter supplies optics-specific extraction. `carbon/design_search/tasks.py` is named by the task but absent at this checkout revision; interface integration is a prospective dependency, not implemented here.
