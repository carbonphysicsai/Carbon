# Motor precision joint — Carbon-owned design optimizer (DEVELOPMENT proposal)

**Decision source:** [round-1 motor buyer packet](../round1/motor-precision-joint.md) and its [numeric companion](../round1/first-three-requirements.json). **Status:** decision specification only; the revised geometry grammar is in progress. This is neither an adopted Score Pack nor a qualified joint design tool. The cost of ~0.7 core-hour per GetDP point is a planning input from the optimizer task brief, not a measured cost in the buyer packet.

## 1. Buyer decision and exact outcome

The buyer chooses **one motor cross-section plus one precision command and one peak command**, before reference access. The current cross-section has six continuous geometry parameters: magnet thickness 1.5–4 mm, embrace 0.55–0.95, gap 0.3–1 mm, slot opening 1–6°, tooth width 2.5–5 mm, and slot-bottom radius 34–42 mm. The current 8-pole/24-slot, 92-mm outer diameter, 35-mm stack and 104-turn representation is historical scope, **not** proof that these choices can satisfy the packet. New topology, skew or shaping variables, their types, bounds, validity grammar and scoreable lattice are **HUMAN_INPUT pending the revised design space**. Do not optimize an unspecified topology.

For each geometry, the packet's command options are discrete: precision current density `J ∈ {8,10} A/mm²`, peak `J ∈ {12,15} A/mm²`, phase angle `∈ {0,15}°`, and an unpowered `J=0`, angle `0` cogging curve. Current density is not phase current; use the geometry-dependent winding/slot-area mapping. Holding and slow tracking reuse the same static curves and are one magnetic observation, not independent trials. The acceleration stratum is a static magnetic probe; its 2-second thermal/dynamic rating is **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**. Unpowered positioning is a separate absolute-cogging check. Real deployment `P` and any hidden scenario variation remain **HUMAN_INPUT**; the packet supplies a finite diagnostic command panel, not a deployment-frequency law.

The hard magnetic limits are: selected holding mean torque ≥6 N·m, selected peak mean ≥12 N·m, energized peak-to-peak ripple ≤5% of `abs(mean)` **and** ≤0.30 N·m at holding or ≤0.60 N·m at peak, and unpowered cogging peak-to-peak ≤0.05 N·m. Negative or zero mean cannot meet a positive floor. The product-context tip disturbance ≤10 µm at a 0.20-m lever is a separate mechanical/controller requirement; it cannot be certified by this magnetic model. The buyer objective among fully verified feasible cross-sections is **minimum holding ripple fraction** (dimensionless), with holding peak-to-peak N·m also reported. Tie: lower selected holding current density, then a frozen canonical design order. No weighted trade-off may exchange a torque/cogging failure for smoother torque. If no design is verified feasible, answer `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_DESIGN`; if reference uncertainty or coverage prevents that conclusion, answer `UNRESOLVED`, never a guessed feasible pick.

**HUMAN_INPUT recommended close-call triggers, not verdict bands:** flag mean torque within 0.10 N·m of either floor and absolute ripple/cogging within 0.02 N·m of their respective ceilings for the packet's paired-refinement review; also flag every 5% relative-ripple crossing after propagating both mean and peak-to-peak uncertainty. These scales come from the packet's selected numerical comparison checks. They are not measured error bounds, and only resolved reference intervals can decide which side of a hard limit a curve lies on.

## 2. Fixed optimizer proposal and budget

**Class (b), deterministic multi-start local search snapped to a scoreable lattice**, is the provisional class for a mixed revised space. Enumerate each registered discrete topology and command choice; use Carbon-produced hidden starts and a frozen neighbor/order rule for continuous parameters, snap before every model query, deduplicate canonical designs, and count every attempted full curve. This is model-family neutral: differentiability of a Level-4 graph model cannot give that model a different grade-time search opportunity. A gradient method may be an independently registered diagnostic comparator after its applicable model set is declared, not a silent substitute for the primary optimizer. If the approved revision yields a small finite space, reconsider class (a) **before data**, never after observing outcomes. Seed identities, starts, candidate order and protected scenarios stay operator-side; a miner supplies model predictions only.

**HUMAN_INPUT recommended evaluation budget:** at most 200 unique cross-sections and the packet's nine command curves per cross-section (1,800 model curve queries), with a hard stop before the next unaffordable complete command panel. Rationale: this exactly matches the packet's unmeasured 200 × 9 ≤180-s inference target at p95 0.10 s/curve; it does not establish validator capacity. Freeze topology allocation, start count, neighbor steps, lattice spacing and stopping rule after the design-space revision and before any protected outcomes. Invalid geometry and model failures consume attempted-query accounting and cannot trigger unlimited redraws.

## 3. Verification and cost

Reference is the pinned Gmsh/GetDP torque curve, including 60→120→240 angle refinement on compatible meshes and Maxwell-stress versus virtual-work/energy checks where the packet demands them. The present reference adequacy at the new buyer limits is **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**. At a planning ~0.7 core-hour per base point, independently checking one committed geometry's precision, peak and cogging commands is **at least ~2.1 core-hours** before refinements and failures. A complete nine-command diagnostic panel is ~6.3 core-hours per geometry at that input rate. A 200-geometry, nine-command truth lattice would be ~1,260 core-hours before refinement, so no per-submission on-demand exhaustive truth is proposed. These are multiplication of the task brief's rough cost, not approved execution estimates.

Cheapest potentially decisive route: pre-solve and independently refine a **versioned finite candidate bank** once, then reuse its immutable quantitative truth across model arms and different supported requirement questions **within the bank's exposure `E`**. The bank must contain verified feasible designs, near-limit and clearly infeasible controls under the registered question range, and enough reference coverage to identify each question's best feasible bank design; otherwise regret against that bank is not identifiable. The bank size, revised geometry support, amortization count, solver grant and per-task incremental verification cost are **HUMAN_INPUT**. A selected design outside the bank can be committed and checked adaptively, but an unchecked global optimum must not be asserted. Boundary-overlapping intervals go to refined truth; exhausted or failed reference work remains `UNRESOLVED`, separate from candidate failure.

## 4. Resistance, sizing and customer use

The Test Lead owns the eventual attack and score rule. Required development controls are: boundary **edge-optimist**, feasible-design **over-cautious**, localized torque/ripple **sign-error**, and **optimizer-aware** models accurate only near published starts. Hide producer starts and scenario identities; compare the primary choice with a separately preregistered alternative optimizer on the same model under equal query accounting as an audit. Judge the committed choice by reference outcome. Penalizing over-caution requires physical decision regret or lost feasible opportunity; a hard feasibility gate alone is insufficient. Define a dimensionless holding-ripple regret only against a reference-verified feasible comparator and report the underlying N·m curve quantities. Hard-limit violations stay separate. An `UNRESOLVED` interval triggers refinement; any conservative uncertainty backstop must be registered by the Test Lead and must never turn reference/infra failure into scientific failure.

**Hidden-batch power:** `HUMAN_INPUT / NOT_DEMONSTRATED`. The [packet's prospective question population](../round1/motor-precision-joint.md) permits different supported torque/ripple requirements on one solved geometry/command bank; no `P_job` or protected `Q_job` is adopted. **Recommended conditional pilot, not an adopted hidden-batch size:** 12 eligible, unexposed questions with verified answer diversity after the design-space revision. Bank reuse saves solves, but every draw consumes exposure `E`; shared joint-bank questions remain clustered in power analysis. Estimate each behavior-defined control's separation from known-good models, then choose the smallest registered batch meeting the Test Lead's owner-approved error target. Do not count 60 angles, command variants or reused holding/tracking curves as fresh questions. Weights and target power remain **HUMAN_INPUT**.

The proposed customer tool accepts the declared joint load/command roles and geometry grammar, then returns a selected geometry, commands, predicted full torque curves, ripple/cogging margins, reference status and alternatives or `NO_VERIFIED_FEASIBLE_DESIGN`. It is for an offline engineering workstation. The packet targets warm-model p95 ≤0.10 s per 60-angle curve and shortlist latency ≤10 min, both **unmeasured**. It could replace first-pass static magnetic sweeps; final FEA, bench torque, thermal rating and controller commissioning remain separate.
