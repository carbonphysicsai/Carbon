## Shared answer, boundary and exposure rules

One question is one complete buyer decision with eligible actions, mandatory
conditions, requirement vector, objective and immutable reference/observer
versions. A timestep, frequency, outlet pixel or threshold draw is not an
independent new solved bank. P defines forward buyer questions; Q2 separately
enriches diagnostics; Q3 recommends forward P, not redraw-until-feasible.
w for outer questions is separate from within-question integration/aggregation.
All densities, dependencies, numeric widths, power, k/E/B/n and weighting
acceptance are HUMAN_INPUT. Uniform/product recommendations are development
scenarios, not measured buyer frequencies.

Retain **NONE_FEASIBLE** draws if every eligible action has an independently
resolved mandatory failure. An unresolved/reference/infra row is not failure;
it prevents a complete NONE_FEASIBLE conclusion unless that action is already
proven infeasible on another mandatory constraint. No claim of infeasibility
outside the registered menu. An unresolved optimum/tie remains UNRESOLVED or
an accepted value-equivalent set; never substitute favorable zero margins.

**T2(a): Test Lead working value — five distinct resolved feasible and five
distinct resolved infeasible designs, BOTH within two refinement bands of the
binding limit, per question family and mandatory stratum.** One-band widths
are HUMAN_INPUT numerical/reference uncertainty, not the full error tolerance
automatically divided into a band. With signed slack m and accepted width
delta, near means `abs(m)<=2*delta`; the sign must also be resolved by the
retained reference interval. Report the active constraint; distant violations
of unrelated limits and duplicate aliases do not fill a frontier quota.
Exact geometric metadata may have zero numerical uncertainty; the Test Lead
must specify its applicable boundary/count rule, not invent a wider band to
fill quotas. Add frontier resolution when short; never widen bands or weaken
limits. Also report meaningful margin spread, a complete all-condition
feasible answer and changing best/value-equivalent answers. No value pass
or hidden-bank permission follows from these documents.
