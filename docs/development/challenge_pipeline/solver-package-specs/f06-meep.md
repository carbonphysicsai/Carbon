## 2. Deck and measurement inputs

Use µm as length unit and `f=1/lambda_um`; Meep's
[unit convention](https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Introduction/)
sets c=1. The five **wavelengths** are 1.530/1.540/1.550/1.560/1.570 µm.
Register their exact inverse frequencies; equally spaced frequencies are
not the same grid as equally spaced wavelengths.

Keep 220-nm Si on 2-µm BOX over a converged substrate extent, air cladding,
12–20 periods, pitch 580–680 nm, unetched duty 0.35–0.65, etch 70–130 nm and
width 8–12 µm. Keep the 500-nm input guide and 10-µm linear taper. The
synthetic lossless nondispersive indices (Si 3.48, silica 1.44, air 1) are
selected DEVELOPMENT inputs, not real-material adequacy. Freeze all nine
correlated fabrication/fibre-offset vectors from the packet; do not form a
new Cartesian tolerance population. Fibre target: 10.4-µm mode-field
diameter, waist 2 µm above grating top, 10° axis in x/z, with the registered
dx/dy offsets. Pin source polarization, phase, bandwidth and launch plane.

**2D x/z adapter:** uniform invariant-y cross-section, per-unit-length power,
its declared polarization and in-plane target overlap. Export proxy quantities
as SCREENING_ONLY. It has no finite-width aperture/taper/y-offset decision.
**3D adapter:** complete finite-width geometry and finite-domain absorber,
vector TE0 launch/measurement and tilted Gaussian target E/H overlap. No
transverse periodic boundary or symmetry unless it preserves the actual
geometry, source, offsets and observables and is independently checked.

Pin incident-guide normalization run, mode band/parity, incident/scattered
field subtraction, monitor positions/normals, quadrature, target-mode
normalization, Fourier sign/time window and image/deck identity. The
[mode-decomposition guide](https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Mode_Decomposition/)
describes unit-power modal coefficients; raw field magnitude is not coupling
efficiency. The [Python API](https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Python_User_Interface/)
exposes complex DFT fields/metadata. DC must implement and verify the target
vector overlap; an upward flux monitor alone is not fibre-coupled power.

Normalization caches require exact geometry of the incident guide, grid,
source/frequencies, monitor/partition and build identity. Wavelength sharing
via one broadband pulse is a candidate optimisation, not assumed equivalence.
Prove agreement against five independently normalized single-frequency runs
at the same geometry/offset before reducing launches. Frequency convergence,
source spectrum and DFT sampling must be retained.

## 3. Output and convergence/conservation contract

Export all **9 vectors × 5 wavelengths = 45 rows** per design: coupling and
reflection fractions, complex incident/reflected TE0 amplitudes, target
overlap, upward/downward/other boundary fluxes, material absorption and raw
independent power residual. Retain mode fields/metadata needed to reproduce
the overlap and normalization. Report coupling minimum, reflection maximum
and p10 as the **fifth ordered coupling value** (nearest rank), not a fitted
quantile or nine independent questions. Gates remain coupling≥0.30 and
reflection≤0.10 at every point; objective maximises the p10.

Use non-overlapping surfaces/channels for accounting. **Fibre power is a
subset of upward radiation: do not add it to upward flux again.** Incident,
reflected and outward powers must be independently extracted, with normals
and sign conventions pinned; neither negative-power clipping nor a residual
defined to guarantee closure is allowed. Transient storage/source terms must
be included if a steady spectral power balance has not converged. Lossless
input does not permit omitting radiation channels.

Retain packet DEVELOPMENT requirements: spatial rungs 30/20/10 nm; adjacent
rung changes in coupling≤0.02 and reflection≤0.01 absolute fraction;
independent padding/PML/duration doubling changes≤0.01; power residual≤0.03.
Converge physical domain, PML, grid and observer quadrature independently. The
[PML guide](https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Perfectly_Matched_Layer/)
explains why finite PML must be tested, not trusted by name. Record the
Courant factor and field-decay/stopping rule; the exact stopping rule is
**HUMAN_INPUT recommendation:** approve a decay criterion and doubling
check before running, not “finish when the desired efficiency appears.”

## 4. Memory before launch

The [Meep FAQ](https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/FAQ/) gives **96 bytes per
cell** for an empty real-field Cartesian single-chunk grid. It is a lower
bound, not peak RSS. The earlier panel's bare 10-nm geometry estimate is
573 million–1.20 billion cells. Scaling the *same bare volume* gives:

| Grid | Bare cells low/high | 96-byte lower bound low/high, GiB |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 30 nm | 21.22 / 44.44 million | 1.90 / 3.97 |
| 20 nm | 71.625 / 150 million | 6.40 / 13.41 |
| 10 nm | 573 / 1,200 million | 51.23 / 107.29 |

Actual domain adds air/substrate/ports/PML; tensors, auxiliary fields,
complex fields, DFT accumulators, MPI duplication and Python/export copies
add memory. Forecast actual rounded Nx×Ny×Nz and every retained array before
launch. For example, six complex128 E/H components at five frequencies on
Nmonitor points require `6 * 5 * 16 * Nmonitor` bytes **just for those arrays**;
this is an array calculation, not a claim about Meep's total storage.
Prefer necessary monitor surfaces over full-volume DFT exports, without
dropping the required observer. Forecasts must cover export-time peak too.

Full 10-nm fit is **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**. Neither 32 GiB nor the historical
256-GiB ceiling establishes capacity. The older 12/32-GiB p50/p95 hypothesis
is for coarse 3D triage, not fine-grid sizing. Reject any plan whose lower
bound exceeds the available enforced allocation; a lower bound below it is
not approval. **HUMAN_INPUT recommendation:** DC reserves an owner-approved
headroom factor after measuring the actual build/domain, before fine-grid
dispatch. No local variable-resolution Meep grid is assumed.
