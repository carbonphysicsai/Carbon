# f06 data acquisition plan (DEVELOPMENT, SPECIFIED)

Decision: [F06-SCIENCE-VALUES-01](../../../../.agent/decisions/2026-10-15-F06-SCIENCE-VALUES-01.md); machine-readable: [f06.json](f06.json).

**Blocker.** The slab TE0 control (c1) fails its registered |Δn_eff| ≤ 0.003 band at every grid (40→10 nm: 0.48/0.41/0.16/0.21/0.11 worst; 220-nm slab −0.13…−0.04, non-monotone). That points to a deck/eigensolver-call defect, not discretisation. No grating or 3D data is generated until it is diagnosed; the band is not loosened.

**Cost basis.** 60 CPU-h per complete 30-nm 3D design is an ASSUMPTION (serial Meep, 28–48 M mirror-halved cells, ~20 k steps at 50–100 ns per cell-step), to be replaced by the 300-step probes in `f06_controls.py` before any spend.

**3D witness (RunPod cpu3m, 16 vCPU / 128 GB, $0.88/h).** short-narrow (12 × 580 nm, duty 0.5, 70-nm etch, 8 µm) at 30 nm, normalization plus five geometry runs, mirror-Y, all 45 rows; 5.18 M / 27.82 M cells; forecast ≤ 5.5 GiB per process with 1.25 headroom; run a 300-step probe first, then the five geometry runs in parallel (~14 h wall, ~$12, ASSUMPTION).
