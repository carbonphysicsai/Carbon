# f08 data acquisition plan — resonance-resistant support (DEVELOPMENT)

Machine-readable twin: [f08.json](f08.json). Sizes and laws are the delegated
DEVELOPMENT values of `.agent/decisions/2026-10-15-F08-SCIENCE-VALUES-01.md`
(Q1–Q13 and addendum R1–R10). The question law is in
[structures-readiness/question-law.json](../structures-readiness/question-law.json).
This plan generates nothing, grants no spend and makes no qualification claim.

**Unit.** One geometry is one ccx launch: static, 48-mode eigen and the three
constant-modal-damping (0.005/0.01/0.02) 80–600 Hz sweeps, at the V9 bank mesh
(h 3 mm unless the witness ladder says otherwise). The observer refines every
crest from the stored basis in under a second. Measured locally (cgroup CPU, 12
launches, 25k–137k nodes): a typical h 3 geometry costs about 178 CPU-s and
1.3 GiB, and an h 1.7 refinement about 1,500 CPU-s and 6 GiB
(`f08_deck.COST_MODEL`). The largest-lattice h 1.7 refinement (about 340k nodes,
3,000 CPU-s, 10 GiB) is an extrapolation: measure it at the bulk pilot stage.

**Commands.** Freeze a role with
`f08_bank.py freeze --registration structures-readiness/registration.json --seed S --count N --role R --out DIR [--public-seed] [--exclude OTHER_MANIFEST ...]`,
run its manifest with `triage_run.py` or the bulk runner (each case command is
plain shell, `ccx -i case`, so it runs in the Docker image or natively with
`OMP_NUM_THREADS=OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=CCX_NPROC_EQUATION_SOLVER=1` and the
pinned Gmsh 4.15.2 via `CARBON_F08_NATIVE_GMSH=1`), then observe with
`f08_freeze.py --observe DIR --ledger LEDGER --out OBSERVED.json`.
Roles are whole-geometry disjoint by action digest (`--exclude`).

**Concurrency on a large host.** One ccx per vCPU (`--cpus 1`, single-threaded
BLAS/OpenMP). h 3 launches peak ≤ ~2.3 GiB, so 32 run in ≤ 75 GiB; h 1.7
refinements peak up to ~10 GiB, so cap them at RAM/10 GiB at once. The public
total is about 4.9 wall-hours on a 32-vCPU host.

**Finding to carry.** On the eight-case public panel only short-wide meets the
0.15 mm/N dynamic anchor (0.098 mm/N), and it misses the 0.45 kg mass cap by
1.8 %; every other geometry resonates in band at ζ = 0.005 well above the cap.
The 27-cell P_job grid (0.35/0.45/0.55 kg, 0.10–0.20 mm/N) still yields
feasible answers, but the anchor cell may be NONE_FEASIBLE on many banks.
The V-check discrimination (V2) must be measured on the decision study before
any hidden bank, not assumed.
