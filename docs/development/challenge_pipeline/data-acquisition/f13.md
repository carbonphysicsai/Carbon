# f13 data acquisition plan — compact compressor silencer (DEVELOPMENT)

Machine-readable twin: [f13.json](f13.json). Sizes and laws are the delegated
DEVELOPMENT values of `.agent/decisions/2026-10-15-F13-SCIENCE-VALUES-01.md`
(F1–F10 and section G). This plan generates nothing, grants no spend and makes
no qualification claim.

**Unit.** One geometry is one ElmerSolver process scanning the 201-point
500–2500 Hz curve on the accepted v2 deck (60 mm leads de-embedded to the 10 mm
panel planes, 8 mm second-order mesh, BiCGStabl+ILU2), image
`sha256:61be1cb7…` (`silencer_readiness.IMAGE`). Measured locally (cgroup
CPU, contended 4-vCPU host): 1.2 (duct) to 8.3 (nominal, 33 k nodes) CPU-s per
frequency system, ≈ 8.3·(nodes/32 566)^1.29; peak ≈ 5.5 KiB/node (157 MiB
nominal, 1.2 GiB at 4 mm). A typical 35 k-node curve is ≈ 0.5 CPU-h
(0.76 with the ×1.5 safety the manifests carry). The large-offset full
curve and 4 mm full curves are extrapolations: measure at the bulk pilot.

**Commands.** `f13_bank_freeze.py freeze --role R --count N (--public-seed S | --seed-file F) --out DIR [--exclude M ...]`,
then `frontier` (boundary group from solved near-floor designs) and `refine`
(4 mm and 5 Hz rungs for designs within 0.5 dB of a floor). Manifests use
`carbon.reference-triage.frozen-decks.v1` with `estimate_cpu_s/wall_s/mem_mib`.
Case commands are plain shell (`ElmerGrid … && ElmerSolver case.sif`), so they
run in the image or on a native pod build; freeze-time meshing uses the
pinned Gmsh image or `CARBON_F13_NATIVE_GMSH=1`. Roles are whole-geometry
disjoint by action digest and from the public recipe panel.

Refinements dominate: a near-floor design's 4 mm + 5 Hz rungs cost ≈ 14 ×
its 8 mm curve. TRAIN at the full 2,048 ladder top would add ≈ 780 CPU-h
(≈ USD 34); extend only if Phase G shows no plateau at 4×.

**Concurrency on a large host.** One ElmerSolver per vCPU (`--cpus 1`,
serial BLAS); 8 mm curves peak ≤ ~0.5 GiB, 4 mm rungs ≤ ~2.5 GiB (large
offset, extrapolated), so memory never binds below ~4 GiB/vCPU. The public
total is ≈ 36 wall-hours on a 32-vCPU host.
