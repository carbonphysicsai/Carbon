## Industry reports: what is and is not known

CIMdata's publicly accessible 2025 report contents describes simulation/analysis **provider revenue** by sector and geography, not customer design labour or Carbon-addressable spend. The current 2026 report listing was also searched; the accessible material did not expose sector tables. No paid report was purchased. NAFEMS's 2026 public survey abstract covers composites simulation adoption, not sector euros. **Current simulation spend by sector = HUMAN_INPUT**, and no market number enters V2/V3 or investor appeal.

The sweep therefore uses disclosed actual engineering jobs and software use across packaging, acoustics, precision measurement, power-grid equipment, connectors, valves, polymer processing, automotive electronics, appliances, biotech MEMS, medical devices, aerospace recuperators, bioprocess equipment, forming and PCB thermal design. POLNA explicitly describes purchased CAE software/workstations; this confirms a use/purchase workflow, not a spending amount. These are reports/cases selected for discovery, not an exhaustive or unbiased industry sample.
