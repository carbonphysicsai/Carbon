## Evidence gaps

Independent source **authors/organisations** count, not hostnames: a university paper on COMSOL is independent of a manufacturer brochure, but two pages from one vendor are one origin. Solver documentation is not a second buyer source. Academic optimisation supports the existence of an engineering activity; it does not by itself establish a current buyer's production decision.

No supplier/customer interview, customer CAD, approved bill of materials, purchase order, current latency trace, owned material curve, manufacturing tolerance, clinical/aviation qualification or observation of annual decision volume was provided. These are HUMAN_INPUT. This limitation is decisive for the requested hard filters.

SOFA addition for the gripper's primary route: [SOFA consortium](https://www.sofa-framework.org/), open LGPL framework; [official source](https://github.com/sofa-framework/sofa). Exact Linux scene/plugins and measurement contract remain HUMAN_INPUT. CalculiX is an optional independent mechanical witness, not an automatic transfer of the Fin-QD SOFA model.
