# Screening, scoring and value/cost method

## Admission, uncertainty and order

The task requires 60–100 hypotheses, a 15-item screen and five packet drafts. This report supplies 80, 15 and five respectively, but **zero admitted finalists**. A candidate may enter the admitted ranking only after every hard filter passes. HOLD is absence of proof and removes it from that set; FAIL_DESK_CASE rejects this proposed scope for lack of a plausible residual, not as a claim of physical impossibility. FAIL_SCENARIO rejects affordability under the stated cost envelope, not a measured bill.

The five-dossier queue is a research ordering drawn from non-rejected desk hypotheses by base value/cost index. It grants no execution or adoption permission. Air heat sink and duct fitting are retained below the first five; cheap-library competitors remain serious. Every new proposal must pass a separate proof panel.

| Gate | Evidence needed before PASS | Current disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 real decision | Named current buyer role, exact action/output contract used with simulation today, at least two independent origins; public analogue plus customer confirmation | Source pairs support hypotheses; exact buyer packet HUMAN_INPUT |
| G2 open solver | Open licence and Linux build reviewed; immutable image/deck/material/mesh/extractor pins; exactly the graded outputs | Source routes exist; exact pinned workflows HUMAN_INPUT |
| G3 feasible | Customer-sourced every hard limit; converged witness meeting all limits in every mandatory stratum | No new witness; HUMAN_INPUT |
| G4 contested | At least five feasible and five near-infeasible designs per stratum, with prospective uncertainty/refinement bands and all other limits respected | Counts absent; HUMAN_INPUT |
| G5 baseline | Plausible residual followed by matched-admissibility complete-decision baseline comparison including verification and amortisation | Plausible nonlinear residual only; no earned advantage |
| G6 affordable | Estimated complete startup bill at most EUR100 on user-priced CCX63, including failures, witnesses and other charges | PASS_ESTIMATE where the high scenario fits; actual C2 acceptance unearned |
| G7 no relaxation | Traceable owner/customer limits; no relaxation, narrowed safety claim disguised as a pass, or scientific limit selected by researcher | No numerical limits set; source/owner decision still required |

For G4 use one prospective refinement band as in the framework screen; #921/#922's two-band working draft can be recorded as a separate sensitivity, never silently substituted. Counts never justify relaxing either a limit or band. Reference failure/uncertain sign is UNRESOLVED, not candidate near-infeasibility or NONE_FEASIBLE.

## V1–V5 rubric

Scores are research judgements, **ASSUMPTION on a defined 0–5 scale**. Displayed base b has low/base/high [max(0,b−1),b,min(5,b+1)]; all triples are in data.json. Anchors are definitions, not measured science. Scores are not summed into the ranking.

| Score | 0 anchor | 3 anchor | 5 anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 real buyer decision | No exact simulation workflow | Two independent technical analogues, exact buyer still absent | Confirmed current customer job and independent matching workflow |
| V2 per-decision value | No causal benefit chain | Plausible quantified time/rework chain with sourced buyer inputs | Measured incremental net benefit versus adequate incumbent |
| V3 annual eligible volume | No population/cadence | Traceable eligible cohort/cadence, adoption explicitly separate | Observed current annual matching decisions and eligibility |
| V4 incremental value | No plausible residual versus cheapest adequate tool | Named residual, matched panel proposed | Positive measured full-workflow advantage at equal admissibility |
| V5 credibility route | Exact outputs unsupported | Pinned capable model and feasible independent witness plan | Applicable independently qualified reference evidence |

Here V2 is scored conservatively because hours and rates are assumptions; V3 is low because cohort/cadence is not observed. V5 scores describe a route and never award a credibility tier. Earned reference credibility is NOT_DEMONSTRATED for all 15.

## Excitement rubric (tiebreaker only)

Three defined axes use the same 0–5 scale/ranges. **Frontier technology:** 0 ordinary mature catalogue task; 3 advanced integration with concrete bottleneck; 5 important new physical/device architecture. **Investor appeal:** 0 unclear buyer problem; 3 understandable repeat engineering job; 5 compelling expansion hypothesis tied to a buyer workflow. This is subjective narrative appeal, with no TAM, paid traction, investor demand or revenue evidence. **Miner appeal:** 0 inaccessible inputs/prohibitive compute; 3 reproducible moderate-cost optimisation; 5 open bounded geometry task with diverse algorithmic approaches.

Excitement is the arithmetic mean of those axes; uncertainty is [max(0,mean−1),mean,min(5,mean+1)]. It breaks only exact base-index ties among eligible hypotheses, never a failed gate. Package warpage and ordinary RF tuning have the same base index here; warpage's excitement orders that diagnostic tie, without admission.

## V2/V3 and the common index

All 15 economic inputs are **ASSUMPTION low/base/high**, not market facts. V2 = avoidable engineering hours/revision × EUR/hour; V3 = hypothetical teams × matching revisions/team/year. Rates [60,90,120] EUR/hour are scenario inputs reused from #921. The six replacement cards retain #921's effort, cohort and cadence hypotheses; new candidates' inputs are explicitly in data.json. Gross = V2 × V3. Realised/adopted/net benefit and observed annual decisions are HUMAN_INPUT; demonstrated commercial lower bound is zero. Do not equate gross labour released with a cash saving or willingness to pay.

Use the framework index `I = (V2 × V3)/(C2 + 52 × C3_weekly)`, in EUR/EUR, a dimensionless scenario ratio. The defined annual accounting convention has 52 weeks ([52,52,52]); it is not a scientific threshold. Base ranking uses base numerator/base denominator. Display uncertainty uses low gross/high denominator and high gross/low denominator. These are sensitivity corners, not probabilities. Workload-linked value and cost may correlate; overlapping ranges are not a robust ranking. Cross-Challenge scientific scores remain incomparable.

C3 models charged full physical-bank refresh, not one successful question or cheap inference. Explicit sensitivity R=[0.5,1,2] windows/week and E=[20,10,5] windows/refresh gives C3=C2×R/E. R/E is [0.025,0.1,0.4]. These are ASSUMPTION bookkeeping scenarios, not adopted pool or exposure policy. They do not transfer battery/motor draft laws. Different real buyers can reverse this ordering.

## C1/C2 cost ledger

C1 is CPU-hours for a complete design/condition history or excitation panel. No C1 here is measured p50/p95; both fields remain null. RAM ranges are ASSUMPTION in data.json. Meshing, convergence, extraction, retried failures and witness tools require a measured ledger later. Timing one matrix solve or one stroke point is inadequate.

For the common bank scenario let primary=designs×mandatory strata. Equivalent cases U=primary + 2×refined cases + charged failures + 8×2 independent-witness cases. Draft primary/refinement/failure/witness counts and the refinement multiplier are **ASSUMPTION**, reused exactly for #921 cards. Witness costs equal the primary complete case is an unverified assumption. The solenoid C1 is the complete nine-stroke by three-current panel inherited from #921, not one field solve. The proposed planar C1 includes all winding excitation states of the frozen winding topology; widening topology requires repricing. Other histories are specified in their dossiers.

`C2 = 1.37 × (U × C1 × 1 + 4) × 1.19 + 10 EUR`.

EUR1.37/node-hour is the user price [1.37,1.37,1.37], corroborated approximately by HETZ; no discount or core-count division. Elapsed/CPU ratio [1,1,1], setup/idle/fit allowance [4,4,4] node-hours, tax stress [1.19,1.19,1.19], other-charge reserve [10,10,10] EUR and incremental buyer-tool licence [0,0,0] EUR are **ASSUMPTION** inherited from #921. Tax stress is not a tax/legal conclusion. Fees exceeding reserves or an unavailable witness licence fail affordability. Acquisition is shared with baseline fitting when rights allow; fit/benchmark overhead must fit the allowance or be charged additionally. Human authoring/rights work is not priced: HUMAN_INPUT. No all-in or positive-net-benefit claim is earned.

The C2 high scenario must fit EUR100 to pass the discovery's estimate-based G6. This is not measured C2 acceptance; actual tails and charges still gate collection/qualification. C4 rebuild/inference and retained-verification requirements are HUMAN_INPUT; the separate V4 experiment includes them. Startup and first-panel bills are alternative cumulative scenarios, not summed as two identical setups or divided by hypothetical reuse.

## V4 experiment before broader collection

Freeze the same buyer request population, permitted information and hard admissibility for reference, cheapest tool and fast model. Compare analytical model; literature/industry lookup; interpolation; calibrated response surface; reduced physics; and buyer's current tool. Choose the strongest adequate one, not a knowingly weak neural or coarse solver. Record full acquisition, fit/build, queries, search, retained high-fidelity verification and failed attempts at served revision counts M=[1,10,100] (ASSUMPTION sensitivity from #857), with units and bank reuse stated.

Report agreement on feasible/near-infeasible designs, false-feasible decisions, winner/regret under approved objective, abstentions, reference discrepancies and complete decision latency/cost. Tolerances and pass thresholds are HUMAN_INPUT, selected prospectively by their owners. Reject if the baseline makes the same admissible decisions cheaply. Reject if the reference is inadequate, even when a surrogate is accurate relative to that reference.

## Definitions versus evidence

Dates, IDs, section numbers, actual inventory counts, rubric anchors and source-reported exact values are descriptive/definitional, with degenerate low/base/high if applicable. Every estimate elsewhere is labelled ASSUMPTION. Missing physical limits and population/qualification values are HUMAN_INPUT, not assigned a numeric range. This preserves the difference between implementing a decision and making one.
