## Scope searched

Primary sources reviewed include OEM/supplier engineering publications,
vendor customer cases, academic/conference studies and an employer job posting.
Searches covered fast-charge calibration/programme cadence, battery thermal
DOE, robot magnetic design programmes and per-study counts, package warpage
DOE/NPI cadence, and customer-specific solenoid development. Selected query
strings:

- `battery fast charge calibration programmes per year simulation`
- `motor design robot per year simulation supplier`
- `site.mathworks.com fast charging battery thermal management customer`
- `site.kollmorgen.com Universal Robots lightweight helpers`

This is a bounded public-source search, not proof that no confidential or
unreviewed public annual ledger exists. The sources retained in
[evidence.json](evidence.json) did not publish annual frequency for the exact
engineering questions.

| Vendor blanket speedups or simulation-sector spend | Not the buyer's matched-admissibility incremental benefit or annual exact decision count |

No salary, shipment, sector revenue, marketing speedup or anonymous purchasing
story was used to raise a score.

## A receipt that would close the gap

Recommend an owner-approved buyer interview or rights-cleared aggregate log.
No outreach was performed. The minimum record is:

| Field | What to obtain |
|---|---|
| Identity and buying role | Company/team and accountable calibration, magnetics, packaging or actuator design role; purchaser and benefit recipient |
| Annual denominator | Dated reporting window, distinct in-scope programmes and shortlist-changing revisions; classify new design, variant, recalibration and carried-over family |
| Simulation ledger | Attempted/failed/successful complete reference panels, per-panel physical scope and commands, cheap predictions, cached reuse, setup and validation; named tools and hardware |
| Frequency and triggers | Product releases, chemistry/material changes, new thermal context, supplier changes and measurement recalibration; actual occurrence dates |
| Eligibility | Fraction of decisions inside the exact packet; for battery deployment, eligible sessions after state/chemistry/operating-context and adoption filters |
| Before/after outcome | Same hard limits and reference quality; accepted design, decision error/rework, engineer review hours, elapsed critical-path time, compute/test expenditure and downstream value |
| Attribution and rights | Distinguish solver/model/search effects; confirm reuse rights and who captures value; exclude overlapping time, labour and test-cost savings |

Count an outsourced OEM/supplier project once. Count new reference work when
the previous verified library is genuinely inadequate. Preserve infeasible
and reference-failed cases; do not retrospectively count only successful
programmes.

All missing quantities stay `HUMAN_INPUT` with
**HUMAN_INPUT / HUMAN_INPUT / HUMAN_INPUT**. A buyer estimate, if later
provided, must be labelled ASSUMPTION and carry its range/source; it does not
become an observed log.

## Proposed decision

Accept these provenance/unit corrections if useful; leave numeric V2/V3
scenario values and all scientific feasibility/admissibility limits unchanged.
Prioritize a buyer log for battery engineering revisions and motor custom
magnetic revisions before presenting annual value as verified. For solenoid,
obtain the incumbent metamodel rebuild/reuse ledger. For warpage, obtain the
full thermal-history DOE/FE ledger. Adoption belongs to the owner.
