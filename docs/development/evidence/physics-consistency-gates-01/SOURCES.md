## Primary external sources

- **S01: [PyBaMM thermal equations](https://docs.pybamm.org/en/v23.5_a/source/examples/notebooks/models/thermal-models.html).** Lumped and distributed thermal balances; signed reversible heating. Historical explanatory manual, not proof of the exact pinned implementation.
- **S02: [PyBaMM DFN equations](https://docs.pybamm.org/en/v26.4.2/source/examples/notebooks/models/DFN.html).** Solid/electrolyte current and lithium balances, boundary conditions, and Doyle–Fuller–Newman origin. Current reduced outputs do not expose these fields.
- **S03: [Pinned OKane2022 parameters](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/blob/79fb27f190d2be468bec704f735ade20389dd93a/packages/pybamm/src/pybamm/input/parameters/lithium_ion/OKane2022.py).** Exact pybamm-v26.8.0.0 release: nominal capacity 5 Ah; both OCP entropic coefficients zero. Confirmed through GitHub file API, blob 3533fb6915747df7584d2c66258f0ce8dd06cb73.
- **S04: [Pinned lumped thermal implementation](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/blob/79fb27f190d2be468bec704f735ade20389dd93a/packages/pybamm/src/pybamm/models/submodels/thermal/lumped.py).** RHS includes total heating, contact heating and cooling divided by effective volumetric heat capacity. API-confirmed blob a0305e23cf0ef85b1caac8667d765834f3b88671.
- **S05: [Pinned total-heating implementation](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/blob/79fb27f190d2be468bec704f735ade20389dd93a/packages/pybamm/src/pybamm/models/submodels/thermal/base_thermal.py).** Total contains ohmic, reaction, reversible, mixing and hysteresis terms; zero entropic coefficients alone do not establish the sign of every term. API-confirmed blob 6a00e1ef702f9ad4ccefad725abb22c87333d31f.
- **S06: [Pinned charge integrator](https://github.com/pybamm-team/PyBaMM/blob/79fb27f190d2be468bec704f735ade20389dd93a/packages/pybamm/src/pybamm/models/submodels/external_circuit/discharge_throughput.py).** Discharge-capacity derivative I/3600; throughput derivative abs(I)/3600. These state traces differ from Carbon's cycle discharge endpoint outputs. API-confirmed blob 4037f01a9c9cf6d544fc742f9fd8a1b12c9e6fbf.
- **S07: [Richardson and Korotkin, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01590).** DFN chemical-energy conservation needs stored energy and spatial loss terms. Do not substitute terminal I*V or an inferred heat trace for this full balance.
- **S08: [Haus and Melcher, Electromagnetic Fields and Energy §11.7](https://web.mit.edu/6.013_book/www/chapter11/11.7.html).** Virtual-work/coenergy and reciprocal terminal relations. Current-held derivative differs from a derivative along a rotating-current trajectory.
- **S09: [COMSOL force calculation](https://www.comsol.com/blogs/modeling-magnetic-bearings-in-comsol-multiphysics/).** Independent Maxwell-stress and virtual-work routes; torque-only outputs cannot compare them.
- **S10: [Elmer Models Manual, CSC, 2026, §§1.2.1 and 1.2.5](https://www.nic.funet.fi/index/elmer/doc/ElmerModelsManual.pdf).** Conduction/convection energy equation and Robin/symmetry boundaries. Linearity fails with temperature-dependent coefficients or radiation.
- **S11: [MIT heat-equation maximum principle notes](https://www.ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-303-linear-partial-differential-equations-fall-2006/d11b374a85c3fde55ec971fe587f8a50_heateqni.pdf).** Elementary source-free Dirichlet maximum principle. The report's nonnegative-source and passive-Robin comparisons are stated derivations for the declared model, not a quotation claiming this note covers all boundary models.
- **S12: [OpenCFD CHT solver source](https://api.openfoam.com/2506/chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam_8C_source.html).** Steady conjugate heat transfer solver; Carbon's pinned deck and postprocessor own the exact model and retained balance terms.
- **S13: [BIPM SI](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/) and [SI Brochure, ninth edition](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/41483022/SI-Brochure-9-EN.pdf/2d2b50bf-f2b4-9661-f402-5f9d66e4b507?t=1671101192839&version=1.11).** Unit definitions, Celsius/Kelvin conversion and coherent dimensions. Correct dimensions do not identify a physically correct numerical prediction.
- **S14: [Angelopoulos and Bates, 2022 tutorial](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511).** Split conformal intervals, calibration separation and marginal-coverage interpretation; not individual-design safety.
- **S15: [Tibshirani et al., NeurIPS 2019](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/8fb21ee7a2207526da55a679f0332de2-Abstract.html).** Covariate-shift conformal extensions require additional assumptions/weights.
- **S16: [Fannjiang et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03613).** Model-guided design creates feedback covariate shift; ordinary random-point coverage need not cover optimizer-selected points.
- **S17: [Ovadia et al., NeurIPS 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02530).** Uncertainty behaviour under dataset shift needs direct testing.
- **S18: [Guo et al., ICML 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html).** Classification confidence calibration; temperature scaling is not a calibration method for a degC regression trace.
