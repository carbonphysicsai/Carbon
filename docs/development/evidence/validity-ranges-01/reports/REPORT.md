## Owner summary

The sources support a limited battery voltage-test anchor and a cooling property-fit band. They do **not** establish a physically validated joint envelope for the current fast-charge/ageing reference, an unidentified motor magnet, or f02's unnamed TIM. A solver completing a case does not close those gaps.

- **Battery:** preserve the published voltage window as a proposed reference cutoff. Use the published temperature and rate conditions as anchors for a prospective applicability study; do not describe the current fast-charge, lumped-temperature or degradation outputs as physically validated over a box. The source-calibrated anchor does not cover the current charging family.
- **Motor:** identify the actual magnet grade and its temperature-dependent demagnetization curve before declaring a physical field/temperature envelope. Arnold N35SH is a concrete optional source family, not a retroactive identification of Carbon's material. A torque-only model can retain a model-relative claim while demagnetization safety remains unresolved.
- **f02:** the honest present regime is transient, constant-property solid conduction with the declared interfaces and boundaries. Real package temperature bounds need material identities and coefficient/phase/contact evidence. The job's temperature ceiling is not that evidence.
- **Cooling:** the queue describes an older scorer. Current main already defaults to both fluid extrema in **30–99 °C** and corrected outlet **Re ≤2,000** ([R5/R6](../SOURCES.md)). Retain the local temperature band; do not widen it to upstream metadata. The Re cap is a chosen laminar-model restriction, not universal experimental proof. Phase, geometry and cached-map applicability remain separate checks.

The smallest owner decisions are the claim tier for battery and f02; the motor material identity and knee margin; and cooling's geometry-specific regime evidence or conservative provisional cap. All proposed endpoints, margins and extensions remain HUMAN_INPUT. None changes P/Q/w, rewards a candidate for invalid reference evidence, or grants launch qualification.

## Reading the tables

An **anchor** is a documented test or source-simulated condition, not an approved continuous interval. An **implementation bound** is already in Carbon's development source, not a sourced physical validation claim. A **proposal** is always HUMAN_INPUT. Apply any adopted bounds to all relevant trusted reference states over the evaluated trajectory; ambient/inlet temperature alone is insufficient.

For exact source constants, low = base = high = the displayed value. Where a source gives a min/nominal/max, that triple is shown explicitly. A documented interval has exact sourced endpoints; its middle/base value is **not supplied** unless the source supplies one. For unsupported bounds and uncertainty margins, low/base/high are all **HUMAN_INPUT**. These conventions are provenance labels, not confidence intervals.
