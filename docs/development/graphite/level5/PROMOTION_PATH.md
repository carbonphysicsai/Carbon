# Construction capability promotion: empty registered set

PLT-32/S4 implements OWNER-CONSTRUCTION-ANYTHING-01 section6 and the S4
construction plan. A measured L5 win belongs in the **base of a future combined
contract**, available to everyone. It is not a new permission level, private
arm, frontier event, score, reward, qualification or deployment decision.

`carbon.reconstruction.construction_promotions` defines a prospective record
and an EMPTY registered application set. A record names its Challenge,
capability, exact source base/composition digests, content-addressed evidence
references and a decision reference. `target` is
`NEXT_COMBINED_CONTRACT_BASE`; no next version or digest is invented here.
Source references resolve against immutable composition and expansion history.
Returned record documents are copies, not mutable registry authority.

The evidence and decision references are pointers. Schema validity does not
prove that a measurement ran, a candidate won, or a human signed off. The
promotion bar (which evidence and whose sign-off) remains **HUMAN_INPUT**,
represented by null in the registry. `promote(record)` therefore refuses with
`promotion_bar_unset`; a forged non-null bar refuses as unregistered. No
request/env acceptance Boolean exists. Nonempty registry application refuses
before compilation rather than silently discarding entries or admitting them.

With the empty set, `base_contract(challenge)` returns the existing exact base
object; `composition_document(name_or_digest)` returns the original metadata;
`compile` delegates to the unchanged composition compiler and its budget
default. The promotion helper installs no new miner/validator route. Existing
callers retain required-check, transport and containment responsibilities.
No S3-owned source, registered pin, expansion history or served config changes.

The three candidates in L5_CANDIDATES_2026-10-12.md are still UNMEASURED and are
not in the set. PLT-27 build readiness and OPT-17 comparison receipts do not
constitute adoption. Synthetic comparison results and registration alone never
open the bar. No protected/holdout data, provider or host execution is needed
for this empty-set mechanism.

A future nonempty promotion needs the owner's registered evidence/sign-off
bar, verified measured evidence, its recorded decision and a prospective base
and combined-contract implementation/version with normal expansion history.
This slice deliberately supplies no guessed positive policy. Original contracts
and evidence keep their identities; real measured adoption is still unavailable.
S5 serving remains a separate owner-go action. Default required-check activation
and the named dual-loss refusal remain unchanged. This earns DEVELOPMENT
record/empty-application software evidence, not scientific/security acceptance.
