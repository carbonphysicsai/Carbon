# Carbon Decisions v2 — Integrated Decision Continuation

**Status:** OWNER-RECOMMENDED integrated decision ledger for review.  
**Relationship to v1:** D-001 through D-050 in `docs/context/Decisions.md` remain historical/reconciled decisions except where a later decision below explicitly supersedes their terminal interpretation. This file continues the ledger rather than rewriting that provenance.

- **D-051 Qualified exam first:** before LIVE authority, Carbon qualifies task semantics, target population, SamplingPlan, generator conformance, reference/truth policy, representation, measurements, statistical sufficiency, secrecy, and limitations through the Validation Dossier.
- **D-052 Envelope != population != sampling != weighting:** `P(x)`, `Q(x)`, and score/evidence weighting `w(x)` remain separate semantics. Sampling frequency never silently becomes real-world prevalence or scientific importance.
- **D-053 Generator is implementation, not truth authority:** deterministic generation does not certify labels or population. Generator conformance and reference/truth adequacy are separate dossier claims.
- **D-054 Challenge-specific truth policy:** truth/reference authority is Challenge-specific. Julia/SciML is one possible qualified backend/witness, not a universal oracle.
- **D-055 Truth-dominance stop-ship:** within qualified resolution, generator-specific numerical error must not be rewarded over agreement with qualified physical truth.
- **D-056 Candidate input completeness:** every causal input needed to determine the registered target must be supplied through the candidate contract or fixed by the Challenge.
- **D-057 Score Pack = Evidence Use Contract:** generalized Score Pack converts already-qualified evidence into eligibility, admissibility, Challenge-bound estimands, soft objectives, and deterministic ranking evidence. ScoreEngine does not invent science.
- **D-058 Admissibility precedes ranking:** `physics > loss` means mandatory scientific failure cannot be compensated by soft performance. P0 45/30/25 is a pack profile, not the constitutional definition.
- **D-059 Explicit result states:** preserve at least `REJECTED_INVALID`, `FAILED_INFRA`, `SCIENTIFIC_INADMISSIBLE`, `INDETERMINATE_EVIDENCE`, and `VALID_RANKED`; do not collapse all non-success into scalar zero.
- **D-060 Cross-Challenge scores not automatically comparable:** ScoreResult is Challenge-bound evidence. Base portfolio economics does not pool raw scores across Challenges.
- **D-061 Frontier reward replaces incumbent rent:** the base performance market rewards verified frontier advance. Remaining the incumbent does not create a new performance entitlement. This supersedes continuous score-proportional / winner-decay designs as the owner-recommended future architecture.
- **D-062 New leader is an evidence state:** leader replacement is determined by a registered `LeaderReplacementPolicy`, not merely `new_score > old_score`. Where variance matters, incumbent and challengers share a fresh common promotion experiment.
- **D-063 Batched frontier settlement:** default performance settlement selects at most one strongest verified frontier advance per Challenge/window; arrival order does not determine scientific reward.
- **D-064 FrontierBaseline required:** every reward-enabled Challenge begins with an independently registered frontier baseline. The first miner must advance it under the same promotion doctrine.
- **D-065 Equal Challenge opportunity:** a frozen `ChallengeSetEpoch` of `N` reward-enabled qualified Challenges gives each equal notional performance opportunity `1/N`. This is a breadth policy, not a claim of equal difficulty/value.
- **D-066 No automatic unused-slot redistribution:** a Challenge with no frontier event creates no performance entitlement for that period, and its notional opportunity is not silently reassigned to other Challenge winners.
- **D-067 Phase-0 breadth:** P0 launch slice proves one Challenge; Phase-0 expansion targets roughly 4–7 concurrent qualified academic Challenges, preserving the original seven-PDE breadth ambition where feasible.
- **D-068 Frontier event bridges science to settlement:** `FrontierAdvanceEvent` is the normal bridge from scientific promotion to performance payout and binds exact Challenge, prior frontier, winning method, promotion evidence, Score Pack/Dossier identity, recipient, and entitlement.
- **D-069 Treasury settlement separation:** preferred future transport sends miner-side subnet emission to a separately governed treasury neuron/vault. Scientific evidence determines frontier state; treasury governance verifies/executes event-bound entitlement. Treasury is not a scientific judge.
- **D-070 Event-bound treasury proposals:** performance payout proposal binds an exact finalized `FrontierAdvanceEvent` digest and is protected against amount/recipient mutation and double payment.
- **D-071 Scientific state survives payout failure:** chain/RPC/governance/timelock failure may create `PAYOUT_PENDING_*` but cannot rewrite a confirmed frontier advance.
- **D-072 Treasury requires chain qualification:** treasury route is owner-recommended architecture, not production fact. Localnet/testnet must verify treasury-neuron emission behavior, Bittensor burn semantics, transfer precompiles, governance, rate limits, double-pay protection, and Challenge accounting before production.
- **D-073 Landscape cannot dynamically reweight base portfolio:** Landscape may propose Challenge activation/retirement, future versions, and separate information bounties. It may not dynamically change equal base performance opportunity inside a frozen `ChallengeSetEpoch`.
- **D-074 Information value remains separate:** novelty, replication, ablation, transfer, and uncertainty reduction may receive separate future bounties; they do not contaminate the performance frontier event.
- **D-075 Burgers first authoritative repair:** owner-recommended first authoritative Burgers slice fixes `nu=5e-3`, keeps `u0 -> u(T)`, uses qualified periodic Cole–Hopf primary reference, replaces the mischaracterized final-time residual as mandatory authority with qualified final-state measurements, and measures reconstruction/rank stability before final promotion thresholds.
- **D-076 Historical PoC immutability:** old Burgers PoC code/config/results remain historical evidence. The repaired Challenge receives new identities; do not retroactively relabel old runs as qualified under the new science.
- **D-077 Canon/design/paper authority order:** scientific canon constrains claims; domain design specs define mechanisms; runtime specs/code implement ratified mechanisms; papers/deck communicate without becoming protocol authority.
- **D-078 Documentation status is explicit:** superseded and historical documents remain available for provenance but must be labeled through `DOCUMENTATION_STATUS.md`; engineers should not infer authority from recency or filename alone.
