# Review These Preliminary Decisions — Investor / Financial Engine

**Status:** owner-recommended decisions for founder/finance/investor review.  
**Scope:** company business model only. Scientific authority is unaffected.

---

## IF1 — Beachhead verticals

**Recommendation:** concentrate first customer discovery on two of:

1. aerospace/defense/space;
2. energy/turbomachinery/industrial physics;
3. engineering/CAE software vendors.

Do not spread founder GTM across every simulation-heavy vertical simultaneously.

**Decision needed:** select first two.

---

## IF2 — Evidence Audit first wedge

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Use Evidence Audit as the simplest paid proof of Carbon's independent-evidence value.

---

## IF3 — Sponsored Discovery second product

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Use as the first product demonstrating network-backed discovery and multi-rail revenue.

---

## IF4 — Fiat-first procurement

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Do not force customer Alpha acquisition.

---

## IF5 — Revenue mix target

**Recommendation:** orient the model toward increasing recurring/software/OEM share over time rather than setting a rigid near-term percentage before real customers exist.

---

## IF6 — Scenario pricing bands

**Recommendation:** use the gauntlet pricing bands only for investor/unit-economics sensitivity modeling. Do not publish them as price cards before 10–20 buyer conversations and at least one serious proposal.

---

## IF7 — Gross-margin objective

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT DIRECTION. The business should demonstrate improving gross margin as workflows productize; do not lock a specific 5-year margin before paid delivery data.

---

## IF8 — Sponsor reward accounting

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Treat sponsor participant rewards separately from company platform/service revenue in internal economics.

---

## IF9 — Success fees

**Recommendation:** optional upside only. Never make core Carbon revenue solely dependent on a positive scientific outcome.

---

## IF10 — Enterprise platform timing

**Recommendation:** do not build a broad standalone platform before repeated customer programs reveal the minimum recurring workflow. Build platform primitives behind paid delivery first.

---

## IF11 — OEM strategy

**Recommendation:** HIGH PRIORITY after direct customer proof. CAE/engineering software partners can provide distribution and strengthen Carbon's neutral evidence-layer position.

---

## IF12 — Private deployment

**Recommendation:** HIGH PRIORITY commercialization dependency for defense/aerospace and proprietary solver customers. Start with customer-hosted truth RPC / controlled VPC before full air-gap.

---

## IF13 — Capital planning

**Recommendation:** model an 18–24 month seed runway and a hypothetical $3M–$5M financing envelope, but lock actual raise size only after bottom-up hiring/compute/legal planning and initial customer evidence.

---

## IF14 — Headcount philosophy

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Stay senior/small initially; hire against demonstrated bottlenecks, not an imagined mature org chart.

---

## IF15 — Investor market-size posture

**Recommendation:** avoid inflated TAM-first storytelling. Use large engineering-software recurring-revenue incumbents as category evidence, then prove Carbon's wedge bottom-up through buyer count, ACV, expansion, platform/OEM, and vertical adjacency.

---

## IF16 — Alpha narrative

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Investor case is OpCo-first. Alpha utility grows through commercially useful subnet work; do not promise automatic OpCo revenue pass-through or token returns.

---

## IF17 — Network leverage proof

**Recommendation:** make network leverage an explicit empirical business milestone. Compare network-backed discovery with an internal/centralized comparator on cost, diversity, time, and frontier performance.

---

## IF18 — First recurring product

**Recommendation:** treat lifecycle/requalification as the likely first natural recurring attach to technical programs, while enterprise platform subscription develops from repeated multi-program customers.

---

## IF19 — Investor KPI discipline

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. Build a KPI dashboard now that evolves from design-partner metrics to paid-pilot economics to ARR/NRR/CAC once those metrics genuinely exist.

---

## IF20 — Commercial invalidation conditions

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT. If customers will not pay for independent evidence, delivery remains permanently bespoke, expansion is weak, or network leverage cannot be demonstrated, revise the business architecture rather than defending it rhetorically.

---
