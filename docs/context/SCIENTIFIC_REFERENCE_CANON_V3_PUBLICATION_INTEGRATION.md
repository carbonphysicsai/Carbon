## Revised scientific identity

> **Carbon is an incentivized physics-intelligence system for discovering better ways to construct fast physical models.** Carbon begins with neural-operator training strategies, but its deeper scientific loop is technology-neutral: participants propose model-construction interventions; validators independently reconstruct and evaluate them under protected, registered scientific contracts; provenance-rich successes and failures become experimental memory; and Landscape is intended to learn which interventions transfer across physical structures and regimes while preserving the distinction between prediction and causation. Selected artifacts move through a separate, context-of-use qualification path with explicit answerability and escalation boundaries. Bittensor supplies the competitive economic substrate; physics and qualified evidence supply the external constraint. Carbon's own experiments must establish whether this loop actually improves future search, scientific evidence, qualification, and engineering decisions.

## Short-form thesis hierarchy

**Scientific / whitepaper:** Carbon is an incentivized experimental system for discovering, independently testing, learning from, and qualifying methods for constructing fast physical models.

**Litepaper:** Carbon turns open competition into evidence about how to build fast, trustworthy physics models.

**Summit stage:** Carbon pays people and agents to find better ways to build fast physics models — starting with neural operators — and independently tests what survives.

## Reconciliation summary

The symbolic-numeric expansion changes Carbon's scientific case in four ways: broaden the long-term object without broadening P0; make measurement explicit; make physics intelligence prospective; and make composition evidence-bounded. It does **not** reposition Carbon as a symbolic-computing company, SciML framework, automatic verification engine, or generic model marketplace.

> **Bittensor creates the competitive search pressure. Carbon defines the scientific objective and independent evidence process. Physics remains the authority no producer, model family, symbolic representation, or learned intelligence layer can vote away.**
