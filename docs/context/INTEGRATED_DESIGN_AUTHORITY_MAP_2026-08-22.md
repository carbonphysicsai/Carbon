- production treasury deployment;
- production-qualified Burgers Challenge;
- empirically proven subnet-wide correct incentives;
- proven superiority over centralized search;
- successful seven-Challenge LIVE operation.

> Integrated design gauntlets identified and corrected concrete Challenge defects, including a missing causal input, an under-resolved generator regime, a truth-vs-generator ranking inversion, a mischaracterized residual diagnostic, and material reconstruction variance.
