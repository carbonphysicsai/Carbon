# Carbon Commercial Operating Kit v1

**Status:** working commercial playbook for founder/GTM/product use.  
**Purpose:** turn the buyer-discovery gauntlet into reusable sales operating tools across Carbon's product portfolio.

---

# 1. Universal discovery call structure

## Opening

Do not open with Bittensor, Alpha, or internal architecture.

Use:

> **Where are fast physical models already valuable to you, and what prevents you from relying on them more broadly?**

Follow with:

1. What physics/simulation workflow matters?
2. What decision/job depends on it?
3. Do you already have a fast model?
4. What is the trusted reference today?
5. Where does the current process fail or become too expensive?
6. Who owns technical acceptance?
7. Who owns the budget?
8. What data/solver/IP cannot leave your environment?
9. What would make a pilot obviously valuable?
10. What would happen next if the pilot succeeds?

# 3. Discovery scorecard

Score 0, 1, or 2 internally.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain | vague | real but not urgent | costly/urgent |
| Decision value | unclear | useful | mission/business critical |
| Truth path | absent | possible | accessible |
| Champion | none | interested | active owner |
| Economic buyer | unknown | identifiable | engaged |
| Budget | none | plausible | allocated/strong path |
| Trigger | none | medium-term | current deadline |
| Security feasibility | blocked | uncertain | workable |
| Deliverable clarity | vague | partial | explicit |
| Expansion | none | possible | obvious |
| Repeatability | pure bespoke | partly reusable | standardizable |
| Network fit | none | optional | differentiated |

Do not mechanically reject from total score; use the score to surface why an opportunity is weak.

# 4. Evidence Audit pilot SOW skeleton

## Objective

Independently evaluate `[candidate/model]` for `[defined engineering job]` under `[registered evidence scope]`.

## Customer provides

- candidate artifact/API;
- intended use and input/output semantics;
- truth/reference access;
- technical contacts;
- security/privacy requirements.

## Carbon provides

- scoped Challenge/evidence design;
- truth/reference integration;
- independent evaluation;
- evidence/failure report;
- provenance package;
- recommended next action.

## Included budget

Explicitly define:

- number of candidate artifacts;
- reference calls/cases or compute budget;
- target strata/regimes;
- adapter scope;
- delivery period;
- review sessions.

## Out of scope unless added

- guaranteed model improvement;
- universal certification;
- production deployment;
- arbitrary new truth adapter;
- unlimited additional models/cases;
- Product Qualification unless specifically included.

## Commercial success

Delivery of the agreed independent evidence program and report, including honest negative results.

# 5. Sponsored Discovery pilot SOW skeleton

## Objective

Discover and independently verify methods that improve the registered frontier for `[physical modeling problem]`.

## Work packages

1. Challenge/feasibility authoring.
2. Truth integration.
3. Challenge qualification.
4. participant/network campaign.
5. evaluation and frontier promotion.
6. sponsor report.
7. optional winner/candidate handoff.
8. optional qualification/deployment.

## Commercial structure

Separate:

```text
Carbon service/program fee
truth/evaluation budget
participant reward pool
reward administration fee
optional follow-on services
```

## No-winner clause

A scientifically valid `NO_FRONTIER_ADVANCE` is a completed research outcome. Sponsor reward disposition follows prospective contract terms.

# 9. Vertical outbound hypotheses

## Aerospace / space / defense

Hypothesis:

> Teams building fast models for design exploration, surrogate-assisted optimization, or digital engineering will pay for independent evidence if it reduces uncertainty around where those models may safely influence engineering decisions.

Lead targets:
- simulation/digital engineering leaders;
- surrogate-model/SciML teams;
- V&V/model credibility teams;
- innovation/R&D programs.

Do not promise safety certification.

## Energy / turbomachinery / industrial physics

Hypothesis:

> Organizations repeatedly running expensive thermal/flow/structural simulations will pay for independent evidence and model lifecycle infrastructure where fast-model failures in rare regimes limit adoption.

Lead targets:
- digital twin leaders;
- rotating equipment / turbine modeling teams;
- simulation platform leads;
- R&D/optimization teams.

## CAE / engineering software

Hypothesis:

> Vendors can use Carbon as an independent evidence and future discovery layer behind their existing model-generation and simulation workflows rather than building every credibility workflow themselves.

Lead targets:
- product GMs;
- platform CTOs;
- AI/SciML product leaders;
- strategic partnerships.

# 10. Objection library

## “You don't have enough customer history yet.”

Correct. Offer a tightly scoped paid design-partner pilot with explicit maturity labeling and measurable deliverables.

## “Why not use benchmark datasets?”

Benchmarks are useful, but Carbon's commercial value proposition is job-specific, qualified evidence under a defined physical population and context.

## “Why do we need independent evaluation?”

Because model producer and grader incentives differ, and the customer needs evidence a separate technical reviewer can inspect.

## “Why a network?”

For eligible discovery programs, the network is intended to expand search supply and diversity. Carbon must demonstrate this empirically; it is not asserted as automatically superior.

## “Can you guarantee you'll find a better model?”

No. Carbon can contract to run a qualified search/evidence program, not manufacture scientific progress.

## “Can our solver stay private?”

The target commercial architecture supports customer-hosted truth/VPC/on-prem modes, but those deployments must be implemented and security-qualified before they are sold as production capability.

## “Do we have to buy tokens?”

No. Enterprise procurement can remain fiat-first.

# 12. Design-partner selection criteria

Prefer customers where:

- pain is real and technically specific;
- a decision depends on the model;
- truth is accessible;
- buyer and technical authority are engaged;
- pilot can complete without building five future product layers;
- customer accepts honest negative scientific outcomes;
- privacy/rights can be defined;
- follow-on opportunity exists;
- engagement teaches reusable product infrastructure.

Avoid prestige-only logos with no budget, no truth path, or impossible scope.
