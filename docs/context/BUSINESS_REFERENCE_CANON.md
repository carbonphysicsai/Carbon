# Carbon Business Reference Canon v1

**Status:** OWNER-CANONICAL business section of the Carbon canon.  
**Relationship to scientific canon:** companion to `SCIENTIFIC_REFERENCE_CANON.md`. The scientific canon governs evidence/claim discipline for Carbon's scientific thesis; this business canon governs durable company/business principles, business claim discipline, and commercial strategy outside the scientific judge.  
**Canonical detailed source:** [`../../Business/Business_Canon.md`](../../Business/Business_Canon.md).

---

# Business canon synthesis

> **Carbon is building the discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models.**

> **Services get Carbon into the customer. Platform makes the relationship recurring. The network makes discovery scalable. Qualification increases contract value. Lifecycle increases retention. Physics intelligence compounds the moat.**

> **Build a real company, not a token-dependent story.**

> **Every new commercial engagement should, where compatible with customer rights and scientific integrity, create revenue today, reusable product infrastructure for tomorrow, and additional economically useful work for the Carbon network.**

> **Commercial pressure does not rewrite scientific truth.**

---

# Business claim discipline

Carbon should distinguish:

```text
BUSINESS DESIGN
        !=
CUSTOMER-DISCOVERY EVIDENCE
        !=
PAID PILOT
        !=
REPEATABLE SERVICE
        !=
RECURRING REVENUE
        !=
PLATFORMIZATION
        !=
NETWORK LEVERAGE
```

Investor and public materials must not present a designed revenue stream, assumed price, illustrative margin, or future product as commercial traction.

# Durable business laws

1. **Carbon OpCo must stand on its own without speculative Alpha appreciation.**
2. **Services are the wedge, not the terminal margin structure.**
3. **Repeated expert workflows should become productized infrastructure.**
4. **The first preferred SKU is Carbon Evidence Audit.**
5. **The first preferred network-differentiated SKU is Sponsored Discovery.**
6. **Qualification and lifecycle are recurring revenue layers, not afterthoughts.**
7. **Enterprise platform, API/OEM, and marketplace are scale layers.**
8. **Physics Intelligence is sold only after prospective utility is demonstrated.**
9. **Customer pricing never changes the scientific standard for the same registered claim.**
10. **Negative scientific findings can still constitute successful commercial delivery.**
11. **Customer data/method evidence compounds only where rights permit.**
12. **Strategic neutrality across CAE, model builders, engineering-AI, and infrastructure providers is valuable.**
13. **Network/decentralization advantage must be empirically demonstrated.**
14. **Enterprise fiat-first purchasing is permitted and expected.**
15. **OpCo revenue does not automatically create Alpha value.**
16. **Alpha utility should arise from genuine economically useful network work.**
17. **Market size should be modeled bottom-up and overlapping top-down markets must not be blindly added.**
18. **If delivery scales only with bespoke headcount, the business architecture has failed.**
19. **Investor materials separate actuals, pipeline, planning assumptions, and scenarios.**
20. **The business and the scientific judge remain separate authorities.**

For the complete business canon, product architecture, GTM, financial engine, network-value design, and unresolved lead decisions, use the `Business/` folder on `main`.
