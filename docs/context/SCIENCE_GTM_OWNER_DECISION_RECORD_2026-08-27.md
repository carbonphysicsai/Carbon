## Decisions

1. **First qualified judge:** One qualified fixed-viscosity Burgers Challenge remains sufficient for the first qualified-testnet scientific gate.
2. **MMS scope:** A generic MMS Factory is not a universal testnet or mainnet prerequisite. Challenge-specific MMS evidence may still be required where the relevant Dossier calls for it.
3. **Hybrid qualification identity:** The exact product/system qualification identity is deferred to a post-launch owner review. Prelaunch work may preserve a compatibility seam and must preserve the no-silent-composition invariant, but may not ratify a production hybrid qualification object from this decision.
4. **Wave B integration:** The minimum compatibility amendments in the Science/GTM integration plan are approved for incorporation into existing Wave B acceptance criteria. They do not authorize an expanded Wave B program.
5. **Parallel research before mainnet:** Mainnet work has priority. A bounded MMS, reference, portfolio, or hybrid research ticket may run before mainnet only when it does not contend with the launch-critical implementation, scientific review, security review, protocol review, owner-decision, or incident-response path. Each ticket requires separate owner authorization and pauses upon material contention.
6. **Commercial discovery ownership:** Customer-job intake and thermal/laminar-CHT discovery are owned by the Carbon project owner and `Harshaa765`. This assigns Strategy work only; it does not transfer scientific threshold, Dossier, Score Pack, Product Battery, or qualification authority.
7. **Post-Burgers regime:** The first regime after Burgers remains intentionally unresolved until reference evidence and customer learning are reviewed prospectively.

## Immediate roadmap effects

- Finish Wave A without adding Science/GTM scope to A11 or A12.
