# Carbon Scientific Reference Canon v3 — Full Reconciled Canon

**Status:** Research/evidence map and scientific exploration framework for team review  
**Purpose:** Evidence base, argument architecture, and bounded exploration substrate for Carbon's whitepaper, litepaper, scientific positioning, claim audits, Challenge design, Landscape research, and future agentic model-construction discovery.  
**Authority:** This canon does **not** define protocol behavior, scoring, qualification, or LIVE status. Current code and normative Carbon specifications remain authoritative for implementation and intended mechanics. External references support premises; they do not prove Carbon's mechanism works.  
**Scope rule:** Long-term scientific ontology does not broaden P0 implementation scope.

---

# 1. The canon's role

The Carbon canon now has **four related roles**.

## 1.1 Evidence base

The canon records the strongest scientific, engineering, statistical, and mechanism-design evidence relevant to Carbon's thesis. It distinguishes foundational evidence, deeper design rationale, and frontier work.

## 1.2 Argument map

The canon structures the scientific case Carbon can responsibly make in public: what the literature supports, what Carbon specifies, what Carbon still has to demonstrate, and what must remain a hypothesis.

## 1.3 Design and claim-control instrument

The canon helps audit new protocol/product ideas against external science and Carbon's epistemic constitution. It prevents citations from silently becoming protocol authority and prevents implementation plans from being mistaken for tested scientific claims.

## 1.4 Agentic Exploration Zone

The canon also becomes a **bounded scientific exploration substrate for research agents and miners**.

This is an addition to—not a replacement for—the three roles above.

The Agentic Exploration Zone exists so agents can use the canon, Challenge semantics, and accumulated Landscape evidence to generate scientifically grounded hypotheses about training recipes, architectures, representations, model-construction strategies, hybrid decompositions, reduced/symbolic-numeric constructions, truth-acquisition policies, numerical methods, measurements, experiments, and eventually new construction algorithms.

The exploration zone is **generative, not authoritative**.

A canon-supported idea is not thereby correct. A cited method is not thereby preferred. An agent-generated construction is not thereby safe. A proposed measurement is not thereby score-bearing. Every proposal must cross the appropriate independent execution, measurement, evidence, governance, and qualification boundaries.

> **Canon informs hypotheses. Carbon experiments adjudicate them.**

The canon therefore serves as a scientific prior and vocabulary for exploration while preserving Carbon's external judge.

# 2. The scientific case for Carbon

Carbon's public scientific argument should remain a chain of independently supportable premises rather than a list of product features.

## 2.1 Case outline

1. **Fast physical models create a real opportunity.** Neural operators, reduced models, hybrid models, symbolic/numeric reductions, and other approximations can accelerate repeated physical-model queries in suitable regimes.
2. **Predictive fit does not establish engineering credibility.** Low loss alone does not establish physical consistency, robustness, uncertainty behavior, rollout stability, answerability, or fitness for a context of use.
3. **Physics can be a distinct modeling/evaluation objective.** Physical laws, invariants, admissibility conditions, and structural constraints can be evaluated separately from average predictive error.
4. **Engineering credibility is bounded and evidence-backed.** V&V/VVUQ practice treats credibility as dependent on intended use, evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and lifecycle state.
5. **Adaptive search can overfit an exposed exam.** Repeated interaction with evaluation information can invalidate naive holdout guarantees; protected evaluation and controlled feedback are scientifically justified.
6. **Open competition can be useful for uncertain search.** Diverse independent solvers can uncover strong or complementary solutions when outputs can be independently evaluated.
7. **Agentic science makes machine-driven exploration plausible.** Autonomous research systems demonstrate loops of hypothesis generation, execution, analysis, and iteration.
8. **Producer independence requires reproducibility and provenance.** Randomness, software/hardware state, data, construction procedure, execution identity, and reporting quality materially affect computational claims.
9. **Experimental memory can improve future search.** Active learning and autonomous experimentation show that accumulated outcomes can guide subsequent experiments.
10. **Observational memory is not automatically causal knowledge.** Strategy choices and outcomes are confounded by selection, budget, architecture, regime, feedback, and participant behavior.
11. **Surrogates inside optimizers need trust boundaries.** Imperfect fast models can be exploited by downstream optimizers; trust regions, truth calls, answerability, and escalation remain important.
12. **Qualification is a lifecycle, not a benchmark score.** Material changes to artifact, context, measurement, composition, or execution can invalidate prior evidence.
13. **Physical AI is not one model family.** Scientific computing includes learned, reduced, symbolic-numeric, hybrid, compositional, and classical representations; Carbon should test them rather than privilege one by ideology.
14. **Measurement is its own scientific object.** A governing property does not uniquely determine how it should be measured numerically, calibrated, aggregated, or thresholded.
15. **Component credibility does not automatically compose.** Interfaces, coupling realizations, joint envelopes, and assembled behavior require evidence.
16. **Agentic exploration can move beyond tuning into method discovery.** A sufficiently mature Carbon can allow agents to propose new construction algorithms while keeping construction and official evaluation under separate trust domains.
17. **Bittensor supplies a programmable incentive substrate.** Carbon can define validator-side scientific objectives while Bittensor supplies open competition and emissions mechanics.
18. **Carbon's synthesis is falsifiable.** The full system must demonstrate better scientific and engineering decisions than credible alternatives.

## 2.2 Carbon synthesis

> **If credible physical generalization is the unresolved commercial target, make physics-surviving generalization the target of an open intelligence market.**

> **Carbon is an incentivized experimental system for discovering, independently testing, learning from, and qualifying methods for constructing fast physical models.**

The literature establishes the opportunity, known failure modes, and relevant scientific disciplines. It does **not** establish that Carbon's exact scoring, protected-exam design, economic mechanism, Landscape, agentic exploration architecture, or product outcomes are optimal.

# 3. Canon discipline

## 3.1 Evidence tiers

- **Tier I — Foundational:** durable sources appropriate for public scientific claims.
- **Tier II — Deep canon:** whitepaper depth, design rationale, and narrower claims.
- **Tier III — Frontier / Exploration Zone:** recent, developing, or hypothesis-generating work that should not carry foundational claims alone.

## 3.2 Evidence-strength labels

- STANDARD / AUTHORITATIVE GUIDANCE
- FOUNDATIONAL PRIMARY
- PRIMARY EMPIRICAL
- BENCHMARK / DATASET
- REVIEW
- THEORETICAL PRIMARY
- PRIMARY SYSTEMS / SCIENTIFIC-COMPUTING WORK
- FRONTIER PRIMARY

## 3.3 Citation semantics

For public use, distinguish **Supports**, **Does not prove**, and **Use**.

> **External literature supports premises. Carbon specifications define the mechanism. Carbon experiments determine whether the mechanism works.**

## 3.4 Exploration-zone semantics

For agentic use, add:

- **Search affordance:** hypotheses the source can motivate.
- **Applicability conditions:** regimes/assumptions where it may matter.
- **Failure/limitation cues:** reasons it may not transfer.
- **Evidence maturity:** foundational, replicated, benchmarked, or frontier.
- **Prohibited inference:** what must not be promoted from citation to protocol/scientific truth.

The exploration zone should favor structured uncertainty over false completeness.

# 4. Pillars A–N — retained foundations, broadened where required

The detailed v2 entries remain the canonical source inventory for Pillars A–N. Their scientific roles are preserved. The following reconciled interpretations govern their use in v3.

## Pillar A — Learned operators make fast learned physics possible

Retain FNO, Neural Operator, DeepONet and related sources. Neural operators remain Carbon's first model class, not its terminal ontology.

## Pillar B — Predictive fit does not automatically preserve physics

Retain physics-informed, conservation-aware, and structure-preserving sources.

> **Physics > loss means mandatory physical failure cannot be compensated by predictive fit. It does not require explicit symbolic physics inside the candidate.**

## Pillar C — Generalization, robustness, uncertainty, and rollout remain open

Retain PDEBench, UQ, OOD and active-learning sources. Distinguish candidate generalization, intervention transfer, measurement/evidence portability, and qualification generalization.

## Pillar D — Engineering credibility is bounded by context of use

Retain ASME/NASA V&V/VVUQ sources. Extend the principle to assembled systems: component qualification does not automatically compose.

## Pillar E — Adaptive evaluation requires protected information boundaries

Retain adaptive-data-analysis/holdout/leaderboard sources. Machine-readable physics and generated diagnostics add Goodhart surfaces rather than removing the need for protected evaluation.

## Pillar F — Open competition can discover diverse solutions under uncertain search

Retain scientific-contest/innovation-challenge evidence. Novelty itself is not a primary performance objective.

## Pillar G — Agentic science makes closed-loop research plausible

Retain autonomous-lab/research-agent evidence. Distinguish registered-method search from future construction-method discovery.

> **Agent autonomy expands hypothesis generation, not scientific authority.**

## Pillar H — Reproducibility and provenance are first-class scientific objects

Retain reproducibility/provenance evidence.

> **Producer-independent reconstruction is the invariant; fresh retraining is its learned-model subtype.**

## Pillar I — Experimental memory can improve future search

Retain active-learning/autonomous-experimentation evidence. Preserve successes, failures, censoring, reconstruction, measurements, and qualification lineage.

## Pillar J — Observational memory is not causal knowledge

Retain causal-inference foundations. Preserve selection provenance, temporal feature role, measurement identity, and stronger evidence requirements for causal promotion.

## Pillar K — Fast physical models inside optimizers need truth boundaries

Retain surrogate/model-management literature. Add prospective answerability, abstention, routing, and escalation semantics.

## Pillar L — Qualification is a lifecycle

Retain lifecycle credibility evidence. Qualification identity may include assembled artifact graph, physical context, measurements, runtime realization, coverage, and context of use.

## Pillar M — Bittensor is the incentive substrate, not scientific authority

Retain official Bittensor evidence. Consensus coordinates reward; it does not determine physical truth.

## Pillar N — Carbon's thesis remains falsifiable

Retain H1–H18 and maturity labels: MOTIVATED, SPECIFIED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, REPLICATED, PRODUCTION-QUALIFIED.

No external citation can move a Carbon-specific claim from MOTIVATED to TESTED.

# 5. Pillar O — Symbolic-numeric models separate physical semantics from numerical realization

### O1. ModelingToolkit
Ma et al. (2021), *ModelingToolkit: A Composable Graph Transformation System For Equation-Based Modeling* — https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05244

**Supports:** equation-based symbolic representation, composition, structural transformations, and generation of numerical implementations.

**Does not prove:** symbolic correctness, operational equivalence, Carbon dependence on ModelingToolkit, or automatic gate generation.

### O2. Modelica / equation-based acausal modeling
Foundational Modelica literature — https://modelica.org/papers/1998-06-fritzson-para98LNCS1541-equationbasedmodelingandHighPerformance.pdf

**Supports:** equation-based/acausal modeling and reusable physical component composition.

> **Authored physical semantics, transformed symbolic semantics, and numerical realization are related but distinct evidence layers.**

> **Symbolic equivalence does not automatically imply numerical or operational equivalence.**

This is the durable search question.

# 9. Pillar S — Composition creates interface-level evidence and non-compositional qualification

Equation-based modeling supports composition; engineering credibility remains bounded.

Carbon should distinguish component semantics, coupling/interface semantics, numerical coupling realization, component measurements, interface measurements, assembled-system measurements, and product-context measurements.

> **If independently qualified components are assembled, system credibility still requires evidence about interfaces, joint envelope, numerical coupling, and assembled behavior.**

# 10. Pillar T — Physics intelligence must demonstrate prospective decision value

Recommended definition:

> **Physics intelligence is provenance-bearing knowledge about how model-construction interventions interact with physical structure, regime, measurement, and engineering context, demonstrated by improved prospective decisions rather than retrospective narrative alone.**

Requirements include baseline comparison, feature provenance, exact measurement identity, selection provenance, failure/censoring retention, and prospective evaluation.

# 11. Pillar U — Agentic Exploration Zone and construction-algorithm discovery

The canon's new exploration role is grounded in the combination of agentic science, scientific-computing diversity, protected evaluation, and Carbon's producer/judge separation.

## U1. Exploration hierarchy

```text
parameter search
  ↓
recipe / strategy search
  ↓
architecture / composition search
  ↓
construction-algorithm discovery
```

Carbon should begin with bounded registered strategy spaces. Future maturity may permit agents to propose executable construction procedures that Carbon did not preimplement.

## U2. Agentic Exploration Zone inputs

A research agent may reason over:

```text
Scientific Canon
      +
Challenge / PhysicalSystem semantics
      +
public permitted construction methods
      +
Landscape evidence allowed by disclosure policy
      +
resource / truth-access constraints
```

and produce hypotheses such as:

- a new architecture;
- a new model decomposition;
- a new training/fitting procedure;
- a reduced/symbolic construction;
- a hybrid learned closure;
- a new truth-acquisition policy;
- a new construction algorithm;
- a proposed measurement or information-value experiment.

## U3. Authority boundary

None of these proposals become truth by generation.

```text
agent hypothesis
      ↓
registered/sandboxed independent reconstruction
      ↓
protected official evaluation
      ↓
ExperimentRecord
      ↓
reproduction / Landscape evidence
      ↓
possible method registration
```

> **The construction-method producer cannot define the official measurement that proves its own success.**

> **Construction and official evaluation are separate security domains.**
