# Full Carbon Defensibility Gauntlet — 2026-08-23

**Status:** completed adversarial diligence gauntlet.  
**Purpose:** test whether Carbon can defend its scientific, technical, economic, security, governance, commercial, legal, investor, and network claims under hostile questioning.  
**Rule:** a defensible answer may be either (a) a supported answer, or (b) an explicit bounded non-claim plus the exact evidence/decision required before a stronger claim is made.

---

# 1. Executive verdict

Carbon is **architecturally defensible but not yet empirically or institutionally complete**.

The strongest areas today are:
- authority separation;
- exam-before-candidate qualification doctrine;
- admissibility-before-ranking;
- challenge-bound evidence;
- distinction between scientific result, frontier event, and treasury settlement;
- separation of OpCo economics from scientific authority;
- explicit maturity/non-claim discipline;
- A0–A7 bounded implementation provenance;
- coherent long-term agentic construction path.

The weakest areas are not hidden. They are concentrated in:
- first LIVE scientific qualification;
- finite-evidence resolution and leader replacement;
- real execution/security qualification;
- validator agreement/reproducibility;
- production randomness;
- treasury/governance implementation;
- network-vs-centralized empirical advantage;
- commercial IP/privacy/legal operating policy;
- first product packaging/pricing evidence;
- actual buyer/traction evidence;
- institutional authority assignments.

The correct next phase is therefore **evidence hardening**, not broader architecture.

# 3. Numerical physicist / V&V attack

## Attack: “Why should I trust your exam?”

**Defensible answer:** Carbon does not treat the exam as self-authorizing. The scientific architecture requires qualification of the task, population, sampling plan, generator, truth/reference path, representation, measurements, and finite evidence before the exam may authoritatively grade candidates.

**Status:** `DEFENDED_BY_CANON`, `DESIGNED_BUT_UNPROVEN` for the first LIVE Challenge.

**Gap:** first LIVE Validation Dossier and exact Burgers qualification evidence.

## Attack: “What population does your score refer to?”

**Defensible answer:** Carbon separates target population `P(x)`, proposal/sampling distribution `Q(x)`, and evidence/score weighting `w(x)`. A physical envelope alone is insufficient.

**Status:** `DEFENDED_BY_CANON`, `REQUIRES_HUMAN_DECISION` for first LIVE instantiation.

## Attack: “Why is your truth source correct?”

**Defensible answer:** truth authority is Challenge-specific. For the first repaired Burgers direction, the intended hierarchy is analytic/semi-analytic Cole–Hopf primary truth with independently qualified numerical corroboration; no language/runtime is universal truth.

**Status:** `DEFENDED_BY_CANON`, `REQUIRES_EXPERIMENT` for implementation qualification.

## Attack: “What if the reference fails?”

**Defensible answer:** reference failure is operational/evidence failure, not candidate scientific failure.

**Status:** `DEFENDED_BY_CANON`; bounded infra/science separation is already implemented in A5/A7 seams.

## Attack: “Can the exam distinguish two nearly equal models?”

**Defensible answer:** Carbon does not yet claim arbitrary resolution. Frontier promotion must respect scientific resolution and can return `INDETERMINATE`.

**Status:** `DESIGNED_BUT_UNPROVEN`, `REQUIRES_EXPERIMENT`.

**Required evidence:** sample-size/rank-stability studies, repeated reconstruction/evaluation seed matrix, minimum resolvable improvement policy.
