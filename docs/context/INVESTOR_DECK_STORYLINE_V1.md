# Carbon Investor Deck Storyline v1

**Status:** investor-narrative working architecture for founder review.  
**Purpose:** translate Carbon's scientific, commercial, and network architecture into a venture-investor story without requiring the audience to understand protocol internals first.

---

# 1. Narrative rule

The investor deck should explain three businesses in the correct order:

```text
1. ENTERPRISE PROBLEM + PRODUCT
2. COMPANY BUSINESS MODEL
3. NETWORK ADVANTAGE
```

Do not start with Bittensor, Alpha, protocol objects, or seven PDEs.

---

# 2. Recommended 14-slide story

## Slide 1 — Carbon

Headline:

> **Discovery + Evidence Infrastructure for Physics AI**

Subhead:

> **Carbon helps engineering teams discover better fast physical models, independently determine what survives, and maintain the evidence needed to use them.**

What the investor should understand:
- this is an industrial/engineering AI infrastructure company;
- evidence and discovery are central;
- Carbon is broader than one model family.

---

## Slide 2 — Physical prediction is getting cheaper faster than credibility

Story:
- high-fidelity simulation is powerful but expensive to repeat;
- fast learned/reduced models expand design, optimization, control and autonomy;
- model generation is accelerating;
- the cost of proving where a model can be relied upon remains high.

Key line:

> **The next bottleneck is not merely making fast models. It is knowing which method works, where the model fails, and what evidence survives change.**

Do not lead with an unsourced market-size number.

---

## Slide 3 — The new infrastructure gap

Three columns:

```text
DISCOVERY
Which method should we try?

EVIDENCE
Does the model actually survive?

LIFECYCLE
What remains valid after change?
```

Explain that existing CAE, AI-model platforms, test systems and internal teams each solve parts of the stack, but no single vendor relationship should automatically define independent evidence.

---

## Slide 4 — Carbon's answer

Simple flow:

```text
DEFINE THE PHYSICS JOB
        ↓
QUALIFY THE EXAM
        ↓
TEST OR OPEN THE SEARCH
        ↓
INDEPENDENTLY DETERMINE WHAT SURVIVES
        ↓
QUALIFY + MAINTAIN EVIDENCE FOR USE
```

For investors, keep protocol names off the slide.

### Carbon Sponsored Discovery

Message:

## Slide 7 — Why customers pay

Use buyer-level economics, not protocol features.

Potential value drivers:
- fewer expensive simulation/test iterations;
- faster design loops;
- less internal research duplication;
- independent diligence on model risk;
- better use of existing simulation/test data;
- faster method discovery;
- controlled qualification and requalification;
- evidence that can be handed to a skeptical technical authority.

Do not state numerical ROI until measured in customer pilots.

## Slide 8 — Market

Position initial segments:

```text
AEROSPACE / DEFENSE / SPACE
ENERGY / TURBOMACHINERY / INDUSTRIAL PHYSICS
```

Strategic channel:

```text
CAE / ENGINEERING SOFTWARE VENDORS
```

Market-sizing methodology:

- show adjacent multi-billion-dollar engineering-software categories as category proof;
- build SAM bottom-up from named target enterprises, programs and plausible spend;
- build SOM from actual GTM capacity and conversion assumptions.

Avoid summing overlapping CAE, AI and digital-twin market reports.

---

## Slide 9 — Competitive landscape

Four groups:

```text
CAE / simulation incumbents
AI-native engineering platforms
engineering test/data AI
compute/orchestration infrastructure
```

Carbon position:

> **Independent discovery + evidence + qualification across permitted model/tool stacks.**

Important nuance:
- PhysicsX / Neural Concept can compete on model-development budgets;
- they can also be upstream candidate suppliers or evidence customers;
- incumbents can be truth sources, OEM partners or competitors.

The strategic advantage is neutrality and external research-market capability, not pretending there are no competitors.

---

## Slide 12 — Why the network matters

Investor-friendly explanation:

> **Carbon's subnet is a variable external R&D supply layer: independent researchers and agents compete against a defined objective, while Carbon retains an independent judge.**

Hypothesized advantages to prove:
- more hypotheses per dollar;
- broader method diversity;
- faster frontier improvement;
- specialized external talent;
- lower marginal discovery cost.

Show clear boundary:

```text
OPCO
enterprise product + contracts + qualification + lifecycle

NETWORK
external optimizers + validators + frontier rewards + Alpha economy
```

No claim that revenue automatically flows to Alpha.

## Slide 13 — De-risking milestones

```text

```

Investors should see exactly what the next round of execution is meant to prove.

## Slide 14 — Vision

> **As engineering becomes increasingly AI-native and autonomous, every fast physical model will need an evidence boundary. Carbon aims to become the infrastructure that discovers better methods, records what survives, and tells engineering systems when a fast answer deserves to influence a real decision.**

End with company ambition, not token mechanics.

# 5. Investor soundbites

### What is Carbon?

> **Carbon is the independent discovery, evidence, and qualification layer for fast physical models.**

### Why now?

> **Physics AI is making fast physical models easier to create, which makes independent evidence about where they work more valuable.**

### What do you sell first?

> **We start by auditing the fast models engineering teams already have. Then we expand into competitive discovery, qualification, and lifecycle infrastructure.**

### Why is this venture-scale rather than consulting?

> **The services wedge productizes into repeatable evidence workflows, recurring qualification/lifecycle software, APIs/OEM distribution, and a network marketplace that scales the research supply side.**

### Why Bittensor?

> **It gives Carbon a persistent open market of optimizers and validators. Carbon defines the scientific objective and independent judge; the network scales who can search.**

### Why doesn't an incumbent win?

> **Incumbents own important upstream tools. Carbon's opportunity is to become the independent layer across them — and to add an external competitive research market they do not naturally provide.**

# 6. Deck discipline

Do:
- separate architecture from traction;
- use category evidence carefully;
- show real competitors;
- explain the initial buyer/product concretely;
- make recurrence and productization visible;
- state what the network must still prove.

Do not:
- call hypothetical scenario revenue a forecast;
- use token price in company valuation logic;
- claim universal certification;
- claim broad industrial deployment before customer evidence exists;
- imply all future revenue streams launch together;
- bury the enterprise product under subnet mechanics.
