# Review These Preliminary Decisions — GTM Across All Commercial Opportunities

**Status:** owner-recommended decisions for founder/commercial/product review.  
**Purpose:** isolate the GTM choices surfaced by the all-opportunity buyer-discovery gauntlet.

---

## G1 — Evidence Audit as first commercial wedge

**Recommendation:** accept.

Reason: strongest near-term fit to Carbon's judge/evidence architecture; can sell before generalized discovery/network maturity; natural expansion into discovery, qualification, lifecycle, and platform.

---

## G2 — Challenge Design / Feasibility as paid entry product

**Recommendation:** accept.

Use when customer has a valuable physics problem but no sufficiently authored task/truth/evidence plan.

---

## G3 — Sponsored Discovery as first flagship network-differentiated product

**Recommendation:** accept.

Program revenue must be separated from sponsor participant-reward capital.

---

## G4 — Model Development as expansion, not primary initial wedge

**Recommendation:** accept.

High ACV but custom delivery burden is materially larger; attach after Audit/Discovery when possible.

---

## G5 — Qualification as strategic contract-value and retention engine

**Recommendation:** accept.

Productize only when Product Qualification Pack workflow is sufficiently mature; retain bounded-claim semantics.

---

## G6 — Lifecycle / Requalification as primary recurring attach

**Recommendation:** accept.

Design early because this is one of the clearest sources of recurring revenue and workflow lock-in based on genuine model change, not artificial subscription packaging.

---

## G7 — Enterprise Evidence Platform as core long-term ARR product

**Recommendation:** accept.

Do not build full platform before repeated service workflows reveal which orchestration, registry, truth-adapter, qualification, and audit features actually repeat.

---

## G8 — Evidence API / OEM as major scale channel

**Recommendation:** accept as strategic track.

Start partner discovery with CAE/engineering-software vendors before building the full API product.

---

## G9 — Frontier Market as network-native marketplace end state

**Recommendation:** accept directionally.

Requires qualified frontier economics, treasury settlement, enough sponsor demand, and participant liquidity. Do not present as current revenue product.

---

## G10 — Model / Method licensing as optional rights-dependent rail

**Recommendation:** accept as optional, not core forecast dependency.

Rights ownership must be explicit before licensing revenue can be assumed.

---

## G11 — Physics Intelligence remains later product

**Recommendation:** accept.

No commercial claim beyond descriptive evidence intelligence until prospective decision lift is demonstrated for the sold use.

---

## G12 — First vertical discovery tracks

**Recommendation:** prioritize two direct-industry tracks:

1. aerospace / space / defense;
2. energy / turbomachinery / industrial physics.

Maintain CAE/software vendors as a parallel strategic/OEM channel track.

This is a discovery priority, not a permanent market exclusion.

---

## G13 — Do not lead enterprise sales with token/network mechanics

**Recommendation:** accept.

Lead with engineering pain, evidence/discovery outcome, and customer value. Explain the network where it creates differentiated supply or economics.

---

## G14 — Fiat-first commercial procurement

**Recommendation:** already locked; retain.

Customers need not purchase Alpha directly to contract with Carbon.

---

## G15 — Standard sales stages and CRM evidence

**Recommendation:** accept.

Use:

```text
S0 TARGET
S1 DISCOVERY
S2 TECHNICAL QUALIFICATION
S3 COMMERCIAL QUALIFICATION
S4 PROPOSED
S5 SECURITY / LEGAL / PROCUREMENT
S6 PILOT / CONTRACTED
S7 DELIVERED
S8 EXPANSION IDENTIFIED
S9 RECURRING / PLATFORM
```

Do not call technical interest pipeline without a credible buyer/budget path.

---

## G16 — Every pilot must remain valuable under a negative scientific outcome

**Recommendation:** accept as commercial constitution.

An Audit that finds the model inadequate and a Discovery Program that produces no frontier advance can both be successful contracted work when the agreed evidence program was delivered correctly.

---

## G17 — Bounded evaluation/truth budgets in every proposal

**Recommendation:** accept.

No unlimited expensive truth or evaluation inside fixed pricing without explicit economic protection.

---

## G18 — Pilot SOW productization

**Recommendation:** create standardized SOW templates for:

- Evidence Audit;
- Challenge Feasibility;
- Sponsored Discovery;
- Qualification;
- customer-hosted truth integration.

Legal counsel review remains required before production contracting.

---

## G19 — Expansion hypothesis required at delivery

**Recommendation:** accept.

At the close of every engagement, explicitly record whether the next best product is:

```text
none
remediation
discovery
model development
qualification
deployment
lifecycle
platform
OEM/API
```

This is commercial planning, not forced upselling.

---

## G20 — GTM validation milestone

**Recommendation:** next external proof program should aim to establish:

1. repeated buyer pain around Evidence Audit;
2. one paid pilot;
3. second comparable paid pilot;
4. one expansion event;
5. one recurring/lifecycle or repeated usage commitment;
6. one network-backed Sponsored Discovery design partner.

These are milestones to pursue, not current traction claims.
