# Review These Preliminary Decisions — Revenue & Alpha Value Capture

**Status:** owner-recommended decisions for founder/product/economic/legal/technical review.  
**Rule:** ACCEPT / MODIFY / REJECT each item before migration into normative commercial or token-economic policy.

---

## RA1 — Hybrid commercial architecture

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Carbon should use services to land customers, enterprise/platform products to create recurring revenue, the subnet/network to scale discovery/evidence work, qualification/lifecycle to increase ACV and retention, and physics intelligence later to compound moat.

Do not choose services-only, SaaS-only, or network-only as the terminal business.

## RA2 — Evidence Audit as the first commercial wedge

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Prioritize a sellable independent model/evidence audit because it monetizes the judge before requiring full generalized miner construction support.

## RA3 — Sponsored Discovery as the first strongly network-aligned product

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Use sponsored discovery pilots to create a customer-to-network pathway while generating authoring, integration, evaluation and downstream qualification revenue.

## RA4 — Qualification and lifecycle are separate paid products

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Do not bundle unlimited qualification/requalification into basic discovery pricing by default. Treat product qualification and lifecycle support as distinct value/evidence burdens.

## RA5 — Enterprise platform is a core later ARR rail

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Productize private Challenge/evidence/qualification infrastructure into an enterprise platform once core engagement workflows stabilize.

## RA6 — OEM / evidence rail is strategic

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Allow CAE/SciML/engineering-AI platforms to integrate Carbon as evidence/qualification infrastructure rather than forcing Carbon to own every user workflow.

## RA7 — Physics intelligence remains later-stage revenue

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Do not sell “physics intelligence” as a mature subscription until prospective decision lift is demonstrated for the sold decision role.

## RA8 — CommercialEconomicsRecord

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Create a non-scientific engagement economics record tracking contracted revenue, recurring value, direct cost, truth/evaluation cost, network incentives, pass-through rewards, contribution margin, network activity and evidence reuse.

No field may enter scientific score.

## RA9 — Internal revenue-rail identities

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Even when bundled, separately identify authoring, truth integration, program/platform, evaluation usage, reward administration, development, qualification, deployment, subscription, license, support, lifecycle and intelligence revenue.

This prevents accidental unlimited costly services inside one SKU.

## RA10 — Fiat-first enterprise UX

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Customers should be able to buy Carbon using ordinary fiat contracts/invoices. Do not force enterprise procurement to operate crypto wallets unless the engagement explicitly requires a network-native payment rail.

## RA11 — Commercial revenue does not automatically accrue to Alpha

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT as a constitutional economic statement.

Token/network value requires explicit useful bridges from customer activity into network work, reward, service demand, staking/collateral utility, or other reviewed mechanisms.

## RA12 — Useful network work is the primary Alpha-value bridge

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Prioritize externally funded scientific work executed through the network over artificial financial engineering.

Track commercial network jobs and externally funded participant work.

## RA13 — Sponsored Frontier Reward bridge

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT CONCEPT; implementation requires economic/legal/treasury review.

Allow sponsors to fund extra rewards bound prospectively to `FrontierAdvanceEvent` or another exact scientific event.

Potential fiat-to-Alpha settlement should be tested separately before adoption.

## RA14 — Alpha/network credits

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT FOR RESEARCH.

Study network-native Alpha or Alpha-backed credits for services such as sponsored Challenge activation, evaluation capacity, bounty escrow or protocol-native deposits, while allowing fiat customer UX.

Do not ratify exact utility before mechanism testing.

## RA15 — Alpha stake/collateral roles

**Recommendation:** RESEARCH ONLY.

Potential sponsor/author/evaluator collateral may improve commitment or abuse resistance, but economic stake must not become scientific authority.

## RA16 — Buyback/burn/revenue-share policy

**Recommendation:** DO NOT RATIFY NOW.

Any use of OpCo revenue for token buybacks, burns, distributions or holder-linked financial returns requires dedicated legal, tax, treasury, securities and market-structure analysis.

The business model must succeed without assuming this mechanism.

## RA17 — NetworkUtilityConversion metric

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT internally.

Track the share of eligible commercial value associated with useful network-executed work. Low early values may be legitimate while private-network infrastructure is incomplete, but the roadmap should increase useful network integration over time.

This metric is not scientific evidence and not token valuation.

## RA18 — Sponsor reward pass-through is not gross revenue

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Commercial financial reporting should distinguish Carbon service/platform revenue from customer funds passed through as miner/participant rewards.

## RA19 — Rights-aware evidence reuse

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Every commercial engagement must declare whether evidence is customer-exclusive, confidential-but-aggregatable, anonymized-aggregatable, method-reusable, public, internal-only or non-reusable.

Do not assume a flywheel that customer contracts legally prohibit.

## RA20 — Product sequence

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT as go-to-market hypothesis to test.

Initial portfolio:

1. Carbon Evidence Audit;
2. Challenge Design / Feasibility;
3. Sponsored Discovery Pilot;
4. paid truth-source integration.

Then attach qualification, deployment, lifecycle and enterprise platform products.

## RA21 — Network conversion should be maturity-gated

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Classify commercial workflows as `OFFCHAIN_ONLY`, `NETWORK_ELIGIBLE`, or `NETWORK_REQUIRED`. Do not force sensitive/private jobs through network topology before confidentiality and execution controls are qualified.

## RA22 — Unit economics before subsidy scale

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Do not scale customer-funded network evaluation/rewards without measuring whether customer economics support truth cost, validator work, incentives, support and OpCo margin.

## RA23 — Pricing structures now, exact prices later

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Define charging units and package boundaries now; determine price points from customer discovery, value, delivery cost and willingness to pay rather than invented figures.

## RA24 — Token price is not the product KPI

**Recommendation:** ACCEPT.

Primary alignment metrics should be externally funded useful network work, repeat sponsor demand, participant productivity, scientific outcomes and sustainable economics. Alpha price/liquidity are important market observations but not the sole product-success measure.

# Recommended next gauntlets after review

If RA1–RA24 are broadly accepted, run in this order:

1. **First Product / Pricing Gauntlet** — Evidence Audit + Discovery Pilot: buyer, deliverable, sales motion, scope, pricing units, acceptance, gross margin.
2. **Sponsor Reward Gateway Gauntlet** — fiat payment → reward custody → possible Alpha acquisition → event-bound payout/refund, including slippage and treasury controls.
3. **Private Industrial Challenge Gauntlet** — customer-hosted solver, confidentiality/IP, qualified evaluator topology, rights-aware evidence reuse.
4. **Enterprise Platform Gauntlet** — which workflows become software/API versus expert service.
5. **Alpha Utility Gauntlet** — network-native credits/deposits/collateral/marketplace use that create utility without contaminating science.
