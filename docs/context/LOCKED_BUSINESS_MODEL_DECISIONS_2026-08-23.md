# Locked Carbon Business-Model Decisions — 2026-08-23

**Status:** OWNER-LOCKED commercial strategy.  
**Scope:** company/business architecture outside Carbon's scientific judge.  
**Does not lock:** untested dollar pricing, legal/tax treatment, securities conclusions, market-size claims, future product performance, or scientific thresholds.

---

## Locked decisions

### BM1 — Hybrid company model

**LOCKED.** Carbon will deliberately combine:

- high-value services / expert-led programs;
- enterprise software/platform revenue;
- scalable network/marketplace activity;
- qualification and lifecycle recurring revenue;
- licensing / OEM / API opportunities;
- later physics-intelligence products only after prospective validation.

Services are the market-entry wedge, not the terminal business model.

### BM2 — Standalone OpCo viability

**LOCKED.** Carbon the company must have a credible standalone revenue/margin story independent of speculative Alpha appreciation.

### BM3 — Network as scaling layer

**LOCKED.** The subnet is Carbon's scalable external research/optimizer and verification market. The OpCo owns enterprise productization, customer integration, privacy/rights contracting, qualification, lifecycle, support, and commercial packaging.

### BM4 — First product wedge

**LOCKED DIRECTION.** `Carbon Evidence Audit` is the preferred first commercial wedge because it monetizes the judge before generalized discovery infrastructure is required.

### BM5 — First network-differentiated product

**LOCKED DIRECTION.** `Carbon Sponsored Discovery Program` is the preferred first product demonstrating the full network-backed search model.

### BM6 — Land and expand

**LOCKED.** Default expansion architecture:

```text
Evidence Audit / Feasibility
→ Remediation or Sponsored Discovery
→ Model Development
→ Qualification
→ Deployment
→ Lifecycle / Requalification
→ Enterprise Platform / API / OEM
→ Physics Intelligence when earned
```

### BM7 — Multiple revenue rails

**LOCKED.** Carbon will preserve separate commercial revenue rails rather than collapsing everything into one consulting/project price. Revenue may include authoring, truth integration, platform/program, evaluation usage, reward administration, development, qualification, deployment, software subscriptions, licenses, OEM royalties, support, lifecycle, requalification, and later intelligence.

### BM8 — Sponsor reward is not ordinary revenue

**LOCKED.** Sponsor-funded participant/frontier reward pools are accounted separately from Carbon service/platform revenue unless accounting/legal treatment explicitly establishes otherwise.

### BM9 — Recurring revenue is first-class

**LOCKED.** Lifecycle, requalification, support, enterprise platform, usage, API/OEM, and licensing are designed from the beginning as expansion/recurring rails rather than post-hoc additions.

### BM10 — Productization mandate

**LOCKED.** Every repeated expert workflow should be assessed for automation, reusable adapters, templates, APIs, or platform primitives. Delivery should become less labor-dependent over customer cohorts.

### BM11 — Fiat-first enterprise UX

**LOCKED.** Enterprise customers do not need to understand or directly acquire Alpha to buy Carbon services. Carbon may abstract network settlement behind conventional procurement rails.

### BM12 — Explicit Alpha bridge

**LOCKED.** Off-chain company revenue is not assumed to benefit Alpha automatically. Network value must be created through economically useful network work, sponsor-funded network rewards, network-native service utility, or another explicit reviewed bridge.

### BM13 — Scientific independence

**LOCKED.** Commercial pricing, customer importance, success fees, sponsor reward size, or investor priorities may not change scientific evidence requirements after the relevant scientific contract is frozen.

### BM14 — Rights-aware compounding

**LOCKED.** Carbon reuses customer-derived evidence/method knowledge only where privacy, IP, and contractual rights permit it. Commercial optimization does not imply ownership.

### BM15 — Investor story

**LOCKED.** The investor narrative should be understandable without requiring token appreciation:

> Carbon is building the discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models. It lands through high-value evidence and sponsored R&D programs, converts repeated workflows into enterprise software and APIs, uses an open research network to scale discovery, and monetizes qualification and model lifecycle as recurring infrastructure.

### BM16 — Evidence-before-financial-claim

**LOCKED.** Investor materials distinguish design, customer discovery, paid pilot, repeatable service, expansion, recurrence, platformization, and network leverage. Carbon will not represent hypothetical revenue or margins as traction.

---

## Strategic north star

> **Every new commercial engagement should, where compatible with customer rights and scientific integrity, create revenue today, reusable product infrastructure for tomorrow, and additional economically useful work for the Carbon network.**
