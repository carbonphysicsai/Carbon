The Canon has four roles:

1. **Evidence base** — what external science, engineering, statistics, mechanism design, and systems literature actually support.
2. **Argument map** — which Carbon claims are justified at which maturity level.
3. **Claim-control instrument** — a guard against converting citations, code, market outcomes, or attractive narratives into unjustified scientific authority.

# 3. Integrated premise chain

The v3 premise chain remains in force. The following additions are now required.

20. **A physical envelope is not a probability distribution.** A bounded admissible region does not specify how real or test cases are distributed inside it.
21. **The target population is part of the scientific claim.** A statement about expected performance, tail risk, or coverage is meaningless without the population to which it refers.
22. **Finite sampling is a separate scientific design problem.** Target population `P(x)`, proposal/sampling distribution `Q(x)`, and evidence/score weighting `w(x)` are different semantics.
23. **A deterministic generator can still be scientifically wrong.** Correct code execution does not establish that generated cases conform to the intended population or that their labels are sufficiently accurate.
24. **The exam itself requires qualification.** Carbon must establish the adequacy of the task definition, population, SamplingPlan, generator, truth/reference path, representation, measurements, and finite evidence before the exam may authoritatively grade miners.
25. **Measurement is not implied by a governing equation.** Numerical method, sampling, normalization, aggregation, precision, uncertainty floor, applicability, and implementation version are part of measurement identity.
26. **Admissibility precedes ranking.** Mandatory scientific failure cannot be compensated by superior soft performance elsewhere.
27. **A scalar score is Challenge-bound evidence, not a universal unit of competence.** Cross-Challenge economic allocation cannot assume numerical comparability without explicit calibration.
28. **Frontier improvement is an evidence state.** A “new leader” is not established merely because one floating-point score exceeds another score produced under a different random draw.
29. **Economic reward and scientific truth are separate authorities.** Scientific evidence determines whether the frontier moved; settlement determines whether the corresponding entitlement is paid.
30. **Infrastructure failure does not erase scientific outcome.** A confirmed frontier advance can remain scientifically valid while payout is pending or blocked operationally.
31. **Persistent incumbency is not new scientific work.** Carbon's base performance market should reward verified frontier advance rather than indefinite ownership of the current leaderboard position.
32. **Parallel Challenges can preserve breadth without cross-Challenge score comparison.** Equal notional economic opportunity across a frozen portfolio is compatible with Challenge-specific scientific rulers.
33. **A treasury can decouple Bittensor transport from scientific settlement.** Receiving miner-side emissions into a separately governed treasury neuron can avoid forcing Challenge accounting into normalized validator weights, provided custody, governance, and payout binding are independently hardened.
34. **The producer must receive all causal inputs required by the registered task.** If two physically different outputs are possible for the same visible candidate input, the task contract is ill-defined.
35. **Truth sources are Challenge-specific.** “Use Julia” or “use CFD” is not a universal truth policy. Analytic/semi-analytic solutions, experiments, datasets, high-fidelity solvers, or qualified services may each be primary or corroborating depending on the problem.
36. **Independent solver does not mean independent truth.** A second solver can have its own numerical bias and must be qualified for the regime in which it is used as corroboration.

23. **Carbon qualifies the exam before the exam qualifies a candidate.**

# 5. Qualified Challenge doctrine

The durable scientific chain is:

```text
PhysicalSystemSpec
+ CandidateOutputContract
+ Claim / Operating Envelope
        ↓
Target Population P(x)
        ↓
InstanceDistributionContract
        ↓
SamplingPlan / proposal Q(x)
        ↓
ChallengeInstanceGenerator
        ↓
Generator conformance
        ↓
CanonicalChallengeCase
        ↓
Reference / Truth Realization
        ↓
Measurement applicability + MeasurementContracts
        ↓
Validation Dossier
        ↓
Score Pack / Evidence Use Contract
        ↓
protected official evaluation
        ↓
ScoreResult / ExperimentRecord
```

The order is scientific, not merely software architecture.

### 5.1 Envelope != population

An envelope states where a claim may apply. A target population states how cases within or across that scope are distributed for a particular estimand.

### 5.2 P/Q/w

```text
P(x) = target population
Q(x) = finite proposal / sampling distribution
w(x) = evidence weighting / scientific or consequence importance
```

Oversampling a stress region under `Q(x)` does not imply that it is common under `P(x)`, and neither implies its score importance unless explicitly registered.

### 5.3 Intended vs realized evidence population

Censoring, reference failure, timeouts, and non-applicability can change the realized evidence population. Carbon must record the difference rather than silently average only over surviving cases.

The dossier qualifies, rather than authors, the registered scientific chain. Its evidence classes include:

- physical-system adequacy;

- target-population adequacy;
- SamplingPlan / finite-evidence adequacy;
- generator implementation integrity;
- generator distribution conformance;
- reference/truth adequacy;
- representation fidelity;
- measurement adequacy and applicability;

- secrecy/decontamination/role separation;
- censoring, limitations, and residual uncertainty.

LIVE status fails closed when required evidence is absent or blocked.

# 7. Score Pack doctrine

> **A Score Pack is a versioned Evidence Use Contract.**

It does not decide whether the generator is physically right or whether a metric is scientifically valid. It states how already-qualified evidence may become:

```text
evidence eligibility
      ↓
mandatory admissibility
      ↓
score-bearing estimands
      ↓
soft objective values
      ↓
deterministic Challenge-bound ranking evidence
```

### 7.1 Physics > loss

The strongest interpretation is:

> **Mandatory physical failure cannot be compensated by predictive accuracy or another soft objective.**

P0's 45/30/25 profile is one narrow implementation, not Carbon's universal scientific law.

### 7.2 Scientific states

At minimum distinguish:

```text
REJECTED_INVALID
FAILED_INFRA
SCIENTIFIC_INADMISSIBLE
INDETERMINATE_EVIDENCE
VALID_RANKED
```

Scientific zero is evidence. Invalidity and infrastructure failure are not scientific zeros.

# 8. Frontier reward doctrine

Carbon's base performance market should reward **verified frontier advance**, not continuous incumbent rent.

For a Challenge settlement window:

```text
registered FrontierBaseline / incumbent
        +
eligible challengers
        ↓
common fresh promotion experiment
        ↓
LeaderReplacementPolicy
        ↓
SUPERIOR | NOT_SUPERIOR | INDETERMINATE
        ↓
FrontierAdvanceEvent (if SUPERIOR)
```

A `FrontierAdvanceEvent` should bind:

- Challenge and ChallengeSetEpoch identity;
- prior frontier identity;
- winning strategy/method identity;
- payout identity/address;
- promotion-exam identity/digest;
- Score Pack and Dossier identity/digests;
- scientific result and uncertainty/repeat evidence;
- settlement entitlement.

### 8.1 Batched settlement

Where multiple challengers arrive in one settlement window, arrival order should not determine scientific reward. Evaluate the eligible batch and settle at most the strongest qualified frontier advance for that Challenge/window under the registered policy.

### 8.2 Self-improvement

An incumbent miner may earn again if it submits a genuinely new method/strategy that independently advances the frontier.

### 8.3 Known limitation

No protocol can observe a miner's unreleased stronger method. Strategic staged disclosure is therefore a mechanism-design risk to mitigate, not a condition Carbon can prove absent.

# 9. Parallel Challenge portfolio doctrine

Carbon's breadth mechanism can operate with a small frozen portfolio of reward-enabled Challenges.

Owner intent:

- P0 launch slice: prove one complete narrow Challenge loop;
- Phase-0 expansion: progress toward roughly **4-7 concurrent qualified academic Challenges**, preserving the original breadth goal of **seven PDE families** where operationally feasible.

Each active Challenge in a `ChallengeSetEpoch` receives equal **notional** performance-reward opportunity `1/N`.

This does **not** mean:

- equal scientific difficulty;
- equal raw score scale;
- equal commercial value;
- equal information value;
- raw score comparability.

It means Carbon deliberately buys breadth in scientific search.

If a Challenge has no verified frontier advance in a period, its notional period allocation must not be silently redistributed to the winners of other Challenges.

# 10. Treasury settlement doctrine

Bittensor's normalized weight transport is not itself Carbon's scientific settlement semantics.

A credible architecture is:

```text
Bittensor / Yuma miner-side emission
        ↓
registered Carbon Treasury neuron
        ↓
separately governed Treasury Vault
        ↓
Carbon Challenge-period accounting
        ↓
FrontierAdvanceEvent-bound entitlement
        ↓
validator-governed / timelocked payout
        ↓
winning miner or authorized payout address
```

### 10.1 Reference precedent: Enigma treasury

qBittensor Labs' Enigma repository demonstrates a relevant implementation pattern:

- a registered treasury neuron;
- `TreasuryController` for typed proposals, voting, quorum, and spend limits;
- `TreasuryVault` as asset holder and timelocked executor;
- active trusted-validator voting tied to Bittensor state;
- native, ERC20, and Alpha-transfer pathways;
- execution-time rate limits;
- explicit proposal lifecycle and cancellation/expiry states.

**What this supports:** feasibility of a separately governed Bittensor-connected treasury neuron and validator-governed Alpha settlement.

**What it does not prove:** that Enigma's governance parameters, admin powers, contracts, or exact custody model are correct for Carbon.

Carbon's preferred adaptation is stricter scientific binding: payout proposals should reference an exact `FrontierAdvanceEvent` digest rather than asking treasury voters to determine scientific merit subjectively.

### 10.2 Treasury separation law

> **Validators may verify that a payout matches an authoritative frontier event; treasury governance must not become a second scientific judge.**

### 10.3 Operational state separation

A confirmed event may have:

```text
FRONTIER_ADVANCE_CONFIRMED
PAYOUT_PENDING_GOVERNANCE
PAYOUT_TIMELOCKED
PAYOUT_PENDING_INFRA
PAID
```

These settlement states do not mutate the scientific result.

# 11. Burgers qualification lessons

The Burgers study is a methodological case, not production qualification.

It demonstrated why Carbon must qualify its own judge.

### 11.1 Hidden causal input defect

The original Burgers generator varied viscosity `nu`, while the P0 FNO candidate received only `u0` as input. Independent Cole-Hopf comparisons showed materially different final states for identical `u0` at different `nu`.

**Lesson:** a task is ill-defined if a causal parameter changes the target but is absent from the CandidateOutputContract.

**P0 repair:** fix `nu` for the first authoritative Burgers Challenge; later introduce `(u0, nu) -> u(T)` as a separate conditioned task.

### 11.2 Generator resolution defect

Independent Cole-Hopf comparison exposed material low-viscosity error in the existing `nx=128` IMEX-Fourier generator while refinement converged strongly.

**Lesson:** deterministic/reproducible numerical generation does not establish sufficient truth fidelity across the declared envelope.

### 11.3 Truth-dominance stop-ship

A synthetic candidate matching generator labels could outrank a candidate matching independently qualified physical truth in an under-resolved region.

**Lesson:**

> **Within qualified resolution, the official path must not reward generator-specific numerical error over qualified physical truth.**

### 11.4 Residual naming defect

The existing final-time spatial diagnostic `|u u_x - nu u_xx|` omitted `u_t` and was therefore not a full Burgers PDE residual.

**Lesson:** measurement names themselves are claim-bearing. Diagnostic convenience does not authorize stronger scientific language.

### 11.5 Reconstruction variance

Real JAX pilots showed that the same strategy could cross admissibility boundaries under different reconstruction seeds.

**Lesson:** if Carbon claims method quality, one lucky reconstruction may be insufficient. Reconstruction variance and evaluation-instance variance must remain distinct.

# 13. Updated falsifiable hypotheses

The v3 hypotheses remain. Add:

- **H-POP:** explicit target-population + SamplingPlan contracts reduce scientific ambiguity and generator-induced claim error relative to envelope-only Challenge authoring.
- **H-GEN:** independently qualified generator/truth paths reduce false miner rankings caused by numerical reference error.
- **H-MEAS:** explicit MeasurementContract identity improves validator reproducibility and reduces metric-semantic drift.
- **H-ADM:** admissibility-first scoring reduces compensatory Goodhart failures relative to a purely blended scalar objective.
- **H-RANK:** repeated common-exam frontier promotion produces lower false leader-replacement rates than comparing scores from independent draws.
- **H-PORT:** a 4-7 Challenge equal-opportunity portfolio produces broader verified physical-method progress than a single pooled score market under comparable resources.
- **H-FRONTIER:** paying verified frontier advances produces more progress per unit emission than continuous incumbent rent.
- **H-TREASURY:** a separately governed treasury-neuron settlement layer can preserve Carbon's Challenge accounting without corrupting scientific evidence or requiring cross-Challenge score comparability.
- **H-DECISION:** the complete Carbon loop improves later scientific or engineering decisions relative to credible centralized or benchmark-only alternatives.

None of these are established by design alone.
