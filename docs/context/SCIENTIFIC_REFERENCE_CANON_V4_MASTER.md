# 1. Canon charter

The Canon has four roles:

1. **Evidence base** — what external science/engineering literature can support.
2. **Argument map** — which Carbon claims are justified at which maturity level.
3. **Claim-control instrument** — guard against converting citations, code, economic outcomes, or attractive narratives into scientific authority.
4. **Agentic Exploration Zone** — allow broad hypothesis generation while keeping the judge outside the optimizer.

Fundamental rule:

> **Canon informs hypotheses. Carbon experiments adjudicate them.**

Publication discipline:

```text

      !=

      !=
Carbon implementation
      !=
Carbon evidence
      !=

      !=
```

# 3. Integrated scientific premise chain

The literature/evidence premises in the earlier canon remain useful. The following integrated premises are constitutionally required:

1. Fast learned physical models can be valuable, but benchmark fit is not engineering credibility.
2. Physical admissibility, robustness, uncertainty, and context-of-use evidence are distinct from scalar predictive loss.
3. Adaptive search can overfit exposed evaluation; protected examination is scientifically justified.
4. Producer-independent reconstruction is necessary because producer-generated artifacts/scores are not sufficient authority.
5. A physical operating envelope is not a probability distribution.
6. The target population is part of the scientific claim.
7. Target population `P(x)`, sampling/proposal `Q(x)`, and evidence/score weighting `w(x)` are separate semantics.
8. A deterministic generator can still be scientifically wrong.
9. The exam itself requires qualification before it can qualify candidates.
10. Measurement is an identified scientific object, not merely a formula name.
11. Measurement definition, qualification, and use are separate decisions.
12. Mandatory scientific failure is non-compensable by soft performance.
13. A scalar score is Challenge-bound evidence, not a universal unit of model competence.
14. Frontier improvement is an evidence state, not merely a numerical inequality.
15. Persistent incumbency is not new scientific work.
16. Parallel Challenges can preserve scientific breadth without requiring cross-Challenge score comparability.
17. Scientific winner determination and economic settlement are separate authorities.
18. Infrastructure or treasury failure cannot rewrite a scientific outcome.
19. The producer must receive all causal inputs required by the registered task.
20. Truth/reference authority is Challenge-specific.
21. A second independent solver is corroboration only to the extent that it is itself qualified.
22. A learned internal relation is not validated merely because an end-to-end system succeeds.
23. Component qualification does not automatically compose into system qualification.
24. Physical similarity, accumulated evidence, and Physics Intelligence must earn predictive value prospectively.
25. Agentic autonomy is useful for search, but search authority must not become grading authority.
26. Decision uncertainty is joint: material reconstruction-by-case, reference,
    representation, and execution dependence must be preserved rather than
    erased by an unsupported additive budget.
27. Compute-saving screens may allocate evidence or nominate contenders, but
    they cannot substitute for the producer-independent reconstruction and
    repeat evidence required by the scientific claim.

# 4. Scientific constitution

## C1 — No layer certifies itself

The producer of a candidate, construction method, generator, measurement, score policy, or commercial deliverable cannot unilaterally establish the scientific claim that makes its own output successful.

## C2 — Evidence crosses boundaries; authority remains scoped

Artifacts may be reused across authoring, evaluation, qualification, product, and business layers. Their presence does not transfer the authority of the layer that created them.

## C3 — Scientific contracts are prospective and versioned

Task, population, generator, truth, measurement, Score Pack, frontier policy, and qualification contracts change explicitly and prospectively. Historical results are not silently reinterpreted.

## C4 — Physics remains external authority

No validator vote, token price, network consensus, customer payment, or learned representation can vote away a registered physical requirement.

## C5 — Admissibility precedes ranking

> **Mandatory scientific failure cannot be compensated by soft performance.**

## C6 — The exam must be qualified first

> **The exam must be qualified before it may qualify candidates.**

## C7 — Construction and evaluation are separate security domains

> **Construction and official evaluation are separate security domains.**

## C8 — Rank nominates; evidence qualifies

A leaderboard/ranking result may nominate a candidate or method. It does not automatically establish a product claim.

## C9 — A leader is an evidence state

> **A new leader is an evidence state, not a floating-point inequality.**

## C10 — Reward verified frontier advance

> **Carbon rewards verified frontier advances, not permanent incumbency.**

## C11 — Treasury is not scientific authority

Treasury may settle a scientific entitlement. It may not create, erase, or modify that entitlement.

## C12 — Agent autonomy expands hypotheses, not authority

Agents may search more broadly over methods, architectures, programs, diagnostics, or experiments. They do not define official success after seeing candidate behavior.

## C13 — Dependence is part of the decision

The operative decision interval must preserve every material dependence among
candidate reconstruction, physical case or trajectory, reference uncertainty,
representation, and execution. Named uncertainty components are diagnostic
bookkeeping, not evidence of independence. The Challenge Dossier must qualify
the interval procedure and its applicability test; the exact incumbent-versus-
challenger evidence must also satisfy that registered test. Carbon may combine
components by quadrature only when both levels show that omitted covariance
and interaction cannot change the decision. Otherwise it uses joint
propagation or conservative bounds.

## C14 — Evidence authority cannot be downsampled

Static schema, dependency, hermeticity, and resource checks may reject a typed
protocol or resource inadmissibility. Before the registered complete base
reconstruction evidence exists, every quality forecast, partial build, proxy,
or screen may affect scheduling only; it cannot permanently deny that evidence
on predicted performance or create any scientific outcome. An unevaluated or
deferred path is recorded as typed `EVIDENCE_DEFERRED`, never as
`NOT_SUPERIOR`, gate failure, or candidate physics failure.

After complete base reconstruction, official evidence may be allocated
sequentially only under the preregistered, coverage-qualified decision rule.
That rule may return `SUPERIOR`, `NOT_SUPERIOR`, or `INDETERMINATE` at its
registered boundaries. A heuristic futility stop can conserve resources only
by returning `EVIDENCE_DEFERRED`; it cannot make a negative scientific
decision. If Carbon cannot obtain enough evidence, it returns `INDETERMINATE`
with reason `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, or narrows the claim rather than lowering
the bar.

A LIVE exam must address, as applicable:

1. physical-system definition;
2. claim/envelope;
3. target population;
4. finite SamplingPlan;
5. generator implementation;
6. distribution conformance;
7. truth/reference adequacy;
8. representation/transformation validity;
9. measurement qualification/applicability;
10. statistical sufficiency for the intended decision resolution;
11. secrecy/role separation;
12. censoring, exclusions, and limitations.

Missing required evidence fails closed for LIVE.

Forbidden sequence:

```text
run candidates
→ inspect preferred winner
→ alter exam
→ call it the same qualified exam
```

# 7. Measurement doctrine

A governing relation does not uniquely define a measurement.

Measurement identity can include:

- observable or diagnostic;
- discretization/numerical method;
- sampling points / quadrature;
- normalization;
- aggregation;
- tolerance/uncertainty floor;
- precision;
- population/stratum applicability;
- implementation version;
- qualification evidence.

Physical representation does not certify physical correctness.

# 9. Truth/reference doctrine

There is no universal truth backend.

A Challenge `ReferencePolicy` may establish primary or corroborating authority using:

- analytic or semi-analytic solution;
- qualified numerical solver;
- experiment;
- customer-hosted solver service;
- precomputed dataset;
- third-party reference service;
- multi-fidelity combination.

Every source still requires scope, provenance, uncertainty, failure policy, and applicability.

Reference failure is not candidate failure.

# 10. Burgers constitutional lesson

The historical Burgers PoC revealed several general failures that must remain visible:

- varying viscosity while hiding viscosity from the candidate makes the mapping underdetermined;
- the historical spatial-balance proxy is not a full Burgers PDE residual because it omits `d_t(u)`;
- a biased generator can make a generator-matching candidate appear better than a more physically correct candidate;
- low-viscosity regimes require explicit numerical qualification;
- reconstruction variance can be scientifically material.

Owner-recommended first authoritative repair direction:

- fix `nu = 5e-3` for the first qualified Burgers Challenge;
- use periodic Cole–Hopf as primary truth where applicable;
- use an independently qualified numerical solver only as a corroborating witness;
- measure field error plus qualified physical invariants and stress behavior;
- qualify reconstruction/evaluation variability before high-resolution economic decisions.

This is scientific design direction, not a claim of production-qualified Burgers today.

# 11. Frontier doctrine

Single-Challenge frontier flow:

```text
candidate screen
        ↓
common fresh frontier-promotion exam
incumbent + contenders
        ↓
LeaderReplacementPolicy
        ↓
SUPERIOR / NOT_SUPERIOR / INDETERMINATE
        ↓
FrontierAdvanceEvent only if SUPERIOR
```

Rules:

- every Challenge needs an explicit baseline/frontier identity;
- equivalent/unresolved challengers do not receive arbitrary scientific promotion;
- scientific resolution should bound economic reward resolution;
- screen ranking and staged evidence allocation nominate only; no partial
  build, proxy, or screen result can create `SUPERIOR`;
- the promotion interval is constructed from the full paired evidence path and
  preserves material reconstruction-by-case and other cross-source dependence;
- version changes that destroy comparability require a new frontier relation rather than silent scalar comparison;
- one paid frontier event must not be duplicated.

# 12. Portfolio doctrine

A frozen `ChallengeSetEpoch` may define a set of qualified Challenges and equal notional opportunity without claiming their scores are scientifically comparable.

Rules:

- membership/allocation freezes prospectively for an epoch;
- Challenge scores remain Challenge-specific rulers;
- unused Challenge allocation does not silently become another Challenge's scientific reward;
- equal opportunity does not imply equal scientific or computational cost;
- one participant may advance multiple Challenges if it independently earns those events.

# 13. Settlement doctrine

Scientific event and payment are separate:

```text
qualified Challenge
→ FrontierAdvanceEvent
→ SettlementObligation
→ treasury/custody governance
→ payout
```

Treasury laws:

1. treasury cannot create scientific entitlement;
2. settlement failure does not erase scientific frontier state;
3. no payout without an exact identity/evidence chain;
4. one event cannot be paid twice;
5. treasury governance verifies settlement legitimacy, not scientific merit;
6. inactive/unadvanced Challenge allocation must not silently reward an unrelated miner.

A treasury-neuron approach is a candidate transport/economic architecture, not production-qualified merely because it is specified.

# 14. Generalized construction doctrine

Long-term discovery ladder:

```text
parameters
→ recipes / TrainingStrategy
→ architectures / compositions
→ ModelConstructionStrategy
→ construction methods
→ ConstructionProgram / construction algorithms
```

Stable abstraction rule:

> **Abstract the interface now; do not broaden execution freedom now.**

P0 fresh retraining is the first form of producer-independent reconstruction. Future `ReconstructionProtocol` may permit richer registered rebuilding while preserving security and provenance.

A complete reconstructed artifact may be frozen and reused across the
authorized cases in its evaluation window; Carbon does not retrain once per
case. Every scientifically scored or nominated candidate receives the
Challenge-registered complete base reconstruction evidence, which may require
one or more builds according to the construction family. A Challenge may then
stage repeated promotion builds and random stability audits under a
preregistered `ReconstructionEvidencePolicy`. Producer-independent repeat
realizations may use registered pairing or common random numbers, but shared
data, backbone, seed-role, hardware, and implementation dependence remains in
the analysis. Scientific sequential stopping follows the qualified decision
boundaries; a separate heuristic futility stop returns only
`EVIDENCE_DEFERRED`. Exhaustion without resolution is `INDETERMINATE` with
reason `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not permission to promote on weaker evidence.

No producer-supplied arbitrary executable gains official-evaluation authority by default.

# 15. Agentic exploration doctrine

Agentic search may:

- generate hypotheses;
- propose methods;
- explore parameterizations;
- propose Challenges or experiments through governed interfaces;
- synthesize evidence for human/scientific review;
- learn from structured ExperimentRecords.

Agentic search may not:

- redefine a current Challenge after seeing outcomes;
- expose or reconstruct the hidden exam;
- certify its own construction method;
- change mandatory physics criteria to improve its rank;
- make Landscape or Physics Intelligence the source of scientific truth.

> **Landscape proposes; registered contracts decide; independent experiments adjudicate.**

# 16. Physics Intelligence doctrine

> **Physics Intelligence is provenance-bearing knowledge about how model-construction interventions interact with physical structure, regime, measurement, and engineering context, demonstrated by improved prospective scientific or engineering decisions rather than retrospective narrative alone.**

Possible future roles include:

- method recommendation;
- transfer prediction;
- experiment allocation;
- failure-risk prediction;
- qualification prioritization.

No such role is production-proven merely because Carbon accumulates ExperimentRecords.

---

# 17. Product qualification doctrine

Search and product qualification are separate planes.

> **Rank nominates. Evidence qualifies.**

A Product Qualification identity should bind an exact artifact/system, environment/runtime where material, operating context, evidence pack/battery, limitations, and version.

A new adaptation, material model change, or changed deployment context may require a new qualification identity.

Carbon qualification is not universal regulatory approval and does not replace the customer's governing engineering authority.

---

# 18. Claim-control non-claims

Unless later evidence explicitly establishes otherwise, Carbon should not claim:

- production-qualified Burgers;
- universal truth from Julia/SciML or any one solver;
- successful multi-Challenge LIVE portfolio;
- empirically proven superiority over centralized search;
- production-qualified generalized `ConstructionProgram` execution;
- production treasury settlement;
- guaranteed commercial product fitness from subnet rank;
- validated Physics Intelligence from retrospective evidence alone.

# 19. Roadmap doctrine

> **First prove the judge. Then prove the portfolio. Then deepen the physics. Then widen the search. Bring industry in throughout.**

Recommended scientific progression:

```text
Stage 1A — prove one judge
Stage 1B — prove the Phase-0 Challenge portfolio
Stage 2  — deepen physics
Stage 3  — prove model-family neutrality
Stage 4  — commercial discovery
Stage 5  — product qualification
Stage 6  — broaden construction freedom
```

Avoid increasing physics depth, model freedom, and commercial realism simultaneously unless evidence and operational maturity justify it.
