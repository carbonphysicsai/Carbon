# Wave K: Generalized agentic construction discovery

**Status:** PLANNED<br>
**Purpose:** Widen search from training strategies toward construction methods and programs.

## What this wave is

Wave K expands the participant search object from bounded TrainingStrategy values toward richer ModelConstructionStrategy objects and later ConstructionPrograms.

## Why Carbon needs it

The strongest construction improvements may involve architecture, composition, objectives, curricula, representations, solver hybrids, or staged procedures rather than parameter tuning alone.

## What success means

Agents can propose broader methods through registered representations while the independent evaluator keeps control of official measurements and grades.

## What it unlocks

A larger discovery space without surrendering evaluator independence.

## What it still does not mean

Construction programs cannot access official evaluator internals or define the measurements that prove their own success.

## Key objects

- `ModelConstructionStrategy`
- `ConstructionProgram`
- `Registered strategy graph`
