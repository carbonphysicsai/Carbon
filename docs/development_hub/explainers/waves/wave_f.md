# Wave F: Product qualification and specialist systems

**Status:** PLANNED  
**Purpose:** Separate search success from deployable product evidence.

## What this wave is

Wave F qualifies an exact model artifact, runtime, deployment context, limitations, answerability policy, and requalification triggers using a Product Qualification Pack and Product Battery.

## Why Carbon needs it

A candidate can rank well in a Challenge and still lack enough evidence for a customer deployment or a specialist product.

## What success means

Carbon can state what one exact artifact can support, in which context, with which limitations and lifecycle obligations.

## What it unlocks

Qualified model programs, deployment, specialist banks, and recurring requalification work.

## What it still does not mean

Challenge rank does not automatically qualify a product, and component evidence does not automatically qualify a composed system.

## Key objects

- `ProductQualificationPack`
- `ProductBatteryRecord`
- `QualificationRecord`
- `Runtime identity`
- `Requalification triggers`
