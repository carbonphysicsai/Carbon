# Wave N: Prospective Physics Intelligence

**Status:** PLANNED  
**Purpose:** Prove that accumulated evidence improves future registered decisions.

## What this wave is

Wave N tests whether Carbon's evidence can improve method choice, intervention choice, transfer prediction, experiment prioritization, risk detection, or qualification evidence allocation.

## Why Carbon needs it

Historical pattern fit can look impressive without helping the next decision. Carbon must measure prospective lift against a registered baseline.

## What success means

Prospective experiments show that Physics Intelligence improves a specified decision with adequate evidence and known limitations.

## What it unlocks

A stronger scientific and commercial intelligence product when the measured lift survives.

## What it still does not mean

Retrospective correlations or a compelling dashboard do not establish decision value.
