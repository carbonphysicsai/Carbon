# Wave H: Frontier promotion and Challenge portfolio

**Status:** PLANNED<br>
**Purpose:** Separate ordinary Challenge ranking from verified frontier advance.

## What this wave is

Wave H introduces persistent frontier records, promotion exams, Challenge-set epochs, and explicit SUPERIOR, NOT_SUPERIOR, and INDETERMINATE outcomes.

## Why Carbon needs it

A local ranking or floating-point improvement does not prove that a contender moved the scientific frontier on comparable fresh evidence.

## What success means

Carbon promotes a contender only through the registered promotion policy and records one exact frontier event when the evidence resolves it.

## What it unlocks

Event-bound economic settlement and a governed portfolio of Challenges.

## What it still does not mean

Incumbency does not earn perpetual rewards, unresolved evidence does not create a winner, and scores remain non-comparable across Challenges by default.
