# Wave J: Model-family neutrality

**Status:** PLANNED<br>
**Purpose:** Evaluate the registered physical job across qualified model families.

## What this wave is

Wave J adds reconstruction adapters for additional qualified families such as neural operators, reduced-order models, and hybrid or symbolic-numeric systems.

## Why Carbon needs it

Carbon should reward evidence on the physical task instead of favoring one architecture label or implementation fashion.

## What success means

Different families enter through registered input, output, reconstruction, and evaluation contracts and face the same applicable scientific requirements.

## What it unlocks

Broader discovery supply and fair comparison across model families.

## What it still does not mean

No family receives scientific privilege because it is neural, mechanistic, symbolic, hybrid, or popular.

## Key objects

- `Family-specific reconstruction adapters`
- `Common task contracts`
- `Qualified model-family support`
