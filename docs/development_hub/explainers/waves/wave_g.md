# Wave G: Commercial, private, and sponsored engagement plane

**Status:** PLANNED<br>
**Purpose:** Serve real enterprises without moving business authority into the judge.

## What this wave is

Wave G connects the business canon to scientific execution through engagement, rights, privacy, reference-access, deliverable, acceptance, and economics records.

## Why Carbon needs it

Customers need private deployment, clear rights, bounded deliverables, and commercial acceptance without gaining the power to change scientific evidence rules.

## What success means

Carbon can structure enterprise work with explicit rights, privacy, truth access, deliverables, payment, and lifecycle controls.

## What it unlocks

Evidence Audits, Sponsored Discovery, qualification, lifecycle, private/VPC delivery, and later enterprise platform workflows.

## What it still does not mean

A designed product is not a live capability. Carbon cannot sell an unqualified privacy mode or let payment alter the scientific ruler.
