# Wave D: Human scientific qualification

**Status:** PLANNED<br>
**Purpose:** Earn the right to call the first exam LIVE.

## What this wave is

Wave D collects the human-owned scientific decisions that software cannot invent: physical envelope, population adequacy, truth hierarchy, measurement qualification, thresholds, uncertainty policy, disclosure, and exact activation.

## Why Carbon needs it

Passing engineering tests cannot prove that the exam measures the intended physical claim or resolves the decision at the required scientific standard.

## What success means

The responsible scientific owners approve the exact Challenge and Launch Bar inputs, then authorize the first LIVE activation prospectively.

## What it unlocks

Carbon can operate a scientifically qualified Challenge and later distinguish ordinary ranking from frontier promotion.

## What it still does not mean

One LIVE Challenge does not prove a broad portfolio, product qualification, commercial repeatability, or settlement correctness.

## Key objects

- `Qualified population`
- `Qualified reference hierarchy`
- `Measurement qualification`
- `Scientific thresholds`
- `Launch Bar`
- `LIVE activation`
