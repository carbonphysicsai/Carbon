# Carbon launch path — v1.0.6 prospective payment amendment

**Decision:** OWNER-C0-REWARD-01, 2026-09-09 owner execution mandate.
**Predecessor:** Carbon_Testnet_to_Mainnet_Launch_Path_v1.0.5.md.
**Scope:** payment-route and C0 execution amendment; no deployment approval.

DIRECT_WINNER_PLUS_BURN is the persistent supported direct-payment option.
An optional treasury reserve and treasury migration are outside the direct
launch critical path. Mandatory-treasury and temporary-direct-only clauses in
v1.0.4/v1.0.5 and related sequencing records are superseded prospectively only
on those points. Historical approvals and receipts remain unchanged.

C0 executes NET-1, NET-2, NET-3, C-REWARD, NET-4A, NET-4B, NET-5 and NET-6 as
sequential implementation deliveries under OWNER-DX-03. NET-0 dispositions and
the exact reward/test boundary are recorded in
`.agent/plans/C0_score_reward_program.md`. A disposable localnet may use
synthetic accepted fixtures through a nominal localnet intent, throwaway keys
and valueless tokens. Fixture authority cannot construct C1 scientific evidence,
public-testnet intents, LIVE results or valuable-network settlement rights.

G2 LOCALNET_READY still requires actual localnet submission, inclusion/finality,
applicable reveal, stored weight rows, verified burn semantics, epoch observations
and recovery evidence. A source pin, mock or SDK import is not that evidence.
If the executor lacks a working node environment, complete independent artifacts,
retain the precise failure and leave G2 not ready with the exact operator command.

C1 preserves the real scientific vertical, accepted receipt/evidence archive,
independent reconstruction, reference/measurement/scoring, isolation and failure
requirements. C2 preserves the existing C-W1 through C-W4 DoD and public-testnet
integration evidence, prospectively consuming the accepted-record reward core
instead of a free full-allocation winner window. No public-testnet execution is
authorized by this amendment. Scientific values come from their owning contracts.

Substantive Wave D qualification, Wave H frontier/finality where applicable,
validator economics, security, custody, observability, consensus and settlement
reconciliation remain required by their domains. Their implementation or
qualification is not implied by this payment-route change. The direct route
does not require I-00 treasury custody, I-02 treasury routing or I-05 treasury
migration. Applicable ledger, exactly-once rights and validator-economics work
remain; changing destination is not completion of those requirements. G6's old
treasury-only label cannot be required solely to exclude the direct option;
an eventual direct mechanism qualification must supply equivalent applicable
scientific/economic/operational evidence under a separately owned contract.
No G4-G7 readiness or mainnet authority is granted here.

No treasury is deployed or funded. A later optional reserve ticket must define
custody, destination health, settled plus pending/committed/unrevealed accounting,
conservative emission bounds and withdrawal rights. It must not promise exact
per-Challenge purses, a hard deposit cap, automatic jackpots, old-balance winner
rights or cross-Challenge spending. Missing treasury state routes new unearned
allocation to burn. A stopped publisher can leave prior weights effective;
only actual chain reconciliation establishes current exposure.

B-E4 remains OPTIONAL / DEFERRED / NON-BLOCKING, empirical effectiveness remains
UNMEASURED, and B-01G retains its actual unfinished non-blocking status.
