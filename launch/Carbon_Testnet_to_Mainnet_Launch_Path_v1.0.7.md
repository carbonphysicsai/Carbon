# Carbon launch path — v1.0.7 DEVELOPMENT testnet amendment

**Decision:** `OWNER-C-W1-DEV-TESTNET-01`, 2026-09-15 owner direction.
**Predecessor:** `Carbon_Testnet_to_Mainnet_Launch_Path_v1.0.6.md`.
**Scope:** public/synthetic DEVELOPMENT testnet demonstration only.

This amendment adds a bounded path from already accepted authenticated and
isolated DEVELOPMENT execution to an explicitly approved Bittensor public-
testnet transaction. It does not redefine G3, official C-W1, C-09, C-EA2,
protected processing, scientific/security qualification, mainnet or LIVE.

```text
G2 checked publication mechanism
  + C-08 authenticated public/synthetic request
  + C-03 isolated reconstruction
  + C-06/C-07 signed DEVELOPMENT evidence
  + C-10 quarantine
  + bounded local review/export evidence
  + exact testnet registration/capability and one-dispatch authorization
    -> C-W1-D1 DEVELOPMENT testnet demonstration
```

The profile may proceed without a real archive acknowledgement because its
receipts remain structurally ineligible for official/protected consumers and
its same-host retention limitation is explicit. Unresolved scientific evidence
can produce only a complete all-burn vector, never a candidate winner. Actual
submission requires exact network/genesis/netuid/runtime, registered public
identity and UID, applicable permit/stake or owner capability, a finalized
block window, and a separately recorded one-dispatch authorization. Inclusion,
finalization, stored row and epoch behavior remain separate observations.

AWS deployment/spending is deferred. Hippius is the preferred future storage
provider but supplies no present capability or claim until a later bounded
qualification examines Carbon's archive, custody, retention and recovery
contract. Treasury and B-E4 remain optional/deferred/non-blocking.
