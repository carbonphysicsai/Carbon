## 1. Owner sequencing correction

B-E4's experimental research program is `OPTIONAL`, `DEFERRED`, and
`NON_BLOCKING`. Prior-utility superiority, experimental diversity thresholds,
qualifying agent gauntlets, shadow research campaigns, and their separate
research calibration or ratification are not prerequisites for B-GATE, Wave C,
network integration, testnet, mainnet, or Research Concierge delivery. An
absent, negative, or inconclusive result limits claims about the prior; it does
not create a release defect or stop delivery.

The retained B-E4 runner, resource accounting, journals, authorization
mechanics, and historical evidence remain reusable research infrastructure.
No deferred experiment is relabelled passed, qualified, or completed. Any
future run needs its own then-current budget and execution authority.

## 2. Research Concierge engineering contract

The first Research Concierge is a product capability, not a separately
qualified scientific product. Its launch candidate must:

- use only approved public/bootstrap sources and requester-authorized context;
- preserve protected-data and official-evaluator boundaries;
- cite source identities/versions for factual guidance where applicable;
- distinguish suggestions, hypotheses, and user context from official results;
- enforce authentication, authorization, tenant/session isolation, quotas,
  timeouts, tool limits, and resource/spend controls;
- represent unavailable sources, unsupported questions, and provider failures
  truthfully without inventing evidence or results;
- retain response/source/policy lineage and collect bounded user feedback;
- expose operational monitoring and kill switches that can disable reasoning,
  retrieval classes, updates, or the entire feature; and
- preserve official evaluation capacity by degrading or disabling Concierge
  first when shared resources are constrained.

Focused deterministic tests and bounded integration checks verify that
contract. A paid benchmark campaign, statistically significant utility lift,
independent research qualification, or new certification framework is not a
release prerequisite. Empirical effectiveness remains `UNMEASURED` until
supported by ordinary operational feedback or separately budgeted research.

The Concierge does not gate the C0/C1/C2 network spine. If it is not ready when
the core network path is ready, Carbon may ship it disabled and enable it later
after its engineering, security, privacy, and operations checks pass. This
does not weaken authentication, protected-data, failure, isolation, or
deployment approval requirements.
