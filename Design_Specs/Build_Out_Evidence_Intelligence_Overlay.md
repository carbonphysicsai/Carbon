## 1. Purpose

This overlay adds the owner-approved evidence/archive, launch agent communication, research-demand, and paid-research implementation reservations to Carbon's existing wave structure.

It does **not** replace `Build_Out.md`, activate a future wave, or modify the current Wave B selected ticket. When a future controlling board activates one of these lanes, the exact ticket contract becomes the bounded implementation authority.

## 3. Why Wave C owns capture

Carbon cannot recover evidence it failed to preserve when real official workloads begin. Therefore production official orchestration must include durable evidence capture before a completed official result finalizes.

This is a production foundation, not a Landscape feature. Landscape may arrive later and consume the archive.

## 4. Why Wave C owns launch dialogue

The owner wants agent communication open at launch. Authenticated external transport, identity, quotas, operational isolation, and production runtime belong to Wave C. The launch Concierge can provide value using public/approved knowledge before learned official-derived Landscape products exist.

Wave C dialogue therefore uses bootstrap-approved resources only. It cannot claim Carbon-learned official intelligence unless later Wave E release gates authorize the exact artifact.

## 5. Why Wave E owns D12 and EvidenceBriefs

D12 and EvidenceBriefs depend on the Landscape/release layer:

- D12 aggregates permission-eligible research demand for planning and commercial intelligence.
- EvidenceBriefs are offline approved derivatives of eligible evidence and public science.
- Correction propagation connects archive evidence to Landscape claims and external guidance.

None changes official scoring authority.

## 6. Why Wave G owns paid Research Copilot

Paid research requires customer bounds, rights, quote/spend authority, execution isolation, contribution accounting, and commercial validation. These align with the existing customer/commercial Wave G lane.

Paid pilot preregistration precedes paid execution implementation.

## 7. Launch minimum

For first authenticated external miner launch, the owner target is:

```text

+

+

+

+
explicit evidence-gap capture
+
permission-aware demand observations
+
feedback / private update subscriptions
+
kill switch
+
shared-resource isolation from official evaluation
```

Under `OWNER-ROADMAP-02`, this list is the Concierge feature's engineering
enablement contract, not a dependency of the C0/C1/C2 network spine. The core
network may ship with Concierge disabled. Concierge enablement requires its
deterministic/bounded engineering, security, privacy, and operations checks;
separate empirical utility qualification is optional later work and empirical
effectiveness remains `UNMEASURED` until evidence exists.

Learned official-derived EvidenceBriefs, paid hosted research, and Research Scientist capabilities are not launch prerequisites unless a later owner decision explicitly promotes them.

## 8. Cross-wave hard rules

1. Official evidence capture precedes finalization of completed real official results.
2. Landscape outages cannot erase source evidence.
3. The live Concierge has no protected evidence-vault credential.
4. An agent-facing model never decides its own source permissions.
5. D12 demand never becomes scientific truth, score, or emission weight.
6. Paying cannot reveal privileged official evidence or alter official treatment.
7. Port B remains operations-only.
8. Port C proposes research/future changes; governance decides.
9. Specialist Bank retains its independent qualification authority.
10. Corrections append and propagate; historical evidence/responses do not mutate.
11. Non-essential workloads cannot starve required official evidence capture.
12. Paid-pilot metrics are fixed before paid execution is evaluated.

## 9. Future board activation

When Wave C is activated, its controlling board may select the already materialized C-EA0..3 and C-DC1..4 ticket contracts, or prospectively approve an equivalent decomposition while preserving identity mapping and semantics.

When Wave E is activated, its board may select E-EA4..7, E-EB1, E-D12, and E-RI1.

When Wave G is activated, its board may select G-PR0..5.

Do not pre-mark any of these tickets `done`, `implemented`, or `tested` because this overlay exists.

The ticket files are a passive reservations register. They do not compete with `.agent/WAVE.md`, create a controlling board, activate a wave, or authorize implementation.
