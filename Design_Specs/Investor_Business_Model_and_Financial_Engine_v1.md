# Carbon Investor Business Model and Financial Engine v1

**Status:** OWNER-RECOMMENDED company-building architecture for founder/finance/investor review.  
**Purpose:** translate the locked hybrid business model into a financeable operating model with explicit revenue classes, margin drivers, customer-economics metrics, capital allocation, and evidence gates.  
**Boundary:** this document sits outside Carbon's scientific authority. No financial target, pricing decision, investor pressure, or token objective may alter scientific Challenge/qualification semantics.

---

## 1. Company model

Carbon's company-level architecture is:

```text
LAND WITH HIGH-VALUE PROGRAMS
Evidence Audit / Challenge Design / Sponsored Discovery
        ↓
EXPAND INTO TECHNICAL VALUE
Model Development / Qualification / Deployment
        ↓
CREATE RECURRENCE
Lifecycle / Requalification / Support / Usage
        ↓
PLATFORMIZE
Enterprise Evidence Platform / API / OEM
        ↓
SCALE SUPPLY
Network-backed discovery / evaluator market
        ↓
COMPOUND
Rights-permitted Physics Intelligence and method knowledge
```

The business should become progressively less dependent on founder/expert labor per revenue dollar.

## 3. Core finance metrics

Track from the first paid engagement:

### Growth / sales

- bookings;
- recognized revenue;
- qualified pipeline;
- win rate;
- average sales cycle;
- pilot ACV;
- expansion ACV;
- ARR / recurring-like revenue;
- net revenue retention once recurring cohort exists.

### Margin

- gross margin by product family;
- gross contribution per engagement;
- truth/evaluation cost as % of revenue;
- delivery labor as % of revenue;
- reward pass-through excluded from gross-margin distortion where appropriate;
- revenue per technical FTE.

### Productization

- custom engineering hours per engagement;
- reusable adapter/template coverage;
- automated evidence percentage;
- repeat-delivery cycle time;
- platform revenue share;
- API/OEM revenue share.

### Land-and-expand

- Evidence Audit -> remediation/discovery attach;
- Audit -> qualification attach;
- program -> lifecycle attach;
- customer second-project rate;
- customer platform conversion;
- expansion revenue within 12 months.

### Network leverage

- commercial workload eligible for network;
- commercial workload actually network-executed;
- network participant count per program;
- qualified contender cost;
- discovery performance versus centralized comparator;
- `NetworkUtilityConversion`.

## 4. Revenue-quality ladder

Revenue should migrate over time:

```text
LOWER SCALABILITY
one-off bespoke integration
custom scientific program
        ↓
repeatable packaged service
        ↓
usage commitment / lifecycle retainer
        ↓
subscription platform
        ↓
API / OEM / license
        ↓
HIGHER SCALABILITY
marketplace / network and intelligence layers
```

Bespoke high-ACV work is strategically acceptable when it creates reusable product infrastructure, reference customers, rights-permitted evidence, or an expansion path.

## 6. Pricing governance

Price from four layers:

```text
1. BASE PRODUCT VALUE
2. VARIABLE TRUTH / EVALUATION CAPACITY
3. SECURITY / DEPLOYMENT COMPLEXITY
4. RIGHTS / EXCLUSIVITY / COMMERCIAL RISK
```

Optional success fees may sit above a base program price but cannot replace the base price when Carbon performs legitimate work regardless of scientific outcome.

No engagement receives unlimited truth/evaluation resources without explicit commercial treatment.

## 7. Margin expansion plan

### Stage FM0 — bespoke pilots

Expected cost concentration:
- founder/scientist time;
- one-off truth adapter;
- report construction;
- manual orchestration.

### Stage FM1 — repeatable Evidence Audit

Standardize:
- intake;
- scientific authoring templates;
- evidence objects;
- report generation;
- standard truth adapters;
- security patterns.

### Stage FM2 — platform-assisted delivery

Automate:
- engagement workspace;
- experiment registry;
- report/evidence package;
- lifecycle tracking;
- billing/usage metering;
- customer/auditor projections.

### Stage FM3 — scalable network and OEM

Margin improves through:
- external research supply;
- standardized network execution;
- API/OEM distribution;
- software subscriptions;
- repeated qualification/requalification.

## 8. Headcount architecture

Early company should remain senior and cross-functional.

Required capability pools:

```text
scientific / SciML
protocol / backend engineering
product / enterprise platform
security / private deployment
commercial / partnerships
customer engineering
finance/legal/IP (initially partly external)
```

Do not build large functional teams before revenue evidence requires them.

Hiring gates should map to proven bottlenecks:

- first sales hire after founder-led motion shows repeatable pipeline;
- customer success/support after repeat customer base emerges;
- specialized deployment/security after private enterprise demand validates it;
- broader marketplace ops after network-backed commercial programs exist.

---

## 9. Capital-allocation priorities

Investor capital should primarily de-risk:

1. first scientifically credible product;
2. first paid customer;
3. repeatable Evidence Audit workflow;
4. private truth/data integration;
5. first Sponsored Discovery Pilot;
6. qualification/lifecycle productization;
7. platform primitives;
8. network commercial qualification;
9. enterprise security and OEM integration.

Do not spend early capital on broad horizontal feature surface before a buyer repeatedly pays for the wedge.

---

## 10. Financing milestone logic

A financing round should ideally move Carbon across a clear proof boundary.

### Pre-seed / founder capital

Prove:
- architecture;
- qualified first scientific loop;
- customer discovery;
- first pilot design.

### Seed

Prove:
- paid pilots;
- repeatable Evidence Audit;
- expansion attachment;
- initial recurring/lifecycle revenue;
- network-backed commercial discovery;
- early gross-margin/productization improvement.

### Series A-type proof

Would ideally require evidence of:
- repeatable enterprise sales;
- material recurring/software component;
- multi-customer retention/expansion;
- improving unit economics;
- private/enterprise deployment maturity;
- network leverage or OEM distribution that materially increases scale.

These labels are illustrative fundraising stages, not fundraising commitments.

## 13. Investor narrative discipline

Separate clearly:

```text
WHAT EXISTS
WHAT IS SPECIFIED
WHAT HAS BEEN TESTED
WHAT CUSTOMERS HAVE PAID FOR
WHAT HAS RECURRED
WHAT IS A MODELLED FUTURE STATE
```

Do not use scientific sophistication as a substitute for commercial evidence.

Do not use token performance as a substitute for customer adoption.

Do not use market-size estimates as a substitute for a credible wedge.

---

## 14. Venture-scale test

Carbon is venture-scale only if at least several of these become true:

- Evidence Audit becomes repeatable and high-ACV;
- customer expansion materially exceeds first-contract value;
- qualification/lifecycle creates durable recurrence;
- enterprise platform or OEM becomes material;
- network lowers discovery cost or improves output enough to create marketplace leverage;
- evidence/adapter infrastructure creates meaningful switching/integration value;
- rights-permitted evidence/intelligence improves future products;
- Carbon sells across multiple physics-intensive verticals without custom rebuilding the company each time.

If Carbon remains only bespoke scientific consulting, it can still be a useful business but fails the locked venture-scale objective.

---

## 15. Final financial-engine principle

> **Use services to discover and finance the product, use software and lifecycle to create recurrence, use the network to scale external scientific supply, and measure every transition in revenue quality, margin, retention, and customer value rather than assuming the architecture automatically creates a venture-scale company.**
