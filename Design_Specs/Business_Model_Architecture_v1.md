# Carbon Business Model Architecture v1 — LOCKED HYBRID MODEL

**Status:** OWNER-LOCKED business architecture for company-building, investor materials, commercial planning, and product sequencing.  
**Scope:** Carbon the business outside the scientific decision loop. This document does not alter Challenge science, scoring, qualification authority, or miner evaluation semantics.  
**Relationship:** operationalizes `Commercial_Value_Capture_Architecture_v1.md`, `Commercial_Engagement_Architecture_v1.md`, and `Alpha_Network_Value_Accrual_v1.md`.

## 1. Locked business thesis

Carbon will pursue a **hybrid business model**:

```text
SERVICES
land the customer and solve high-value bespoke integration problems
        ↓
ENTERPRISE PLATFORM
make repeated evidence / qualification workflows recurring and software-led
        ↓
NETWORK MARKETPLACE
scale discovery and external research supply through the subnet
        ↓
QUALIFICATION + LIFECYCLE
increase contract value, retention, and recurring revenue
        ↓
PHYSICS INTELLIGENCE
compound evidence into higher-margin decision products only when prospectively validated
```

> **Services get Carbon into the customer. Platform makes the relationship recurring. The network makes discovery scalable. Qualification increases contract value. Lifecycle increases retention. Physics intelligence compounds the moat.**

This is the company-level business architecture. It is intentionally capable of standing on its own even if some network-native mechanisms mature later than the commercial company.

## 2. Company economy and network economy are separate but connected

```text
CARBON OPCO
customer contracts
services
software subscriptions
licenses
support
qualification
OEM/API
        ↕ explicit auditable bridges
CARBON NETWORK
miners
validators
sponsored frontier rewards
network execution
Alpha utility
scientific treasury
```

Rules:

1. off-chain revenue is real company revenue and must be commercially viable on its own;
2. Alpha value is not assumed to arise automatically from OpCo revenue;
3. network value accrues when commercially valuable work genuinely uses or requires the subnet;
4. enterprise customers may pay in fiat; crypto UX is not a mandatory sales obstacle;
5. token economics never become scientific authority;
6. network-connected products should increase useful demand for miner/validator work rather than create artificial token choreography.

## 5. Locked go-to-market sequencing

### Phase B0 — sell the judge

First commercial wedge:

> **Carbon Evidence Audit** — independently evaluate a customer's existing fast physical model against a qualified problem-specific exam and return evidence about what survives, where it fails, and what stronger evidence or remediation is required.

Why first:

- strongest fit to current architecture;
- does not depend on a mature open construction market;
- directly addresses engineering credibility pain;
- creates a natural upsell into remediation, discovery, qualification, and lifecycle;
- generates reusable product workflows and evidence infrastructure.

### Phase B1 — sell competitive discovery

> **Carbon Sponsored Discovery Program** — customer supplies a meaningful physical problem and truth path; Carbon authors/qualifies the Challenge, operates competitive search, independently judges contenders, and delivers frontier/candidate evidence.

### Phase B2 — sell bounded deployment evidence

Qualified Model Program + deployment + requalification.

### Phase B3 — sell recurring infrastructure

Enterprise Evidence Platform, private Challenge workspaces, lifecycle registry, API/OEM integrations.

### Phase B4 — monetize compounding intelligence

Only after prospective performance is proven.

## 6. Standalone-business test

Carbon OpCo must be able to present an investor with a coherent company story independent of speculative token appreciation.

The business must demonstrate or plausibly scale toward:

- high-value enterprise ACV;
- repeatable productized services;
- increasing software/usage share;
- recurring revenue and renewals;
- gross-margin expansion as workflows standardize;
- proprietary workflow/evidence infrastructure;
- rights-aware reusable knowledge assets;
- network supply-side leverage that reduces marginal R&D/search cost;
- qualification/lifecycle attachment revenue;
- multi-industry applicability without requiring all verticals at once.

The investor case must not depend on “Alpha goes up.”

Alpha is an additional network-economic asset whose utility should strengthen as commercially valuable scientific work routes through Carbon.

## 8. Commercial constitutional rules

1. **Build a real company, not a token-dependent story.**
2. **Services are a wedge, not the terminal margin structure.**
3. **Every repeatable service should be a candidate for software/productization.**
4. **Every customer engagement should have a designed expansion path.**
5. **Recurring lifecycle and qualification revenue are first-class, not afterthoughts.**
6. **Marketplace/network economics should scale supply, not create customer friction.**
7. **Enterprise fiat UX is permitted and expected.**
8. **Commercial revenue and network reward accounting remain distinct.**
9. **The company retains reusable rights only where contracts actually permit them.**
10. **No business model may weaken scientific evidence to manufacture commercial success.**
11. **Physics Intelligence is sold only when its prospective utility is demonstrated.**
12. **Investor materials separate implemented, piloted, contracted, and future revenue streams.**

## 9. Investor-facing company identity

Preferred company-level framing:

> **Carbon is building the discovery, evidence, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models. We land through high-value independent evidence and sponsored R&D programs, convert repeated workflows into enterprise software and APIs, use an open research network to scale discovery, and monetize qualification and model lifecycle as recurring infrastructure.**

Network relationship:

> **The subnet is Carbon's scalable external research and verification market; the company is the enterprise product, integration, rights, qualification, and lifecycle layer around it.**
