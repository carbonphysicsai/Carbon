# Landscape Agent vNext Reconciliation — Frontier Portfolio and Qualified-Challenge Integration

**Status:** OWNER-RECOMMENDED reconciliation overlay; current `Landscape_Agent.md` remains implementation/narrative authority until migrated.  
**Purpose:** Preserve Landscape's epistemic role while removing stale assumptions that it dynamically aims base performance emissions.

## 1. Unchanged constitutional rule

> **Landscape proposes. Registered contracts decide. Independent experiments adjudicate.**

Landscape remains decision support, not scientific or economic execution authority.

## 2. Inputs become richer

Landscape should ingest, where available:

```text
PhysicalSystemSpec / context features
InstanceDistributionContract identity
SamplingPlan identity
ReferencePolicy identity
MeasurementContract identities
Score Pack identity
reconstruction provenance
ScoreResult state
promotion-exam outcome
FrontierAdvanceEvent
settlement state (separate economic metadata)
product-qualification outcomes
```

This preserves the distinction between scientific performance, frontier selection, and payment.

## 3. Port A — miner orientation

Still allowed:

- disclosure-limited/noisy prior guidance;
- hypothesis suggestions;
- safe aggregate evidence;
- broad failure-mode orientation.

Still forbidden:

- official realizations/seeds;
- exact hidden stress composition;
- reversible rich diagnostic vectors;
- treasury/private governance material not intended for miners.

## 4. Port B — validator-private efficiency / scrutiny

Port B may:

- schedule scarce evaluation resources;
- propose supplemental scrutiny;
- identify cases/strata needing deeper future evidence;
- prioritize reproducibility/audit work.

Port B may not:

- silently change the mandatory Score Pack per miner;
- change the target population after seeing a candidate;
- vary frontier-promotion semantics ad hoc;
- convert an infra/reference failure into a candidate physics failure.

## 5. Port C — governance / research-economy proposals

The previous “challenge-weight / emission targeting / decay tuning” framing is superseded for the base performance market.

Port C may propose:

- new Challenge authoring priorities;
- Challenge activation, freeze, retirement, or requalification;
- future `ChallengeSetEpoch` membership;
- changes to a future Challenge version based on observed failure modes;
- separate information-value bounties for replication, ablation, transfer, uncertainty reduction, or targeted experiments;
- treasury/governance risk analysis;
- portfolio-health metrics such as participation, frontier velocity, evidence maturity, and saturation.

Port C may **not** dynamically change equal `1/N` base performance opportunity inside a frozen `ChallengeSetEpoch`.

> **Equal Challenge opportunity is a prospective portfolio rule; Landscape does not continuously vote it away.**

## 6. Port D — product routing

Port D remains separate from performance reward. It may nominate frontier or non-frontier candidates for deeper product evidence based on job relevance and available evidence.

A `FrontierAdvanceEvent` is useful evidence but is not a Product Qualification Record.

> **Rank nominates. Evidence qualifies.**

## 7. Physics intelligence and frontier data

Frontier events are selected outcomes and therefore create additional selection bias. A history containing only winners is not a causal dataset.

Landscape should retain:

```text
all eligible authoritative experiments
inadmissible results
indeterminate results
selection/promotion mechanism
frontier outcome
censoring
reconstruction variability
measurement identity
population context
```

Physics intelligence still requires prospective decision lift, not retrospective storytelling about winners.

## 8. Challenge similarity and transfer

Similarity may help propose where to search or which Challenge to author next. It does not justify:

- raw cross-Challenge score normalization;
- automatic transfer of thresholds;
- automatic transfer of MeasurementContracts;
- economic reweighting of base Challenge slots;
- product qualification transfer.

## 9. Information-value economy

If Carbon later funds informative experiments, Landscape may estimate expected information value, but that market is separate from the base frontier-performance market.

Possible separately governed bounties:

- reproduce a surprising frontier event;
- test transfer of a construction method;
- resolve a reference disagreement;
- measure a weak stratum;
- ablate a suspected causal mechanism;
- improve a truth/reference path.

Novelty alone is not performance reward.

## 10. Portfolio metrics

Useful governance-facing metrics may include:

- qualified Challenge count;
- participation by Challenge;
- admissible-candidate rate;
- time between frontier advances;
- reconstruction/rank stability;
- evidence maturity by Challenge;
- rate of Challenge freeze/requalification;
- product-promotion conversion;
- information-bounty outcomes.

These metrics inform future decisions; they do not retroactively alter current Challenge science.

## 11. Final rule

> **Landscape may help Carbon decide what Challenge, experiment, or bounty to authorize next. It does not decide who won the current scientific exam or how much an already-frozen Challenge slot is worth.**
