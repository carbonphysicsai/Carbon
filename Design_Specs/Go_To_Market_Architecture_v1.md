# Carbon Go-To-Market Architecture v1

**Status:** OWNER-RECOMMENDED for founder/commercial/product review.  
**Purpose:** convert Carbon's locked hybrid business model into a portfolio-level buyer, sales, packaging, expansion, and recurrence architecture without changing scientific authority.

---

# 1. GTM doctrine

> **Sell the customer's engineering decision problem, not the subnet.**

Carbon's commercial message changes by product, but the company-level sequence is:

```text
PROVE PAIN
      ↓
SELL A BOUNDED OUTCOME
      ↓
DELIVER INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE / DISCOVERY
      ↓
ATTACH QUALIFICATION / DEPLOYMENT
      ↓
CREATE RECURRING LIFECYCLE
      ↓
PLATFORMIZE REPEATED WORK
      ↓
USE THE NETWORK TO SCALE SUPPLY
```

Bittensor/Alpha are explained when relevant to why Carbon can scale external search/evaluation, not used as the opening enterprise value proposition.

# 2. GTM product groups

## G1 — Land products

- Carbon Evidence Audit
- Challenge Design / Feasibility

Commercial objective: low-friction paid entry, prove customer pain, learn truth/security workflow, create expansion opportunity.

## G2 — Expansion programs

- Sponsored Discovery
- Model Development
- Qualified Model Program

Commercial objective: increase ACV, solve larger customer problem, demonstrate differentiated network/search value.

## G3 — Recurring products

- Model Lifecycle / Requalification
- Enterprise Evidence Platform
- Support / private deployment

Commercial objective: ARR, retention, workflow embedding, multi-model expansion.

## G4 — Scale/channel products

- Evidence API / OEM rail
- Sponsored Frontier Market
- Model / Method licensing where rights permit

Commercial objective: distribution leverage, usage revenue, marketplace economics.

## G5 — Compounding product

- Physics Intelligence / Experiment Allocation

Commercial objective: high-margin decision support after prospective utility is proven.

# 4. Sales segmentation

## Segment A — model producers

Organizations building their own fast physical models.

Best products: Audit, Qualification, Lifecycle, Platform.

## Segment B — model seekers

Organizations with expensive physics workflows but no satisfactory fast model.

Best products: Feasibility, Sponsored Discovery, Model Development, Qualification.

## Segment C — model platforms / CAE vendors

Organizations distributing engineering modeling software.

Best products: Audit/Qualification initially, later API/OEM and network-backed discovery.

## Segment D — research sponsors

Government, industry consortia, large R&D organizations.

Best products: Challenge Design, Sponsored Discovery, Frontier Market.

# 5. Buyer roles

Carbon sales should map at least four roles in meaningful opportunities:

```text
TECHNICAL CHAMPION
cares whether Carbon solves the modeling/evidence problem

SCIENTIFIC / ENGINEERING AUTHORITY
cares whether evidence is defensible

ECONOMIC BUYER
owns budget and business/program outcome

SECURITY / PROCUREMENT BLOCKER
can stop purchase even if technical team wants it
```

For large enterprise sales, failing to identify any one of these materially raises close risk.

---

# 6. Discovery qualification score

Score each opportunity internally from 0–2 on:

- pain severity;
- decision value;
- truth availability;
- champion strength;
- economic buyer access;
- budget plausibility;
- timeline/trigger;
- privacy/security feasibility;
- deliverable clarity;
- expansion potential;
- product repeatability;
- network fit.

Do not use the score as an automated sales truth. It is prioritization support.

---

# 7. Pilot design rules

A first pilot should:

1. answer one commercially meaningful question;
2. fit one clear customer owner;
3. have bounded truth/evaluation scope;
4. produce a tangible diligence/evidence deliverable;
5. avoid requiring all future Carbon architecture;
6. preserve a credible follow-on path;
7. have a contractually useful result even if the model/frontier fails;
8. generate measurable delivery-cost and willingness-to-pay evidence.

Do not design a first customer pilot that simultaneously requires new private infrastructure, arbitrary model-family reconstruction, new treasury settlement, full Product Qualification, and new enterprise platform features unless the customer is explicitly funding that integration program.

---

# 8. Commercial packaging rules

Every proposal should separate:

```text
BASE OUTCOME
what Carbon is accountable to deliver

INCLUDED BUDGET
truth calls / candidate count / evaluation capacity / timeline

OPTIONAL MODULES
privacy topology / adapters / additional models / qualification / deployment

OUT-OF-SCOPE
what requires change order or future product

SUCCESS SEMANTICS
what constitutes delivery even if scientific result is negative
```

---

# 9. Pricing architecture

Pricing should combine three lenses:

```text
COST FLOOR
expert labor + truth + compute + integration + support

PRODUCT UNIT
audit / program / qualification / annual platform / usage

CUSTOMER VALUE
decision value, urgency, exclusivity, private deployment, risk reduction
```

Avoid exposing raw internal cost-plus logic as the sole value proposition.

No quote should provide unlimited high-fidelity truth/evaluation without an explicit cap or metered expansion.

# 10. Revenue quality metrics

Track by cohort/product:

- average contract value;
- gross contribution;
- custom expert hours;
- productization ratio;
- recurring-like share;
- expansion revenue;
- time to first value;
- sales cycle;
- pilot-to-expansion rate;
- renewal/requalification rate;
- software/usage share;
- network-executed share;
- support burden;
- evidence reuse class.

No single metric is company truth; the combination should show whether Carbon is becoming a scalable infrastructure business.

# 11. First vertical motions

## Aerospace / space / defense

Message around evidence for high-value surrogate use, optimization, design iteration, digital engineering, and private solver workflows.

Likely requirements:
- strong security/IP;
- customer-hosted truth/VPC/on-prem;
- detailed auditability;
- longer sales cycle;
- high contract potential.

## Energy / turbomachinery / industrial physics

Message around repeated simulation, plant/digital-twin workflows, design optimization, rare-regime robustness, and lifecycle evidence.

Likely advantages:
- strong economic value of faster simulation;
- recurring operational model use;
- potentially faster procurement than defense.

## CAE / engineering software strategic track

Message around Carbon as neutral evidence/qualification and external discovery infrastructure rather than replacement UI/toolchain.

Goal: find design partners for future OEM/API rail.

# 12. Network positioning in sales

Customer-facing hierarchy:

```text
1. engineering problem
2. Carbon evidence/discovery product
3. independent scientific process
4. delivery / qualification
5. network as scalable research supply where relevant
```

Do not lead with token mechanics.

For Sponsored Discovery / Frontier Market, network explanation becomes central because it is part of the product's differentiated supply side.

# 14. GTM stop-ships

Do not pursue an opportunity when:

- no material engineering decision depends on the result;
- no credible truth path exists;
- the buyer expects universal certification Carbon cannot provide;
- customer wants unlimited bespoke R&D for fixed low price;
- security promises exceed architecture;
- required rights cannot be obtained;
- success is defined so Carbon must manufacture a positive scientific result;
- product requires excessive one-off implementation with no strategic learning and weak economics;
- customer insists token mechanics become part of scientific grading.

---

# 15. Final GTM rule

> **Carbon should enter accounts through the narrowest high-value problem it can solve honestly, then expand along the customer's model lifecycle until repeated evidence work becomes recurring infrastructure.**
