# 1. Category

Primary:

> **Discovery + Evidence Infrastructure for Physics AI**

Enterprise-readable:

> **Independent discovery, evaluation, and qualification infrastructure for fast physical models.**

Investor shorthand:

> **The evidence and research-market layer for Physics AI.**

---

# 2. Company thesis

As fast physical models become easier to build, the bottleneck shifts toward:

```text
which method to try
which model actually survives
what evidence supports its use
how that evidence changes over the model lifecycle
```

Carbon is designed to own that layer.

# 4. Competitive archetypes

## CAE / simulation incumbents

Strengths:
- entrenched workflows;
- high-fidelity solvers;
- customer trust;
- large installed bases;
- broad engineering software portfolios.

Carbon differentiator:
- independent cross-tool/model evidence;
- competitive external discovery;
- explicit qualification/lifecycle layer.

Risk:
- incumbents can build native evidence and AI workflows.

Required response:
- prove neutrality and measurable value beyond vendor-native validation.

## AI-native engineering platforms

Examples include PhysicsX and Neural Concept.

Strengths:
- strong model-development platforms;
- enterprise integrations;
- workflow acceleration;
- growing engineering AI adoption.

Carbon differentiator:
- does not require being the producer of the candidate;
- separates producer from official judge;
- can evaluate multiple permitted candidate families;
- can open the construction problem to external competition;
- keeps qualification distinct from leaderboard/model-generation success.

Risk:
- these companies may expand into deeper validation/qualification.

Required response:
- win the independent evidence / research-market role rather than copy their full-stack strategy.

## Engineering testing / data AI

Example: Monolith.

Strengths:
- physical-test data;
- validation workflow;
- experiment/test prioritization.

Carbon differentiator:
- initial focus on qualified model exams and construction-method discovery across simulation/truth modes.

Long-term overlap:
- experiment allocation / Physics Intelligence.

## Compute / orchestration

Examples include Rescale, cloud HPC and NVIDIA infrastructure.

Carbon relationship:
- mostly enabling/partner layer;
- potential distribution/integration route.

```text
competitor
candidate supplier
truth-source integrator
customer
OEM partner
evidence consumer
```

This is strategically valuable.

Do not define the market so narrowly that every adjacent engineering-AI company must be an enemy.

# 6. Wedge

> **Carbon Evidence Audit**

This makes Carbon adoptable without requiring a platform replacement.

Second wedge:

> **Carbon Sponsored Discovery Program**

Customer outsources structured competitive search while retaining a qualified independent judge.

# 9. Initial segment priority

Direct customer discovery priority:

1. aerospace / defense / space;
2. energy / turbomachinery / industrial physics.

Strategic channel / partner track:

3. CAE / engineering software vendors.

Reasons:
- expensive simulation/testing;
- high consequence of wrong physical models;
- meaningful design/optimization value from fast models;
- technical organizations with identifiable budget owners;
- private/proprietary physics creates barriers that Carbon's private evidence architecture can address.

Do not expand vertical count until buyer evidence supports it.

# 11. Network advantage

The network is a supply-side scaling mechanism.

Investor test:

> **Can Carbon obtain better or more diverse model-construction progress per relevant dollar/time than a purely centralized research team?**

Relevant evidence:
- submissions/hypotheses per cost;
- diversity;
- verified frontier advances;
- time to frontier improvement;
- external specialist participation;
- central-vs-network comparator performance.

No measured advantage means no earned decentralization claim.

# 13. Category proof from adjacent markets

Public market/category observations relevant to positioning:

- PhysicsX announced a $300M Series C in June 2026 at approximately $2.4B valuation and positions itself as an AI-native engineering platform.
- Neural Concept markets an AI-first engineering platform embedded into CAD/CAE workflows and reports enterprise OEM adoption.
- Monolith markets AI software for engineering testing/validation and test-plan optimization.
- Rescale markets a digital engineering platform combining HPC, simulation data and AI Physics.
- Dassault Systèmes reported €5.64B of 2025 software revenue, with €4.62B subscription/support and 82% recurring software revenue; Industrial Innovation software represented €3.13B.
- Siemens reports its Digital Industries software business has a high recurring mix and includes PLM, simulation, EDA and adjacent engineering software.

These support the thesis that engineering software and Physics AI are meaningful enterprise categories. They do not prove Carbon's exact TAM.

# 14. Positioning laws

1. **Do not compete for full-stack ownership unless it is strategically necessary.**
2. **Be useful to customers regardless of which permitted model-building stack they use.**
3. **Independent evidence is the wedge; external competitive discovery is the differentiated scale layer.**
4. **Qualification and lifecycle turn one-off evidence into recurring infrastructure.**
5. **Model/tool neutrality expands partnership surface.**
6. **Market-size claims must be bottom-up and non-overlapping.**
7. **Token value is not company revenue.**
8. **The investor narrative must distinguish current product maturity from future category ambition.**
