## 1. Executive decision

Keep the launch-critical sequence:

```text
Wave A closeout
    → Wave B science-authoring contracts
    → Wave C real single-Challenge vertical
    → Wave D first human scientific qualification
    → Wave H frontier promotion
    → Wave I event-bound settlement
    → mechanism-complete mainnet
```

One qualified fixed-viscosity Burgers Challenge remains sufficient for the first qualified-testnet scientific gate. The generic MMS platform, multi-regime portfolio, thermal/CHT program, hybrid-solver product, Product Qualification system, Landscape, and Physics Intelligence do not become first-mainnet prerequisites.

The Science-Backed GTM study enters the roadmap through three tracks:

1. **Launch-critical evidence:** work required to qualify the first Burgers exam.
2. **Compatibility hooks:** bounded schema and invariant additions that avoid future architectural dead ends.
3. **Parallel research and commercial work:** separately authorized, interruptible work that cannot contend with the mainnet path.

## 3. Wave A

Finish A11 and A12 under their current bounded contracts. Do not add scientific evidence storage, Challenge-health analytics, MMS, product qualification, hybrid execution, or commercial workflow scope to Wave A.

Customer-job interviews and reference-economics discovery may proceed as non-code Strategy work. They produce hypotheses and candidate contexts of use, not LIVE Challenge or product authority.

## 4. Approved Wave B compatibility criteria

Wave B already owns the critical scientific object model. Integrate the study through existing tickets rather than adding a broad program.

### B-02A: task, population, SamplingPlan, and canonical case

The contract should preserve, where applicable:

- target, sampling, stress, practice, qualification, deployment, and realized-evidence populations as distinct semantics;
- query/observation population and censoring provenance;
- evidence-campaign identity that prevents manufactured cases from silently becoming the target population;
- canonical-case identity usable by analytic, numerical, manufactured, experimental, and later hybrid reference evidence.

### B-02B: candidate assembly compatibility

Keep the declarative Strategy boundary. Permit only a provisional, Challenge-registered compatibility seam for fixed learned-component roles such as warm start, preconditioner action, coarse correction, residual correction, or subdomain operator.

Wave B does not authorize arbitrary solver graphs or participant code. The exact hybrid product/system qualification identity remains a post-launch decision.

### B-03: generator API

Expose enough evidence for deterministic replay, support and exclusion checks, strata, duplicate detection, generator failure, and censoring. Do not build a universal multi-physics generator.

### B-04: `ReferencePolicy` / `TruthAsset`

Support explicit evidence roles:

- analytic or semi-analytic primary;
- manufactured-solution verification anchor;
- converged numerical primary or witness;
- methodologically independent cross-code witness;
- experimental or industrial validation anchor;
- qualified accelerator/surrogate reference;
- prospectively registered hybrid reference policy.

Every role binds applicability, uncertainty, implementation, environment, provenance, failure, disagreement, and independence evidence. MMS normally verifies implementation; it does not establish target-population relevance or physical validation by itself.

### B-05: `MeasurementContract`

Bind the claimed property, observables, numerical operator, discretization, applicability, numerical/reference floor, uncertainty, stratum applicability, role, and qualification evidence.

### B-06: D1-D12 Dossier

Allow evidence links for MMS refinement and observed-order campaigns, planted-defect tests, independent witnesses, reference disagreement, generator-oracle adversarial tests, decision-resolution studies, and residual limitations.

Hard guard:

> MMS verification alone cannot satisfy target-population adequacy, physical model validation, context-of-use adequacy, or product qualification.

### B-07B and B-E1/B-E2/B-E3

- Preserve scientifically meaningful failures across the score distribution.
- Keep infrastructure failure distinct from negative scientific evidence.
- Retain reference, reconstruction, and finite-sampling variability.
- Preserve unsupported, uncertain, disagreement, numerical-failure, and infrastructure-failure states.
- Record what each evidence source can support and what it cannot support.

### B-GATE negative tests

Prove that:

- MMS evidence cannot activate LIVE by itself;
- component evidence cannot silently become an assembled-system product claim;
- product or commercial evidence cannot enter lean scoring;
- another regime cannot inherit Burgers reference authority;
- reference failure cannot become candidate failure.

These criteria do not activate Wave B or authorize implementation outside the active ticket process.

## 5. Wave C and Wave D

Wave C implements one real fixed-viscosity Burgers vertical:

```text
protected case generation
→ Cole-Hopf primary reference where applicable
→ independent conservative numerical witness
→ producer-independent reconstruction
→ protected measurement and scoring
→ evidence receipts
→ testnet-capable transport
```

Wave D decides whether that exact exam earns LIVE authority. Its Dossier should address the physical mapping, population, SamplingPlan, generator conformance, reference implementation and witness, disagreement/failure regions, measurement floors, generator-oracle bias, reconstruction/evaluation variance, finite decision resolution, secrecy, censoring, limitations, and human Launch Bar decision.

A generic MMS Factory is not required when the exact Burgers evidence is otherwise adequate. Challenge-specific MMS may still support witness or measurement verification when the Dossier requires it.

## 6. Conditional pre-mainnet research

Mainnet work has priority. A bounded MMS, reference, portfolio, or hybrid research ticket may run before mainnet only when:

- required B-04/B-05 contracts are ratified;
- the ticket has separate ownership, branch, files, and authority;
- it does not consume a launch-critical engineering, scientific, security, protocol, owner-review, or incident-response bottleneck;
- it cannot delay a critical merge or evidence campaign;
- the project owner authorizes the exact ticket;
- the work pauses upon material contention.

This creates no standing authorization or reserved resource allocation.

Candidate research tracks, when separately authorized:

- **R-MMS-01:** MMS Factory v0 for Poisson, diffusion, nonlinear conduction, linear elasticity, and one interface problem;
- **R-REF-01:** reusable analytic/numerical/witness evidence runner;
- **R-HYB-01:** offchain neural warm-start or preconditioner bake-off against the same conventional solver and final acceptance rule.

Research outputs do not enter official scoring or create LIVE or product claims.

## 7. Branch after the first qualified testnet

```text
                     ┌→ Wave H → Wave I → mechanism-complete mainnet
Wave D / G4 complete ├→ portfolio science
                     ├→ commercial and product qualification
                     └→ evidence memory and prospective Physics Intelligence
```

The mainnet branch does not wait for product, thermal, MMS-platform, or Landscape completion.

The portfolio-science branch should select each new regime prospectively and give it a new population, SamplingPlan, generator, reference policy, measurements, Dossier, Score Pack, and Challenge identity. The first post-Burgers regime remains unresolved until reference evidence and customer learning are reviewed.

The commercial branch may investigate ValidationPacks, solver acceleration, bounded surrogates, sponsored construction search, and thermal/laminar CHT. Thermal and CHT remain a wedge hypothesis until customer and Product Battery evidence support a bounded product claim.

## 8. Hybrid-system decision boundary

Before launch, preserve only the compatibility seam and this invariant:

> Evidence for one component cannot silently qualify the assembled engineering system.

After launch, before any production hybrid Product Battery or Qualification Record implementation, Carbon must decide the qualified-object identity, component/system evidence boundaries, outer-solver and discretization bindings, integration and acceptance rules, fallback, runtime/hardware identity, escalation, and requalification triggers.

## 9. Commercial milestones remain separate

| Milestone | Meaning |
|---|---|
| C0 — Job evidence | A repeated, expensive engineering decision is documented |
| C1 — Reference feasibility | A credible reference campaign and bounded context of use exist |
| C2 — Paid evidence or acceleration pilot | A customer funds a ValidationPack or hybrid experiment |
| C3 — Product qualification | One exact artifact/system passes a Product Battery |
| C4 — Lifecycle evidence | Deployment, escalation, drift, and requalification evidence exist |

None of these creates or changes a scientific score, frontier event, emission, or settlement entitlement.

## 10. Owner decisions

1. One qualified fixed-viscosity Burgers Challenge is sufficient for the first qualified-testnet scientific gate.
2. A generic MMS Factory is not a universal testnet or mainnet prerequisite.
3. The exact hybrid product/system qualification identity is deferred to a post-launch review.
4. The Wave B compatibility criteria above are approved for incorporation into existing ticket acceptance criteria without expanding the board.
5. Mainnet has priority; pre-mainnet research is separately authorized, opportunistic, and interruptible.
6. Customer-job intake and thermal/CHT discovery are owned by the Carbon project owner and `Harshaa765`; scientific authority remains separate.
7. The first post-Burgers regime remains unresolved pending prospective review of reference evidence and customer learning.

## 11. Explicit non-goals

This plan does not authorize:

- production thresholds, solver tolerances, SamplingPlan sizes, or qualification criteria;
- a universal MMS, Julia, or SciML truth oracle;
- multi-regime LIVE activation without regime-specific qualification;
- arbitrary hybrid construction graphs in Wave B;
- product qualification from subnet rank;
- thermal/CHT product claims without customer and Product Battery evidence;
- Landscape influence on a live exam;
- Physics Intelligence claims from retrospective fit.
